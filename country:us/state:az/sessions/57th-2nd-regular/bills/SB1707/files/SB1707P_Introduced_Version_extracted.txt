SB1707 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; artificial intelligence; border security State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SB 1707 Introduced by Senator Rogers AN ACT Appropriating monies to the department of public safety. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; department of public safety; artificial intelligence; border security A. The sum of $5,000,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the department of public safety for artificial intelligence relating to border security. B. The department of public safety may distribute monies appropriated in subsection A of this section to local law enforcement agencies to enforce border security by using artificial intelligence.
