HB2066 - 572R - I Ver PREFILED    DEC 16 2025 REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; Apache Junction; technology; connectivity State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HB 2066 Introduced by Representative Blackman AN ACT Appropriating monies to the state treasurer. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; state treasurer; broadband and fiber optic networks The sum of $8,000,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the state treasurer for distribution to the city of Apache Junction for the expansion of broadband and fiber optic networks.
