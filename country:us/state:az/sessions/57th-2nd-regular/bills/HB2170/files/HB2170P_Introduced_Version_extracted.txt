HB2170 - 572R - I Ver PREFILED    JAN 08 2026 REFERENCE TITLE: state contracts; foreign adversary; prohibition State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HB 2170 Introduced by Representative Diaz AN ACT amending title 41, chapter 23, article 3, Arizona Revised Statutes, by adding section 41-2553; relating to the procurement code. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Title 41, chapter 23, article 3, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended by adding section 41-2553, to read: START_STATUTE41-2553. State contracts; certification letter; civil penalty; applicability; definitions A. A company domiciled in the people's republic of China may not bid on, submit a proposal for or enter into a contract with a state agency for goods or services. Each company that submits a bid or proposal for a state contract for goods or services shall submit a certification letter to the department certifying that the company is not a company domiciled in the people's republic of China. B. If the department determines that a company has submitted a certification letter that is false, all of the following must occur: 1. The company is liable for a civil penalty of $100,000. 2. The state agency or the department shall terminate the contract with the company. 3. The company may not bid on any state contracts for at least sixty months. C. Notwithstanding subsection a of this section, a state agency may enter into a contract for goods manufactured by a company domiciled in the people's republic of China if both of the following apply: 1. There are no other reasonable options for the procurement of the specific goods. 2. Not procuring the specific goods would pose a greater threat to this state than the threat associated with the manufacture of the goods by a company domiciled in the people's republic of China. D. This section does not apply to the department of public safety. E. For the purposes of this section: 1. "Company": (a) Means any of the following that exists for the purpose of making a profit: (i) A sole proprietorship. (ii) An organization. (iii) A corporation. (iv) An association. (v) A partnership. (vi) A joint venture. (vii) A limited partnership. (viii) A limited liability partnership. (ix) A limited liability company. (x) Any other entity or business association. (b) Includes any of the following: (i) A wholly owned subsidiary. (ii) A majority owned subsidiary. (iii) A parent company. (iv) An affiliate of any entity or business association. 2. "Domicile" means any of the following: (a) The country where a company is registered, incorporated, headquartered, issued or listed. (b) The country where the company's affairs are primarily completed. (c) The country where the majority of ownership share is held. END_STATUTE Sec. 2. Short title This act may be cited as the "Protection Procurement Act".
