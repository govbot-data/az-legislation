ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2170: state contracts; foreign adversary; prohibition
DIAZ FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Prohibits a company domiciled in the People's Republic of China and that is
controlled by or majority owned by the People's Republic of China's
government, military or ruling political party from bidding on, submitting a
proposal or entering into certain contracts with a state agency.
2. Specifies that such companies cannot bid on, submit a proposal or enter into
a contract with a state agency for electronic or information technology, rather
than for goods or services.
3. Includes certifying that the company is not reselling products from a
company that is domiciled in the People's Republic of China in the
requirements for the certification letter to the Department of Administration.
4. Stipulates that a company must knowingly submit a false certification letter
for the outlined sanctions to occur.
5. Removes the exemption for the Department of Public Safety.
6. Defines controlled and electronic or information technology.
