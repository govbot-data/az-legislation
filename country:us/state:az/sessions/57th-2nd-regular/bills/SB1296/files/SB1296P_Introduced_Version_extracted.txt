SB1296 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: health insurance; private employers; coverage State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SB 1296 Introduced by Senators Fernandez: Alston, Diaz, Epstein, Sears, Sundareshan AN ACT amending title 38, chapter 4, article 4, arizona revised statutes, by adding section 38-659; relating to compensation and insurance. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Title 38, chapter 4, article 4, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended by adding section 38-659, to read: START_STATUTE38-659. Private employers; natural persons; state health care plan; rules A. Private employers and natural persons who are not employed by this state may participate in this state's health care plans. B. The department of administration may adopt rules to implement this section. END_STATUTE
