HB2898 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; Ganado Chapter; waterline project State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HB 2898 Introduced by Representative Tsosie AN ACT Appropriating monies to the department of administration. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; department of administration; Ganado Chapter; waterline project The sum of $980,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the department of administration to distribute to the Ganado Chapter of the Navajo Nation for construction of a waterline project located in the Ganado Chapter.
