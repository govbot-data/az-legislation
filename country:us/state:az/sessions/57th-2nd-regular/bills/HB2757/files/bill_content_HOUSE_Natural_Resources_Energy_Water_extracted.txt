4 45-553. Transportation of groundwater withdrawn in Butler
5 Valley groundwater
6 A. THE LA PAZ COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS MAY WITHDRAW groundwater
7 may be withdrawn from land owned by this state or by a political
8 subdivision of this state THAT LA PAZ COUNTY OWNS OR LEASES in the Butler
9 Valley groundwater basin for transportation to an initial active
10 management area OUTSIDE OF THE BUTLER VALLEY GROUNDWATER BASIN, SUBJECT TO
11 EITHER OF THE FOLLOWING CONDITIONS:
12 1. THE GROUNDWATER IS USED SOLELY BY THE COUNTY OR BY A POLITICAL
13 SUBDIVISION OR MUNICIPAL PROVIDER LOCATED WITHIN THE COUNTY FOR THE
14 COUNTY'S, POLITICAL SUBDIVISION'S OR MUNICIPAL PROVIDER'S OWN USE.
15 2. THE GROUNDWATER IS SOLD OR LEASED TO A MULTI-COUNTY WATER
16 CONSERVATION DISTRICT ESTABLISHED PURSUANT TO TITLE 48, CHAPTER 22 FOR USE
17 ONLY BY THE MULTI-COUNTY WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICT TO MEET THE
18 DISTRICT'S REPLENISHMENT OBLIGATIONS [, PROVIDED THAT THE TOTAL CUMULATIVE
19 VOLUME OF GROUNDWATER SOLD OR LEASED PURSUANT TO THIS PARAGRAPH DOES NOT
20 EXCEED NINETY PERCENT OF THE TOTAL CUMULATIVE VOLUME OF GROUNDWATER
21 AVAILABLE IN THE BUTLER VALLEY GROUNDWATER BASIN TO A DEPTH OF ONE
22 THOUSAND TWO HUNDRED FEET].
23 B. Title to land in the Butler Valley groundwater basin that is
24 owned by this state or a political subdivision of this state and from
25 which groundwater is withdrawn for transportation to an initial active
26 management area FOR THE PURPOSES OF THIS SECTION may be sold, exchanged or
27 otherwise conveyed only to this state or to another political subdivision
28 of this state LA PAZ COUNTY.
