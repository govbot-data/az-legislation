HB4062 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; Arizona historical society; operations State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HB 4062 Introduced by Representatives Connolly: Garcia AN ACT Appropriating monies to the arizona historical society. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; Arizona historical society; continued operations and public services; intent A. The sum of $775,500 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the Arizona historical society for continued operations and public services. B. The legislature intends that the appropriation made in subsection A of this section be considered ongoing funding in future years.
