FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2134: critical infrastructure; foreign adversaries; prohibition
KUPPER FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Removes the requirement for the Corporation Commission (Commission) to
produce a map of Arizona that details geographic areas serviced by critical
communications infrastructure and where equipment that is manufactured by
Chinese companies are located.
2. Changes technology to equipment.
3. Removes requirements from the Commission to inspect all critical
infrastructure and equipment or conduct continuous monitoring or universal
physical inspections and states the Commission will implement a risk-based
oversight program that includes the following;
a. a requirement that each government entity and critical infrastructure has
competed a self-certification;
b. randomized audit conducted on a statistically significant sample of critical
infrastructure service providers and critical communication infrastructure
providers each year;
c. a targeted audit if there is a creditable intelligence complaint, federal
advisory or identified risk presented;
d. a provision to rely on determinations, advisories or prohibition that are
issued by a federal agency with national security, cybersecurity or
communications equipment in the nation; and
e. safe harbor protections for critical infrastructure service providers and
critical infrastructure providers that rely on manufacture certification and
supply-chain disclosures if there are no evidence of misrepresentation.
4. Excludes passenger vehicles from transportation systems and services.
5. Alters the agreement or contract process that is granted by the Commission.
6. States that the Department of Emergency and Military Affairs (DEMA) will
establish a secure and dedicated communications channel for critical
infrastructure providers and military installations across Arizona.
