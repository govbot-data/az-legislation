4 28-914. Use of portable wireless communication device while

7 A. Unless a motor vehicle is parked or stopped pursuant to section
8 28-645, subsection A, paragraph 3 or section 28-851, a person may not
9 operate a motor vehicle on a street or highway if the person does [either]
10 [ANY] of the following:
11 1. Physically holds or supports with any part of the person's body
12 either of the following:
13 (a) A portable wireless communication device, except that a person
14 may use a portable wireless communication device with an earpiece,
15 headphone device or device worn on a wrist to conduct a voice-based
16 communication.
17 (b) A stand-alone electronic device.
18 2. Writes, sends or reads any text-based communication, including a
19 text message, AN instant message, AN email or internet data, on a portable
20 wireless communication device or stand-alone electronic device. This
21 paragraph does not apply to either of the following:
22 (a) The use of voice-based communications, including through the
23 use of a portable wireless communication device or stand-alone electronic
24 device, to direct the writing, sending, reading or other communicating of
25 any text-based communication.
26 (b) The use of a portable wireless communication device or
27 stand-alone device when used in a hands-free manner for:
