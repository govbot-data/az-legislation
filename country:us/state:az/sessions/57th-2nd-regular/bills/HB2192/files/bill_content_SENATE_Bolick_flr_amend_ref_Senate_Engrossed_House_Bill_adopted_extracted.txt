FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
1. Excludes, from the definition of online hosting platform:
a) telecommunications services as prescribed by the federal Telecommunications Act of 1996;
and
b) broadband internet access service as prescribed by the Code of Federal Regulations.
2. Makes technical and conforming changes.
