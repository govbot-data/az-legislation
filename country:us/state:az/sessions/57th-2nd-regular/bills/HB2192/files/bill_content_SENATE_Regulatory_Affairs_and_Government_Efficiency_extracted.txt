4 ARTICLE 6.2. CONTENT CREATION
5 23-345. Definitions
6 IN THIS ARTICLE, UNLESS THE CONTEXT OTHERWISE REQUIRES:
7 1. "CONTENT CREATOR":
8 (a) MEANS A PARENT OR LEGAL GUARDIAN WHO RESIDES IN THIS STATE AND
9 WHO CREATES AN IMAGE OR VIDEO CONTENT THAT IS PERFORMED IN THIS STATE IN
10 EXCHANGE FOR COMPENSATION.
11 (b) INCLUDES A PARENT OR LEGAL GUARDIAN WHO IS A:
12 (i) VLOGGER.
13 (ii) PODCASTER.
14 (iii) ONLINE INFLUENCER.
15 (iv) STREAMER.
16 (c) DOES NOT INCLUDE ANY MINOR WHO PRODUCES THE MINOR'S OWN VIDEO
17 CONTENT.
18 2. FRIENDING FUNCTION DOES NOT INCLUDE SUBSCRIBING TO CONTENT FROM
19 ANOTHER ACCOUNT HOLDER.
20 3. "MINOR" MEANS A NATURAL PERSON WHO IS UNDER EIGHTEEN YEARS OF
21 AGE AND WHO RESIDES IN THIS STATE.
22 4. "ONLINE HOSTING PLATFORM" MEANS A PUBLIC OR SEMIPUBLIC
23 INTERNET-BASED SERVICE OR APPLICATION THAT HAS ACCOUNT HOLDERS IN THIS
24 STATE AND THAT MEETS ANY OF THE FOLLOWING CRITERIA:
25 (a) CONNECTS ACCOUNT HOLDERS WITH OTHER ACCOUNT HOLDERS, WHICH
26 ALLOWS THE ACCOUNT HOLDERS TO INTERACT SOCIALLY WITHIN THE SERVICE OR
27 APPLICATION.

Senate Amendments to H.B. 2192
1 (b) ALLOWS PUBLIC POSTING OR ACCOUNT HOLDER-GENERATED CONTENT
2 WITHOUT VISIBILITY BEING LIMITED TO A PARTICULAR SOCIAL GRAPH.
3 (c) ALLOWS INTERACTION WITH OTHER ACCOUNT HOLDERS' CONTENT OUTSIDE
4 A LIMITED SOCIAL GRAPH THAT INCLUDES CONTENT RECOMMENDATIONS FROM
5 INDIVIDUALS THAT THE ACCOUNT HOLDER DOES NOT FOLLOW.
6 5. "SOCIAL GRAPH" MEANS THE LIST OF INDIVIDUALS ON AN ONLINE
7 HOSTING PLATFORM THAT AN ACCOUNT HOLDER IS CONNECTED TO BY USING A
8 FRIENDING FUNCTION OR OTHER SIMILAR FUNCTIONALITY.
9 6. "UNIQUELY IDENTIFIABLE":
10 (a) MEANS AN INDIVIDUAL WHO IS ABLE TO IDENTIFY ANOTHER INDIVIDUAL
11 BASED ON PERSONAL IDENTIFYING INFORMATION THAT INCLUDES:
12 (i) THE NAME.
13 (ii) THE CONTACT INFORMATION.
14 (iii) AN IMAGE.
15 (iv) A VOICE.
16 (b) DOES NOT INCLUDE THE INDIVIDUAL WHO SUBMITS A PRIVACY REMOVAL
17 REQUEST.
18 7. "VIDEO CONTENT":
19 (a) MEANS CONTENT SHARED ON AN ONLINE HOSTING PLATFORM IN EXCHANGE
20 FOR COMPENSATION.
21 (b) INCLUDES CONTENT THAT IS CREATED BY A CONTENT CREATOR.
22 23-346. Content creators; compensation to minors; reporting
23 period; civil action
24 A. IF A MINOR IS INCLUDED IN VIDEO CONTENT AT ANY TIME WITHIN A
25 PREVIOUS TWELVE-MONTH PERIOD, THE MINOR SHALL BE COMPENSATED IF ALL OF THE
26 FOLLOWING CRITERIA ARE MET:
27 1. AT LEAST THIRTY PERCENT OF THE CONTENT CREATOR'S COMPENSATED
28 VIDEO CONTENT PRODUCED WITHIN A THIRTY-DAY PERIOD INCLUDES THE LIKENESS,
29 NAME OR PHOTOGRAPH OF THE MINOR. CONTENT PERCENTAGE IS MEASURED BY THE
30 PERCENTAGE OF TIME THE LIKENESS, NAME OR PHOTOGRAPH OF THE MINOR VISUALLY
31 APPEARS OR IS THE SUBJECT OF AN ORAL NARRATIVE IN THE VIDEO SEGMENT AS
32 COMPARED TO THE TOTAL LENGTH OF THE SEGMENT.
33 2. THE NUMBER OF VIEWS RECEIVED PER VIDEO SEGMENT ON THE ONLINE
34 HOSTING PLATFORM SATISFIES THE ONLINE HOSTING PLATFORM'S THRESHOLD TO
35 GENERATE COMPENSATION OR THE CONTENT CREATOR RECEIVES ACTUAL COMPENSATION
36 FOR THE VIDEO CONTENT EQUAL TO OR MORE THAN $.10 PER VIEW.
37 3. THE CONTENT CREATOR RECEIVES ACTUAL COMPENSATION FOR THE VIDEO
38 CONTENT OF AT LEAST $15,000 IN THE PRIOR TWELVE-MONTH PERIOD.
39 B. A MINOR WHO IS AT LEAST THIRTEEN YEARS OF AGE MAY PRODUCE,
40 CREATE AND PUBLISH THEIR OWN VIDEO CONTENT AND IS ENTITLED TO ALL
41 COMPENSATION FOR THEIR OWN CONTENT CREATION.
42 C. A CONTENT CREATOR WHO FEATURES A MINOR IN THEIR VIDEO CONTENT AS
43 PRESCRIBED IN SUBSECTION A OF THIS SECTION SHALL MAINTAIN ALL OF THE
44 FOLLOWING RECORDS UNTIL THE MINOR REACHES TWENTY-ONE YEARS OF AGE:

26 23-347. Trust account for minors; requirements; civil action
27 A. A CONTENT CREATOR SHALL COMPENSATE A MINOR WHO WAS FEATURED IN
28 THE CONTENT CREATOR'S VIDEO CONTENT. THE CONTENT CREATOR SHALL DEPOSIT
29 GROSS EARNINGS ON THE VIDEO CONTENT THAT INCLUDES THE LIKENESS, NAME OR
30 PHOTOGRAPH OF THE MINOR IN A TRUST ACCOUNT THAT IS TO BE PRESERVED FOR THE
31 BENEFIT OF THE MINOR UNTIL THE MINOR IS AT LEAST EIGHTEEN YEARS OF AGE
32 ACCORDING TO EITHER OF THE FOLLOWING DISTRIBUTIONS:
33 1. IN WHICH ONLY ONE MINOR MEETS THE CONTENT THRESHOLD PRESCRIBED
34 IN SECTION 23-346, SUBSECTION A, THE PERCENTAGE OF TOTAL GROSS EARNINGS ON
35 ANY VIDEO SEGMENT, INCLUDING THE LIKENESS, NAME OR PHOTOGRAPH OF THE
36 MINOR, THAT IS EQUAL TO OR MORE THAN HALF OF THE CONTENT PERCENTAGE THAT
37 INCLUDES THE MINOR.
38 2. IN WHICH MORE THAN ONE MINOR MEETS THE CONTENT THRESHOLD
39 PRESCRIBED IN SECTION 23-346, SUBSECTION A AND A VIDEO SEGMENT INCLUDES
40 THE LIKENESS, NAME OR PHOTOGRAPH OF MORE THAN ONE MINOR, THE PERCENTAGE
41 PRESCRIBED IN PARAGRAPH 1 OF THIS SUBSECTION MUST BE EQUALLY DIVIDED
42 BETWEEN ALL MINORS, REGARDLESS OF ANY DIFFERENCE IN CONTENT PERCENTAGE
43 PROVIDED THE MINORS AND DEPOSITED IN A SEPARATE TRUST FOR EACH MINOR.
44 B. AT A MINIMUM, A TRUST ACCOUNT REQUIRED IN THIS SECTION MUST:
45 1. BE HELD BY A BANK, CORPORATE FIDUCIARY OR TRUST COMPANY.

20 23-348. Content removal requests; online hosting platforms;
21 private right of action
22 A. ON THE EFFECTIVE DATE OF THIS SECTION, AN INDIVIDUAL WHO IS AT
23 LEAST EIGHTEEN YEARS OF AGE OR IS AN EMANCIPATED MINOR AND WHO WAS
24 FEATURED IN A CONTENT CREATOR'S VIDEO CONTENT AS A MINOR MAY REQUEST THAT
25 THE CONTENT CREATOR DELETE OR EDIT THE VIDEO CONTENT FROM THE ONLINE
26 HOSTING PLATFORM TO REMOVE THE MINOR'S UNIQUELY IDENTIFIABLE INFORMATION.
27 B. ONLINE HOSTING PLATFORMS SHALL PROVIDE AN EASILY ACCESSIBLE
28 MECHANISM THROUGH WHICH A REMOVAL REQUEST CAN BE SUBMITTED TO DELETE OR
29 EDIT THE VIDEO CONTENT FROM THE ONLINE HOSTING PLATFORM TO REMOVE THE
30 MINOR'S UNIQUELY IDENTIFIABLE INFORMATION. THE MECHANISM MAY INCLUDE
31 TOOLS THAT ENABLE INDIVIDUALS TO SUBMIT A REQUEST DIRECTLY TO THE CONTENT
32 CREATOR.
33 C. REMOVAL REQUESTS MUST INCLUDE ALL INFORMATION REASONABLY
34 REQUESTED TO IDENTIFY THE MINOR IN THE VIDEO CONTENT.
35 D. ONLINE HOSTING PLATFORMS THAT DO NOT PROVIDE A MECHANISM THAT
36 ENABLES AN INDIVIDUAL TO SUBMIT A REQUEST DIRECTLY TO THE CONTENT CREATOR
37 SHALL NOTIFY THE CONTENT CREATOR WITHIN A REASONABLE TIME PERIOD BUT NOT
38 LONGER THAN THIRTY DAYS ON RECEIPT OF A REQUEST TO REMOVE VIDEO CONTENT.
39 E. THE CONTENT CREATOR SHALL DELETE THE VIDEO CONTENT FROM THE
40 ONLINE HOSTING PLATFORM OR EDIT THE VIDEO CONTENT FROM THE ONLINE HOSTING
41 PLATFORM TO REMOVE THE MINOR'S UNIQUELY IDENTIFIABLE INFORMATION WITHIN
42 SEVENTY-TWO HOURS OF RECEIPT.
43 F. IF THE CONTENT CREATOR FAILS TO REMOVE THE CONTENT WITHIN A
