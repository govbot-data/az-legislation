SB1439 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: municipalities; counties; recycling; prohibition State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SB 1439 Introduced by Senator Hoffman AN ACT amending sections 9-500.07 and 11-269, Arizona Revised Statutes; amending title 49, chapter 4, article 1, Arizona Revised Statutes, by adding section 49-707; relating to recycling. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Section 9-500.07, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended to read: START_STATUTE9-500.07. Recycling and waste reduction; definition a. A city or town shall provide its residents with an opportunity to engage in recycling and waste reduction. B. Notwithstanding subsection A of this section, as of the effective date of this amendment to this section, a city or town may not recommend or require that a resident of the city or town place any product in a recycling bin that is not being actively recycled at present time. C. For the purposes of this section, "actively recycled" means a product that is reprocessed into a new product. END_STATUTE Sec. 2. Section 11-269, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended to read: START_STATUTE11-269. Recycling and waste reduction; definition a. A county shall provide its residents with an opportunity to engage in recycling and waste reduction. B. Notwithstanding subsection A of this section, as of the effective date of this amendment to this section, a county may not recommend or require that a resident of the county place any product in a recycling bin that is not being actively recycled at present time. C. For the purposes of this section, "actively recycled" means a product that is reprocessed into a new product. END_STATUTE Sec. 3. Title 49, chapter 4, article 1, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended by adding section 49-707, to read: START_STATUTE49-707. Recycling; new recycling materials; prohibition; definition A. Notwithstanding any other law, as of the effective date of this section, a municipality, county or other political subdivision of this state may not recommend or require that a resident place any product in a recycling bin that is not being actively recycled at present time. B. For the purposes of this section, "actively recycled" means a product that is reprocessed into a new product. END_STATUTE
