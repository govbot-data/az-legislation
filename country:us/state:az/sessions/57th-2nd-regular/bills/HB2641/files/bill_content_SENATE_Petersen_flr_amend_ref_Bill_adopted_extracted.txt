2 Section 1. Section 36-1696, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended to
3 read:
4 36-1696. Firefighting foam; prohibition; public airport
5 exception; definitions
6 A. Beginning January 1, 2020, EXCEPT AS OTHERWISE PROVIDED IN THIS
7 SUBSECTION, a person, local government or state agency may not discharge
8 or otherwise use for training purposes ANY PURPOSE class B firefighting
9 foam that contains intentionally added PFAS chemicals unless otherwise
10 required by law or federal regulation. THE OWNER OR OPERATOR OF A PUBLIC
11 AIRPORT IS NOT REQUIRED TO COMPLY WITH THIS SUBSECTION UNTIL FROM AND
12 AFTER DECEMBER 31, 2030.
13 B. Beginning January 1, 2020, a person, local government or state
14 agency may not discharge or otherwise use for testing purposes class B
15 firefighting foam that contains intentionally added PFAS chemicals unless
16 otherwise required by law or federal regulation or the testing facility
17 has implemented appropriate containment, treatment and disposal measures
18 to prevent uncontrolled releases of firefighting foam into the
19 environment.
20 C. This section does not restrict the manufacture, sale or
21 distribution of class B firefighting foam that contains intentionally
22 added PFAS chemicals or the discharge or other use of class B firefighting
23 foam in emergency firefighting or fire prevention operations.
24 D. B. For the purposes of this section:
25 1. "Class B firefighting foam" means foam designed to extinguish a
26 fire that involves a flammable liquid.
27 2. "Local government" includes any county, city, town, fire
28 district or other special district that provides firefighting services.

Senate Amendments to H.B. 2641
1 3. "PFAS chemicals" means perfluoroalkyl and polyfluoroalkyl
2 substances that are a class of fluorinated organic chemicals containing at
3 least one fully fluorinated carbon atom, that are designed to be fully
4 functional in class B firefighting foam formulations and that are used as
5 a firefighting agent.
6 4. "Testing" includes calibration testing, conformance testing and
7 fixed system testing.
8 4. "PUBLIC AIRPORT" MEANS AN AIRPORT THAT IS OWNED BY A POLITICAL
9 SUBDIVISION OF THIS STATE OR THAT IS OTHERWISE OPEN TO THE PUBLIC.
