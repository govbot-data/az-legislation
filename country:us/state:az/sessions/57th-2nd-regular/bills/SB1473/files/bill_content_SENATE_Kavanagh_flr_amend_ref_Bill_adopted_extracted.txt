Bill Number: S.B. 1473
Kavanagh Floor Amendment
Reference to: printed bill
Amendment drafted by: Leg. Council
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
1. Removes assisted living facilities from the prescribed municipal and county occupancy limit
regulations.
2. Prohibits a municipality or county from imposing a cap on the number of residents who may reside
at an assisted living home that is lower than 10 residents, rather than lower than the number of
residents allowed by statute, rule or an issued license.
3. Prohibits a municipality or county from restricting an assisted living home from operating on a
property that is zoned for residential use based on the number of residents who reside at the home
if the number is 10 or fewer, rather than in compliance with statute, rule or an issued license.
4. Removes the prohibition on a municipality or county from restricting an assisted living home from
operating on a property that is zoned for residential use based on its classification as a health care
institution.
5. Allows a municipality or county to enforce and maintain zoning, building, fire, safety and health
codes and regulations (codes and regulations), rather than only enforce applicable codes and
regulations.
6. Removes the requirement for the codes and regulations to be applied uniformly to all similar
residential dwellings.
7. Allows the codes and regulations to be enforced and maintained if the effect is not imposing an
occupancy limit on an assisted living home that is more restrictive than allowed by an issued
license, rather than by statute, rule or an issued license.
8. States that the occupancy limit, rather than the occupancy and zoning regulation, of an assisted
living home is of statewide concern and not subject to further regulation by a municipality or
county.
9. States that assisted living occupancy regulations do not prohibit or restrict a municipality or county
from adopting a distance requirement in accordance with statute.
10. Applies the assisted living occupancy regulations to an assisted living home that is licensed after
the effective date.
11. Makes conforming changes.
