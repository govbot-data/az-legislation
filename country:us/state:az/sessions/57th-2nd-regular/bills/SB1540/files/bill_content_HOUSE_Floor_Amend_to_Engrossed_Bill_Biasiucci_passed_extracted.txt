ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
SB 1540: motor fuel theft; classifications
BIASIUCCI FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Amends the offense, theft of motor fuel, to require that the person breaching the
retail fuel dispenser must have used a device constructed for the purpose of
fraudulently altering or interrupting the normal operation of the dispenser.
2. Amends the classification for committing theft of motor fuel to be a class 5 felony
only if more than 50 gallons of motor fuel is taken.

1 The bill as proposed to be amended is reprinted as follows:
2 Section 1. Title 13, chapter 18, Arizona Revised Statutes, is
3 amended by adding section 13-1821, to read:
4 13-1821. Theft of motor fuel; classification; definition
5 A. A PERSON COMMITS THEFT OF MOTOR FUEL IF THE PERSON WILFULLY,
6 KNOWINGLY AND WITHOUT AUTHORIZATION:
7 1. BREACHES A RETAIL FUEL DISPENSER OR ACCESSES ANY INTERNAL
8 PORTION OF A RETAIL FUEL DISPENSER [WITH ANY DEVICE THAT IS CONSTRUCTED
9 FOR THE PURPOSE OF FRAUDULENTLY ALTERING, MANIPULATING OR INTERRUPTING THE
10 NORMAL OPERATION OF A RETAIL FUEL DISPENSER].
11 2. PHYSICALLY TAMPERS WITH, MANIPULATES, REMOVES, REPLACES OR
12 INTERRUPTS ANY MECHANICAL OR ELECTRONIC COMPONENT THAT IS LOCATED ON THE
13 INTERNAL OR EXTERNAL PORTION OF A RETAIL FUEL DISPENSER.
14 3. USES ANY FORM OF ELECTRONIC COMMUNICATION TO FRAUDULENTLY ALTER,
15 MANIPULATE OR INTERRUPT THE NORMAL OPERATION OF A RETAIL FUEL DISPENSER.
16 B. A VIOLATION OF SUBSECTION A, PARAGRAPH 1 OF THIS SECTION IS A
17 CLASS 6 FELONY, EXCEPT THAT IF THE VIOLATION RESULTS IN A PERSON OBTAINING
18 [MORE THAN FIFTY GALLONS OF] MOTOR FUEL IT IS A CLASS 5 FELONY.
19 C. A VIOLATION OF SUBSECTION A, PARAGRAPH 2 OR 3 OF THIS SECTION IS
20 A CLASS 5 FELONY.
21 D. FOR THE PURPOSES OF THIS SECTION, "MOTOR FUEL" HAS THE SAME
22 MEANING PRESCRIBED IN SECTION 28-5601.
