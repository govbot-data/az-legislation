1 The bill as proposed to be amended is reprinted as follows:
2 Section 1. Title 13, chapter 18, Arizona Revised Statutes, is
3 amended by adding section 13-1821, to read:
4 13-1821. Theft of motor fuel; classification; definition
5 A. A PERSON COMMITS THEFT OF MOTOR FUEL IF THE PERSON WILFULLY,
6 KNOWINGLY AND WITHOUT AUTHORIZATION:
7 1. BREACHES A RETAIL FUEL DISPENSER OR ACCESSES ANY INTERNAL
8 PORTION OF A RETAIL FUEL DISPENSER.
9 [2. POSSESSES ANY DEVICE THAT IS CONSTRUCTED FOR THE PURPOSE OF
10 FRAUDULENTLY ALTERING, MANIPULATING OR INTERRUPTING THE NORMAL OPERATION
11 OF A RETAIL FUEL DISPENSER.]
12 [3.] [2.] PHYSICALLY TAMPERS WITH, MANIPULATES, REMOVES, REPLACES
13 OR INTERRUPTS ANY MECHANICAL OR ELECTRONIC COMPONENT THAT IS LOCATED ON
14 THE INTERNAL OR EXTERNAL PORTION OF A RETAIL FUEL DISPENSER.
15 [4.] [3.] USES ANY FORM OF ELECTRONIC COMMUNICATION TO FRAUDULENTLY
16 ALTER, MANIPULATE OR INTERRUPT THE NORMAL OPERATION OF A RETAIL FUEL
17 DISPENSER.
18 B. A VIOLATION OF SUBSECTION A, PARAGRAPH 1 [OR 2] OF THIS SECTION
19 IS A CLASS 6 FELONY, EXCEPT THAT IF THE VIOLATION RESULTS IN A PERSON
20 OBTAINING MOTOR FUEL IT IS A CLASS 5 FELONY.
21 C. A VIOLATION OF SUBSECTION A, PARAGRAPH [3 OR 4] [2 OR 3] OF THIS
22 SECTION IS A CLASS 5 FELONY.
23 D. FOR THE PURPOSES OF THIS SECTION, "MOTOR FUEL" HAS THE SAME
24 MEANING PRESCRIBED IN SECTION 28-5601.
25 Enroll and engross to conform
26 Amend title to conform
And, as so amended, it do pass
