4 36-447.03. Surveys; statement of deficiencies; initiation of
5 complaint investigations; time frames; record
6 retention
7 A. [BEGINNING JULY 1, 2027,] THE DEPARTMENT SHALL ISSUE ANY
8 STATEMENT OF DEFICIENCIES TO A NURSING CARE INSTITUTION WITHIN TEN

10 DATE THAT THE STATEMENT OF DEFICIENCIES IS ISSUED, THE NURSING CARE
11 INSTITUTION SHALL COMPLY WITH THE STATEMENT OF DEFICIENCIES AND SUBMIT A
12 PLAN OF CORRECTION TO THE DEPARTMENT.]
13 B. THE DEPARTMENT MAY NOT INITIATE A COMPLAINT INVESTIGATION
14 RELATING TO A NURSING CARE INSTITUTION MORE THAN TWELVE MONTHS AFTER THE
15 ALLEGED BASIS OF THE INCIDENT OCCURRED UNLESS THE COMPLAINT INVOLVES
16 ALLEGATIONS OF CRIMINAL ABUSE OR NEGLECT [AND] [OR] A FAILURE BY A PERSON
17 WHO HAS A DUTY TO REPORT THE POSSIBLE ABUSE OR NEGLECT PURSUANT TO SECTION
18 13-3620 OR 46-454. IF RECORDS THAT ARE MAINTAINED PURSUANT TO ARTICLE 4
19 OF THIS CHAPTER HAVE BEEN LAWFULLY DESTROYED PURSUANT TO RETENTION
20 REQUIREMENTS, THE DEPARTMENT MAY NOT CITE THE LICENSEE FOR FAILURE TO
21 PRODUCE THE RECORDS FOR AN INVESTIGATION INITIATED MORE THAN TWELVE MONTHS
22 AFTER THE ALLEGED BASIS OF THE INCIDENT OCCURRED.
23 C. THE DEPARTMENT SHALL INITIATE COMPLAINT INVESTIGATIONS RELATING
24 TO NURSING CARE INSTITUTIONS CONSISTENT WITH THE CENTERS FOR MEDICARE AND
25 MEDICAID SERVICES TIME FRAMES BASED ON THE TRIAGED SEVERITY LEVEL OF EACH
26 COMPLAINT.
27 36-447.04. Off-site preliminary reviews; compliance
28 inspections; complaint investigations
29 [THE DEPARTMENT MAY CONDUCT AN OFF-SITE PRELIMINARY REVIEW OF A
30 COMPLAINT OR SELF-REPORT USING WRITTEN OR VERBAL COMMUNICATIONS OR
31 DOCUMENTATION FROM THE LICENSEE TO DETERMINE WHETHER AN ON-SITE COMPLIANCE
32 INSPECTION OR COMPLAINT INVESTIGATION IS NECESSARY. IF THE REVIEW
33 CONFIRMS THAT THE LICENSEE HAS IMPLEMENTED SYSTEMS AND PROCESSES THAT MAKE
34 FURTHER VIOLATIONS UNLIKELY AND THE LICENSEE PROVIDES SUFFICIENT EVIDENCE
35 OF COMPLIANCE, THE DEPARTMENT SHALL CLOSE THE COMPLAINT AND NO FURTHER
36 INVESTIGATION IS REQUIRED.]

39 36-2402. Quality assurance activities; sharing of quality
40 assurance information; immunity; confidentiality
41 A. State health care providers, hospitals and outpatient surgical
42 centers shall, and other health care entities may, conduct quality
43 assurance activities.
44 B. A health care entity may share quality assurance information
45 with appropriate state licensing or certifying agencies and with licensed
46 health care providers who are the subject of quality assurance
47 activities. A hospital may share quality assurance information with other

3 C. A health care entity may share quality assurance information
4 with other health care entities only for the purpose of conducting quality
5 assurance activities.
6 D. A health care entity or person that provides or receives

10 for or in the course of quality assurance activities is not subject to

13 E. Quality assurance activities conducted by state, county or local
14 medical, pharmacy and dental associations and societies on behalf of a

16 the facility for which the review activities are conducted.
17 F. Health care entities may jointly conduct quality assurance
18 activities.
19 G. This section does not relieve any health care entity from
20 liability arising from the treatment of a patient or from negligent
21 credentialing decisions.
