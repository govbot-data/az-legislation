SB1001 - 572R - I Ver PREFILED    NOV 17 2025 REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; older individuals; blind State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SB 1001 Introduced by Senator Carroll AN ACT appropriating monies to the department of economic security. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; department of economic security; older individuals who are blind program The sum of $1,000,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the department of economic security for the older individuals who are blind program.
