Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona:

Section 1. Appropriation; department of economic security; older individuals who are blind program

The sum of $1,000,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the department of economic security for the older individuals who are blind program.
