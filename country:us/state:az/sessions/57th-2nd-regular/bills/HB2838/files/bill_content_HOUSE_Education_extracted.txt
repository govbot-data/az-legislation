5 A. The education and workforce innovation pilot program is
6 established within the state board of education to develop and test
7 innovative models of education that are aligned to student outcomes,
8 attainment and workforce readiness. The state board of education shall:
9 1. Administer the pilot program [and require each public school
10 that participates in the pilot program to offer students at least two of
11 the following:
12 (a) A concurrent enrollment course.
13 (b) An industry-recognized credential for a high-demand or
14 high-wage occupation in this state.
15 (c) A youth apprenticeship program].
16 2. Establish an advisory committee that is composed of not more
17 than seven total members who are appointed by the executive director of
18 the state board of education. The advisory committee must include members
19 who represent the state board of charter schools and members who represent
20 the state board of education. Advisory committee members are not eligible
21 to receive compensation but are eligible for reimbursement of expenses
22 pursuant to title 38, chapter 4, article 2, Arizona Revised Statutes. In
23 the first meeting of the advisory committee, the advisory committee shall
24 select one member to serve as chairperson. The state board of education
25 shall provide staff and meeting space for the advisory committee
26 established pursuant to this paragraph. The advisory committee members
27 shall do all of the following:
28 (a) Develop and recommend to the state board of education:
