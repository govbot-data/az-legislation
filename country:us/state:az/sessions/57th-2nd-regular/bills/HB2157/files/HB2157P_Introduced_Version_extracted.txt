HB2157 - 572R - I Ver PREFILED    JAN 08 2026 REFERENCE TITLE: livestock loss inspector; appropriation State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HB 2157 Introduced by Representative Diaz AN ACT appropriating monies to the arizona game and fish department. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; Arizona game and fish department; livestock loss inspector One FTE position is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the Arizona game and fish department for a livestock loss inspector.
