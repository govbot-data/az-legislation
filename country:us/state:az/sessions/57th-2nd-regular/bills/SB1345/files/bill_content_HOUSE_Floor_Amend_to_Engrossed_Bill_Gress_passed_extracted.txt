ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
SB 1345: health facilities; anonymous complaints; prohibition
GRESS FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Removes the provision that prohibits DHS from accepting or investigating
anonymous complaints against health care institutions unless the complaint
is from the subject of the complaint allegation or a witness to the alleged
conduct in the complaint.
