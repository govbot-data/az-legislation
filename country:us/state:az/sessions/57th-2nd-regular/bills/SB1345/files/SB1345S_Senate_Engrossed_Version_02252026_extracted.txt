SB1345 - 572R - S Ver Senate Engrossed health facilities; anonymous complaints; prohibition State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SENATE BILL 1345 AN ACT Amending title 36, chapter 4, article 1, Arizona Revised Statutes, by adding section 36-420.06; relating to health care institutions. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1.1. Title 36, chapter 4, article 1, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended by adding section 36-420.06, to read: START_STATUTE36-420.06. Anonymous complaints; prohibition; exceptions The DEPARTMENT of health services may not accept or investigate an anonymous complaint against a health care institution unless the complaint is from either of the following: 1. The subject of the allegation within the complaint. 2. A witness to the conduct being alleged in the complaint.END_STATUTE
