Werner Floor Amendment
Reference to: printed bill
Amendment drafted by: Leg. Council
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
• Removes the Arizona Health Care Cost Containment System from the prohibition on accepting or
investigating anonymous complaints that are not submitted by the subject of or a witness to the
allegation.
