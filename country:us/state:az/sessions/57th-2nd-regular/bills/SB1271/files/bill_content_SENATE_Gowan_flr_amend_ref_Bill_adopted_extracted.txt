Bill Number: S.B. 1271
Gowan Floor Amendment
Reference to: printed bill
Amendment drafted by: Leg. Council
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
• Specifies that the prohibition on a municipality imposing a fine or penalty on a business or
commercial entity based on the frequency of public safety services does not apply to faulty or
repeated nonemergency alerts from alarm systems.
