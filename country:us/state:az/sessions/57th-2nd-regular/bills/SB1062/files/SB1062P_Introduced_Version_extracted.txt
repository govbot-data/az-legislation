SB1062 - 572R - I Ver PREFILED    DEC 19 2025 REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; Route 60; intersection improvements State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SB 1062 Introduced by Senator Rogers AN ACT Appropriating monies to the Department of Transportation. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; department of transportation; US Route 60 and Superstition mountain drive; left turn lane; intersection improvements The sum of $1,000,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the department of transportation for costs associated with planning, designing, construction and the acquisition of right-of-way or easements for an additional left turn lane and other intersection improvements at the intersection of U.S. Route 60 and Superstition Mountain Drive.
