SB1362 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; resilience plan State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SB 1362 Introduced by Senators Kuby: Alston, Gabaldón, Hatathlie;  Representatives Garcia, Gutierrez, Márquez, Sandoval, Stahl Hamilton AN ACT Appropriating monies to the university of ARizona. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; university of Arizona; resilience plan The sum of $2,000,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the university of Arizona, Aegis consortium for the preparation of a state resilience plan.
