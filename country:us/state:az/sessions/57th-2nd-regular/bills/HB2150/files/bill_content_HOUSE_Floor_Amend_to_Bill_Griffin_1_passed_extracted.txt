6 41-3030.19. State land department; termination July 1, 2030
7 A. THE STATE LAND DEPARTMENT TERMINATES ON JULY 1, 2030.
8 B. TITLE 37, CHAPTERS 1 AND 2 AND THIS SECTION ARE REPEALED ON
9 JANUARY 1, 2031.
10 Sec. 3. Purpose
11 Pursuant to section 41–2955, subsection B, Arizona Revised Statutes,
12 the legislature continues the state land department to have charge of and
13 control over all lands owned by this state, except lands under the
14 specific use and control of state institutions, and the resources in and
15 on those lands and to regulate the use of and access to those lands and
16 resources as prescribed by the enabling act, the Constitution of Arizona
17 and state law and to perform such other functions as prescribed by law.
