FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2150: continuation; state land department
KOLODIN FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Removes the specification that an adjusted royalty rate, adopted by the State
Land Commissioner for renewed mineral and mineral material leases, be
adopted by rule.
2. Specifies that the Conceptual Land Use Plan's authority applies to State
Lands that have not been annexed as of the effective date of this act.
