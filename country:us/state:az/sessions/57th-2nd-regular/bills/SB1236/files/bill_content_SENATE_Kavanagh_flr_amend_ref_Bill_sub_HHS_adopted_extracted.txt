Kavanagh SUBSTITUTE Floor Amendment
Reference to: printed bill
Amendment drafted by: Leg. Council
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
1. Requires AHCCCS to redetermine, on a quarterly basis, the eligibility of able-bodied adult
members who are not American Indians or Alaska Natives.
2. Makes technical and conforming changes.
