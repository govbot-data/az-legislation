FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2429: vacation rentals; short-term rentals; occupancy
S/E: same subject
BLISS FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Specifies a local government may suspend a local regulatory license or permit
after one verified violation relating to the failure to remedy a documented
building or fire code violation that presents a serious and immediate threat to
public health or safety.
2. Allows, rather than requires, a local government that requires a local
regulatory permit or license to adopt an ordinance requiring sex offender
background checks on a vacation rental or short-term rental booking guest.
3. Makes clarifying changes.
