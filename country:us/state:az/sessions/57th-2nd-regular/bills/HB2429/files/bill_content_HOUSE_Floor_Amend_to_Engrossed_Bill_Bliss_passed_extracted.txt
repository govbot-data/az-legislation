ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2429: short-term rentals; vacation rentals; occupancy
BLISS FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Requires, rather than allows, a local government to adopt an ordinance
requiring sex offender background checks on a vacation or short-term rental
booking guest.
2. Includes a delayed effective date of January 1, 2027.
