Kavanagh Floor Amendment

Amendment drafted by: Mason Holler
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
• Specifies that the cardiopulmonary resuscitation and automated external defibrillator training must
be consistent with national standards in addition to being based on the most current national
evidence-based emergency cardiovascular care guidelines.
