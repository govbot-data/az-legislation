ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HCR2056: medical mandates; right to refuse
KUPPER FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Incorporates the HHS Committee amendment.
2. Prohibits government entities from mandating, requiring, coercing, or
compelling an individual to administer accept or receive a medical product or
treatment as a condition of employment, education, access to any facilities,
participation in services, or the exercise of any right or benefit.

2 1. Article II, Constitution of Arizona, is proposed to be amended

5 38. Right to refuse medical mandate, product or
6 treatment; definition
7 SECTION 38. A. THE RIGHT TO REFUSE MEDICAL MANDATES IS
8 HEREBY RECOGNIZED AND PROTECTED AS A FUNDAMENTAL AND INHERENT
9 RIGHT OF EVERY INDIVIDUAL. A GOVERNMENT ENTITY MAY NOT
10 MANDATE, REQUIRE, COERCE OR COMPEL ANY INDIVIDUAL TO ACCEPT,
11 RECEIVE OR ADMINISTER ANY MEDICAL PRODUCT OR TREATMENT THAT
12 INVOLVES INVASION OF OR AFFIXING ANY ITEM OR ARTICLE TO THE
13 BODY FOR ANY REASON OR PURPOSE, INCLUDING AS A CONDITION OF
14 EMPLOYMENT, EDUCATION, ENTRY OR ACCESS TO ANY [PUBLIC]
15 FACILITY OR SPACE, PARTICIPATION IN SERVICES OR THE EXERCISE
16 OF ANY RIGHT, PRIVILEGE OR BENEFIT.
17 B. THIS SECTION DOES NOT:
18 1. LIMIT A COURT OF COMPETENT JURISDICTION IN A
19 SPECIFIC INDIVIDUAL'S CASE BASED ON A FINDING OF CLEAR AND
20 CONVINCING EVIDENCE THAT THE INDIVIDUAL IS A DANGER TO SELF OR
21 OTHERS, INCLUDING THE ORDERING OF TREATMENT FOR A MENTAL
22 HEALTH OR SUBSTANCE USE DISORDER INVOLVING THE ADMINISTRATION
23 OF MEDICATIONS OR OTHER TREATMENTS TO ADDRESS MENTAL HEALTH OR
24 A SUBSTANCE USE DISORDER.
25 2. APPLY TO PERSONS WHO ARE IN THE CUSTODY OF A LAW
26 ENFORCEMENT AGENCY OR ON PROBATION FOR AN OFFENSE OR LIMIT OR
