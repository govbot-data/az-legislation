7 Section 1. Title 15, chapter 7, article 1, Arizona Revised
8 Statutes, is amended by adding section 15-712.02, to read:
9 15-712.02. Instruction on fetal development; requirements;
10 parental notice and opt out
11 A. THE STATE BOARD OF EDUCATION SHALL:
12 1. DETERMINE THE GRADE LEVEL OR LEVELS IN WHICH INSTRUCTION ON
13 FETAL AND PRENATAL DEVELOPMENT IS APPROPRIATE.
14 2. ADOPT ACADEMIC STANDARDS IN THE AREA OF SCIENCE THAT REQUIRE
15 INSTRUCTION ON FETAL AND PRENATAL HUMAN DEVELOPMENT THAT IS CONSISTENT
16 WITH THE REQUIREMENTS PRESCRIBED IN THIS SECTION AND THAT IS
17 AGE-APPROPRIATE, FACTUAL AND FOCUSED ON ONLY PRENATAL HUMAN DEVELOPMENT AS
18 A BIOLOGICAL PROCESS.
19 3. INCLUDE INSTRUCTION ON FETAL AND PRENATAL HUMAN DEVELOPMENT IN
20 THE COMPETENCY REQUIREMENTS PRESCRIBED PURSUANT TO SECTION 15-701 OR
21 15-701.01, OR BOTH, IN THE AREA OF SCIENCE.
22 4. REQUIRE THAT INSTRUCTION PROVIDED PURSUANT TO THIS SECTION
23 INCLUDE AN OVERVIEW OF ALL OF THE FOLLOWING:
24 (a) PRENATAL HUMAN DEVELOPMENT THAT EXPLAINS THE BIOLOGICAL PROCESS
25 BY WHICH A HUMAN DEVELOPS FROM A SINGLE CELL, THROUGH SUCCESSIVE STAGES,
26 BEFORE BIRTH.
27 (b) FERTILIZATION AND EARLY CELLULAR DEVELOPMENT, INCLUDING:
28 (i) THE FORMATION OF A ZYGOTE THROUGH THE UNION OF HUMAN
29 REPRODUCTIVE CELLS.
30 (ii) EARLY CELL DIVISION AND GROWTH.
31 (iii) THE DEVELOPMENT OF A BLASTOCYST.
32 (iv) IMPLANTATION OF THE BLASTOCYST INTO THE UTERINE WALL.
33 (c) THE STAGES OF PRENATAL DEVELOPMENT, INCLUDING AN OVERVIEW OF
34 ALL OF THE FOLLOWING:
35 (i) THE GERMINAL STAGE THAT DESCRIBES EARLY DEVELOPMENT FROM THE
36 TIME OF FERTILIZATION UNTIL THE TIME OF IMPLANTATION.
37 (ii) THE EMBRYONIC STAGE THAT DESCRIBES THE EARLY STRUCTURAL
38 FORMATION, INCLUDING THE DEVELOPMENT OF FOUNDATIONAL TISSUES AND EARLY
39 ORGAN SYSTEMS.
40 (iii) THE FETAL STAGE THAT DESCRIBES THE GROWTH AND MATURATION OF
41 ORGANS AND BODY SYSTEMS FROM APPROXIMATELY THE NINTH WEEK OF DEVELOPMENT
42 UNTIL BIRTH.
43 (d) MAJOR DEVELOPMENTAL MILESTONES, INCLUDING:
44 (i) THE FORMATION OF THE BRAIN AND SPINAL CORD.
45 (ii) THE DEVELOPMENT OF THE HEART AND CIRCULATORY SYSTEM.
46 (iii) THE FORMATION OF LIMBS, FACIAL FEATURES AND OTHER
47 IDENTIFIABLE HUMAN STRUCTURES.
