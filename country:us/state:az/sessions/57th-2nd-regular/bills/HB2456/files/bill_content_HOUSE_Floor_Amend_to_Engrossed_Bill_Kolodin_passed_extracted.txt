ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2456: small modular reactors; zoning; co-location
KOLODIN FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Specifies that this act applies to extra high load factor customer as defined by
the Corporation Commission.
2. Modifies definitions.
3. Removes the requirement that the Corporation Commission adopt rules to
adopt this act.
