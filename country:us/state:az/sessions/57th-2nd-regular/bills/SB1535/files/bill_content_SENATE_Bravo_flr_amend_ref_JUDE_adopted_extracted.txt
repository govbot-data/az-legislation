Bravo Floor Amendment

Committee Amendment
Amendment drafted by: Zack Dean
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
1. Specifies that a victim of sex trafficking who is convicted of prostitution and whose conviction is
subsequently vacated is eligible to petition the court to seal the person's case records immediately
following the completion of all nonmonetary terms and conditions of the person's sentence.
2. Requires the court to seal all records relating to the arrest, conviction and sentence of a person who
is convicted of prostitution as a result of being a victim of sex trafficking and whose conviction is
vacated by the court.
3. Excludes the sealed records of a sex trafficking victim from being used in any subsequent matter
or future prosecution.
