HB2740 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; office of African-American Affairs State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HB 2740 Introduced by Representatives Crews: Abeytia, Aguilar, Contreras P, Garcia, Liguori, Mathis, Peshlakai, Sandoval, Villegas AN ACT appropriating monies to the Arizona office of african-american affairs. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; Arizona office of African-American affairs The sum of $500,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the Arizona office of African-American affairs for operating expenses.
