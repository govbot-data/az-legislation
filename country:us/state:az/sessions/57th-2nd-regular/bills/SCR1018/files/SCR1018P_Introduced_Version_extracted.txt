SCR1018 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: same-sex marriage; constitutional right State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SCR 1018 Introduced by Senators Kuby: Alston, Bravo, Epstein, Gabaldón, Hatathlie, Miranda, Ortiz; Representatives Garcia, Gutierrez, Márquez, Sandoval, Stahl Hamilton A CONCURRENT RESOLUTION proposing an amendment to the Constitution of Arizona; repealing article XXX, section 1, Constitution of Arizona; amending article XXX, Constitution of Arizona, by adding a new section 1; relating to marriage. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it resolved by the Senate of the State of Arizona, the House of Representatives concurring: 1. Article XXX, section 1, Constitution of Arizona, is proposed to be repealed as follows if approved by the voters and on proclamation of the Governor: Article XXX, section 1, Constitution of Arizona, relating to marriage, is repealed. 2. Article XXX, Constitution of Arizona, is proposed to be amended by adding a new section 1 as follows if approved by the voters and on proclamation of the Governor: 1. Marriage Section 1. Marriage between two individuals shall not be prohibited or void on the basis of the sex, race, ethnicity or national origin of those individuals. 3. The Secretary of State shall submit this proposition to the voters at the next general election as provided by article XXI, Constitution of Arizona.
