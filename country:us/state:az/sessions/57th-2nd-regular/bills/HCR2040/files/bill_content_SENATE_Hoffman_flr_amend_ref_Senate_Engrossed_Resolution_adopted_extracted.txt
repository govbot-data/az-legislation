Hoffman ADD COW Floor Amendment
Reference to: Senate engrossed resolution
Amendment drafted by: Leg. Council
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
1. Removes the prohibition on school district and charter school teachers striking or engaging in an
organized work stoppage against the school and the requirement for a teacher who violates the
prohibition to forfeit outlined employment rights and benefits.
2. Makes technical and conforming changes.
