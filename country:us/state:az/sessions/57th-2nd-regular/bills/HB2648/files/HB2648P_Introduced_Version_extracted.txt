HB2648 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: appropriations; child care assistance State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HB 2648 Introduced by Representatives Liguori: Abeytia, Aguilar, Austin, Cavero, Connolly, De Los Santos, Gutierrez, Márquez, Peshlakai, Sandoval, Simacek, Stahl Hamilton, Villegas, Volk AN ACT appropriating monies to the department of economic security. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriations; department of economic security; child care assistance; exemption A. The sum of $160,000,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the department of economic security for child care assistance. B. The appropriation made in subsection A of this section is exempt from the provisions of section 35-190, Arizona Revised Statutes, relating to lapsing of appropriations.
