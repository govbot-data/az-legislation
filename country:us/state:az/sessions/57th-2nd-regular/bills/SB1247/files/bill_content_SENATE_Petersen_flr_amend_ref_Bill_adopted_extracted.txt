Bill Number: S.B. 1247
Petersen Floor Amendment
Reference to: printed bill
Amendment drafted by: Leg. Council
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
1. Allows an individual who does not receive supervisory, personal or directed care services to live
with a resident in an assisted living center or home, rather than only an assisted living center.
2. Adds an emergency clause.
