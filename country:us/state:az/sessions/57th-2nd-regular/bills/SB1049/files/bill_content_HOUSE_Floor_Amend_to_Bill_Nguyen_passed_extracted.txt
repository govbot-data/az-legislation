FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
SB 1049: spousal maintenance; income; property
NGUYEN FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Directs the court to terminate spousal maintenance, unless a written agreement
provides otherwise, if the spouse receiving maintenance remarries or if the court
finds that the spouse receiving maintenance has habitually cohabitated with an
individual in a relationship analogous to marriage.
2. Directs the court, if spousal maintenance is terminated due to one of the
aforementioned circumstances, to order the reimbursement of any improperly paid
maintenance to the spouse who originally paid the maintenance.
3. Outlines the types of evidence that the court is allowed to consider when deciding
whether a spouse has habitually cohabitated with another individual while in a
relationship analogous to marriage.
4. Allows a spouse to petition the court for termination of an award of spousal
maintenance at any time.
5. Stipulates that this Act applies to all proceedings relating to spousal maintenance
regardless of the date of the filing of a petition for dissolution of marriage or legal
separation, the date of the entry for an award of spousal maintenance or the date of
filing of a petition for modification of spousal maintenance.
