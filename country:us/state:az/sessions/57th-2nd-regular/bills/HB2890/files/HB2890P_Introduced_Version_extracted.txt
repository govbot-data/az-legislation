HB2890 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; Ganado unified school district State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HB 2890 Introduced by Representative Tsosie AN ACT Appropriating monies to the department of education. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; department of education; Ganado unified school district; safety, maintenance and renovation projects The sum of $12,772,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the department of education to distribute to the Ganado unified school district of the Navajo Nation for safety, maintenance and renovation projects in the Ganado unified school district in the following amounts: 1. District fire alarm system.                       $ 6,000,000 2. District HVAC replacement.                        $ 6,000,000 3. District vape sensors.                            $ 20,000 4. Transportation second floor exit.                 $ 12,000 5. Ganado high school flooring.                      $  250,000 6. Ganado primary school flooring.                   $  250,000 7. Ganado primary school outside stairs.             $  240,000
