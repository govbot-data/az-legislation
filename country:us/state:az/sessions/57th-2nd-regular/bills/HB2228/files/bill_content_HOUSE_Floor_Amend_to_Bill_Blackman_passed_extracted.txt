FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2228: elder abuse; registry; attorney general
BLACKMAN FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Directs the Arizona Prosecuting Attorney's Advisory Council to develop a
statewide training curriculum on Arizona mandatory reporting laws and
make it available to individuals who have a duty to report.
