HB2674 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: abating sentencing requirements; deceased defendants State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HB 2674 Introduced by Representatives Hernandez C: Hernandez A, Hernandez L, Luna-Nájera, Volk AN ACT amending section 13-106, Arizona Revised Statutes; relating to criminal defendants. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Section 13-106, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended to read: START_STATUTE13-106. Death of convicted defendant; dismissal of appellate and postconviction proceedings and defendant's sentence A. On a convicted defendant's death, the court: 1. Shall dismiss any pending appeal or postconviction proceeding. 2. Except as provided in subsection B of this section, may dismiss all requirements of the defendant's sentence. B. A convicted defendant's death does not abate the defendant's criminal conviction or sentence of imprisonment or any restitution, fine or assessment imposed by the sentencing court.  END_STATUTE
