18 Sec. 11. Legislative findings
19 A. The legislature finds that:
20 1. This state has a compelling interest in protecting children from
21 abuse and neglect and ensuring lawful, timely and effective child welfare
22 interventions.
23 2. Independent oversight improves transparency, strengthens
24 accountability, enhances public confidence and supports continuous
25 improvement in systems that are charged with child protection.
26 3. Oversight must preserve the confidentiality of child and family
27 information while allowing meaningful review of systemic performance and
28 critical incidents.
29 4. Effective oversight requires timely access to records,
30 independent staffing and clear reporting and response obligations.
31 B. The purpose of this act is to establish an independent oversight
32 body to do the following:
33 1. Evaluate the policies, practices and performance of the
34 department of child safety.
35 2. Review critical incidents and systemic risks.
36 3. Receive complaints and whistleblower information.
