38 Sec. 7. Legislative findings
39 The legislature finds:
40 1. That the public policy of this state is to promote the best
41 interests of the child by encouraging frequent, meaningful and continuing
42 contact with both parents following a legal separation or dissolution of
43 marriage.
44 2. That children benefit emotionally, academically and
45 developmentally when both parents are significantly involved in the
46 child's life.
