14 employees.

17 care.

19 following pregnancy-related services:
20 (i) Pregnancy tests.
21 (ii) Sexually transmitted infection testing.
22 (iii) Counseling for pregnant women by a licensed counselor,
23 psychologist, psychiatrist or other similarly licensed professional.
24 (iv) Ultrasound services.
25 2. May not be any of the following:
