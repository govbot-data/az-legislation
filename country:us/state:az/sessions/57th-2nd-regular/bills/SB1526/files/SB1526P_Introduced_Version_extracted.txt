SB1526 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: technical correction; AHCCCS; capitation rates State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SB 1526 Introduced by Senator Hatathlie: Representative Sandoval AN ACT amending section 36-2901.06, Arizona Revised Statutes; relating to the arizona health care cost containment system. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Section 36-2901.06, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended to read: START_STATUTE36-2901.06. Capitation rate adjustments; limitation Capitation rate adjustments shall be are limited to utilization of existing services and inflation unless policy changes, including creation or expansion of programs, have been approved by the legislature or are specifically required by federal law or court mandate. END_STATUTE
