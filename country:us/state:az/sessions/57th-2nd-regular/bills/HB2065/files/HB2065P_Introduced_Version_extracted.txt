HB2065 - 572R - I Ver PREFILED    DEC 16 2025 REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; Apache Junction; housing State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HB 2065 Introduced by Representative Blackman AN ACT Appropriating monies to the state treasurer. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; state treasurer; housing infrastructure and rehabilitation The following amounts are appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the state treasurer to distribute to the city of Apache Junction for the following purposes: 1. $6,500,000 to fund affordable housing infrastructure. 2. $3,000,000 for housing rehabilitation and blight abatement.
