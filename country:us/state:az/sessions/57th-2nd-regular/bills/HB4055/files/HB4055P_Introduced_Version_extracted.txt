HB4055 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; veterans' services; mental health State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HB 4055 Introduced by Representatives Peshlakai: Márquez AN ACT Appropriating monies to the department of veterans' services. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; department of veterans' services; grants; mental health; tribal nations A. The sum of $10,000,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the department of veterans' services for grants to veterans with post-traumatic stress disorder in this state who need a mental health counselor. B. Of the amount appropriated in subsection A of this section, the department of veterans' services shall distribute $3,000,000 to federally recognized tribal nations within this state for veterans with post-traumatic stress disorder who need a mental health counselor.
