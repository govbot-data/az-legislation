ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2022: elections; July primary; curing; observers
KOLODIN FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Decreases, from within 10 to within 7 calendar days after a general election
that includes an election for a federal office, the time frame in which a
provisional ballot must be compared to the signature roster for the precinct in
which the voter was listed.
2. Clarifies that a provisional ballot must be compared to the signature roster for
the precinct in which the voter was listed within five calendar days after the
election, rather than five business days, for any election other than one that
includes a federal election.
3. Adjusts the day on which primary elections are required to be held from the
last Tuesday in July to the second-to-last Tuesday in July and reflects that
adjustment in statute where election days are outlined as not legal holidays.
4. Amends, for candidate elections, the required day for primary or first elections
in even-numbered years to be held to the second-to-last Tuesday in July, rather
than the last Tuesday in July.
5. Clarifies that non-candidate elections, including special and recall elections,
may be held on the second-to-last Tuesday in July, rather than the last
Tuesday in July, in addition to other dates outlined by statute.
6. Removes a provision from the underlying bill that specified county recorders
or officers in charge of an election must allow signatures to be corrected not
later than the fifth calendar day, rather than the fifth business day, after a
primary, general or special election that includes a federal office.
7. Clarifies, as session law, that a person who collects signatures for the 2026
primary election and uses a petition, nomination paper form or electronic
petition before, on or after the effective date of the act with the former primary
election date of August 4, 2026 on the form or petition may lawfully submit
those signatures for the 2026 primary election to be held July 21, 2026.
8. States, as session law, that signatures submitted for the primary election with
the August 4, 2026, primary election date on the form or petition cannot be

9. Prohibits, as session law, a filing officer from rejecting petitions collected under
the August 4, 2026, primary election date and states this applies to the online
signature collection system provided by the Secretary of State.
10. Clarifies, as session law, that any city, town or county initiative petition that
is circulated before, on or after the effective date of the act and that is on a
petition form that includes the former primary election date of August 4, 2026
may lawfully submit those petitions and signatures for the primary election to
be held on July 21, 2026.
11. States, as session law, that signatures collected for a city, town or county
initiative petition with the former primary election date of August 4, 2026 and
that otherwise comply with legal requirements are deemed to be as valid as
signatures collected on an initiative petition with the newly designated
primary election date of July 21, 2026 and may not be ruled invalid due solely
to the changed primary election date.
12. Clarifies, as session law, that a prospective primary election candidate
collecting signatures does not need to file a new or amended statement of
interest based on the change in 2026 primary election date.
13. Requires, as session law, that any election item that was duly called by a city,
town or county for the August 4, 2026, primary election date must be placed on
the ballot for the newly designated primary date of July 21, 2026.
14. Adds a legislative intent clause.
