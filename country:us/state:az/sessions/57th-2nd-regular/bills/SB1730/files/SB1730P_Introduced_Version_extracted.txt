SB1730 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: Kachina Point Road State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SB 1730 Introduced by Senators Hatathlie: Alston, Gonzales, Kuby, Sundareshan AN ACT Amending title 41, chapter 4.1, article 3, Arizona Revised Statutes, by adding section 41-837.02; relating to historic names. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Title 41, chapter 4.1, article 3, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended by adding section 41-837.02, to read: START_STATUTE41-837.02. Kachina Point Road A. Notwithstanding this article, Leupp Road is designated and shall be known as "Kachina Point Road". B. All official acts, state records, documents and papers relating to Leupp Road after the effective date of this section shall include the designation "Kachina Point Road".END_STATUTE
