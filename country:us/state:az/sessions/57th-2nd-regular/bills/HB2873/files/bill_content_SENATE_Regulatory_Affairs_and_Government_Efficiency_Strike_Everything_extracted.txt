Fifty-seventh Legislature Regulatory Affairs and Government Efficiency
Second Regular Session H.B. 2873
COMMITTEE ON REGULATORY AFFAIRS AND GOVERNMENT EFFICIENCY
SENATE AMENDMENTS TO H.B. 2873
(Reference to House engrossed bill)
1 Strike everything after the enacting clause and insert:
2 "Section 1. Title 19, chapter 1, article 4, Arizona Revised
3 Statutes, is amended by adding section 19-144, to read:
4 19-144. Referendum petitions; cities and towns; notice of
5 withdrawal
6 NOTWITHSTANDING ANY OTHER LAW, A PERSON OR ORGANIZATION THAT FILES A
7 REFERENDUM PETITION WITH A CITY OR TOWN CLERK MAY WITHDRAW THE REFERENDUM
8 PETITION BY FILING A WRITTEN NOTICE OF WITHDRAWAL WITH THE CITY OR TOWN
9 CLERK AT ANY TIME BEFORE THE MEASURE HAS QUALIFIED FOR THE BALLOT PURSUANT
10 TO SECTION 19-121.04.
11 Sec. 2. Retroactivity; vote tally and canvass; prohibition
12 This act applies retroactively to all written notices of withdrawal
13 that are filed with a city or town clerk from and after December 31, 2025
14 regardless of whether the withdrawal was accepted or rejected. Votes for
15 any city or town referendum for which a written notice of withdrawal has
16 been filed from and after December 31, 2025 may not be tallied or
17 canvassed."
18 Amend title to conform
