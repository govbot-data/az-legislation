SB1498 - 572R - S Ver Senate Engrossed appropriation; police department; training center State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SENATE BILL 1498 AN ACT Appropriating monies to the state treasurer. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1.1. Appropriation; state treasurer; Paradise Valley; law enforcement training center The sum of $2,500,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the state treasurer to distribute to the Paradise Valley police department to construct a law enforcement training center that includes classrooms and facilities for firearm training and physical training.
