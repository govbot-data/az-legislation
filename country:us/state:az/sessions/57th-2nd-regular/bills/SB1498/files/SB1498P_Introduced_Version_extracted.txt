SB1498 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; police department; training center State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SB 1498 Introduced by Senators Werner: Angius AN ACT Appropriating monies to the department of public safety. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; department of public safety; Paradise Valley; law enforcement training center The sum of $2,500,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the department of public safety to distribute to the Paradise Valley police department to construct a law enforcement training center that includes classrooms and facilities for firearm training and physical training.
