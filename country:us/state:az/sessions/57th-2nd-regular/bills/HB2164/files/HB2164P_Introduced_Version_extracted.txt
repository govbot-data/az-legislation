HB2164 - 572R - I Ver PREFILED    JAN 08 2026 REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; transportation project; Reay Lane State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HB 2164 Introduced by Representative Diaz AN ACT Appropriating monies to the department of transportation. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; department of transportation; Reay Lane improvements The sum of $3,730,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the department of transportation to distribute to Graham county to improve Reay Lane between U.S. Route 70 and Safford Bryce Road.
