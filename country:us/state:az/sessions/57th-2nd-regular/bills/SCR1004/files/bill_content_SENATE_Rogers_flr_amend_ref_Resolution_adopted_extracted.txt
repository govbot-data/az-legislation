Bill Number: S.C.R. 1004
Rogers Floor Amendment
Reference to: printed resolution
Amendment drafted by: Leg. Council
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
1. Restores the ability of local authorities and state agencies to use photo enforcement systems when
specified conditions are met, as well as the requirements relating to the use of photo enforcement
systems.
2. Prohibits a local authority or state agency from using a photo enforcement system unless the local
authority or state agency has a signed contract to operate the photo enforcement system by
December 31, 2026.
3. Requires a local authority that has a signed photo enforcement contract to seek approval of the
voters at the general election immediately following the effective date to continue operation of the
photo enforcement system.
4. Requires a local authority to cease operation of a photo enforcement system within 90 days of the
general election at which the voters fail to approve the operation of the photo enforcement system.
5. Stipulates that, if the voters approve the photo enforcement system, the local authority may
continue to operate the system for 10 years.
6. Requires a local authority that has received voter approval for the use of photo enforcement to
seek voter approval every 10 years to continue use of the photo enforcement system.
