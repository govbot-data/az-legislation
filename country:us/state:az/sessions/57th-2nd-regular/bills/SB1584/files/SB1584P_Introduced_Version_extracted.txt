SB1584 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; DOC; recruitment; retention State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SB 1584 Introduced by Senator Payne AN ACT Appropriating monies to the state department of corrections. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; state department of corrections; recruitment and training The sum of $1,000,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the state department of corrections for recruitment and training.
