HB2983 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; Houck Chapter; capital improvements State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HB 2983 Introduced by Representative Tsosie AN ACT Appropriating monies to the department of administration. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; department of administration; Houck Chapter; Navajo Nation; capital improvement projects The sum of $5,152,600 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the department of administration to distribute to the Navajo Nation for the Houck Chapter for capital improvement projects.
