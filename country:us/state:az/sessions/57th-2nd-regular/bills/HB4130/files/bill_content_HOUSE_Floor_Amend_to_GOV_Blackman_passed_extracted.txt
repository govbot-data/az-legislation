ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 4130: growth zones; housing and economic
BLACKMAN FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Requires a proposed budget amendment to be placed on a regular meeting
agenda for consideration and discussion before a vote at the next regular
meeting.
2. Prohibits the proposed budget amendments to be placed on a consent agenda.
