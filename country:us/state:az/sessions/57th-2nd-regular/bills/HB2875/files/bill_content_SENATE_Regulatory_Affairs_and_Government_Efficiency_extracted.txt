2 Section 1. Title 9, chapter 4, article 8, Arizona Revised Statutes,
3 is amended by adding section 9-500.54, to read:
4 9-500.54. Prohibition on unmanned aircraft regulation;
5 exceptions; definitions
6 A. EXCEPT AS OTHERWISE PROVIDED BY LAW, A CITY OR TOWN MAY NOT
7 ENACT OR ADOPT ANY ORDINANCE, POLICY OR RULE THAT RELATES TO THE OWNERSHIP
8 OR OPERATION OF AN UNMANNED AIRCRAFT, UNMANNED AIRCRAFT SYSTEM OR
9 COMMERCIAL DRONE DELIVERY SYSTEM OR OTHERWISE ENGAGE IN REGULATING THE
10 OWNERSHIP OR OPERATION OF AN UNMANNED AIRCRAFT, UNMANNED AIRCRAFT SYSTEM
11 OR COMMERCIAL DRONE DELIVERY SYSTEM. ANY ORDINANCE, POLICY OR RULE THAT
12 VIOLATES THIS SUBSECTION, WHETHER ENACTED OR ADOPTED BY THE CITY OR TOWN
13 BEFORE OR AFTER AUGUST 6, 2016, IS VOID.
14 B. THIS SECTION DOES NOT:
15 1. PROHIBIT A CITY OR TOWN FROM ENACTING OR ADOPTING AN ORDINANCE
16 OR RULE ON ANY OF THE FOLLOWING:
17 (a) OPERATING OR USING A PUBLIC UNMANNED AIRCRAFT THAT IS OWNED BY
18 THE CITY OR TOWN.
19 (b) TAKING OFF OR LANDING A MODEL AIRCRAFT IN A PARK OR PRESERVE
20 THAT IS OWNED BY THE CITY OR TOWN IF EITHER OF THE FOLLOWING APPLIES:
21 (i) THERE ARE OTHER PARKS OR PRESERVES THAT ARE WITHIN THE CITY OR
22 TOWN AND THAT ARE AVAILABLE FOR OPERATING MODEL AIRCRAFT.
23 (ii) THE CITY OR TOWN HAS ONLY ONE PARK OR PRESERVE THAT IS WITHIN
24 THE CITY OR TOWN.
25 (c) LAND USE APPROVALS FOR TAKING OFF, DOCKING AND LANDING A
26 COMMERCIAL DRONE DELIVERY SYSTEM IN AN AREA ZONED FOR RESIDENTIAL USE OR
27 WITHIN A ONE HUNDRED FIFTY-FOOT RADIUS OF THE OUTER BOUNDARY OF THE AREA
28 ZONED FOR RESIDENTIAL USE.

1 (d) TAKING OFF, DOCKING OR LANDING A COMMERCIAL DRONE DELIVERY
2 SYSTEM FROM A CITY OR TOWN OWNED FACILITY OR PROPERTY.
3 2. PROHIBIT A CITY OR TOWN FROM REQUIRING OR ENFORCING EXISTING
4 REGULATIONS THAT ARE NOT EXPLICITLY FOR AN UNMANNED AIRCRAFT, UNMANNED
5 AIRCRAFT SYSTEM OR A COMMERCIAL DRONE DELIVERY SYSTEM.
6 3. ALLOW A CITY OR TOWN TO WITHHOLD ISSUING A PERMIT OR APPROVAL
7 FOR OPERATING, TAKING OFF, DOCKING OR LANDING AN UNMANNED AIRCRAFT,
8 UNMANNED AIRCRAFT SYSTEM OR A COMMERCIAL DRONE DELIVERY SYSTEM IN AN AREA
9 THAT IS NOT ZONED FOR RESIDENTIAL USE.
10 4. LIMIT A CITY'S OR TOWN'S AUTHORITY TO APPLY AND ENFORCE
11 GENERALLY APPLICABLE ZONING, LAND USE, SITE PLAN, BUILDING, FIRE AND
12 PUBLIC SAFETY REGULATIONS TO THE PHYSICAL LOCATION OF FACILITIES THAT ARE
13 USED FOR TAKING OFF, DOCKING OR LANDING AN UNMANNED AIRCRAFT OR COMMERCIAL
14 DRONE DELIVERY SYSTEM WITHIN [ONE MILE] [TWO AND ONE-HALF MILES] OF A
15 [MEDIUM OR] LARGE HUB AIRPORT'S LATITUDE AND LONGITUDE AS RECORDED IN THE
16 FEDERAL AVIATION ADMINISTRATION'S MASTER AIRPORT RECORDS, IF THE
17 REGULATIONS DO NOT GOVERN THE FLIGHT PATH, NAVIGATION OR OPERATION OF THE
18 UNMANNED AIRCRAFT AND DO NOT HAVE THE EFFECT OF PROHIBITING COMMERCIAL
19 DRONE DELIVERY SYSTEMS WITHIN THE JURISDICTION [AND IF THE COMMERCIAL
20 DRONE DELIVERY SYSTEM OPERATOR CONSULTS WITH THE AIRPORT REGARDING THE
21 REGULATIONS].
22 C. FOR THE PURPOSES OF THIS SECTION:
23 1. "COMMERCIAL DRONE DELIVERY SYSTEM" MEANS AN UNMANNED AIRCRAFT
24 SYSTEM ENGAGED IN THE DELIVERING OF GOODS VIA DRONE AND IS APPROVED TO
25 OPERATE FOR THAT PURPOSE BY THE FEDERAL AVIATION ADMINISTRATION.
26 2. "MODEL AIRCRAFT" MEANS AN UNMANNED AIRCRAFT THAT IS:
27 (a) CAPABLE OF SUSTAINED FLIGHT IN THE ATMOSPHERE.
28 (b) FLOWN WITHIN THE VISUAL LINE OF SIGHT OF THE PERSON OPERATING
29 THE MODEL AIRCRAFT.
30 (c) FLOWN FOR HOBBY OR RECREATIONAL PURPOSES.
31 3. "PUBLIC UNMANNED AIRCRAFT" MEANS AN UNMANNED AIRCRAFT OR
32 UNMANNED AIRCRAFT SYSTEM THAT IS OPERATED BY A PUBLIC AGENCY FOR A
33 GOVERNMENT-RELATED PURPOSE.
34 4. "UNMANNED AIRCRAFT" MEANS AN AIRCRAFT, INCLUDING AN AIRCRAFT
35 COMMONLY KNOWN AS A DRONE, THAT IS OPERATED WITHOUT THE POSSIBILITY OF
36 DIRECT HUMAN INTERVENTION FROM WITHIN OR ON THE AIRCRAFT.
37 5. "UNMANNED AIRCRAFT SYSTEM" MEANS AN UNMANNED AIRCRAFT AND
38 ASSOCIATED ELEMENTS, INCLUDING ANY COMMUNICATION LINKS AND COMPONENTS THAT
39 CONTROL THE UNMANNED AIRCRAFT.

13 Sec. 3. Section 13-3729, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended to
14 read:
15 13-3729. Unlawful operation of model or unmanned aircraft;
16 state preemption; classification; definitions
17 A. It is unlawful for a person to operate a model aircraft or a
18 civil unmanned aircraft if the operation:
19 1. Is prohibited by a federal law or regulation that governs
20 aeronautics, including federal aviation administration regulations.
21 2. Interferes with a law enforcement, firefighter or emergency
22 services operation.
23 B. It is unlawful for a person to operate or use an unmanned
24 aircraft or unmanned aircraft system to intentionally photograph or loiter
25 over or near a critical facility in the furtherance of any criminal
26 offense.
27 C. Except as authorized by law, a city, town or county may not
28 enact or adopt any ordinance, policy or rule that relates to the ownership
29 or operation of an unmanned aircraft or unmanned aircraft system or
30 otherwise engage in the regulation of the ownership or operation of an
31 unmanned aircraft or an unmanned aircraft system. Any ordinance, policy or
32 rule that violates this subsection, whether enacted or adopted by the
33 city, town or county before or after August 6, 2016, is void.
34 D. C. This section does not:
35 1. Apply to a person or entity that is authorized or allowed by the
36 federal aviation administration to operate or use an unmanned aircraft
37 system if the person's or entity's operation or use complies with the
38 authorization granted to the person or entity or with federal aviation
39 administration rules.
40 2. Prohibit a city, town or county from enacting or adopting
41 ordinances or rules on the operation or use of a public unmanned aircraft
42 that is owned by the city, town or county.
43 3. Prohibit a city, town or county from enacting or adopting
44 ordinances or rules that regulate the takeoff or landing of a model
45 aircraft in a park or preserve owned by the city, town or county if:
46 (a) There are other parks or preserves that are within the city,
47 town or county and that are available for model aircraft operation.
