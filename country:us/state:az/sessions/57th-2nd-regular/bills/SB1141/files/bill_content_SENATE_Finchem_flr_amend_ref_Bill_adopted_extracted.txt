Finchem Floor Amendment
Reference to: printed bill
Amendment drafted by: Leg. Council
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
• Requires a presidential elector to cast an electoral college vote for an appropriate successor
candidate who is properly nominated by the party of the winning candidate in the event of a
winning candidate for U.S. President or Vice President becoming incapacitated, in addition to
dying or withdrawing before the date that presidential electors are scheduled to meet.
