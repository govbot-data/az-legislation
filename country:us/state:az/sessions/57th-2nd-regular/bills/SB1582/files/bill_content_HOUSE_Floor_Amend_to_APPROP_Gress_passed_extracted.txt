ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
SB 1582: appropriation; school safety program
S/E: backyard fowl; pets; planned communities
GRESS FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Specifies an association cannot prohibit or limit a dog or cat by breed, size or
weight, rather than a household pet.
2. Clarifies the association may adopt reasonable rules regarding the number or
behavior of dogs or cats.
3. Removes provisions relating to regulating fowl.
