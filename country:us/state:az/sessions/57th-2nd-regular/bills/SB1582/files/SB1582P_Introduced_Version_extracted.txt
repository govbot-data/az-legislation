SB1582 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; school safety program State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SB 1582 Introduced by Senator Payne AN ACT Appropriating monies to the department of education. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; department of education; school safety program The sum of $3,200,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the department of education for the school safety program established by section 15-154, Arizona Revised Statutes.
