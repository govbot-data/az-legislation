HB2884 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; Black Mesa; veterans center State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HB 2884 Introduced by Representative Tsosie AN ACT Appropriating monies to the department of veterans' services. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; department of veterans' services; Black Mesa Chapter; veterans center The sum of $2,500,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the department of veterans' services to distribute to the Black Mesa Chapter of the Navajo Nation to survey, design and construct a veterans center for the Black Mesa Chapter.
