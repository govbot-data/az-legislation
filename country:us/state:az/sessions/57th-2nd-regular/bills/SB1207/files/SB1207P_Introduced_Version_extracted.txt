SB1207 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; study; transportation infrastructure State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SB 1207 Introduced by Senators Carroll: Shamp AN ACT Appropriating monies to the department of transportation. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; department of transportation; study; U.S. Route 60 and State Route 303 The sum of $150,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the department of transportation to conduct a study to assess financing mechanisms for new development to support regional and statewide transportation infrastructure along U.S. Route 60 and State Route 303.
