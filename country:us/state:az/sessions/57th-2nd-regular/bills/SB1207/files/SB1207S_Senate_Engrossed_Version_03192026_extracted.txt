SB1207 - 572R - S Ver Senate Engrossed appropriation; study; transportation infrastructure State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SENATE BILL 1207 AN ACT Appropriating monies to the department of transportation. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; department of transportation; study; U.S. Route 60 and State Route 303 The sum of $150,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the department of transportation to conduct a study to assess financing mechanisms for new development to support regional and statewide transportation infrastructure along U.S. Route 60 and State Route 303.
