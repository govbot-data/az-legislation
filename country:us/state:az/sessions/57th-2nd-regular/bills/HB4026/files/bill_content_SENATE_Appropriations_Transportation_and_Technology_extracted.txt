23 1. "Associated improvement" includes any public infrastructure
24 improvement that is made for the benefit of the manufacturing facility
25 outside of the parcel or parcels of real property where the manufacturing
26 facility is located.
27 2. "Capital investment" means an expenditure to acquire, lease or
28 improve property that is used for the benefit of a manufacturing facility,
29 including land, buildings, machinery and fixtures.
30 3. "Manufacturing facility":
31 (a) Means an establishment that is engaged in the mechanical,
32 physical or chemical transformation or fabrication of materials,
33 substances or components into new products in this state, that is
34 classified within sections 31 through 33 inclusive of the 2007 edition of
35 the North American industry classification system as published by the
36 national technical information service of the United States department of
37 commerce and that agrees to either:
38 (i) Make at least [$500,000,000] [$3,000,000,000] in capital
39 investment if the manufacturing facility is located in a county that has a
40 population of eight hundred thousand persons or more.
41 (ii) Make at least [$50,000,000] [$100,000,000] in capital
42 investment if the manufacturing facility is located in a county that has a
43 population of less than eight hundred thousand persons.
44 (b) Does not include mining, milling or smelting mineral ore or
45 generating electricity.
