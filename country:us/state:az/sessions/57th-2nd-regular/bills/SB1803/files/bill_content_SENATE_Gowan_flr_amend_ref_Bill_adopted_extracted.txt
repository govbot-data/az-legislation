FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
1. Prohibits a person who receives compensation for advising, consulting, guiding, coaching or
training an individual for an initial or supplemental claim or higher level review from contacting
the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs (U.S. VA) by impersonating the individual or an
accredited representative or by initiating contact with the U.S. VA while falsely representing that
the person has the authority to act on behalf of the individual, rather than contacting the U.S. VA
directly to obtain any veterans' benefits information or payment information regarding the
individual without prior authorization.
2. Prohibits a person who receives compensation for advising, consulting, guiding, coaching or
training an individual for an initial or supplemental claim or higher-level review from:
a) submitting paperwork, forms, evidence or any documentation to the U.S. VA or to any
governmental entity on behalf of the individual;
b) receiving, holding or exercising a power of attorney to act on behalf of the individual in
connection with a veteran's benefits matter; and
c) representing or purporting to represent the individual in any communication. negotiation,
hearing, review or proceeding before the U.S. VA or any governmental entity.
