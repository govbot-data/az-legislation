35 duties; report; delayed repeal
36 A. The vulnerable adult system study committee is established

43 2. Two members of the senate who are appointed by the president of
44 the senate and who are members of different political parties. The
45 president of the senate shall designate one of these members to serve as
46 cochairperson of the committee.
