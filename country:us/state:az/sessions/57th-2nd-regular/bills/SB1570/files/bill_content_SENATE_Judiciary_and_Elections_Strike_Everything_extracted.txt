SENATE AMENDMENTS TO S.B. 1570

1 Strike everything after the enacting clause and insert:
2 "Section 1. Federal immigration law enforcement personnel;
3 county recorder; board of supervisors; 2026
4 election
5 A. Notwithstanding any other law, for the 2026 general election
6 only, each county recorder and each county board of supervisors, either
7 separately or jointly with one or more other county recorders or county
8 boards of supervisors, shall enter into a written agreement with a federal
9 immigration law enforcement agency to provide for a federal immigration
10 law enforcement presence at each location within this state where ballots
11 are cast or deposited.
12 B. An agreement prescribed by subsection A of this section shall
13 provide for the presence of federal immigration law enforcement personnel
14 during all hours in which voting is conducted or ballots are deposited,
15 including early voting locations, election day polling places and ballot
16 drop box locations.
17 C. Federal immigration law enforcement personnel who are present
18 pursuant to this section may observe election activities and perform
19 lawful duties within the scope of their federal authority, but shall not

1 interfere with the casting or depositing of ballots except as otherwise
2 authorized by law.
3 D. This section does not authorize a county recorder, a county
4 board of supervisors or federal immigration law enforcement personnel to
5 question, detain or arrest a voter solely for the purpose of determining
6 voter eligibility, except as otherwise allowed under state or federal law.
7 Sec. 2. Legislative findings and intent
8 A. The legislature finds that maintaining the integrity of
9 elections conducted in this state is a compelling state interest.
10 B. The legislature further finds that cooperation between state and
11 federal law enforcement agencies is authorized under federal law and may
12 assist in enforcing existing election and immigration statutes.
13 C. The purpose of this act is to require county recorders to
14 coordinate with federal immigration law enforcement agencies to ensure
15 compliance with voter eligibility requirements at locations where ballots
16 are cast or deposited during the 2026 general election."
