4 9-485.01. Mobile food vendors; mobile food units; operation
5 A. In relation to a mobile food vendor or mobile food unit, a city

20 36-1761. Mobile food vendors; mobile food units; rules;
21 health and safety licensing standards
22 A. The director shall adopt rules that do all of the following:
23 1. Establish health and safety licensing standards for mobile food
24 vendors and mobile food units that apply on a statewide basis. The
25 licensing standards shall:
26 (a) Include three categories of mobile food units that are based on
27 the type of food dispensed and the amount of handling and preparation
28 required.
29 (b) Include general physical and operation requirements of a mobile
30 food unit, including:
31 (i) Installation of compressors, generators and similar mechanical

33 equipment.
34 (ii) Necessary commissary or other servicing area agreements.
35 (iii) Vehicle and equipment cleaning requirements.
36 (iv) Waste disposal requirements during and after operation on
37 public or private property, which may not include the size or dimensions
38 of any required solid waste receptacle.

40 objective factors for use by the county health departments.

44 (b) Requires a license to be renewed annually.

46 the mobile food vendor's commissary is located the licensing and health
47 and safety inspection for state licensure using the statewide inspection
