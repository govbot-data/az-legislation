SB1521 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; Diné College; remedial education State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SB 1521 Introduced by Senators Hatathlie: Epstein AN ACT Appropriating monies to the department of administration. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; department of administration; Diné College; remedial education; exemption A. The sum of $1,000,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the department of administration to distribute to Diné College for remedial education. B. The appropriation made in subsection A of this section is exempt from the provisions of section 35-190, Arizona Revised Statutes, relating to lapsing of appropriations.
