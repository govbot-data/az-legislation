1 The bill as proposed to be amended is reprinted as follows:
2 Section 1. Section 32-2242, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended to
3 read:
4 32-2242. Application for certification as veterinary
5 technician; qualifications; definitions
6 A. A person desiring to be certified as a veterinary technician
7 shall apply in writing to the board on a form furnished by the board.
8 B. The applicant shall be at least eighteen years of age and shall
9 furnish EITHER:
10 1. Satisfactory evidence of graduation from a two-year curriculum
11 in veterinary technology, or the equivalent of such graduation as
12 determined by the board, in a NONPROFIT ORGANIZATION, [LARGE ANIMAL
13 LIVESTOCK OPERATION,] college or other institution approved by the board.
14 2. A RECORD OF WORKING FOR AT LEAST FOUR THOUSAND HOURS WITHIN SIX
15 YEARS UNDER THE DIRECT SUPERVISION OF A VETERINARIAN WHO IS LICENSED IN
16 THIS STATE, DURING WHICH TIME THE APPLICANT LEARNED SKILLS PURSUANT TO THE
17 VETERINARY TECHNOLOGY STUDENT ESSENTIAL AND RECOMMENDED SKILLS LIST
18 PRESCRIBED BY A NATIONAL NONPROFIT ORGANIZATION FOR VETERINARIANS THAT
19 PRESCRIBES AND ENFORCES STANDARDS FOR VETERINARY EDUCATION. AN APPLICANT
20 WHO SATISFIES THE REQUIREMENTS FOR THE ALTERNATIVE SUPERVISED WORK ROUTE
21 OF CERTIFICATION IS ELIGIBLE TO TAKE THE VETERINARY TECHNICIAN NATIONAL
22 EXAMINATION AND ANY STATE EXAMINATION FOR VETERINARY TECHNICIANS APPROVED
23 BY THE BOARD. THE SUPERVISED WORK MUST INCLUDE:
24 (a) A DESCRIPTION OF THE SKILL OR SKILLS THE APPLICANT DEVELOPED.

1 (b) THE AFFIDAVIT OF THE RESPONSIBLE VETERINARIAN.
2 C. The application shall be accompanied by the application and

4 D. BEFORE BEGINNING ANY SUPERVISED WORK, THE RESPONSIBLE

15 provide all of the following:
16 1. Proof that the applicant's original score meets the minimum
17 score required by the board.
18 2. Proof that the applicant holds an active license in good

22 F. FOR THE PURPOSES OF THIS SECTION[,][:
23 1. "LARGE ANIMAL LIVESTOCK OPERATION" MEANS A LIVESTOCK AUCTION
24 MARKET, RODEO GROUNDS, A HORSE RACING TRACK, A COUNTY FAIR FACILITY, A
25 CONCENTRATED ANIMAL FEEDING OPERATION, A FEEDLOT, A DAIRY FARM, A DUDE
26 RANCH, A COW-CALF OPERATION OR A COMMERCIAL AGRICULTURAL OR EQUESTRIAN
27 BREEDING OPERATION IN THIS STATE.
28 2.] "RESPONSIBLE VETERINARIAN" MEANS A VETERINARIAN WHO IS LICENSED
29 IN THIS STATE AND WHO IS RESPONSIBLE FOR THE SUPERVISED WORK OF AN
30 APPLICANT FOR CERTIFICATION UNDER THIS SECTION AND FOR ANY VETERINARIANS
31 WHO PROVIDE DIRECT SUPERVISION OF THE APPLICANT.
