2 Section 1. Section 32-2242, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended to
3 read:
4 32-2242. Application for certification as veterinary
5 technician; qualifications; definition
6 A. A person desiring to be certified as a veterinary technician
7 shall apply in writing to the board on a form furnished by the board.
8 B. The applicant shall be at least eighteen years of age and shall
9 furnish EITHER:
10 1. Satisfactory evidence of graduation from a two-year curriculum
11 in veterinary technology, or the equivalent of such graduation as
12 determined by the board, in a NONPROFIT ORGANIZATION, college or other
13 institution approved by the board.
14 2. A RECORD OF [RECEIVING AT LEAST FOUR THOUSAND HOURS WITHIN SIX
15 YEARS OF ON-THE-JOB TRAINING OR] WORKING FOR AT LEAST FOUR THOUSAND HOURS
16 WITHIN SIX YEARS UNDER THE DIRECT SUPERVISION OF A VETERINARIAN WHO IS
17 LICENSED IN THIS STATE, DURING WHICH TIME THE APPLICANT LEARNED SKILLS
18 PURSUANT TO THE VETERINARY TECHNOLOGY STUDENT ESSENTIAL AND RECOMMENDED
19 SKILLS LIST PRESCRIBED BY A NATIONAL NONPROFIT ORGANIZATION FOR
20 VETERINARIANS THAT PRESCRIBES AND ENFORCES STANDARDS FOR VETERINARY
21 EDUCATION. AN APPLICANT WHO SATISFIES THE REQUIREMENTS FOR THE
22 ALTERNATIVE SUPERVISED WORK [OR ON-THE-JOB TRAINING] ROUTE OF
23 CERTIFICATION IS ELIGIBLE TO TAKE THE VETERINARY TECHNICIAN NATIONAL
24 EXAMINATION AND ANY STATE EXAMINATION FOR VETERINARY TECHNICIANS APPROVED
25 BY THE BOARD. THE SUPERVISED WORK [OR ON-THE-JOB TRAINING] MUST INCLUDE:
26 (a) A DESCRIPTION OF THE SKILL OR SKILLS THE APPLICANT DEVELOPED.
