Petersen Floor Amendment

FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
• Allows a moving civil traffic violation to be issued by a peace officer or a prosecutor, rather than
only a peace officer.

4 28-1592. Commencement of action; original citation changes
5 A. A civil traffic violation case is commenced by issuance or
6 filing of a uniform traffic ticket and complaint as provided in this
7 article.
8 B. A civil traffic violation case shall be commenced as follows:
9 1. If a case is commenced by issuance, it shall be issued within
10 sixty days after the alleged violation.
11 2. If the case is commenced by filing, it shall be filed within
12 sixty days after the alleged violation and shall be served within ninety
13 days after the filing date.
14 3. Except as provided in paragraph 4 of this subsection, within one
15 hundred eighty days after the alleged violation if the alleged violation
16 is under investigation in conjunction with a traffic accident.
17 4. Within one year after the alleged violation if the alleged
18 violation is under investigation in conjunction with a traffic accident
19 resulting in death.
