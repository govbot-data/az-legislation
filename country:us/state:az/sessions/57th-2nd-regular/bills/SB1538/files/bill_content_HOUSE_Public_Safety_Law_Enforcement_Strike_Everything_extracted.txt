7 A. EMPLOYERS SHALL PROVIDE WORKERS' COMPENSATION COVERAGE TO
8 FIREFIGHTERS AND CERTIFIED PEACE OFFICERS WHO HAVE BEEN DIAGNOSED WITH
9 POST-TRAUMATIC STRESS DISORDER BY A LICENSED MENTAL HEALTH PROFESSIONAL
10 AND WHO HAVE AN ACCEPTED WORKERS' COMPENSATION CLAIM FOR POST-TRAUMATIC
11 STRESS DISORDER UNDER THIS TITLE. IF AN INDEPENDENT MEDICAL EXAMINATION
12 REVEALS THAT A TREATMENT PROTOCOL OF MIDOMAFETAMINE IS DEEMED A REASONABLE
13 AND NECESSARY TREATMENT AND FOLLOWS THE TREATMENT GUIDELINES ESTABLISHED
14 BY THE COMMISSION, WORKERS' COMPENSATION COVERAGE MAY INCLUDE ONE COMPLETE
15 COURSE OF A TREATMENT PROTOCOL OF MIDOMAFETAMINE AS PRESCRIBED BY A
16 PSYCHIATRIST.
17 B. MIDOMAFETAMINE PRESCRIBED UNDER THIS SECTION MUST MEET THE
18 REQUIREMENTS UNDER TITLE 36, CHAPTER 27 FOR A CONTROLLED SUBSTANCE.
19 C. ON OR BEFORE JANUARY 1, 2029 AND EACH JANUARY 1 THEREAFTER, THE
20 COMMISSION SHALL SUBMIT TO THE JOINT LEGISLATIVE BUDGET COMMITTEE A REPORT
21 OF THE COSTS OF TREATMENT FOR MIDOMAFETAMINE UNDER THIS SECTION FOR
22 FIREFIGHTERS AND CERTIFIED PEACE OFFICERS.
23 D. FOR THE PURPOSES OF THIS SECTION:
24 1. "CERTIFIED PEACE OFFICER" HAS THE SAME MEANING PRESCRIBED IN
25 SECTION 38-842.
26 2. "FIREFIGHTER" MEANS A PROFESSIONAL FIREFIGHTER WHO IS A MEMBER
27 OF A CITY, TOWN, COUNTY OR FIRE DISTRICT FIRE DEPARTMENT.
28 3. "LICENSED MENTAL HEALTH PROFESSIONAL" MEANS AN INDIVIDUAL WHO
29 SPECIALIZES IN TRAUMA AND CRISIS, WHO USES EVIDENCE-BASED TREATMENT
30 OPTIONS AND WHO IS LICENSED AS EITHER:
31 (a) A PSYCHIATRIST PURSUANT TO TITLE 32, CHAPTER 13 OR 17.
32 (b) A PSYCHOLOGIST PURSUANT TO TITLE 32, CHAPTER 19.1.
33 4. "TREATMENT PROTOCOL OF MIDOMAFETAMINE" MEANS A PROCESS OF
34 TREATMENT THAT HAS RECEIVED APPROVAL FROM THE UNITED STATES FOOD AND DRUG
35 ADMINISTRATION FOR POST-TRAUMATIC STRESS DISORDER AND THAT USES
36 MIDOMAFETAMINE IN CONJUNCTION WITH THERAPY SESSIONS.
