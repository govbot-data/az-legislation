2 Section 1. Section 1-502, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended to
3 read:
4 1-502. Eligibility for state or local public benefits;
5 documentation; violations; classification; citizen
6 suits; court costs and attorney fees; definition
7 A. Notwithstanding any other state law and to the extent allowed by
8 federal law, any agency of this state or a political subdivision of this
9 state that administers any state or local public benefit shall require
10 each natural person who applies for the state or local public benefit to
11 submit at least one of the following documents to the entity that
12 administers the state or local public benefit demonstrating lawful
13 presence in the United States:
14 1. An Arizona driver license issued after 1996 or an Arizona
15 nonoperating identification license.
16 2. A birth certificate or delayed birth certificate issued in any
17 state, territory or possession of the United States.
18 3. A United States certificate of birth abroad.
19 4. A United States passport.
20 5. A foreign passport with a United States visa.
21 6. An I-94 form with a photograph.
22 7. A United States citizenship and immigration services employment
23 authorization document or refugee travel document.
24 8. A United States certificate of naturalization.
25 9. A United States certificate of citizenship.
26 10. A tribal certificate of Indian blood.
27 11. A tribal or bureau of Indian affairs affidavit of birth.
