1 The bill as proposed to be amended is reprinted as follows:
2 Section 1. Light rail construction; state participation;
3 prohibition; report; delayed repeal; definitions
4 [A. Notwithstanding any other law, this state may not provide state
5 participation for any light rail construction project.]
6 [B.][A.] On or before December 31, 2027, the department of
7 transportation, in coordination with an independent transportation
8 research entity, shall conduct a comprehensive feasibility review of light
9 rail expansion in Maricopa county and submit a report on their findings.
10 The report shall be submitted to the governor, the president of the senate
11 and the speaker of the house of representatives and [shall provide] a copy
12 [shall be provided] to the secretary of state and to the mayor of Phoenix
13 and each member of the Phoenix city council. The comprehensive
14 feasibility review may not presume the superiority of any mode of transit
15 and shall include:
16 1. Capital and operating cost comparisons between a light rail and
17 autonomous or semi-autonomous transit vehicles, including bus rapid
18 transit, passenger vans and shuttle systems.
19 2. Environmental impact per dollar invested for acquiring,
20 constructing and operating a light rail.
21 3. Ridership trends and post-pandemic usage patterns.
22 4. Flexibility, scalability and adaptability to population shifts.
23 5. Long-term maintenance and replacement costs.
24 6. Policy recommendations regarding future state involvement in
25 light rail construction.
26 7. Analysis of whether continuation, modification or
27 discontinuation of state participation in light rail expansion is
28 warranted.
29 [C.][B.] This act is repealed from and after June 30, 2028.

Senate Amendments to S.B. 1332
1 [D.][C.] For the purposes of this section:
2 1. "Light rail construction" means the planning, permitting or
3 physical construction of new fixed-guideway rail transit lines or
4 extensions.
5 2. "State participation" means any of the following:
6 (a) Appropriating state monies.
7 (b) Issuing state bonds or guarantees.
8 (c) Approving, certifying or matching monies by the department of
9 transportation.
10 (d) Approving state permits or other authorizations.
