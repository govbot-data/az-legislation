HB2784 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: cities and towns; technical correction State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HB 2784 Introduced by Representative Olson AN ACT amending section 9-237, arizona revised statutes; relating to cities and towns. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Section 9-237, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended to read: START_STATUTE9-237. Appointive officers In addition to the common council, the officers of every town shall be a town clerk, town marshal,  and town engineer and other officers deemed necessary by the common council, who shall be appointed as provided by ordinance of the town. END_STATUTE
