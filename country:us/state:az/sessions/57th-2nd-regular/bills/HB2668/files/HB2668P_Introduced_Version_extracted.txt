HB2668 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; broadband infrastructure State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HB 2668 Introduced by Representatives Hernandez C: Hernandez A, Luna-Nájera, Volk AN ACT Appropriating monies to the state treasurer. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; state treasurer; broadband infrastructure; cities and towns The sum of $5,000,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the state treasurer to distribute as grants for broadband infrastructure to cities and towns in this state with a population of less than one hundred thousand persons.
