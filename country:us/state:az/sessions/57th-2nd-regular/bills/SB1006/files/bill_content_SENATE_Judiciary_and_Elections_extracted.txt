17 C. For the purposes of reporting under subsection B of this section:
18 1. A contribution is deemed to be received either on the date the
19 committee knowingly takes possession of the contribution or the date of the
20 check or credit card payment. For an in-kind contribution of services, the
21 contribution is deemed made either on the date the services are performed
22 or the date the committee receives the services.
23 2. An expenditure or disbursement is deemed made either on the date
24 the committee authorizes the monies to be spent or the date the monies are
25 withdrawn from the committee's account. For a transaction by check, the
26 expenditure or disbursement is deemed made on the date the committee signs
27 the check. For a credit card transaction on paper, the expenditure or
28 disbursement is deemed made on the date the committee signs the
29 authorization to charge the credit card. For an electronic transaction, an
30 expenditure or disbursement is deemed made on the date the committee
31 electronically authorizes the charge. For an agreement to purchase goods
32 or services, the expenditure or disbursement is deemed made either on the
33 date the parties enter into the agreement or the date the purchase order is
34 issued.
35 3. A committee may record its transactions using any of the methods
36 authorized by this subsection but for each type of contribution,
37 expenditure or disbursement made or received, the committee shall use a
38 consistent method of recording transactions throughout the election cycle.
39 D. The amount of an in-kind contribution of services shall be equal
40 to the usual and normal charges for the services on the date performed.
41 E. If any receipt or disbursement is earmarked, the committee shall
42 report the identity of the person to whom the receipt or disbursement is
43 earmarked.
