ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
SB 1006: campaign finance; aggregate report; amount
KOLODIN FLOOR AMENDMENT
Requires the Secretary of State to biennially adjust the in-state contribution
reporting threshold increased by the underlying Concurrent Resolution based on
changes in the Consumer Price Index for the Phoenix-Mesa-Scottsdale statistical
area.
