2 Section 1. Obesity treatment and prevention advisory council;
3 membership; duties; reporting requirements;
4 delayed repeal
5 A. The obesity treatment and prevention advisory council is
6 established consisting of the following members:
7 1. Two members of the senate who are from different political
8 parties and who are appointed by the president of the senate. The
9 president of the senate shall designate one of these members to serve as
10 cochairperson of the advisory council.
11 2. Two members of the house of representatives who are from
12 different political parties and who are appointed by the speaker of the
13 house of representatives. The speaker of the house of representatives
14 shall designate one of these members to serve as cochairperson of the
15 advisory council.
16 3. The director of the Arizona health care cost containment system
17 or the director's designee.
18 4. The director of the department of health services or the
19 director's designee.
20 5. Two members who represent patient advocacy organizations with
21 expertise in treating people living with obesity and who are appointed by
22 the president of the senate.
23 6. Two members who represent medical societies or physician
24 organizations or who are physicians with experience treating people living
25 with obesity and who are appointed by the speaker of the house of
26 representatives.
27 7. One member who is a registered dietitian with expertise in the
28 therapeutic treatment of people living with obesity through diet,
