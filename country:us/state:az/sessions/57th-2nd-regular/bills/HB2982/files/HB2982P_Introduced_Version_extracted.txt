HB2982 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; Klagetoh Chapter; capital improvements State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HB 2982 Introduced by Representative Tsosie AN ACT Appropriating monies to the department of administration. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; department of administration; Klagetoh Chapter; Navajo Nation; capital improvement projects The sum of $716,500 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the department of administration to distribute to the Navajo Nation for the Klagetoh Chapter for capital improvement projects.
