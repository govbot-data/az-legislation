SB1483 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; Navajo technical university; laboratory State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SB 1483 Introduced by Senators Hatathlie: Diaz, Miranda;  Representative Sandoval AN ACT appropriating monies to the department of administration. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; department of administration; Navajo technical university; environmental testing laboratory; exemption A. The sum of $8,000,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the department of administration to distribute to the Navajo technical university to survey, design, plan and construct an environmental testing laboratory, including any interior build-out or specialized equipment needed. B. The appropriation made in subsection A of this section is exempt from the provisions of section 35-190, Arizona Revised Statutes, relating to lapsing of appropriations.
