Kavanagh Floor Amendment

Committee Amendment
Amendment drafted by: Leg. Council
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
• Modifies the definition of medical intervention to remove any medical procedure, treatment,
device, drug, injection, medication or medical action taken to diagnose a person.
