2 Section 1. Study committee on coal-impacted communities;
3 membership; duties; report; delayed repeal
4 A. The study committee on coal-impacted communities is established
5 consisting of the following members:
6 1. Two members of the senate who are appointed by the president of
7 the senate and who are members of different political parties. The
8 president of the senate shall designate one of these members to serve as
9 cochairperson of the committee. The two members shall reside in or
10 represent a rural or tribal community.
11 2. Two members of the house of representatives who are appointed by
12 the speaker of the house of representatives and who are members of
13 different political parties. The speaker of the house of representatives
14 shall designate one of these members to serve as cochairperson of the
15 committee. The two members shall reside in or represent a rural or tribal
16 community.
17 3. One member who is appointed by the president of the senate and
18 who represents a nonprofit organization that provides food security,
19 entrepreneurship, youth leadership and housing to a community impacted by
20 coal.
21 4. One member who is appointed by the speaker of the house of
22 representatives and who represents a nonprofit organization that works to
23 protect the water of Black Mesa and to bring power back to Indigenous
24 communities impacted by coal.
25 5. One member who is appointed by the president of the senate and
26 who represents the Navajo Nation economic development division.
27 6. One member who is appointed by the speaker of the house of
28 representatives and who represents a public university in this state that

1 has a program that supports communities impacted by an energy transition,
2 including communities where fossil energy is being retired and where new
3 energy is being developed.
4 7. One member of the Coconino county board of supervisors who
5 represents the supervisorial district where the Navajo generating station
6 operated and who is appointed by the president of the senate.
7 B. The committee shall:
8 1. Meet as often as the cochairpersons deem necessary.
9 2. Investigate and analyze the social and economic effects on
10 communities heavily reliant on the coal industry, particularly in
11 communities experiencing job losses or economic decline due to the
12 transition away from coal power generation, with a focus on developing
13 strategies to mitigate impacts and support economic diversification in
14 those communities.
15 C. The committee may use the services of legislative staff to
16 provide necessary administrative and operational support. On request of
17 the committee, an agency of this state or a political subdivision of this
18 state shall provide the committee with services, equipment, documents,
19 personnel and facilities, to the extent possible, at no cost to the
20 committee.
21 D. On or before December 31, 2026, the committee shall submit a
22 report of its findings and recommendations to the governor, the president
23 of the senate and the speaker of the house of representatives and shall
24 provide a copy of this report to the secretary of state.
25 E. This section is repealed from and after September 30, 2027.
26 Sec. 2. Appropriation; office of economic opportunity;
27 workforce development study; exemption
28 A. The sum of [$600,000] [$300,000] is appropriated from the state
29 general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the office of economic
30 opportunity to conduct a workforce development study on the communities in
31 Coconino county impacted by the closure of the Navajo generating station.
32 B. The appropriation made in subsection A of this section is exempt
33 from the provisions of section 35-190, Arizona Revised Statutes, relating
34 to lapsing of appropriations.
