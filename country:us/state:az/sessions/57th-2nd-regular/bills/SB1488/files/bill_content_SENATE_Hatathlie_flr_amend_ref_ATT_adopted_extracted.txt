Hatathlie Floor Amendment

Amendment drafted by: Leg. Council
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
1. Specifies that, of the two Senate members of the Study Committee on Coal-Impacted Communities
(Study Committee), one member is appointed by the President of the Senate and one member is
appointed by the Minority Leader of the Senate, rather than both members being appointed by the
President of the Senate.
2. Specifies that, of the two House of Representatives (House) members of the Study Committee,
one member is appointed by the Speaker of the House and one member is appointed by the
Minority Leader of the House, rather than both members being appointed by the Speaker of the
House.
3. Removes the requirement that the two Study Committee members from the Senate and House be
from different political parties.
4. Makes conforming changes.
