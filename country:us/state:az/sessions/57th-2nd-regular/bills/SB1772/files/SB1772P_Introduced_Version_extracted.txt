SB1772 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: state agencies; tribal liaison State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SB 1772 Introduced by Senator Gonzales AN ACT amending title 41, chapter 1, article 1, Arizona Revised Statutes, by adding section 41-112; relating to the governor. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Title 41, chapter 1, article 1, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended by adding section 41-112, to read: START_STATUTE41-112. State agencies; tribal liaison The governor shall require all state agencies to employ a tribal liaison from a federally recognized Indian tribe located in this state to represent and facilitate communications between each state agency and tribal communities in this state. END_STATUTE
