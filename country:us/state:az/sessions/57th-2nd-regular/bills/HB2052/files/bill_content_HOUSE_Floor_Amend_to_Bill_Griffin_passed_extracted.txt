ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2052: management plan; water loss; percent
GRIFFIN FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Increases, from 8% to 10%, the amount of permissible lost or unaccounted for
water in a city, town or private water company's total distribution system.
2. States that 8% or less if a goal that should be considered a Best Management
Practice.
3. Removes rulemaking requirements of the ADWR Director.
