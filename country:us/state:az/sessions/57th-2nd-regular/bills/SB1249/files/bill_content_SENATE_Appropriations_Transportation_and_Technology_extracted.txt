1 The bill as proposed to be amended is reprinted as follows:
2 Section 1. Title 36, chapter 1, article 1, Arizona Revised
3 Statutes, is amended by adding section 36-123.05, to read:
4 36-123.05. Dementia services program; department duties;
5 Alzheimer's disease state plan; posting;
6 reporting requirement; advisory council;
7 definition
8 A. THE DEPARTMENT IS DESIGNATED AS THE LEAD AGENCY IN THIS STATE TO
9 ADDRESS ALZHEIMER'S DISEASE AND RELATED FORMS OF DEMENTIA.
10 B. THE DIRECTOR SHALL ESTABLISH A DEMENTIA SERVICES PROGRAM WITHIN
11 THE DEPARTMENT THAT DOES ALL OF THE FOLLOWING:
12 1. FACILITATES THE COORDINATION OF PROGRAMS THAT RELATE TO
13 ALZHEIMER'S DISEASE AND RELATED FORMS OF DEMENTIA IN ALL STATE AGENCIES.
14 2. FACILITATES THE COORDINATION, REVIEW, PUBLICATION AND
15 IMPLEMENTATION OF AND UPDATES TO THE ALZHEIMER'S DISEASE STATE PLAN
16 DEVELOPED PURSUANT TO THIS SECTION.
17 3. APPLIES FOR PUBLIC HEALTH FUNDING AND GRANTS RELATED TO
18 ALZHEIMER'S DISEASE AND RELATED FORMS OF DEMENTIA.
19 4. INCORPORATES EVIDENCE-BASED BRAIN HEALTH STRATEGIES INTO
20 RELEVANT DEPARTMENT-LED PUBLIC HEALTH PROGRAMS.
21 C. THE DEPARTMENT SHALL DEVELOP AN ALZHEIMER'S DISEASE STATE PLAN
22 THAT ASSESSES THE CURRENT AND FUTURE IMPACT OF ALZHEIMER'S DISEASE AND
23 RELATED FORMS OF DEMENTIA ON THIS STATE AND THAT:
24 1. ASSESSES AND IDENTIFIES RELEVANT GAPS IN ALL OF THE FOLLOWING:
25 (a) EXISTING STATE SERVICES AND RESOURCES THAT ADDRESS THE NEEDS OF
26 PERSONS LIVING WITH ALZHEIMER'S DISEASE OR A RELATED FORM OF DEMENTIA AND
27 THEIR CAREGIVERS.

Senate Amendments to S.B. 1249
1 (b) THE NEEDS OF PERSONS WHO HAVE ALZHEIMER'S DISEASE OR A RELATED
2 FORM OF DEMENTIA AND HOW THEIR LIVES ARE AFFECTED THROUGHOUT THE
3 PROGRESSION OF THE DISEASE.
4 (c) THIS STATE'S PUBLIC AND PRIVATE HEALTH SYSTEMS, WORKFORCE AND
5 CLINICAL CAPACITY AND CAPABILITY TO PROVIDE EFFECTIVE DETECTION, DIAGNOSIS
6 AND TREATMENT OF ALZHEIMER'S DISEASE AND RELATED FORMS OF DEMENTIA.
7 (d) THIS STATE'S PUBLIC AND PRIVATE NONMEDICAL CARE AND SUPPORT
8 SERVICES FOR PERSONS LIVING WITH ALZHEIMER'S DISEASE OR A RELATED FORM OF
9 DEMENTIA AND THEIR CAREGIVERS.
10 2. PROVIDES STRATEGIC RECOMMENDATIONS WITH MEASURABLE GOALS FOR
11 STATE ACTION TO DO ALL OF THE FOLLOWING FOR PERSONS LIVING WITH
12 ALZHEIMER'S DISEASE OR A RELATED FORM OF DEMENTIA:
13 (a) IMPROVE ACCESS TO CARE, SUPPORT, DIAGNOSTICS AND TREATMENT.
14 (b) IMPROVE THE QUALITY OF DEMENTIA CARE, INCLUDING CRISIS
15 RESPONSE, HEALTH CARE SYSTEMS, LONG-TERM CARE AND IN-HOME CARE.
16 (c) ADVANCE RISK REDUCTION AND EARLY DETECTION AWARENESS AND BRAIN
17 HEALTH.
18 (d) IMPROVE CAREGIVER SUPPORT, CARE PLANNING AND CARE COORDINATION.
19 (e) IMPROVE THE COLLECTION, AVAILABILITY AND USE OF
20 DEMENTIA-RELATED DATA BY STATE AGENCIES.
21 D. THE DEPARTMENT SHALL CONVENE OR DESIGNATE AN ADVISORY COUNCIL OR
22 WORKING GROUP TO ASSIST IN PLANNING, CONDUCTING AND EVALUATING STAKEHOLDER
23 ENGAGEMENT AND STATE PLAN IMPLEMENTATION, REVIEW AND UPDATES. MEMBERSHIP
24 OF THE ADVISORY COUNCIL OR WORKING GROUP SHALL REFLECT THE DIVERSITY OF
25 STAKEHOLDERS IDENTIFIED IN SUBSECTION E, PARAGRAPH 1 OF THIS SECTION.
26 E. THE DEPARTMENT SHALL CONDUCT STAKEHOLDER ENGAGEMENT SESSIONS AT
27 LEAST ONCE EACH CALENDAR YEAR TO SOLICIT INPUT ON THE STATE PLAN. THE
28 DEPARTMENT SHALL:
29 1. SEEK FEEDBACK FROM AND COLLABORATE WITH PERSONS WHO HAVE
30 ALZHEIMER'S DISEASE OR A RELATED FORM OF DEMENTIA, DIRECT CAREGIVERS AND
31 PUBLIC, PRIVATE AND NONPROFIT ORGANIZATIONS FOCUSED ON ALZHEIMER'S CARE
32 SERVICES, RESEARCH, ADVOCACY, HEALTH SERVICES AND CAREGIVER SUPPORT.
33 2. AT LEAST THIRTY DAYS BEFORE EACH ENGAGEMENT SESSION, PROVIDE
34 PUBLIC NOTICE OF THE SESSION, INCLUDING THE DATE, TIME, LOCATION OR
35 VIRTUAL ACCESS INFORMATION, A SUMMARY AGENDA AND INSTRUCTIONS FOR
36 SUBMITTING WRITTEN COMMENTS.
37 3. ENSURE MEANINGFUL PARTICIPATION BY STAKEHOLDERS STATEWIDE,
38 INCLUDING RURAL AND UNDERSERVED COMMUNITIES, AND PROVIDE REASONABLE
39 ACCOMMODATIONS AND LANGUAGE ACCESS.
40 4. ACCEPT WRITTEN COMMENTS FOR AT LEAST FOURTEEN DAYS FOLLOWING
41 EACH ENGAGEMENT SESSION.
42 F. ON OR BEFORE SEPTEMBER 30, 2027 AND AT LEAST EVERY THIRD YEAR
43 THEREAFTER, THE DEPARTMENT SHALL UPDATE AND SUBMIT THE STATE PLAN TO THE
44 GOVERNOR, THE PRESIDENT OF THE SENATE AND THE SPEAKER OF THE HOUSE OF
45 REPRESENTATIVES AND SHALL PROVIDE A COPY TO THE SECRETARY OF STATE. THE
46 DEPARTMENT SHALL PUBLISH THE PLAN ON THE DEPARTMENT'S PUBLIC WEBSITE.
