HB2067 - 572R - I Ver PREFILED    DEC 16 2025 REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; Apache Junction; transportation projects State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HB 2067 Introduced by Representative Blackman AN ACT Appropriating monies to the department of transportation. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; department of transportation; infrastructure and transportation projects The following amounts are appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the department of transportation to distribute to the city of Apache Junction for the following transportation and infrastructure projects: 1. $2,700,000 to widen Tomahawk Road between U.S. Route 60 and Southern Avenue. 2. $6,700,000 to improve Delaware Drive between 16th Avenue and Broadway Avenue with safety and drainage improvements. 3. $1,000,000 to the pavement preservation program in Apache Junction. 4. $12,000,000 for street and drainage projects in Apache Junction. 5. $5,000,000 for improvements to the Ironwood Drive corridor. 6. $2,000,000 for safety improvements at the intersection of Idaho Road and U.S. Route 60.
