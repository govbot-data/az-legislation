FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2379: school district governing boards; training
GRESS FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Specifies each school district governing board (governing board) member must
undergo a total of at least eight hours of instruction on school finance, including
how the specified topics relate to school finance.
2. Sets October 1 as the date by which each governing board member must notify
the county school superintendent that they completed the required training.
3. Requires, by November 1, each county school superintendent to:
a. compile the notifications received from the governing board members; and
b. submit a report to the State Board of Education (SBE) that indicates
whether each governing board member completed the required training.
4. Instructs SBE to submit a compiled report to specified individuals by
December 1 that indicates whether each governing board member completed
the required training.
5. Removes language that makes a governing board member ineligible to be a
candidate for nomination, election or appointment to any governing board if
the county school superintendent determines the governing board member has
failed to complete the required training.
6. Makes a governing board member who fails to complete the required training
ineligible to be a candidate for nomination, election or appointment to any
governing board if they:
a. were not appointed during the current term; and
b. are a member of the governing board of a school district that is identified
by the Auditor General (OAG) as one of the highest risk school districts.
7. Removes the requirement for the Arizona Department of Education (ADE) to
consult with the OAG when developing the training program.
8. Allows ADE, if the OAG disapproves an ADE training program, to submit the
training program to the Joint Legislative Audit Committee (JLAC) for review
and approval.
9. Requires JLAC to hear and approve or disapprove the training program at its
first regular meeting after the training program is submitted.
10. Allows ADE to provide the training program to any governing board member
without approval from the OAG if JLAC approves the training program by a
majority vote.
