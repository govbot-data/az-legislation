10 Sec. 3. Section 15-421, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended to
11 read:
12 15-421. Governing boards; members; qualifications;
13 prohibitions; candidate statements; definitions
14 A. The governing body of a school district shall be a governing
15 board. There shall be three governing board members, except as otherwise
16 provided by this section and section 15-425, subsection A.
17 B. The governing body of a high school district shall be a
18 governing board composed of:
19 1. In a single district, the governing board members of the common
20 school district.
21 2. In a union high school district, five members.
22 C. A person is eligible for election to the office of governing
23 board member if all of the following apply:
24 1. The person is a registered voter of this state.
25 2. The person has been a resident of the school district for at
26 least one year immediately preceding the day of election.
27 3. The person is not subject to registration as a sex offender in
28 this state or in any other jurisdiction.
29 4. THE PERSON IS NOT INELIGIBLE TO BE A CANDIDATE FOR NOMINATION OR
30 ELECTION TO THE OFFICE OF GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER PURSUANT TO SECTION
31 15-349, SUBSECTION E.
32 D. An employee of a school district, including a person who
33 directly provides certified or classified services to the school district
34 as an employee of a third-party contractor, or the spouse of such an
35 employee may not hold membership on the governing board of a school
36 district by which the employee is employed.
37 E. Notwithstanding subsection D of this section and title 38,
38 chapter 3, article 8, a small school district may employ, including
39 employment through a third-party contractor that provides services to the
40 small school district, a substitute teacher who is related to a member of
41 the governing board as immediate family and who has had the same household
42 of residence within the preceding four years. If a small school district
43 employs a substitute teacher pursuant to this subsection, the member of
44 the governing board who is related to the substitute teacher shall be
45 recused from voting on any matter relating to substitute teachers.
