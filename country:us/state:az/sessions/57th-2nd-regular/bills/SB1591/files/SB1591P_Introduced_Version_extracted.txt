SB1591 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: housing trust fund; appropriation State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SB 1591 Introduced by Senator Alston: Representative Connolly AN ACT appropriating monies to the housing trust fund. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; housing trust fund The sum of $200,000,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the housing trust fund established by section 41-3955, Arizona Revised Statutes.
