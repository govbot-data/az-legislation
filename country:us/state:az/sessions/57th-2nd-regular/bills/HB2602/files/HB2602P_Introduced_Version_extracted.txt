HB2602 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; DPS; pay increase State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HB 2602 Introduced by Representative Blackman AN ACT Appropriating monies to the department of public safety. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; department of public safety; pay increase The sum of $24,500,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the department of public safety for a ten percent pay increase for all department employees.
