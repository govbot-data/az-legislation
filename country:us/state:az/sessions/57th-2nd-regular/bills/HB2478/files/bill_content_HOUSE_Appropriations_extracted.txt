2 Section 1. Arizona commission on student outcomes; members;
3 duties; reporting requirements; delayed repeal
4 A. The Arizona commission on student outcomes is established to
5 analyze, review and recommend improvements to the current K-12 education
6 system in this state.
7 B. The commission is composed of the following members:
8 1. Three members who are appointed by the speaker of the house of
9 representatives, not more than two of whom are members of the same
10 political party. The speaker of the house of representatives shall
11 designate one of the members to serve as cochairperson of the commission.
12 2. Three members who are appointed by the president of the senate,
13 not more than two of whom are members of the same political party. The
14 president of the senate shall designate one of the members to serve as
15 cochairperson of the commission.
16 3. One member who is appointed by the governor.
17 4. The superintendent of public instruction, or the
18 superintendent's designee.
19 5. One member who represents a nonprofit organization that produces
20 research on economic and educational policies and who is appointed by the
21 speaker of the house of representatives.
22 6. One person who represents a statewide taxpayer organization and
23 who is appointed by the president of the senate.
24 7. One person who represents an organization that represents school
25 district governing boards and who is appointed by the speaker of the house
26 of representatives.
27 8. One person who represents an organization that represents
28 charter schools and who is appointed by the president of the senate.

31 Sec. 2. Appropriation; Arizona commission on student
32 outcomes; exemption
33 A. The sum of $1,000,000 is appropriated from the classroom site
