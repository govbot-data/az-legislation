Dunn Floor Amendment
Reference to: PUBLIC SAFETY Committee Amendment
Amendment drafted by: Leg. Council
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
• Specifies that a person must obtain written consent, rather than consent, from another person with
legal authority over the ticketed entertainment event to operate a civil unmanned aircraft at a
ticketed entertainment event.

1 F. G. For the purposes of this section:
2 1. "Civil unmanned aircraft" means an unmanned aircraft or unmanned
3 aircraft system that is operated by a person for any purpose other than
4 strictly for hobby or recreational purposes, including commercial
5 purposes, or in furtherance of or incidental to any business or media
6 service or agency.
7 2. "Commercial purposes" means the use of an unmanned aircraft in
8 return for financial compensation and includes aerial photography, aerial
9 mapping or geospatial imaging.
10 3. "Critical facility" means any of the following:
11 (a) A petroleum or alumina refinery.
12 (b) A petroleum, chemical or rubber production, transportation,
13 storage or processing facility.
14 (c) A chemical manufacturing facility.
15 (d) A water or wastewater treatment facility and water development,
16 distribution or conveyance system, including a dam.
17 (e) An electric generation facility, as defined in section
18 42-14156, and any associated substation or switchyard.
19 (f) An electrical transmission or distribution substation.
20 (g) An electrical transmission line of at least sixty-nine thousand
21 volts.
22 (h) An electronic communication station or tower.
23 (i) An energy control center.
24 (j) A distribution operating center.
25 (k) A facility that transfers or distributes natural gas, including
26 a compressor station, regulator station, city gate station or pressure
27 limiting station or a liquefied natural gas facility or supplier tap
28 facility.
29 (l) Any railroad infrastructure or facility.
30 (m) A federal, state, county or municipal court.
31 (n) A public safety or emergency operation facility.
32 (o) A federal, state, county or municipal jail or prison or other
33 facility in which persons are incarcerated.
34 (p) A federal or state military installation or facility.
35 (q) A hospital that receives air ambulance services.
36 4. "Model aircraft" has the same meaning prescribed in section 336
37 of the FAA modernization and reform act of 2012 (P.L. 112-95), as amended.
38 5. "Person" means a corporation, firm, partnership, association,
39 individual or organization or any other group acting as a unit.
40 6. "Public unmanned aircraft" means an unmanned aircraft or
41 unmanned aircraft system that is operated by a public agency for a
42 government-related purpose.
43 7. "TICKETED ENTERTAINMENT EVENT" MEANS A MUSIC, SPORTING OR
44 PERFORMING ARTS EVENT THAT IS HELD IN A LOCATION WITH GATED ENTRIES OR
45 BARRIERS THAT PREVENT ACCESS TO THE GENERAL PUBLIC AND IN WHICH A
46 REVOCABLE LICENSE THAT HAS BEEN ISSUED BY AN OWNER, OPERATOR OR LESSEE OF
47 THE PROPERTY IS REQUIRED FOR ATTENDANCE AT THE EVENT.
