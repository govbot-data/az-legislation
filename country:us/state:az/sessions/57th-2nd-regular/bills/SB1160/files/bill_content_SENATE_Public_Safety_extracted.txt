2 Section 1. Section 13-3729, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended to
3 read:
4 13-3729. Unlawful operation of model or unmanned aircraft;
5 state preemption; classification; definitions
6 A. It is unlawful for a person to operate a model aircraft or a
7 civil unmanned aircraft if the operation:
8 1. Is prohibited by a federal law or regulation that governs
9 aeronautics, including federal aviation administration regulations.
10 2. Interferes with a law enforcement, firefighter or emergency
11 services operation.
12 B. It is unlawful for a person to operate or use an unmanned
13 aircraft or unmanned aircraft system to intentionally photograph or loiter
14 over or near a critical facility in the furtherance of any criminal
15 offense.
16 C. IT IS UNLAWFUL FOR A PERSON TO OPERATE A CIVIL UNMANNED AIRCRAFT
17 IF THE OPERATION IS WITHIN [FOUR HUNDRED] [FIVE THOUSAND TWO HUNDRED
18 EIGHTY] FEET OF OR WITHIN [FOUR HUNDRED] [FIVE THOUSAND TWO HUNDRED
19 EIGHTY] FEET ABOVE A TICKETED ENTERTAINMENT EVENT UNLESS THE PERSON:
20 1. OBTAINS CONSENT FROM ANOTHER PERSON WITH LEGAL AUTHORITY OVER
21 THE TICKETED ENTERTAINMENT EVENT.
22 2. IS AN EMPLOYEE OF THE PROPERTY WHERE THE TICKETED ENTERTAINMENT
23 EVENT IS HELD AND IS CONDUCTING OFFICIAL BUSINESS.
24 3. IS AN EMPLOYEE OF A WATER, SEWER, ELECTRIC, TELECOMMUNICATIONS,
25 CABLE OR OTHER PUBLIC OR REGULATED UTILITY AND IS CONDUCTING OFFICIAL
26 BUSINESS.

22 2. Prohibit a city, town or county from enacting or adopting
23 ordinances or rules on the operation or use of a public unmanned aircraft

25 3. Prohibit a city, town or county from enacting or adopting
26 ordinances or rules that regulate the takeoff or landing of a model
27 aircraft in a park or preserve owned by the city, town or county if:
28 (a) There are other parks or preserves that are within the city,
29 town or county and that are available for model aircraft operation.
30 (b) The city, town or county only has one park or preserve that is
31 within the city, town or county.
32 4. Apply to the operation of an unmanned aircraft, including a
33 public unmanned aircraft, by a first responder as defined in section
34 36-661 while acting in the first responder's official capacity or an
35 emergency worker while engaged in or supporting authorized emergency

38 E. F. A violation of subsection B of this section is a class 6
39 felony, except that a second or subsequent violation is a class 5 felony.
40 A violation of subsection A OR C of this section is a class 1 misdemeanor.
41 F. G. For the purposes of this section:
42 1. "Civil unmanned aircraft" means an unmanned aircraft or unmanned
43 aircraft system that is operated by a person for any purpose other than
44 strictly for hobby or recreational purposes, including commercial
45 purposes, or in furtherance of or incidental to any business or media
46 service or agency.

30 4. "Model aircraft" has the same meaning prescribed in section 336
31 of the FAA modernization and reform act of 2012 (P.L. 112-95), as amended.
32 5. "Person" means a corporation, firm, partnership, association,
33 individual or organization or any other group acting as a unit.
34 6. "Public unmanned aircraft" means an unmanned aircraft or
35 unmanned aircraft system that is operated by a public agency for a
36 government-related purpose.
37 7. "TICKETED ENTERTAINMENT EVENT" MEANS A MUSIC, SPORTING OR

42 7. 8. "Unmanned aircraft" means an aircraft, including an aircraft
43 commonly known as a drone, that is operated without the possibility of
