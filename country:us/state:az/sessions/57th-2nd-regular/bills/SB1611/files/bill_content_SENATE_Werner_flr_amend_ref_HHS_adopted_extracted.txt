FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
1. Excludes services provided to an American Indian or Alaska Native (AI/AN) through a tribal
health program from the oversight of the administrative services organization.
2. Requires the Arizona Health Care Cost Containment System (AHCCCS), as part of any request
for proposals for an American Indian Health Program (AIHP) administrative services organization,
to require each offeror to demonstrate a proven history of competency and experience in providing
robust case management and complaint services to AI/AN populations in coordination with tribal
health programs.
3. Requires AHCCCS, before issuing any request for proposals for an administrative services
organization, to complete a meaningful and timely tribal consultation and urban confer regarding
implementation and subsequently, within 30 days, submit any necessary state plan amendment or
waiver to the U.S. Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services.
4. Replaces legislative appointments of observers to the AHCCCS source-selection committee with
appointments by each federally recognized Indian Tribe in Arizona and requires each observer to
be a member of a different tribe.
5. Requires the administrative services organization to establish a streamlined provider screening and
registration process to ensure only qualified providers are enrolled and prohibits the administrative
services organization from requiring any additional registration.
6. Prohibits the administrative services organization from requiring prior authorization for services
provided to an AIHP member when the member is referred from a tribal health program to a
nontribal provider or facility.
7. Requires the administrative services organization to conduct its duties in an efficient, competent
and culturally informed manner and prohibits the administrative services organization from
interfering with or overriding the clinical determinations or care management decisions of a
referring tribal health program.
8. Requires the administrative services organization to establish an office of tribal relations and
outlines duties of the office.
9. Requires AHCCCS and the administrative services organization to consult quarterly with the
Governor, tribal health programs, federally recognized Indian Tribes, AHCCCS providers and
AIHP members to evaluate the administrative services organization's performance and data
analytics to ensure tribal members are receiving quality, safe and appropriate care.
10. Modifies defined terms.
