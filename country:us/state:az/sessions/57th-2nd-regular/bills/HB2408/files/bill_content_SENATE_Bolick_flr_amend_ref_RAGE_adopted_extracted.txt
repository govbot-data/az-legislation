EFFICIENCY Committee Amendment
Amendment drafted by: Leg. Council
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
1. Removes the requirement for the Arizona State Board of Nursing to have the burden of proof by
clear and convincing evidence for disciplinary matters brought in accordance with outlined
complaint procedures.
2. Clarifies that the Arizona State Board of Nursing must share, rather than share and transfer, any
complaint it receives against a nursing program to the applicable higher education board within
five business days.
3. Makes conforming changes.
