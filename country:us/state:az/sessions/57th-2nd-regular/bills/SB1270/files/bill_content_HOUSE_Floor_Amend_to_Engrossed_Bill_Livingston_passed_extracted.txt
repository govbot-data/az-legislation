SB 1270: correctional officers; supplemental contributions
LIVINGSTON FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Requires the supplemental contribution incentive payment be limited to
$5,000 per participant per employment or service interval, rather than per
year, over a five-year graded vesting schedule.
