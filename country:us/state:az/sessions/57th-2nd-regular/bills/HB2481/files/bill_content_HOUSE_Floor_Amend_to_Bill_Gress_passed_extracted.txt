ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2481: school districts; records; noncompliance; penalties
GRESS FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Removes the $1,000 monthly civil penalty imposed against a school district
superintendent if the school district fails to correct a Uniform System of
Financial Records (USFR) deficiency within 18 months.
2. Requires SBE, if a school district fails to correct a deficiency within 18 months
after receiving notice from the Auditor General (OAG), to notify the school
district governing board (governing board) that the superintendent is ineligible
to receive performance-based pay beginning the first day of the 18th month
after the school district receives notice from the OAG and until the OAG
reports the school district's compliance with the USFR.
3. Prohibits a governing board from calling a district additional assistance
override or bond election if the school district fails to correct a USFR deficiency
within 18 months, rather than 90 days.
