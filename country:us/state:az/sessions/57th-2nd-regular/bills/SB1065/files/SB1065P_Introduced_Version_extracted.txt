SB1065 - 572R - I Ver PREFILED    DEC 19 2025 REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; hyperbaric oxygen therapy; veterans State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SB 1065 Introduced by Senator Rogers AN ACT appropriating monies to the hyperbaric oxygen therapy for military veterans fund. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; hyperbaric oxygen therapy for military veterans fund The sum of $3,640,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the hyperbaric oxygen therapy for military veterans fund established by section 41-610.01, Arizona Revised Statutes.
