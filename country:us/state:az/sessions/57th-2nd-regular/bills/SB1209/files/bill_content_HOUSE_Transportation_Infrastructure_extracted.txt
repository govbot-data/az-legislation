2 Section 1. Section 28-3165, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended to
3 read:
4 28-3165. Nonoperating identification license; immunity;
5 rules; emancipated minors
6 A. On receipt of an application from a person who does not have a
7 valid driver license issued by this state or whose driving privilege is
8 suspended, the department shall issue a nonoperating identification
9 license that contains a distinguishing number assigned to the licensee,
10 the full legal name, the date of birth, the residence address and a brief
11 description of the licensee and either a facsimile of the signature of the
12 licensee or a space on which the licensee is required to write the
13 licensee's usual signature with pen and ink. A nonoperating
14 identification license that is issued to a person whose driving privilege
15 is suspended shall not be valid for more than one hundred eighty days from
16 the date of issuance.
17 B. On request of an applicant:
18 1. The department shall allow the applicant to provide on the
19 nonoperating identification license a post office box address that is
20 regularly used by the applicant.
21 2. If the applicant submits satisfactory proof to the department
22 that the applicant is a veteran, the department shall allow a
23 distinguishing mark to appear on the nonoperating identification license
24 that identifies that person as a veteran.
25 3. If the applicant elects to show and submits satisfactory proof
26 to the department that the applicant is an enrolled member of a federally
27 recognized Indian tribe located in this state, the department shall allow
28 a distinguishing mark to appear on the nonoperating identification license
29 that identifies the applicant as a native American. The distinguishing

1 mark may not identify a specific Indian tribe or other specific personal
2 information that is submitted on the documents that provide satisfactory
3 proof that the person is an enrolled member of a federally recognized
4 Indian tribe located in this state. Documents that provide satisfactory
5 proof that the person is an enrolled member of a federally recognized
6 Indian tribe located in this state include:
7 (a) An enhanced tribal card.
8 (b) A tribal identification card.
9 (c) A tribal certificate of Indian blood.
10 (d) A tribal or bureau of Indian affairs affidavit of birth.
11 C. A person who is issued a license pursuant to this section shall
12 use it only for identification purposes of the licensee. The nonoperating
13 identification license does not grant authority to operate a motor vehicle
14 in this state. The department shall clearly label the nonoperating
15 identification license "for identification only, not for operation of a
16 motor vehicle".
17 D. On issuance of a driver license, the holder of a nonoperating
18 identification license shall surrender the nonoperating identification
19 license to the department, and the department shall not refund any fee
20 paid for the issuance of the nonoperating identification license.
21 E. A nonoperating identification license shall contain the
22 photograph of the licensee. When issuing a nonoperating identification
23 license, the department shall use a process that prohibits as nearly as
24 possible the ability to superimpose a photograph on the license without
25 ready detection. The department shall process nonoperating identification
26 licenses and photo attachments in color.
27 F. On application, an applicant shall give the department
28 satisfactory proof of the applicant's full legal name, date of birth, sex
29 and residence address, if the applicant has a residence address, and that
30 the applicant's presence in the United States is authorized under federal
31 law. The application shall briefly describe the applicant, state whether
32 the applicant has been licensed, and if so, the type of license issued,
33 when and by what state or country and whether any such license is under
34 suspension, revocation or cancellation. The application shall contain
35 other identifying information required by the department.
36 G. The department may adopt and implement procedures to deny a
37 nonoperating identification license to a person who has been deported.
38 The department may adopt and implement procedures to reinstate a person's
39 privilege to apply for a nonoperating identification license if the
40 person's legal presence status is restored.
41 H. A nonoperating identification license issued by the department
42 is solely for the use and convenience of the applicant for identification
43 purposes.
44 I. The department shall adopt rules and establish fees for issuance
45 of a nonoperating identification license, except that the department shall
46 not require an examination.
