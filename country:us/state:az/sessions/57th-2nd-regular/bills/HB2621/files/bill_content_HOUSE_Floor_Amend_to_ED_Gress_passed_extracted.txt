ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2621: public schools; special education; access
GRESS FLOOR AMENDMENT
Pupils Residing in an Unorganized Territory
1. Specifies the parent of a pupil who resides in an unorganized territory may
request a Child Find screening, a special education evaluation or enrollment
in the adjoining school district that is geographically closest to the pupil's
residence.
2. Modifies requirements for a program that provides early intervention services
relating to the process for transitioning a pupil from Part C to Part B of the
Individuals with Disabilities Education Act (IDEA).
3. Details authorizations and requirements relating to enrollment procedures for
a parent and an adjoining school district that is geographically closest to the
residence of the pupil.
4. Specifies the school district that is responsible for all Child Find activities and
for conducting a special education evaluation of a pupil who is parentally
placed in a private school or attends a homeschool.
5. Removes the requirement for the Arizona Department of Education (ADE) to
investigate any substantiated claims or complaints that allege a person is
violating requirements relating to the pupils residing in unorganized territory.
6. Defines special education evaluation.
Certificates of Educational Convenience (CECs)
7. Details CEC application submission requirements for a school district.
8. Prohibits a school district that receives a request for enrollment or a CEC
application from delaying Child Find activities, parental consent for a special
education evaluation or timelines under federal regulations because a CEC
application is processing.
9. Requires each county school superintendent, before FY 2028, to make publicly
available a CEC application form and instructions for submitting the
application form.
10. Narrows the CEC application form and supporting documentation
requirements to school districts that adjoin an unorganized territory.

11. Requires ADE to ensure that the CEC dispute resolution process does not limit
or delay a pupil's or parent's access to the state complaint process or due
process procedures under the IDEA.
12. Asserts specified individuals are not precluded from submitting a CEC
application for the pupil to the county school superintendent.
