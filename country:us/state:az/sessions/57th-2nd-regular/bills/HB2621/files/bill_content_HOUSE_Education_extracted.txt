4 15-816. Definitions
5 In this article, unless the context otherwise requires:
6 1. "Nonresident pupil" means a pupil who resides in this state and

9 2. "Open enrollment" means a policy that is adopted and implemented

11 enroll in any school within the school district, to allow resident pupils
12 to enroll in any school located within other school districts in this state
13 and to allow nonresident pupils to enroll in any school within the district
14 pursuant to section 15-816.01.
15 3. "Resident pupil" means a pupil WHO MEETS ANY OF THE FOLLOWING:

19 (c) WHO RESIDES IN AN UNORGANIZED TERRITORY AS DEFINED IN SECTION

22 4. "Resident school" means a school that is within the designated
