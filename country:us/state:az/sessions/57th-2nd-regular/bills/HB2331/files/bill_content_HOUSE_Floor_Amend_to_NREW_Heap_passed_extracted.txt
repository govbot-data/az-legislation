ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2331: electric service providers; energy reliability
HEAP FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Requires certain public power entities, on or after January 1, 2030 ensure
that at least 85% of the generating capacity that is relied on to serve retail
load customers consist of reliable resources.
2. Defines generating capacity, intermittent availability and reliable resource as
it relates to public power entities.
3. Removes requirements of the Arizona Corporation Commission regarding the
implementation and enforcement of this act.
4. Modifies the definition of reliable resource as it relates to public service
corporations.
5. Removes the definitions of performance standard and resource accreditation.
