SB1059 - 572R - S Ver Senate Engrossed appropriation; ADOT; turn lane State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SENATE BILL 1059 AN ACT Appropriating monies to the Department of Transportation. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; department of transportation; State Route 87 and State Route 260; additional right turn lane The sum of $9,200,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the department of transportation for costs associated with planning, designing, constructing and the acquisition of right-of-way or easements for an additional right turn lane at the intersection of State Route 87 and State Route 260.
