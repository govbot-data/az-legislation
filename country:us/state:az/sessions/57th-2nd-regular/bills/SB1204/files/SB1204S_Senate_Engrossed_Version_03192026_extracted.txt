SB1204 - 572R - S Ver Senate Engrossed appropriation; U.S. 60; SR 303 State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SENATE BILL 1204 AN ACT Appropriating monies to the department of transportation. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; department of transportation; interchange improvements; U.S. Route 60 and State Route 303 The sum of $5,500,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the department of transportation to design and conduct an environmental analysis of interchange improvements at U.S. Route 60 and State Route 303.
