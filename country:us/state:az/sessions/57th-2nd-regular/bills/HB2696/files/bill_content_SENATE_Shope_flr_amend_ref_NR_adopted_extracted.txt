Shope Floor Amendment
Reference to: NATURAL RESOURCES
Committee amendment
Amendment drafted by: Leg. Council
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
1. Allows, rather than requires, the Arizona Commerce Authority (ACA) to use available
resources to improve motor fuel and gasoline resilience, rather than fuel and gas prices, in Arizona.
2. Requires the ACA to consider the improvement of motor fuel and gasoline resilience as one of it's
objectives.
3. Removes the requirement that the ACA consider the reduction of fuel and gas prices as it's primary
objective.
4. Allows, rather than requires, the ACA to use any existing program or incentive to advance the
objective.
5. Extends the date for the repeal of the ACA fuel and gas resilience requirements from January 1,
2030, to January 1, 2031.
6. Requires the ACA to collaborate with members of the Fuel Resiliency Task Force (Task Force),
rather than oil and gas industry, to study and develop recommendations on:
a) the process for the revising the state implantation plan for 2015 national ambient air quality
standards for the Phoenix-Mesa non-attainment area cleaner burning gasoline blend program
and the potential impact on gas prices, motor fuel supplies and air quality in Arizona by
revising the state implementation plan;
b) the construction of additional refineries and storage capacities in Arizona to support the
stability and resiliency of the fuel supply of Arizona; and
c) the availability of potential alternative lower-production-cost fuel blends that could be sold in
Arizona and the potential states or producers that could supply the alternative fuel.
7. Removes the requirements that the ACA study:
a) the impact of and process for the repeal of Arizona's cleaner burning gasoline blend, including
an evaluation of potential alternative lower cost blends;
b) the construction of additional refined products pipelines in Arizona and establishment of a
strategic oil and gas reserve in Arizona; and
c) the opportunity to construct one or more potential refineries in Arizona, including restarting
and reevaluating, a proposed oil refinery in Yuma County.
8. Extends the prescribed ACA reporting deadline from October 1, 2026, to September 30, 2029.

9. Requires the prescribed report to contain
a) the minutes of the fuel resiliency Task Force meetings;
b) the names, titles and employers of each federal official and member of the oil and gas industry
that the Task Force consulted with in developing the recommendations and the nature and
extent of the consultation;
c) recommendations for action on each outlined item that the ACA and Task Force must study
and develop recommendations on for including recommendations for executive action,
legislative action and private sector action; and
d) a description of the areas of agreement or disagreement of the recommendations between
individuals with whom the ACA consulted.
10. Modifies the membership of the Task Force by removing and adding members.
11. Requires the Chief Executive Officer of the ACA, or the Chief Executive Officer's designee, to
serve as the chairperson of the Task Force.
12. Requires the ACA, rather than the Task Force, to keep minutes of each meeting of the Task Force.
13. Removes specified meeting requirements for the Task Force, as outlined.
14. Requires the Task Force to study and develop recommendations of the items related to the outlined
study and recommendations.
15. Requires the Task Force to convene with federal officials, members of the oil and gas industry in
the United States, subject matter experts as determined by the Task Force and:
a) refinery operators;
b) the state energy offices in other states within petroleum administrative defense districts three,
four and five;
c) the Texas Oil and Gas Association;
d) the New Mexico Oil and Gas Association;
e) the Utah Petroleum Association;
f) the Colorado Petroleum Association;
g) the Permian Basin Petroleum Association;
h) the American Fuel and Petrochemical Manufacturers;
i) the Navajo Nation Oil and Gas Company; and
j) the National Tribal Energy Association.
16. Specifies that the Task Force, by September 30, 2029, collaborate with the ACA to submit a report
of the Task Force's findings and recommendations.
17. Extends the repeal date of the Task Force from January 1, 2029, to January 1, 2031.
18. Makes conforming changes.
