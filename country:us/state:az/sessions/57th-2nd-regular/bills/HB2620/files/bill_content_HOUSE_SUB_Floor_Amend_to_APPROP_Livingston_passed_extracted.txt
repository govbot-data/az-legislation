FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2620: appropriation; homeless veterans; shelter services
LIVINGSTON FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Requires an annual report by the Department of Veterans Services to the
Governor, Senate, House, and Chairman and Vice Chairman of the Joint
Legislative Audit Committee detailing a summary of expenditures to aid
homeless veterans, metrics, and the discharge status of veterans aided by the
funding.
2. Requires a final report by June 30, 2031 from the Department of Veterans
Services on the activities funded, outcomes from the program, the number of
individuals served, and recommendations from the findings.
3. Repeals the section from and after December 31, 2031.
