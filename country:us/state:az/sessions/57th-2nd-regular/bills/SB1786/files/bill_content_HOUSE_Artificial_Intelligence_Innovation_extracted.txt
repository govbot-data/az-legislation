2 Section 1. Title 18, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended by adding
3 chapter 8, to read:
4 CHAPTER 8
5 ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE
6 ARTICLE 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS
7 18-801. Artificial intelligence; content verification;
8 definitions
9 A. A COVERED PROVIDER SHALL INCLUDE PROVENANCE DATA IN ANY VIDEO,
10 IMAGE OR AUDIO CONTENT [OR CONTENT THAT IS ANY COMBINATION OF A VIDEO, AN
11 IMAGE OR AUDIO CONTENT] THAT IS CREATED OR MATERIALLY ALTERED BY THE
12 COVERED PROVIDER'S GENERATIVE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM [AND THAT IS
13 AVAILABLE TO BE SHARED WITH USERS OUTSIDE OF THE GENERATIVE ARTIFICIAL
14 INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM. THIS SUBSECTION DOES NOT APPLY TO CONTENT THAT IS
15 GENERATED AS A RESULT OF AN INTERACTIVE EXPERIENCE, INCLUDING VOICE
16 ASSISTANT RESPONSES, VIRTUAL REALITY ENVIRONMENTS, AUGMENTED REALITY
17 APPLICATIONS, REAL-TIME CONVERSATIONAL ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE
18 INTERACTIONS AND OTHER REAL-TIME, DYNAMIC INTERACTIONS BETWEEN A USER AND
19 A GENERATIVE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM].
20 B. A COVERED PROVIDER SHALL USE COMMERCIALLY AND TECHNICALLY
21 REASONABLE METHODS TO MAKE THE PROVENANCE DATA DIFFICULT TO REMOVE OR
22 TAMPER WITH. THE USE OF A COMMONLY SUPPORTED TECHNICAL STANDARD FOR
23 WATERMARKING AND METADATA COMPLIES WITH THE REQUIREMENTS OF THIS
24 SUBSECTION.
25 C. THE CONTENT IS NOT CONSIDERED MATERIALLY ALTERED FOR PURPOSES OF
26 THIS SECTION IF THE ALTERATION CONSISTS OF A MINOR MODIFICATION THAT DOES
27 NOT LEAD TO SIGNIFICANT CHANGES TO THE PERCEIVED CONTENT OR MEANING OF THE
28 CONTENT.

15 Sec. 2. Legislative findings
16 The legislature intends that section 18-801, Arizona Revised
17 Statutes, as added by this act, be broadly construed and applied to
18 promote the underlying purpose of the act, which is all of the following:
19 1. To protect individuals and groups of individuals from known, and
20 unknown but reasonably foreseeable, risks associated with artificial
21 intelligence.
22 2. To provide transparency regarding those risks in the
23 development, deployment or use or artificial intelligence systems.
24 3. To provide reasonable notice regarding the use of artificial
25 intelligence systems.
26 4. To protect the public from fraud or deception.
