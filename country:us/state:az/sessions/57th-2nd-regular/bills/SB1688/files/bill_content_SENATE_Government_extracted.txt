2 Section 1. Title 38, chapter 1, article 1, Arizona Revised

4 38-102. Membership associations; annual revenue; public
5 monies; website; definition
6 A. A MEMBERSHIP ASSOCIATION IN THIS STATE THAT RECEIVES OVER FIFTY
7 PERCENT OF ITS ANNUAL REVENUE FROM DUES THAT ARE PAID BY AN ELECTED OR
8 APPOINTED OFFICIAL [OR A STAFF MEMBER OF AN ELECTED OR APPOINTED OFFICIAL]
9 USING PUBLIC MONIES SHALL PUBLICLY DISCLOSE ALL FEES AND ANNUAL MEMBERSHIP
10 COSTS ON ITS WEBSITE TO ENSURE TRANSPARENCY REGARDING SERVICES AND FEES
11 PAID BY ELECTED OR APPOINTED OFFICIALS [OR STAFF MEMBERS OF ELECTED OR
12 APPOINTED OFFICIALS] USING PUBLIC MONIES.
13 B. IF A PUBLIC BODY VOTES TO JOIN A MEMBERSHIP ASSOCIATION IN THIS
14 STATE AND AN OFFICIAL ON THE PUBLIC BODY [OR A STAFF MEMBER OF THE PUBLIC
15 BODY] ELECTS NOT TO JOIN THE MEMBERSHIP ASSOCIATION, THE DUES ASSESSED TO
16 THAT OFFICIAL [MAY][OR STAFF MEMBER SHALL] NOT BE PAID TO THE MEMBERSHIP
17 ASSOCIATION BY THE PUBLIC BODY.

Senate Amendments to S.B. 1688
1 C. FOR THE PURPOSES OF THIS SECTION, "MEMBERSHIP ASSOCIATION":
