HB2432 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; Yavapai community college; health State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HB 2432 Introduced by Representatives Bliss: Nguyen AN ACT Appropriating monies to the Yavapai county community college district. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; Yavapai county community college district; health sciences; exemption A. The sum of $10,000,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to Yavapai county community college district for Yavapai college's health sciences division. B. The appropriation made in subsection A of this section is exempt from the provisions of section 35-190, Arizona Revised Statutes, relating to lapsing of appropriations, until June 30, 2030.
