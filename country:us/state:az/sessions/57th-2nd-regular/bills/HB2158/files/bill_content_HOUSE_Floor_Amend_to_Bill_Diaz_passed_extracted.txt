2 Section 1. Section 17-101, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended to
3 read:
4 17-101. Definitions
5 A. In this title, unless the context otherwise requires:
6 1. "Angling" means taking fish by one line and not more than two
7 hooks, by one line and one artificial lure, which may have attached more
8 than one hook, or by one line and not more than two artificial flies or
9 lures.
10 2. "Bag limit" means the maximum limit, in number or amount, of
11 wildlife that any one person may lawfully take during a specified period
12 of time.
13 3. "Closed season" means the time during which wildlife may not be
14 lawfully taken.
15 4. "Commission" means the Arizona game and fish commission.
16 5. "Department" means the Arizona game and fish department.
17 6. "Device" means any net, trap, snare, salt lick, scaffold,
18 deadfall, pit, explosive, poison or stupefying substance, crossbow,
19 firearm, bow and arrow, or other implement used for taking wildlife.
20 Device does not include a raptor or any equipment used in the sport of
21 falconry.
22 7. "Domicile" means a person's true, fixed and permanent home and
23 principal residence. Proof of domicile in this state may be shown as
24 prescribed by rule by the commission.
25 8. "Falconry" means the sport of hunting or taking quarry with a
26 trained raptor.
27 9. "Fishing" means to lure, attract or pursue aquatic wildlife in
28 such a manner that the wildlife may be captured or killed.
