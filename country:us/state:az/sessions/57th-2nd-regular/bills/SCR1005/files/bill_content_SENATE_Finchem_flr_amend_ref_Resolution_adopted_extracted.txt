Amendment drafted by: Leg. Council
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
1. Specifies that contributions from foreign nationals, rather than foreign persons or corporations, are
prohibited for ballot measures.
2. Defines foreign national as:
a) an individual who is not a citizen or national of the United States, or who is not lawfully
admitted for permanent residence in the United States;
b) a foreign government or foreign political party; or
c) a partnership, association, corporation, organization or other combination of persons that is
organized under the laws of a country other than the United States, or that has its principal
place of business in a country other than the United States.
