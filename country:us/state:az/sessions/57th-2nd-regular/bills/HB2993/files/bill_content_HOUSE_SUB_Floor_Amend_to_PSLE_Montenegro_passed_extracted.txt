ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2993: DPS; legal representation; appropriation
MONTENEGRO FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Increases the appropriation from the Consumer Protection-Consumer Fraud
Revolving Fund from $5,000,000 to $6,400,000.
2. Redirects the aforesaid monies to be a supplemental appropriation for the
Department of Public Safety.
