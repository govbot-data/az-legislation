HB2897 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; Kin Dah Lichii community State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HB 2897 Introduced by Representative Tsosie AN ACT APPROPRIATING monies to the department of administration. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; department of administration; classroom construction; Kin Dah Lichii Community The sum of $1,800,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the department of administration for construction of a three thousand square foot, one classroom building within the Kin Dah Lichii Community.
