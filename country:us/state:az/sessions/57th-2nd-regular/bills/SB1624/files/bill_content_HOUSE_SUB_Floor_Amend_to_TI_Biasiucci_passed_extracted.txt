ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
SB 1624: photo radar enforcement; civil penalty
BIASIUCCI FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Excludes red light and school crossing violations from civil traffic violations
that result from a photo enforcement system.
2. Stipulates that of the $75 for the civil penalty, $15 must be deposited into the Peace
Officer Training Equipment Fund.
3. Makes technical and conforming changes.

8 A. Notwithstanding any other law, if a person receives a notice of

15 B. The notice of violation must state the following:
16 1. The notice OF VIOLATION is not a court issued document and the

18 notice OF VIOLATION.
19 2. Failure to respond to the notice OF VIOLATION may result in
20 official service that may result in an additional fee being levied.
21 C. Before a citation is issued, a law enforcement agency must
22 review evidence that is recorded by a photo enforcement system to

24 or of a city or town ordinance for excessive speed or failure to obey a
