ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HCR2048: elected officials; salary; prohibition
KUPPER FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Removes the following individuals from provisions relating to withholding
salaries until the general appropriation bill for the next fiscal year is enacted:
a. Secretary of State;
b. Attorney General;
c. State Treasurer; and
d. Superintendent of Public Instruction.
