HB2069 - 572R - I Ver PREFILED    DEC 16 2025 REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; Apache Junction; water infrastructure State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HB 2069 Introduced by Representative Blackman AN ACT Appropriating monies to the department of water resources. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; department of water resources; water infrastructure The following amounts are appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the department of water resources for the following purposes: 1. $18,000,000 to the Apache Junction water district for water and sewer capacity expansion. 2. $10,000,000 to the city of Apache Junction for the Weekes wash regional multi-use detention facility for stormwater retention. 3. $12,500,000 to the Apache Junction water district for water resilience and conservation projects.
