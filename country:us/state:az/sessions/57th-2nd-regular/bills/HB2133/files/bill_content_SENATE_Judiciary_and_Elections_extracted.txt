2 Section 1. Section 13-1425, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended to
3 read:
4 13-1425. Unlawful disclosure of images depicting states of
5 nudity or specific sexual activities;
6 classification; definitions
7 A. It is unlawful for a person to intentionally disclose an image
8 of another person who is identifiable from the image itself or from
9 information displayed in connection with the image if all of the following
10 apply:
11 1. The person in the image is depicted in a state of nudity or is
12 engaged in specific sexual activities.
13 2. The depicted person has a reasonable expectation of privacy.
14 Evidence that a person has sent an image to another person using an
15 electronic device does not, on its own, remove the person's reasonable
16 expectation of privacy for that image. Unless the realistic pictorial
17 representation is created or modified by the depicted person, this
18 paragraph does not apply to an image that is a realistic pictorial
19 representation.
20 3. The image is disclosed with the intent to harm, harass,
21 intimidate, threaten or coerce the depicted person.
22 B. This section does not apply to any of the following:
23 1. The reporting of unlawful conduct.
24 2. Lawful and common practices of law enforcement, criminal
25 reporting, legal proceedings or medical treatment.
26 3. Images involving voluntary exposure in a public or commercial
27 setting.
28 4. An interactive computer service, as defined in 47 United States
29 Code section 230(f)(2), or an information service or cable service, as
30 defined in 47 United States Code section 153, with regard to content
31 wholly provided by another party.

9 as transferred and renumbered, is amended by adding section 44-7302, to
10 read:
11 44-7302. Sexual material on the internet; verification and
12 consent requirements; records; applicability;
13 civil penalties; definitions
14 A. A COMMERCIAL ENTITY THAT KNOWINGLY AND INTENTIONALLY PUBLISHES
15 OR DISTRIBUTES, [OR] [AND] ALLOWS THE PUBLICATION OR DISTRIBUTION OF,
16 SEXUAL MATERIAL ON AN INTERNET WEBSITE SHALL DO ALL OF THE FOLLOWING:
17 1. REQUIRE EACH PERSON WHO UPLOADS OR PLACES THE SEXUAL MATERIAL ON
18 AN INTERNET WEBSITE TO VERIFY, USING REASONABLE CONSENT VERIFICATION
19 METHODS, THAT EACH INDIVIDUAL WHO IS DEPICTED IN THE SEXUAL MATERIAL:
20 (a) HAS PROVIDED EXPLICIT INFORMED CONSENT TO THE CREATION,
21 DISTRIBUTION AND PUBLICATION OF THE SEXUAL MATERIAL.
22 (b) WAS AT LEAST EIGHTEEN YEARS OF AGE AT THE TIME THE MATERIAL WAS
23 CREATED.
24 2. MAINTAIN RECORDS OF THE VERIFICATION FOR AT LEAST SEVEN YEARS.
25 ON REQUEST, THE ATTORNEY GENERAL MAY INSPECT THE RECORDS.
26 3. IMPLEMENT REASONABLE MEASURES TO PREVENT THE UPLOADING OF SEXUAL
27 MATERIAL THAT DOES NOT HAVE VERIFIED CONSENT, INCLUDING USING AUTOMATED
28 DETECTION TOOLS WHERE FEASIBLE.
29 B. A COMMERCIAL ENTITY MAY NOT RETAIN ANY IDENTIFYING INFORMATION
30 OF THE DEPICTED PERSON AFTER THE VERIFICATION IS COMPLETE EXCEPT AS
31 REQUIRED FOR THE RECORDKEEPING REQUIREMENTS PRESCRIBED IN THIS SECTION.
32 THE COMMERCIAL ENTITY MAY NOT CAUSE OR ALLOW ANY IDENTIFYING INFORMATION
33 TO BE TRANSMITTED TO ANY FEDERAL, STATE OR LOCAL GOVERNMENT ENTITY.
34 C. THIS SECTION DOES NOT APPLY TO ANY OF THE FOLLOWING:
35 1. A NEWS OR PUBLIC INTEREST BROADCAST OR PUBLICATION.
36 2. MATERIAL THAT IS DISTRIBUTED FOR BONA FIDE SCIENTIFIC, MEDICAL
37 OR EDUCATIONAL PURPOSES.
38 3. AN INTERNET SERVICE PROVIDER, AN INTERNET SERVICE PROVIDER'S
39 AFFILIATES OR SUBSIDIARIES, A SEARCH ENGINE OR A CLOUD SERVICE PROVIDER
40 THAT SOLELY PROVIDES ACCESS OR A CONNECTION TO OR FROM A WEBSITE OR OTHER
41 INFORMATION OR CONTENT ON THE INTERNET OR ON A FACILITY, SYSTEM OR NETWORK
42 THAT IS NOT UNDER THAT INTERNET SERVICE PROVIDER'S CONTROL, INCLUDING
43 TRANSMISSION, DOWNLOADING, INTERMEDIATE STORAGE, ACCESS SOFTWARE OR OTHER
44 SERVICES TO THE EXTENT THAT THE INTERNET SERVICE PROVIDER, SEARCH ENGINE
45 OR CLOUD SERVICE PROVIDER IS NOT RESPONSIBLE FOR THE CREATION OR DIRECT
46 HOSTING OF THE SEXUAL MATERIAL.

35 (iii) INFORMATION LOCATION TOOLS, INCLUDING SEARCH ENGINES, THAT
36 ONLY INDEX, LINK TO OR REFERENCE CONTENT THAT IS HOSTED BY A THIRD PARTY
37 WITHOUT STORING THE MATERIAL ON THE COMMERCIAL ENTITY'S CONTROLLED
38 SYSTEMS.
