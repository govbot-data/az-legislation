HB2178 - 572R - H Ver House Engrossed agency; chief medical officer; license State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HOUSE BILL 2178 AN ACT Amending title 41, chapter 57, article 1, Arizona Revised Statutes, by adding section 41-5902; relating to state agencies. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Heading change The article heading of title 41, chapter 57, article 1, Arizona Revised Statutes, is changed from "VACCINATION STATUS" to "STATE AGENCIES generally". Sec. 2. Title 41, chapter 57, article 1, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended by adding section 41-5902, to read: START_STATUTE41-5902. Chief medical officer; medical license Notwithstanding any other law, any person who is serving as the chief medical officer for a state agency must possess an active license to practice medicine in this state pursuant to title 32, chapter 13 or 17. END_STATUTE
