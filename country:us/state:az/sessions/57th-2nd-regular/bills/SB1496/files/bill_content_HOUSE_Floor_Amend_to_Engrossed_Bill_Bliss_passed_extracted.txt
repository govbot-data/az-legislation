ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
SB 1496: DCS; policies; procedures
BLISS FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Prohibits foster parents, caregivers, placement facilities, and their employees,
contractors, and agents from retaliating against a child in DCS care for
reporting suspected violations of the child's personal rights or participating in
an investigation, provided the report was made in good faith or based on a
reasonable belief that the violation occurred.
2. Entitles a child in foster care or kinship foster care with the right to voluntarily
participate in enrichment activities and be free of bullying or discrimination.
3. Removes an exception that previously allowed punishment if a child was
proven to have maliciously or wrongfully accused a foster parent.
4. Defines the term retaliatory action.
5. Makes technical and conforming changes.
