FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
SB 1496: DCS; policies; procedures
BLISS FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Removes the requirement that the appointed attorney meet with a child who
is at least five years old once a month.
2. Requires an appointed attorney, when determining a child's capacity, to
consult with persons who have a significant relationship with the child and the
child's family or an authorized representative of the child's family.
3. Requires county offices that employ the appointed attorneys to represent
children in dependency or termination of parental rights proceedings to submit
a report to the Governor and Legislature by January 15, 2027.
4. Requires the report to be compiled monthly for the period of June 1, 2026,
through December 31, 2026, and outlines data requirements.
5. Repeals the reporting requirements on July, 1, 2027.
