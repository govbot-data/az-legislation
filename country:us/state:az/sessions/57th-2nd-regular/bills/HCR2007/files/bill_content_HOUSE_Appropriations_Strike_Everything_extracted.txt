11 Section 1. Title 15, chapter 9, article 1, Arizona
12 Revised Statutes, is amended by adding section 15-917, to
13 read:
14 15-917. Operational spending; direct instructional
15 expenses; direct instruction fund; auditor
16 general; waiver; definition
17 A. EACH SCHOOL DISTRICT THAT HAS A STUDENT COUNT OF AT
18 LEAST FIVE HUNDRED SHALL:
19 1. SPEND AT LEAST SIXTY PERCENT OF THE SCHOOL
20 DISTRICT'S OPERATIONAL SPENDING ON DIRECT INSTRUCTIONAL
21 EXPENSES.
22 2. SPEND AT LEAST FIFTY PERCENT OF THE SCHOOL
23 DISTRICT'S OPERATIONAL SPENDING ON TEACHER PAY.
24 B. BEGINNING IN FISCAL YEAR 2027-2028, ANY SCHOOL
25 DISTRICT THAT IS NOT COMPLYING WITH SUBSECTION A OF THIS
26 SECTION SHALL INCREASE THE PORTION OF THE SCHOOL DISTRICT'S
27 OPERATIONAL SPENDING THAT IS SPENT ON DIRECT INSTRUCTIONAL
28 EXPENSES OR TEACHER PAY, OR BOTH, EACH YEAR BY AT LEAST
29 ONE-HALF OF ONE PERCENT OF THE PORTION OF THE SCHOOL
30 DISTRICT'S OPERATIONAL SPENDING THAT IS SPENT ON DIRECT
31 INSTRUCTIONAL EXPENSES OR TEACHER PAY, OR BOTH, IN THE
32 2024-2025 SCHOOL YEAR UNTIL THE SCHOOL DISTRICT IS IN
33 COMPLIANCE. IF A SCHOOL DISTRICT INCREASES THE PORTION OF THE
34 SCHOOL DISTRICT'S OPERATIONAL SPENDING REQUIRED BY SUBSECTION
35 A OF THIS SECTION BY MORE THAN ONE-HALF OF ONE PERCENT IN ANY
36 YEAR, THE SCHOOL DISTRICT MAY COUNT THE PORTION OF THE
37 INCREASE THAT EXCEEDS ONE-HALF OF ONE PERCENT TOWARD ANY
38 INCREASE THAT IS REQUIRED IN A SUBSEQUENT YEAR. A SCHOOL
39 DISTRICT SHALL USE UNRESTRICTED OPERATIONAL SPENDING TO COMPLY

4 C. IF IN THE ANNUAL REPORT REQUIRED BY SECTION
5 41-1279.03, THE AUDITOR GENERAL IDENTIFIES A SCHOOL DISTRICT

13 (a) THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN THE AMOUNT THAT THE SCHOOL

18 OF THE AUDITOR GENERAL'S DETERMINATION.
19 (b) THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN THE AMOUNT THAT THE SCHOOL
20 DISTRICT WAS REQUIRED TO SPEND ON TEACHER PAY PURSUANT TO
21 SUBSECTION A OF THIS SECTION AND THE AMOUNT THAT THE SCHOOL
22 DISTRICT ACTUALLY SPENT ON TEACHER PAY FOR THE SCHOOL YEAR
23 THAT IS THE SUBJECT OF THE AUDITOR GENERAL'S DETERMINATION.
24 2. IF THE SCHOOL DISTRICT IS SUBJECT TO A WITHHOLDING
25 PURSUANT TO THIS SUBSECTION, THE SCHOOL DISTRICT SHALL
26 ESTABLISH A DIRECT INSTRUCTION FUND. THE SCHOOL DISTRICT

28 EXPENSES.
