ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HCR2007: reporting; teacher salary increases; schools
S/E: school districts; instructional expenses; requirements
GRESS FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Applies the direct instructional expenses requirement to each school district
with a student count of at least 7,500, rather than 500, or that operates a school
in a county with a population of at least 500,000.
2. Removes the requirement that a school district spend at least 50% of its
operational spending on teacher pay.
3. Removes the requirement for a school district to use unrestricted operational
spending to comply with the direct instructional expenses requirement.
4. Prohibits the Auditor General (OAG), if a school district reduces the portion of
its operational spending on direct instructional expenses, from considering the
reduction when calculating the amount of the required annual direct
instructional expenses increase.
5. Stipulates that if a school district reduces the portion of its operational
spending on direct instructional expenses, the school district may count only
the portion of an increase in operational spending on direct instructional
expenses for a subsequent year that results in an increase above the portion of
operational spending before the reduction.
6. Deletes the state aid withholding penalty and direct instruction fund
requirements for a school district that the OAG identifies did not comply with
the direct instructional expenses requirement.
7. Directs the Arizona Department of Education (ADE) to reduce, if the school
district receives Classroom Site Fund (CSF) monies and the OAG determines
a school district did not comply with the direct instructional expenses spending
requirement in the immediately preceding fiscal year, a school district's CSF
allocation for the budget year as follows:
a. 25% of the amount that would otherwise be allocated if the school district
did not comply for one year;
b. 50% of the amount that would otherwise be allocated if the school district
did not comply for two years;
c. 75% of the amount that would otherwise be allocated if the school district
did not comply for three years; and

d. an amount equal to the amount that would be otherwise allocated if the
school district did not comply for at least four years.
8. Instructs Joint Legislative Budget Committee staff to include in the CSF
carryforward the monies that are not distributed because of the required
reductions.
9. Modifies waiver procedures by authorizing the Superintendent of Public
Instruction (SPI) to waive the CSF reduction requirements, except that:
a. the SPI may not issue more than two waivers for a school district during
any 10-year period, unless otherwise allowed; and
b. if the SPI issues a second waiver for a school district more than four years
after the SPI issues a first waiver, the SPI may issue another waiver for the
school district at any time during the 10-year period beginning the date the
SPI issues the second waiver.
10. Clarifies the state or a state officer or agency is not required to maintain the
CSF or allocate monies for a CSF except as otherwise provided by state law.
11. Requires the OAG to define direct instructional expenses and operational
spending.
