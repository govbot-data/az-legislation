FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
• Requires the Arizona Department of Education to annually review information that is readily
available to determine whether an alternative education program or alternative school satisfies the
eligibility requirement that at least 70 percent of the students enrolled are students who are in
grades 6 through 12 and are unable to profit from the regular school course of study and
environment.
