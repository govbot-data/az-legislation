2 Section 1. Title 9, chapter 4, article 8, Arizona Revised Statutes,
3 is amended by adding section 9-500.54, to read:
4 9-500.54. Malicious delay; attorney general; violation; civil
5 penalty; definitions
6 A. NOTWITHSTANDING ANY OTHER LAW, A CITY OR TOWN IN THIS STATE MAY
7 NOT MALICIOUSLY DELAY ADOPTED LICENSING TIME FRAMES PURSUANT TO SECTION
8 9-835 FOR SINGLE-FAMILY RESIDENTIAL CONSTRUCTION OR A RESPONSE TO AN
9 APPLICATION FOR SINGLE-FAMILY RESIDENTIAL CONSTRUCTION.
10 B. THE ATTORNEY GENERAL, BASED ON A WRITTEN COMPLAINT BY A PROPERTY
11 OWNER WITH AN APPLICATION BEFORE A CITY OR TOWN, MAY ENFORCE THIS SECTION.
12 C. FOLLOWING AN INVESTIGATION AND FINDINGS BY THE ATTORNEY GENERAL,
13 A CITY OR TOWN IN THIS STATE THAT IS FOUND TO HAVE VIOLATED THIS SECTION
14 BY CLEAR AND CONVINCING EVIDENCE IS SUBJECT TO A CIVIL PENALTY OF $5,000
15 PER VIOLATION REGARDLESS OF THE DURATION OF THE DELAY.
16 D. THIS SECTION DOES NOT MODIFY THE AUTHORITY OF A BUILDING CODE
17 OFFICIAL TO WITHHOLD A CERTIFICATE OF OCCUPANCY IN ACCORDANCE WITH A
18 CITY'S OR TOWN'S ADOPTED CODES AND ORDINANCES.
19 E. IF THE ATTORNEY GENERAL FINDS THAT A CITY OR TOWN VIOLATED THIS
20 SECTION, THE CITY OR TOWN MAY BRING AN ACTION IN A COURT WITH APPROPRIATE
21 JURISDICTION TO CHALLENGE THE FINDING OF A MALICIOUS DELAY.
22 F. FOR THE PURPOSES OF THIS SECTION:
23 1. "APPLICATION" MEANS A SITE PLAN, DEVELOPMENT PLAN, LAND
24 DIVISION, LOT LINE ADJUSTMENT, LOT TIE, PRELIMINARY PLAT, FINAL PLAT, PLAT
25 AMENDMENT OR BUILDING PERMIT FOR SINGLE-FAMILY RESIDENTIAL CONSTRUCTION.
26 2. "MALICIOUS":
27 (a) MEANS ACTING WITH SPECIFIC INTENT TO OBSTRUCT APPROVAL OF AN
28 APPLICATION BY IMPOSING REQUIREMENTS NOT SPECIFICALLY AUTHORIZED BY CODE,

8 Sec. 3. Title 12, chapter 8, article 2.1, Arizona Revised Statutes,
9 is amended by adding section 12-1139, to read:
10 12-1139. Property right claims; malicious delay; land use
11 claims; expedited review; definitions
12 A. A PLAINTIFF THAT FILES A COMPLAINT IN SUPERIOR COURT THAT
13 ASSERTS A MALICIOUS DELAY CLAIM PURSUANT TO SECTIONS 9-500.54 OR 11-269.31
14 FOR A SINGLE-FAMILY RESIDENTIAL APPLICATION IS ENTITLED TO EXPEDITED
15 JUDICIAL REVIEW OF THE CLAIM.
16 B. WITHIN TEN CALENDAR DAYS AFTER THE SERVICE OF THE COMPLAINT, THE
17 COURT SHALL SET AN INITIAL CASE MANAGEMENT CONFERENCE WITH THE PARTIES TO
18 ESTABLISH AN EXPEDITED SCHEDULE FOR DISCOVERY, BRIEFING AND HEARINGS.
19 C. UNLESS OTHERWISE ORDERED FOR GOOD CAUSE, ALL FACT DISCOVERY IN
20 THE ACTION SHALL BE COMPLETED AS EXPEDITIOUSLY AS POSSIBLE AFTER THE
21 INITIAL CASE CONFERENCE.
22 D. THE COURT SHALL CONSIDER AND RULE ON ALL MOTIONS FOR SUMMARY
23 JUDGMENT OR OTHER DISPOSITIVE MOTIONS AS SOON AS PRACTICABLE.
24 E. THE COURT SHALL SET A TRIAL OR FINAL HEARING DATE AS SOON AS
25 PRACTICABLE AFTER THE CASE MANAGEMENT CONFERENCE, ABSENT A FINDING OF GOOD
26 CAUSE.
27 F. AN APPEAL TO THE COURT OF APPEALS FROM A SUPERIOR COURT JUDGMENT
28 IN AN ACTION DESCRIBED IN SUBSECTION A OF THIS SECTION IS ENTITLED TO A
29 SCHEDULING PREFERENCE ON THE APPELLATE DOCKET, AND THE COURT OF APPEALS
30 SHALL EXPEDITE BRIEFING SCHEDULES AND ORAL ARGUMENT CONSISTENT WITH THE
31 INTERESTS OF JUSTICE UNLESS THE COURT FINDS FOR GOOD CAUSE THAT EXPEDITED
32 REVIEW IS NOT FEASIBLE.
33 G. A PARTY MAY PETITION FOR SPECIAL ACTION RELIEF IN THE COURT OF
34 APPEALS OR SUPREME COURT AND SUCH ACTIONS SHALL RECEIVE EXPEDITED
35 PROCESSING.
36 H. FOR THE PURPOSES OF THIS SECTION:
37 1. "APPLICATION" MEANS ANY OF THE FOLLOWING FOR SINGLE-FAMILY
38 RESIDENTIAL DEVELOPMENT:
39 (a) A SITE PLAN.
40 (b) A DEVELOPMENT PLAN.
41 (c) A LAND DIVISION.
42 (d) A LOT LINE ADJUSTMENT.
43 (e) A LOT TIE.
44 (f) A PRELIMINARY OR FINAL PLAT.
45 (g) A PLAT AMENDMENT.
46 (h) A BUILDING PERMIT.

1 Sec. 4. Legislative findings
2 The legislature finds and declares that the purpose of section
3 12-1139, Arizona Revised Statutes, as added by this act, is to ensure that
4 actions in which a plaintiff asserts a malicious delay property claim
5 pursuant to section 9-500.54 or 11-269.31, Arizona Revised Statutes, as
6 added by this act for a single-family residential application is resolved
7 in a timely and efficient manner to prevent undue harm resulting from
8 prolonged uncertainty.
