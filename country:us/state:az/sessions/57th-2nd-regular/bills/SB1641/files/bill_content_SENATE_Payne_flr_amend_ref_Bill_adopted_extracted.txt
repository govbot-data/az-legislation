FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
1. Removes the requirement for the Department of Health Services (DHS) to conduct an annual
review of market conditions to determine whether consumers would benefit from the issuance of
additional marijuana producer licenses.
2. Allows, beginning in 2032, DHS to issue additional marijuana producer licenses.
