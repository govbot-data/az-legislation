ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB4027: Charlie Kirk highway.
CARBONE FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Changes the re-designation of the Loop 202 from the Charlie Kirk Highway to
the Charlie Kirk Loop 202.
2. Adds a legislative intent to rename the entire Loop 202 as the Charlie Kirk
Loop 202 and clarifies that the underlying segments of the Chalie Kirk Loop
202 would retain their names and designations.
3. Makes a conforming change.
