FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
SB 1479: deeds; identification; forgery; notice; notaries
BLACKMAN FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Stipulates that this legislation does not apply to notarial acts performed for a
remotely located individual, if both of the following apply:
a. a notary journal includes the individual's identification credential
number; and
b. the notary retains the audiovisual recording for at least seven years.
