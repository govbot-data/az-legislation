Carroll ADD COW Floor Amendment
Reference to: Senate engrossed bill
Amendment drafted by: Jason Theodorou
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
• Classifies, as a class 5 felony, rather than a class 4 felony, the act of knowingly recording a forged
or groundless document asserting a claim in real property with the county recorder's office.
