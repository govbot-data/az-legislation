SB1517 - 572R - S Ver Senate Engrossed appropriation; barbering and cosmetology board State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SENATE BILL 1517 AN ACT Appropriating monies to the Barbering and Cosmetology board. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriations; barbering and cosmetology board; salary and operational costs A. The sum of $235,500 and three FTE positions are appropriated from the barbering and cosmetology fund established by section 32-505, Arizona Revised Statutes, in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the barbering and cosmetology board for salaries and operational costs. B. The barbering and cosmetology board shall fill three FTE positions with the following staff: 1. One licensing specialist. 2. One customer service representative. 3. One quality assurance specialist.
