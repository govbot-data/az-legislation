Bolick Floor Amendment

Amendment drafted by: Leg. Council
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
1. Adds duplicated ballots, test ballots, ballots used for logic and accuracy testing and replacement
early ballots to the list of categories that election officials must maintain specified counts for.
2. Requires the prescribed ballot counts to be posted online not later than 48 hours after a county
board of supervisors transmits the county canvass to the Secretary of State.
3. Requires only an officer in charge of elections, rather than a county recorder, to maintain
prescribed ballot counts and post such counts on the appropriate websites.
