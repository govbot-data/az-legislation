FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
SB 1168: ballots; categories; count
LIVINGSTON FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Exempts a recognized dental school, dental therapy school and dental hygiene
school from business entity or facility registration requirements with the
Dental Board.
2. Makes technical changes.
