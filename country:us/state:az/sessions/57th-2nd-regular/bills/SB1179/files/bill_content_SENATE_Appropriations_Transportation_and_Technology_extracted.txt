2 Section 1. Section 36-595.03, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended
3 to read:
4 36-595.03. Developmental disabilities group home monitoring
5 program; clients with complex needs; designated
6 entity duties; expedited referral system;
7 reporting requirements; program review;
8 definition
9 A. The developmental disabilities group home monitoring program is
10 established in the department. Subject to available appropriations, The
11 department shall contract with the entity that has been designated by this
12 state to operate the protection and advocacy system for persons with
13 developmental disabilities in this state pursuant to the developmental
14 disabilities assistance and bill of rights act of 2000 (42 United States
15 Code sections 15041 through 15045) to conduct the program.
16 B. The designated entity, at a minimum, shall do all of the
17 following beginning January 1, 2026:
18 1. Monitor in person the group homes that provide services to
19 clients with complex needs to determine, at a minimum, whether:
20 (a) The client with complex needs receives the services identified
21 in the person-centered service plan of the client with complex needs,
22 including medication monitoring and habilitation treatment, as applicable.
23 (b) The provision of services identified in the person-centered
24 service plan of the client with complex needs has been effective in
25 addressing the complex needs.
26 (c) A behavior treatment plan is in place, is compliant with
27 department rules and has had a positive impact on behaviors that
28 interfered with the ability of the client with complex needs to live
29 safely in the community.

1 2. Use a monitoring tool to assess whether the following criteria
2 were satisfied:
3 (a) The client with complex needs received the physical health and
4 behavioral health services that the client requires as outlined by the
5 client's health care providers and person-centered service plan, including
6 regular physical activity with modification for the client's physical
7 disability, if any.
8 (b) The client with complex needs had the client's dietary
9 requirements met, including compliance with all dietary orders from the
10 client's health care providers.
11 (c) The food provided in the group home met generally accepted
12 dietary standards and guidelines for healthy Americans in accordance with
13 the United States department of agriculture dietary guidelines.
14 (d) The guardian of the client with complex needs and the client,
15 as applicable, were included in all decisions made regarding the client
16 and informed of any changes to the client's regular activities or daily
17 routine.
18 (e) Group home direct care staff demonstrated the knowledge and
19 skills required to meet the medical and behavioral health care needs of
20 the client with complex needs as outlined in the client's person-centered
21 service plan and behavior treatment plan, if applicable.
22 (f) The client with complex needs had access to and used all
23 prescribed adaptive equipment.
24 (g) Group home direct care staff worked with the behavioral health
25 providers serving the client with complex needs as allowed by the client
26 or client's guardian and as outlined in the client's person-centered
27 service plan and behavior treatment plan, if applicable.
28 (h) The group home of the client with complex needs complied with
29 applicable incident reporting policies, including documenting and
30 reporting physical interventions and any other emergency measures taken.
31 3. Complete follow-up monitoring reviews for group homes that were
32 monitored pursuant to paragraph 1 of this subsection and that the
33 designated entity identified as having significant quality of care
34 concerns.
35 4. Compile and complete both of the following:
36 (a) Monthly reports to the department detailing the results of all
37 monitoring from the previous month, including identified systemic issues
38 and recommendations for improvement.
39 (b) A comprehensive annual report of all observations and outcomes
40 during the preceding year.
41 C. On or before January 1, 2026, the department shall establish an
42 expedited referral system to ensure that copies of all incident reports,
43 quality of care complaints, investigation records and client service
44 requests for each group home monitored pursuant to subsection B, paragraph
45 1 of this section are forwarded to the designated entity for review and
46 analysis pursuant to this section. If the designated entity identifies
47 concerns during its follow-up monitoring reviews, the information shall be
