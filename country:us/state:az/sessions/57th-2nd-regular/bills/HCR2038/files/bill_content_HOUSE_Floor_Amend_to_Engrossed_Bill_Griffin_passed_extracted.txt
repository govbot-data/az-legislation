ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HCR2038: Colorado River; seven-state agreement
GRIFFIN FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Expands listed entities who rely on the Colorado River as a vital resource to
include tribal communities and Arizona's major population centers.
2. Clarifies the reductions and conservation of Colorado River water by the
Lower Basin States.
3. Modifies language relating to Colorado River negotiations.
4. Makes technical changes.
