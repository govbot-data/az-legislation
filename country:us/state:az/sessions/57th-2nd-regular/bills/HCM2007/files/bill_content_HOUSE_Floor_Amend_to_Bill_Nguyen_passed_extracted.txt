1 The memorial as proposed to be amended is reprinted as follows:
2 To the Chairperson of the State Board on Geographic and Historic Names:
3 Your memorialist respectfully represents:
4 Whereas, for generations, our nation's veterans have put their lives
5 on the line to protect our country and preserve our democracy, freedom and
6 civil rights; and
7 Whereas, State Route 69, currently named "Black Canyon Highway,"
8 runs from Interstate 17 to the City of Prescott and has a long history of
9 military use; and
10 Whereas, in 1864, State Route 69 was declared the first public road
11 in the new Arizona territory and was soon used by the United States Army
12 as part of its route from Fort Whipple to Fort McDowell; and
13 Whereas, it is the legislature's duty to preserve and honor the
14 legacy of our nation's veterans.
15 Wherefore your memorialist, the House of Representatives of the State of
16 Arizona, the Senate concurring, prays:
17 1. That the State Board on Geographic and Historic Names divide
18 State Route 69 into [five] [six] equal sections and rename the [five]
19 [six] sections as follows:
20 [(a) World War I Veterans Memorial Highway.]
21 [(a)][(b)] World War II Veterans Memorial Highway.
22 [(b)][(c)] Korean War Veterans Memorial Highway.
23 [(c)][(d)] Vietnam War Veterans Memorial Highway.
24 [(d)][(e)] Desert Storm Veterans Memorial Highway.
25 [(e)][(f)] Iraq and Afghanistan War Veterans Memorial Highway.
