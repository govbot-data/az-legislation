Bill Number: H.B. 2404
Payne Floor Amendment
Reference to: House engrossed bill
Amendment drafted by: Leg. Council
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
1. Adds a delayed effective date of January 1, 2030.
2. Removes the requirement for the prohibition against peace officers transporting patients for mental
health services to begin on July 1, 2027.
