SB1032 - 572R - I Ver PREFILED    DEC 17 2025 REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; independent correctional oversight office State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SB 1032 Introduced by Senator Bolick AN ACT Appropriating monies to the corrections oversight fund. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; corrections oversight fund; use of monies A. The sum of $1,500,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the corrections oversight fund established by section 41-7007, Arizona Revised Statutes. B. The director of the independent correctional oversight office may use the monies appropriated in subsection A of this section for the purposes of title 41, chapter 59, Arizona Revised Statutes.
