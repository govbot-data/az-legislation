Angius Floor Amendment
Reference to: printed bill
Amendment drafted by: Leg. Council
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
• Allows guardians and regional behavioral health authorities to waive the 10-day notice of the
intended release of a patient who no longer requires the level of care offered by a mental health
treatment agency.
