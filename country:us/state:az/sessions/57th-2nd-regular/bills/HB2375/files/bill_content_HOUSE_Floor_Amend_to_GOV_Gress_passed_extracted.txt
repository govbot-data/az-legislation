ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2375: housing; zoning; historic neighborhoods
GRESS FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Prohibits middle housing from being developed on a site where a National
Register of Historic Places structure or structure designated as historic by a
municipality was demolished unless the demolition was necessary because of
conditions that constitute danger to public health, safety and welfare.

House Amendments to H.B. 2375
1 1. "Building code":
2 (a) Means a construction code adopted by a municipality.
3 (b) Includes a model building code, commercial code, plumbing and
4 mechanical code, electric code, energy conservation code, fire code,
5 property maintenance code, neighborhood preservation code, antiblight code
6 or other similar code.
7 2. "Central business district" means an area or series of areas
8 designated by a municipality that are primarily nonindustrial and that
9 attract community activity, including the entire geographic area that the
10 municipality has officially designated as its downtown or equivalent on
11 September 14, 2024.
12 3. "Duplex" means two dwelling units on the same parcel or lot in
13 attached, detached or semidetached arrangements that are designed for
14 residential occupancy by not more than two households living independently
15 from each other.
16 4. "Floor area ratio" means the ratio of allowed square footage in
17 a middle housing project to the square footage of the parcel on which it
18 is built.
19 5. "Fourplex" means four dwelling units on the same parcel or lot
20 in attached, detached or semidetached arrangements that are designed for
21 residential occupancy by not more than four households living
22 independently from each other.
23 6. "Household" means either:
24 (a) A single person living or residing in a dwelling or place of
25 residence.
26 (b) Two or more persons living together or residing in the same
27 dwelling or place of residence.
28 7. "Middle housing":
29 (a) Means buildings that are compatible in scale, form and
30 character with single-family houses and that contain two or more attached,
31 detached, stacked or clustered homes.
32 (b) Includes duplexes, triplexes, fourplexes and townhouses.
33 8. "Permitted use" means the ability for a development to be
34 approved without requiring a public hearing, variance, conditional use
35 permit, special permit or special exception, other than a discretionary
36 zoning action to determination DETERMINE that a site plan conforms with
37 applicable zoning regulations.
38 9. "Townhouses" means dwelling units that are constructed in a row
39 of two or more attached units in which each dwelling unit shares at least
40 one common wall with an adjacent unit and that are accessed by separate
41 outdoor entrances.
42 10. "Triplex" means three dwelling units on the same parcel or lot
43 in attached, detached or semidetached arrangements that are designed for
44 residential occupancy by not more than three households living
45 independently from each other.
