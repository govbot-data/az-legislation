SB1373 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; summer youth employment program State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SB 1373 Introduced by Senator Fernandez AN ACT APPROPRIATING monies to the state treasurer. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; state treasurer; Pima county; summer youth employment program The sum of $250,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the state treasurer to distribute to Pima county for the summer youth employment program.
