1 The bill as proposed to be amended is reprinted as follows:
2 Section 1. Title 15, chapter 1, article 5, Arizona Revised
3 Statutes, is amended by adding section 15-154.03, to read:
4 15-154.03. Schools; interoperable communications systems;
5 affidavits; annual readiness report; law
6 enforcement agencies; funding
7 A. [IF SUFFICIENT MONIES ARE AVAILABLE,] EACH SCHOOL DISTRICT AND
8 CHARTER SCHOOL IN THIS STATE SHALL SELECT OR DEVELOP TECHNOLOGY, A SYSTEM
9 OR A METHOD TO ESTABLISH AND MAINTAIN INTEROPERABLE COMMUNICATIONS
10 CAPABILITIES THAT INCLUDE AT LEAST THE FOLLOWING:
11 1. A MECHANISM THAT ENABLES DESIGNATED SCHOOL PERSONNEL TO NOTIFY
12 LOCAL LAW ENFORCEMENT AGENCIES IN REAL TIME IF AN EMERGENCY OCCURS AT THE
13 SCHOOL.
14 2. A MECHANISM THAT ENABLES THE SCHOOL DISTRICT OR CHARTER SCHOOL
15 TO SECURELY TRANSMIT THE CURRENT MAP OF EACH SCHOOL CAMPUS AND FLOOR PLAN
16 OF EACH SCHOOL BUILDING TO LOCAL LAW ENFORCEMENT AGENCIES IN A PRINTABLE
17 FORMAT.
18 3. A MECHANISM THAT ENABLES DESIGNATED SCHOOL PERSONNEL TO
19 COMMUNICATE WITH LOCAL LAW ENFORCEMENT AGENCIES AND LAW ENFORCEMENT
20 OFFICERS IN REAL TIME DURING AN EMERGENCY AT THE SCHOOL.
21 B. A SCHOOL DISTRICT OR CHARTER SCHOOL THAT USES A SECURE,
22 MULTIMEDIA DATA COMMUNICATIONS SYSTEM THAT IS DEPLOYED PURSUANT TO SECTION
23 41-1733 IS NOT REQUIRED TO ESTABLISH A NEW INTEROPERABLE COMMUNICATIONS
24 SYSTEM IF THE EXISTING MULTIMEDIA DATA COMMUNICATIONS SYSTEM SATISFIES THE
25 REQUIREMENTS PRESCRIBED BY SUBSECTION A OF THIS SECTION.
26 C. ON OR BEFORE JULY 1, 2027 AND JULY 1 OF EACH YEAR THEREAFTER,
27 EACH SCHOOL DISTRICT AND CHARTER SCHOOL SHALL SUBMIT TO THE DEPARTMENT, IN
28 A FORM AND MANNER PRESCRIBED BY THE DEPARTMENT, AN AFFIDAVIT ATTESTING
29 THAT [EITHER:
30 1.] THE SCHOOL DISTRICT OR CHARTER SCHOOL:
31 [1.] [(a)] IS MAINTAINING THE INTEROPERABLE COMMUNICATIONS
32 CAPABILITIES REQUIRED BY SUBSECTION A OF THIS SECTION.
33 [2.] [(b)] HAS TRANSMITTED THE CURRENT MAPS AND FLOOR PLANS TO
34 LOCAL LAW ENFORCEMENT AGENCIES THROUGH THE MECHANISM REQUIRED BY
35 SUBSECTION A, PARAGRAPH 2 OF THIS SECTION.

House Amendments to S.B. 1315
1 [3.] [(c)] HAS TESTED EACH MECHANISM REQUIRED BY SUBSECTION A OF
2 THIS SECTION TO ENSURE THAT THE SCHOOL DISTRICT OR CHARTER SCHOOL IS ABLE
3 TO COMMUNICATE WITH THE LOCAL LAW ENFORCEMENT AGENCY IN WHOSE JURISDICTION
4 THE SCHOOL DISTRICT OR CHARTER SCHOOL IS LOCATED AT LEAST ONCE DURING THE
5 PRECEDING YEAR.
6 [2. THE SCHOOL DISTRICT OR CHARTER SCHOOL HAS NOT ESTABLISHED
7 INTEROPERABLE COMMUNICATIONS CAPABILITIES REQUIRED BY SUBSECTION A OF THIS
8 SECTION BECAUSE SUFFICIENT MONIES ARE NOT AVAILABLE.]
9 D. THE DEPARTMENT SHALL DEVELOP GUIDELINES AND BEST PRACTICES TO
10 ASSIST SCHOOL DISTRICTS AND CHARTER SCHOOLS IN MEETING THE REQUIREMENTS
11 PRESCRIBED BY THIS SECTION.
12 E. THE DEPARTMENT SHALL COMPILE THE AFFIDAVITS SUBMITTED PURSUANT
13 TO SUBSECTION C OF THIS SECTION AND SHALL PROVIDE AN ANNUAL STATEWIDE
14 INTEROPERABILITY READINESS REPORT TO THE GOVERNOR, THE PRESIDENT OF THE
15 SENATE AND THE SPEAKER OF THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES AND SHALL PROVIDE A
16 COPY OF THE REPORT TO THE SECRETARY OF STATE.
17 [F. IF A LOCAL LAW ENFORCEMENT AGENCY IN THIS STATE HAS MONIES
18 AVAILABLE TO ESTABLISH AND MAINTAIN INTEROPERABLE COMMUNICATIONS
19 CAPABILITIES WITH SCHOOL DISTRICTS AND CHARTER SCHOOLS THAT ARE LOCATED
20 WITHIN THE AGENCY'S JURISDICTION, THE AGENCY SHALL NOTIFY EACH SCHOOL
21 DISTRICT AND CHARTER SCHOOL THAT IS LOCATED WITHIN THE AGENCY'S
22 JURISDICTION THAT MONIES ARE AVAILABLE FOR THE PURPOSES OF SUBSECTION A OF
23 THIS SECTION.
24 G. IF A LOCAL LAW ENFORCEMENT AGENCY IN THIS STATE DOES NOT HAVE
25 SUFFICIENT MONIES AVAILABLE TO ESTABLISH AND MAINTAIN INTEROPERABLE
26 COMMUNICATIONS CAPABILITIES WITH EACH SCHOOL DISTRICT AND CHARTER SCHOOL
27 THAT IS LOCATED WITHIN THE AGENCY'S JURISDICTION, THE AGENCY SHALL DO BOTH
28 OF THE FOLLOWING:
29 1. SELECT SCHOOL DISTRICTS AND CHARTER SCHOOLS FOR WHICH SUFFICIENT
30 MONIES ARE AVAILABLE TO ESTABLISH AND MAINTAIN INTEROPERABLE
31 COMMUNICATIONS CAPABILITIES.
32 2. NOTIFY EACH SCHOOL DISTRICT AND CHARTER SCHOOL THAT IS SELECTED
33 PURSUANT TO PARAGRAPH 1 OF THIS SUBSECTION THAT SUFFICIENT MONIES ARE
34 AVAILABLE FOR THE PURPOSES OF SUBSECTION A OF THIS SECTION.]
