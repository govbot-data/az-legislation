ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 4103: school districts; property; bonding authority
OLSON FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Prohibits the School Facilities Oversight Board from including in the
calculation to determine the ratio of a school district's enrollment to the school
district's enrollment capacity any existing building that the school district
leases as lessor if the lessee uses the building to provide instruction to students
in kindergarten or any of the 1st through 12th grades.
