8 A. A municipality may assess development fees to offset costs to
9 the municipality associated with providing necessary public services to a
10 development, including the costs of infrastructure, improvements, real
11 property, engineering and architectural services, financing and
12 professional services required for the preparation or revision of a
13 development fee pursuant to this section, including the relevant portion

15 B. Development fees assessed by a municipality under this section
16 are subject to the following requirements:
17 1. Development fees shall result in a beneficial use to the
18 development.
19 2. The municipality shall calculate the development fee based on
20 the infrastructure improvements plan adopted pursuant to this section.
21 3. The development fee shall not exceed a proportionate share of
22 the cost of necessary public services, based on service units, needed to
23 provide necessary public services to the development.
24 4. Costs for necessary public services made necessary by new
25 development shall be based on the same level of service provided to
26 existing development in the service area.
27 5. Development fees may not be used for any of the following:

13 Sec. 6. Legislative findings; purpose
14 A. The legislature finds and declares that:
15 1. The provision of public infrastructure in advance of development
16 is essential to housing affordability, economic development and orderly
17 growth.
18 2. State affordability infrastructure districts provide a uniform,
19 statewide mechanism for financing public infrastructure in a manner that:
20 (a) Protects taxpayers through limited tax rates and debt
21 limitations.
22 (b) Ensures transparency and public accountability.
23 (c) Facilitates early delivery of public infrastructure at lower
24 cost.
25 3. The Arizona finance authority is the appropriate statewide
26 certification authority to ensure uniform financial, engineering, and
27 statutory compliance for such districts.
28 4. The certification and oversight functions provided in this
29 chapter constitute governmental functions serving valid public purposes.
30 B. The legislature further finds and declares that:
31 1. State affordability infrastructure districts exercise limited,
32 special-purpose governmental powers relating solely to the financing,
33 construction, operation and maintenance of public infrastructure.
34 2. The financial obligations of a district, including ad valorem
35 taxes, assessments, and liens, are borne directly and proportionally by
36 real property within the district.
37 3. Because the powers and financial burdens of a district
38 disproportionately affect real property owners, it is reasonable and
39 constitutionally permissible to allocate voting power in district
40 elections based on land ownership and acreage.
41 4. Elections conducted pursuant to this chapter are fiscal
42 determinations relating to property-based obligations and are not general
43 political elections.
