ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2999: infrastructure finance districts
WENINGER FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Renames the State Housing Affordability District to the State Affordability
Infrastructure District.
2. Modifies the definition of maximum authorized tax rate and public
infrastructure.
3. Removes language relating to land that is subject to a current written
annexation or pre-annexation agreement.
4. Adds information that must be included in the petition to form a District.
5. Revises certain District powers granted to further the implementation the
general plan.
6. Includes conditions to calculating the maximum rate of any ad valorem tax.
7. Adds that any public infrastructure financed by the District that constitutes
improvements will remain subject to the municipality's authority to regulate
subdivisions.
8. Makes clarifying changes.
