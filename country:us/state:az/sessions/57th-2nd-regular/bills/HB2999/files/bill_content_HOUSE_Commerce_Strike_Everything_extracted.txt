6 HAVE ANY BONDS OR OTHER OBLIGATIONS OUTSTANDING, SHALL BE DISSOLVED TEN
7 YEARS AFTER THE DATE OF FORMATION UNLESS THE AUTHORITY BY RESOLUTION
8 EXTENDS THE DISTRICT BY AN ADDITIONAL PERIOD OF TEN YEARS.
9 B. THE BOARD SHALL ADOPT A RESOLUTION DISSOLVING THE DISTRICT IF
10 THE FOLLOWING CONDITIONS EXIST:
11 1. ALL OF THE REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY OWNED BY THE DISTRICT HAS
12 BEEN OR WILL BE CONVEYED TO A MUNICIPALITY OR A COUNTY.
13 2. THE AUTHORITY VERIFIES THAT THE DISTRICT HAS NO BONDS OR
14 OBLIGATIONS OUTSTANDING.
15 3. THE AUTHORITY APPROVES THE DISSOLUTION ORDER ON RECEIPT OF A
16 DISSOLUTION PETITION EXECUTED BY THE OWNERS OF A MAJORITY OF THE REAL
17 PROPERTY IN THE DISTRICT.
18 C. ON SATISFACTION OF THE CONDITIONS PRESCRIBED BY SUBSECTION B OF
19 THIS SECTION, THE AUTHORITY SHALL APPROVE THE DISSOLUTION AND ISSUE A
20 DISSOLUTION ORDER. THE BOARD SHALL CAUSE THE DISSOLUTION ORDER TO BE
21 RECORDED IN THE OFFICE OF THE COUNTY RECORDER.
22 D. ALL PROPERTY IN THE DISTRICT, EXCEPT FEDERAL, STATE, COUNTY AND

25 REMAINS SUBJECT TO THE LIEN NOTWITHSTANDING DISSOLUTION OF THE DISTRICT.
26 THE DISTRICT MAY NOT BE DISSOLVED IF ANY GENERAL OBLIGATION BONDS, REVENUE
27 BONDS OR ASSESSMENT BONDS OF THE DISTRICT REMAIN OUTSTANDING UNLESS AN
28 AMOUNT OF MONEY SUFFICIENT, TOGETHER WITH INVESTMENT INCOME THEREON, TO

30 HAS BEEN DEPOSITED WITH A TRUSTEE OR ESCROW AGENT AND PLEDGED TO THE
31 PAYMENT AND REDEMPTION OF THE BONDS. THE DISTRICT MAY CONTINUE TO OPERATE
32 AFTER DISSOLUTION ONLY AS NEEDED TO COLLECT MONIES AND MAKE PAYMENTS ON
33 ANY OUTSTANDING BONDS.
34 Sec. 6. Legislative findings; purpose
35 A. The legislature finds and declares that:
36 1. The provision of public infrastructure in advance of development
37 is essential to housing affordability, economic development and orderly
38 growth.
39 2. State housing affordability districts provide a uniform,
40 statewide mechanism for financing public infrastructure in a manner that:
41 (a) Protects taxpayers through limited tax rates and debt
42 limitations.
43 (b) Ensures transparency and public accountability.
44 (c) Facilitates early delivery of public infrastructure at lower
45 cost.
46 3. The Arizona finance authority is the appropriate statewide
47 certification authority to ensure uniform financial, engineering, and
48 statutory compliance for such districts.
