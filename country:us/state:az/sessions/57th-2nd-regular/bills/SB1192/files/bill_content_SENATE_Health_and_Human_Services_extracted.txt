2 Section 1. Section 32-1421, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended to
3 read:
4 32-1421. Exemptions from licensing requirements; definition
5 A. This article does not apply to any person while engaged in:
6 1. The provision of medical assistance in case of an emergency.
7 2. The administration of family remedies, including the sale of
8 vitamins, health foods or health food supplements or any other natural
9 remedies, except drugs or medicines for which an authorized prescription
10 is required by law.
11 3. The practice of religion, treatment by prayer or the laying on
12 of hands as a religious rite or ordinance.
13 4. The practice of any of the healing arts of and by Indian tribes
14 in this state.
15 5. The lawful practice of any of the healing arts to the extent
16 authorized by a license issued by this state.
17 6. Activities or functions that do not require the exercise of a
18 doctor of medicine's judgment for their performance, THAT are not in
19 violation of the laws of this state and THAT are usually or customarily
20 delegated by a doctor of medicine under the doctor's DOCTOR OF MEDICINE'S
21 direction or supervision or THAT are performed in accordance with the
22 approval of a committee of physicians in a licensed health care
23 institution.
24 7. The official duties of a medical officer in the armed forces of
25 the United States, the United States department of veterans affairs or the
26 United States public health service, or their successor agencies, if the
27 duties are restricted to federal lands.
28 8. Any act, task or function competently performed by a physician
29 assistant in the proper performance of the physician assistant's duties.
