HB 2811: obstructing governmental operations; lawful arrest
GILLETTE FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Removes conflicting language that excludes interference with the making of
an arrest from the definition of obstructing governmental operations.

2 Section 1. Section 13-2402, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended to
3 read:
4 13-2402. Obstructing governmental operations; classification
5 A. A person commits obstructing governmental operations if, by
6 using or threatening to use violence or physical force, such THE person
7 knowingly obstructs, impairs or hinders:
8 1. The performance of a governmental function by a public servant
9 acting under color of his official authority; or
10 2. The enforcement of the penal law or the preservation of the
11 peace by a peace officer acting under color of his official authority;
12 OR .
13 3. THE MAKING OF A LAWFUL ARREST.
14 [B. This section does not apply to the obstruction, impairment or
15 hinderance HINDRANCE of the making of an arrest.]
16 [C.] [B.] OBSTRUCTING GOVERNMENTAL OPERATIONS PURSUANT TO
17 SUBSECTION A, PARAGRAPH 3 [OF THIS SECTION] IS A CLASS 5
18 FELONY. Obstructing governmental operations PURSUANT TO SUBSECTION A,
19 PARAGRAPH 1 OR 2 [OF THIS SECTION] is a class 1 misdemeanor.
