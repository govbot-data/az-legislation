ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2862: unlawful masking; violation
NGUYEN FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Restricts the sentencing enhancement to apply only to crimes outlined in A.R.S.
Title 13.
2. Allows the court to waive the sentencing enhancement if the court finds clear and
convincing evidence that the face covering was worn as a religious covering.
3. Removes an exemption for face covering's worn by law enforcement while acting
within the scope of their employment.
