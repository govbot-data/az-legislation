FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB2437: EMS reciprocity; compact
BLISS FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Specifies that a person applying for EMCT certification pursuant to the EMS
Compact must possess a valid fingerprint clearance card beginning the
effective date of the compact or FBI approval.
2. Clarifies that the confidential records provisions do not prevent DHS from
submitting information in compliance with the EMS compact.
3. Makes conforming changes.

1 SECTION 1. PURPOSE
2 THE PURPOSE OF THIS COMPACT IS TO PROTECT THE PUBLIC THROUGH
3 VERIFICATION OF COMPETENCY AND TO ENSURE ACCOUNTABILITY FOR PATIENT
4 CARE-RELATED ACTIVITIES OF ALL STATES' LICENSED EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICES
5 (EMS) PERSONNEL, SUCH AS EMERGENCY MEDICAL TECHNICIANS (EMTs), ADVANCED
6 EMERGENCY MEDICAL TECHNICIANS (AEMTS) AND PARAMEDICS. THIS COMPACT IS
7 INTENDED TO FACILITATE THE DAY-TO-DAY MOVEMENT OF EMS PERSONNEL ACROSS
8 STATE BOUNDARIES IN THE PERFORMANCE OF THEIR EMS DUTIES AS ASSIGNED BY AN
9 APPROPRIATE AUTHORITY AND TO AUTHORIZE STATE EMS OFFICES TO AFFORD
10 IMMEDIATE LEGAL RECOGNITION TO EMS PERSONNEL WHO ARE LICENSED IN A MEMBER
11 STATE. THIS COMPACT RECOGNIZES THAT STATES HAVE A VESTED INTEREST IN
12 PROTECTING THE PUBLIC'S HEALTH AND SAFETY THROUGH THEIR LICENSING AND
13 REGULATION OF EMS PERSONNEL AND THAT SUCH STATE REGULATION SHARED AMONG
14 THE MEMBER STATES WILL BEST PROTECT THE PUBLIC HEALTH AND SAFETY. THIS
15 COMPACT IS DESIGNED TO ACHIEVE THE FOLLOWING PURPOSES AND OBJECTIVES:
16 1. INCREASE PUBLIC ACCESS TO EMS PERSONNEL.
17 2. ENHANCE THE STATES' ABILITY TO PROTECT THE PUBLIC'S HEALTH AND
18 SAFETY, ESPECIALLY PATIENT SAFETY.
19 3. ENCOURAGE THE COOPERATION OF MEMBER STATES IN THE AREAS OF EMS
20 PERSONNEL LICENSURE AND REGULATION.
21 4. SUPPORT THE LICENSING OF MILITARY MEMBERS WHO ARE SEPARATING
22 FROM AN ACTIVE DUTY TOUR AND THE SPOUSES OF THOSE MILITARY MEMBERS.
23 5. FACILITATE THE EXCHANGE OF INFORMATION BETWEEN MEMBER STATES
24 REGARDING EMS PERSONNEL LICENSURE, ADVERSE ACTIONS AND SIGNIFICANT
25 INVESTIGATORY INFORMATION.
26 6. PROMOTE COMPLIANCE WITH THE LAWS GOVERNING EMS PERSONNEL
27 PRACTICE IN EACH MEMBER STATE.
28 7. INVEST ALL MEMBER STATES WITH THE AUTHORITY TO HOLD EMS
29 PERSONNEL ACCOUNTABLE THROUGH THE MUTUAL RECOGNITION OF MEMBER STATE
30 LICENSES.
