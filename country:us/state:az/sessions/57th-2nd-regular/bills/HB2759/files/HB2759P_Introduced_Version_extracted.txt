HB2759 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; department of veterans' services State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HB 2759 Introduced by Representatives Nguyen: Griffin AN ACT APPROPRIATING monies to the department of veterans' services. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; department of veterans' services; Yavapai county; veterans programs The sum of $500,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the department of veterans' services to partner with an educational institution located in Yavapai county for the provision of veterans programs.
