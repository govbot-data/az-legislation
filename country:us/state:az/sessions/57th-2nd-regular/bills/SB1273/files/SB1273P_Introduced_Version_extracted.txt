SB1273 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; Olga Frontage Road State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SB 1273 Introduced by Senator Gowan: Representatives Diaz, Griffin AN ACT Appropriating monies to the department of transportation. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; department of transportation; pavement rehabilitation; Olga Frontage Road The sum of $14,000,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the department of transportation for pavement rehabilitation of Olga Frontage Road between Bowie and San Simon.
