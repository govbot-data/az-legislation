SB1273 - 572R - S Ver Senate Engrossed appropriation; Olga Frontage Road State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SENATE BILL 1273 AN ACT Appropriating monies to the department of transportation. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; department of transportation; pavement rehabilitation; Olga Frontage Road The sum of $14,000,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the department of transportation for pavement rehabilitation of Olga Frontage Road between Bowie and San Simon.
