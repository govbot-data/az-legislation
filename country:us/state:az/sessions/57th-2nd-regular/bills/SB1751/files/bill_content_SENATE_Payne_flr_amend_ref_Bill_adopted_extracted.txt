Payne Floor Amendment
Reference to: printed bill
Amendment drafted by: Leg. Council
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
1. Removes the requirement that death by firing squad is the only method of death for a defendant
who has murdered a law enforcement officer and knew or should have known the officer was
acting in the officer's line of duty.
2. Makes technical and conforming changes.

20 [C.] [D.] The identity of executioners and other persons who
