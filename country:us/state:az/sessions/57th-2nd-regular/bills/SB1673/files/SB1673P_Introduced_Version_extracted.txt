SB1673 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; crime victim notification fund State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SB 1673 Introduced by Senator Kavanagh AN ACT appropriating monies to the law enforcement crime victim notification fund. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; law enforcement crime victim notification fund; exemption A. The sum of $8,200,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the law enforcement crime victim notification fund established by section 41-2414, Arizona Revised Statutes. B. The appropriation made in subsection A of this section is exempt from the provisions of section 35-190, Arizona Revised Statutes, relating to lapsing of appropriations.
