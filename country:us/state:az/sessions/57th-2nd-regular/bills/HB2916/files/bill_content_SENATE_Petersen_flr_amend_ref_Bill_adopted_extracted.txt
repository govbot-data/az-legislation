Bill Number: H.B. 2916
Petersen Floor Amendment
Reference to: House engrossed bill
Amendment drafted by: Leg. Council
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
1. Prohibits an agency without statutory authority to require a fingerprint clearance card from
requiring a person to obtain a fingerprint clearance card as a condition of licensure, certification,
registration or employment.
2. Allows a person who is required to obtain a valid fingerprint clearance card, for positions that are
pending approval for a federal criminal records check, to be granted a temporary work
authorization while the person's application is being processed if all other requirements for work
authorization, licensure, registration or certification are met.
3. Specifies that a person who is granted a temporary work authorization is authorized to work while
their fingerprint clearance card application is being processed if the person:
a) provides the relevant agency with documentation showing that the person's application is
pending within the Department of Public Safety (DPS); and
b) certifies that they meet all other prescribed conditions, including that the person is not awaiting
trial on, convicted of and has not admitted in open court to committing any of the prescribed
criminal offenses that would preclude the person from obtaining a valid fingerprint clearance
card.
4. Requires DPS to notify the relevant agency if DPS denies the issuance of a fingerprint clearance
card.
5. Specifies that a temporary work authorization expires if DPS denies the fingerprint clearance card.
6. Outlines actions that a relevant agency may perform relating to the outlined temporary work
authorization requirements, including that the agency may deny a temporary work authorization if
the agency has verifiable information that the person is precluded from receiving a fingerprint
clearance card as prescribed.
7. Adds a delayed effective date of January 1, 2027, to the requirement that instructors employed or
contracted with traffic survival schools provide the Arizona Department of Transportation with a
valid fingerprint clearance card.
