2 Section 1. Section 15-708, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended to
3 read:
4 15-708. Mathematics proficiency; parental notification;
5 advanced mathematics courses; annual report
6 A. If the statewide assessment results are available before the
7 start of each school year, each school district and charter school shall
8 provide notification no NOT later than the first half of the second
9 quarter of the school year to parents of pupils in grades six through
10 eight who have not demonstrated proficiency in grade-level mathematics
11 based on available local or statewide assessments. The notification shall
12 MUST include the following information:
13 1. A description of the pupil's mathematics deficiencies as
14 demonstrated by the statewide assessment.
15 2. A description of the current mathematics services provided by
16 the school district or charter school, including supplemental
17 instructional services and supporting programs that are designed to
18 remediate mathematics deficiencies.
19 B. EACH SCHOOL DISTRICT AND CHARTER SCHOOL SHALL:
20 1. ENROLL A STUDENT WHO IS IN SIXTH, SEVENTH OR EIGHTH GRADE IN AN
21 ADVANCED MATHEMATICS COURSE, IF AVAILABLE, FOR THE NEXT MATHEMATICS COURSE
22 IN WHICH THE STUDENT ENROLLS IF THE STUDENT'S PARENT DOES NOT OBJECT AND
23 THE STUDENT MEETS AT LEAST ONE OF THE FOLLOWING:
24 (a) DEMONSTRATES THE HIGHEST PROFICIENCY IN GRADE-LEVEL MATHEMATICS
25 ON THE STATEWIDE ASSESSMENT.
26 (b) DEMONSTRATES THE HIGHEST PROFICIENCY IN GRADE-LEVEL MATHEMATICS
27 ON AN END-OF-COURSE EXAM OR A BENCHMARK ASSESSMENT THAT IS ADMINISTERED BY
28 THE SCHOOL DISTRICT OR CHARTER SCHOOL OR BY THE SCHOOL IN WHICH THE
29 STUDENT WAS ENROLLED IN THE IMMEDIATELY PRECEDING SCHOOL YEAR.
