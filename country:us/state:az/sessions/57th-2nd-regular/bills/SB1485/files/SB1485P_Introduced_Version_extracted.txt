SB1485 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; multipurpose complex; Tuba City State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SB 1485 Introduced by Senators Hatathlie: Diaz, Kuby, Miranda, Ortiz, Sears;  Representatives Garcia, Sandoval AN ACT appropriating monies to the department of administration. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; department of administration; Tuba City Diné youth multipurpose complex project; exemption A. The sum of $6,000,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the department of administration to distribute to the Navajo Nation for the design, planning, construction, maintenance and operation costs of the Tuba City Diné youth multipurpose complex project. B. The appropriation made in subsection A of this section is exempt from the provisions of section 35-190, Arizona Revised Statutes, relating to lapsing of appropriations.
