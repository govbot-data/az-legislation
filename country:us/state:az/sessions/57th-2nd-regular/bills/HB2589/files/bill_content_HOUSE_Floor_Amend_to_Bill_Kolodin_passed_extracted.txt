ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2589: unlawful exposure; minors; drag show
KOLODIN FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Revises the prohibition from a drag show performance to an adult oriented
performance, and changes the definition accordingly.
2. Changes the culpable mental state from knowingly to intentionally.
