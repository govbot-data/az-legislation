ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2758: McMullen Valley; eligible entities; groundwater
KOLODIN FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Requires rules adopted relating to the transportation of groundwater from
the Harquahala INA apply to this act. (Sec. 2)
2. Adds a condition for the sale or lease of groundwater from historically
irrigated acres by an eligible entity in La Paz County that receives
groundwater from the McMullen Valley basin. (Sec. 2)
