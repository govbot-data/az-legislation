FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2533: homeless services; office; board
FINK FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Removes provisions relating to the creation and procedures of the Property
Loss Homelessness Compensation Fund.
2. Removes provisions relating to employing an ombudsman dedicated to the
Office of Homeless Services (Office).
3. Directs the Office Director to employ an ombudsman to assist with the
Office's duties.
4. Requires the ombudsman to provide information to and assist Arizonan
qualified property owners with applying for a refund relating to public
nuisance on the property owner's real property.
