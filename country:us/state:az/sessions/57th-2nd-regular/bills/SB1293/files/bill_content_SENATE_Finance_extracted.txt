7 A. A city or town may abate the tax provided for under this article

16 (a) A city or town shall not designate more than one central
17 business district within its corporate boundaries.
18 (b) A city or town shall not approve or enter into a development
19 agreement or lease for a government property improvement within one year
20 after the designation of the central business district in which the
21 GOVERNMENT PROPERTY improvement is located.
22 (c) "Central business district" means a single and contiguous
23 geographical area that is designated by resolution of the governing body
24 of the city or town and that is geographically compact and not larger than
25 the greatest of the existing total land area of the central business
26 district of the city or town as of January 1, 2018, two and one-half
27 percent of the total land area within the exterior boundaries of the city
28 or town or nine hundred sixty acres. For the purposes of this
