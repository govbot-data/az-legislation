Bill Number: H.B. 2311
Farnsworth ADD COW Floor Amendment
Reference to: Senate engrossed bill
Amendment drafted by: Leg. Council
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
1. Specifies that an operator may not condition access to a conversational AI service on the use of an
age verification digital identification system, unless expressly required by federal law.
2. Prohibits the conversational AI service requirements from being used to implement a system that
tracks any, rather than all, of an account holder's online activity.
3. Prohibits the prescribed conversational AI service requirements from being cited or used to create
or enforce a social scoring system, social credit system or system that rates, ranks, or restricts
access to a service based on an individual's behavior, expressions or associations.
4. Makes technical changes.
