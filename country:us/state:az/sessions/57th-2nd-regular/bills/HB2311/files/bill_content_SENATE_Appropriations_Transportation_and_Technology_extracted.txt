4 CHAPTER 8
5 ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE
6 ARTICLE 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS
7 18-801. Definitions
8 IN THIS CHAPTER, UNLESS THE TEXT OTHERWISE REQUIRES:
9 1. "ACCOUNT HOLDER" MEANS AN INDIVIDUAL WHO HAS, OR OPENS, AN
10 ACCOUNT OR PROFILE TO USE A CONVERSATIONAL [AI] [TECHNOLOGY] SERVICE.
11 2. "CONVERSATIONAL [AI] [TECHNOLOGY] SERVICE":
12 (a) MEANS AN ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE [OR OTHER] SOFTWARE
13 APPLICATION[, WEB INTERFACE OR COMPUTER PROGRAM] THAT IS ACCESSIBLE TO THE
14 GENERAL PUBLIC AND [THAT PRIMARILY] SIMULATES HUMAN CONVERSATION AND
15 INTERACTION THROUGH TEXTUAL, VISUAL OR AURAL COMMUNICATIONS.
16 [(b) DOES NOT INCLUDE AN APPLICATION, WEB INTERFACE OR COMPUTER
17 PROGRAM THAT MEETS ANY OF THE FOLLOWING:
18 (i) IS PRIMARILY DESIGNED AND MARKETED FOR USE BY DEVELOPERS OR
19 RESEARCHERS.
20 (ii) IS A FEATURE WITHIN ANOTHER SOFTWARE APPLICATION, WEB
21 INTERFACE OR COMPUTER PROGRAM THAT IS NOT A CONVERSATIONAL AI SERVICE.
22 (iii) IS DESIGNED TO PROVIDE OUTPUTS RELATING TO A NARROW AND
23 DISCRETE TOPIC.
24 (iv) IS PRIMARILY DESIGNED AND MARKETED FOR COMMERCIAL USE BY
25 BUSINESS ENTITIES.
26 (v) FUNCTIONS AS A SPEAKER AND VOICE COMMAND INTERFACE OR
27 VOICE-ACTIVATED VIRTUAL ASSISTANT FOR A CONSUMER ELECTRONIC DEVICE.
28 (vi) IS USED BY A BUSINESS ENTITY SOLELY FOR INTERNAL PURPOSES.

25 18-802. Artificial intelligence; consumer notices and
26 disclosures; prohibited uses of artificial
27 intelligence service; safety and privacy tools;
28 civil penalty; enforcement by attorney general
29 A. EACH OPERATOR SHALL CLEARLY AND CONSPICUOUSLY DISCLOSE TO [A
30 MINOR] [AN] ACCOUNT HOLDER IN EITHER OF THE FOLLOWING WAYS THAT THE
31 [MINOR] [ACCOUNT HOLDER] IS INTERACTING WITH A CONVERSATIONAL [AI]
32 [TECHNOLOGY] SERVICE:
33 1. AS A PERSISTENT VISIBLE DISCLAIMER.
34 2. AT THE BEGINNING OF EACH SESSION AND APPEARING AT LEAST EVERY
35 THREE HOURS IN A CONTINUOUS CONVERSATIONAL [AI] [TECHNOLOGY] SERVICE
36 INTERACTION.
37 B. [IF AN OPERATOR KNOWS THAT AN ACCOUNT HOLDER IS A MINOR,] THE
38 OPERATOR MAY NOT PROVIDE THE USER WITH POINTS OR SIMILAR REWARDS AT
39 UNPREDICTABLE INTERVALS WITH THE INTENT TO ENCOURAGE INCREASED ENGAGEMENT
40 WITH THE CONVERSATIONAL [AI] [TECHNOLOGY] SERVICE.
41 C. EACH OPERATOR SHALL INSTITUTE REASONABLE MEASURES TO PREVENT THE
42 CONVERSATIONAL [AI] [TECHNOLOGY] SERVICE FROM DOING ANY OF THE FOLLOWING
43 FOR MINOR ACCOUNT HOLDERS:
44 1. PRODUCING VISUAL MATERIAL OF SEXUAL CONDUCT.
45 2. GENERATING DIRECT STATEMENTS THAT THE ACCOUNT HOLDER SHOULD
46 ENGAGE IN SEXUAL CONDUCT.
