FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
• Adds the City of Yuma to the list of entities that must return to the Arizona Department of
Administration any unexpended and unencumbered monies remaining from the School Safety
Interoperability Fund distributions made in FYs 2022 and 2023.
