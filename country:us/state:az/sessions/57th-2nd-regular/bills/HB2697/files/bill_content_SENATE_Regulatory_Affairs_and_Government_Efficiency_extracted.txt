8 Section 1. Section 36-192, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended to
9 read:
10 36-192. County health departments; naloxone kits; definition
11 A. The department and the Arizona health care cost containment
12 system shall continue to distribute naloxone kits as necessary. In
13 addition, a county health department may provide to a person who is at
14 risk of experiencing or who is experiencing an opioid-related overdose a
15 kit that contains naloxone hydrochloride or any other opioid antagonist,
16 INCLUDING ANY EXPIRED OPIOID ANTAGONIST, that is approved by the United
17 States food and drug administration for the treatment of a drug overdose.
18 B. FOR THE PURPOSES OF THIS SECTION, "EXPIRED OPIOID ANTAGONIST"
19 MEANS AN OPIOID ANTAGONIST THAT IS NOT MORE THAN THREE YEARS PAST THE
20 OPIOID ANTAGONIST'S EXPIRATION DATE.
21 Sec. 2. Section 36-798.51, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended to
22 read:
23 36-798.51. Overdose and disease prevention programs;
24 requirements; standards; definition
25 A. A city, town, county or nongovernmental organization, including
26 a local health department or an organization that promotes scientifically
27 proven ways of mitigating health risks associated with drug use and other
28 high-risk behaviors, or any combination of these entities, may establish
29 and operate an overdose and disease prevention program. A program
30 established pursuant to this section shall have all of the following
31 objectives:
32 1. To reduce the spread of viral hepatitis, HIV and other
33 bloodborne diseases in this state.
34 2. To reduce needle-stick injuries to law enforcement officers and
35 other emergency personnel.
36 3. To encourage individuals who inject drugs to enroll in
37 evidence-based treatment.
38 4. To increase proper disposal of used syringes.
39 5. To reduce the occurrence of skin and soft tissue wounds and
40 infections related to injection drug use.
41 B. A program established pursuant to this section shall offer all
42 of the following:
43 1. Disposal of used needles and hypodermic syringes.
