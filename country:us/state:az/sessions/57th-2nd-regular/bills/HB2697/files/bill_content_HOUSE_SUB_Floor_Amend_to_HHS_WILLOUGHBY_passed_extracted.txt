ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2697: expired opioid antagonists; use
WILLOUGHBY FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Incorporates the HHS Committee amendment.
2. Modifies the definition of an expired opioid antagonist to mean an opioid
antagonist that is not more than three years past the opioid antagonists' s
expiration date.
3. Makes technical and conforming changes.
