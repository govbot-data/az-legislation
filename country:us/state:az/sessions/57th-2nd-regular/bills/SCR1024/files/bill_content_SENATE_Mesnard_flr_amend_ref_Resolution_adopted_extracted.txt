Bill Number: S.C.R. 1024
Mesnard Floor Amendment
Reference to: printed resolution
Amendment drafted by: Leg. Council
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
• Requires a member of the Legislature to have been a member of the political party from which the
member is elected for at least one year before the election, in addition to being a resident of the
district.
