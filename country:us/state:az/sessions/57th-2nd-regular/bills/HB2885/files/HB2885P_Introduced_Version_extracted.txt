HB2885 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: cultural impact assessment; state projects State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HB 2885 Introduced by Representative Tsosie AN ACT Amending title 41, chapter 4, article 1, Arizona Revised Statutes, by adding section 41-710.04; relating to state projects. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Title 41, chapter 4, article 1, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended by adding section 41-710.04, to read: START_STATUTE41-710.04. State projects, cultural impact assessment; civil penalty; definition A. Notwithstanding any other law, this state or an agency of this state shall perform a CULTURAL impact assessment for all state projects before the state project may begin. B. If a contractor working on a state project ignores the findings of the cultural impact ASSESSMENT pursuant to subsection A of this section and begins the project, The contractor shall be assessed a civil penalty of $1,500. C. For the purposes of this section, "cultural impact assessment" means a process to identify, evaluate and manage the effects of a proposed project on a community's cultural values, resources and heritage. END_STATUTE
