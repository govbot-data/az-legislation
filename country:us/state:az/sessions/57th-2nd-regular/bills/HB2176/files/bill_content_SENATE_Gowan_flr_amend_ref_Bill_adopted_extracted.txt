Bill Number: H.B. 2176
Gowan Floor Amendment
Reference to: House engrossed bill
Amendment drafted by: Leg. Council
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
• Allows the Department of Health Services to initiate a complaint investigation of a health care
institution for incidents that occurred more than 12 months before the complaint if the complaint
involves an abuse allegation.
