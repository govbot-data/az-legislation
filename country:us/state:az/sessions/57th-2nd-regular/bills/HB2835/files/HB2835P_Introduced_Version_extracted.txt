HB2835 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: labor organizations; paycheck deduction; prohibition State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HB 2835 Introduced by Representative Carter N AN ACT amending title 23, chapter 8, article 1, arizona revised statutes, by adding section 23-1308; relating to labor relations. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Title 23, chapter 8, article 1, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended by adding section 23-1308, to read: START_STATUTE23-1308. Labor organization; paycheck deduction; prohibition A labor organization in this state may not deduct labor organization membership dues directly from an employee's paycheck. END_STATUTE
