HB2007 - 572R - I Ver PREFILED    NOV 17 2025 REFERENCE TITLE: ivermectin; over-the-counter drug State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HB 2007 Introduced by Representative Kupper AN ACT Amending title 32, chapter 18, article 3, Arizona Revised Statutes, by adding section 32-1979.04; relating to the arizona state board of pharmacy. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Title 32, chapter 18, article 3, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended by adding section 32-1979.04, to read: START_STATUTE32-1979.04. Ivermectin; over-the-counter drug; definition A. Notwithstanding any other law, Ivermectin that is formulated for human use may be sold or purchased as an over-the-counter drug without a prescription order or consultation with a health professional. B. For the purposes of this section, "over-the-counter drug" means a drug that may be legally sold and purchased in this state without a prescription order.END_STATUTE
