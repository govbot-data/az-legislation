HB4030: rates; fees; taxes; increase; moratorium
OLSON FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. States that the moratorium on municipal and county fees, taxes and utility
rates does not apply to ad valorem taxes or special assessments levied to pay
for:
a. the principal, interest and redemption charges on bonded indebtedness
and other lawful long-term obligations;
b. property improvement assessment districts, improvement districts and
other special purpose districts; and
c. the support of a school district. (Sec. 1, 3)
2. Excepts a municipality that has not raised utility rates by twelve percent or
more in the prior four fiscal years from the moratorium but only allows an
increase of more than that of inflation determined by the consumer price
index. (Sec. 1)
3. Allows a municipality to adopt, impose or collect a new or increased rate or
fee to fund the acquisition and delivery of new or additional water resources.
(Sec. 1)

3 2. Local government taxes, fees and utility rates materially affect

6 3. Municipalities and counties regularly adopt annual budgets, tax
7 rates and fee and utility rate schedules, which reflect policy judgments

14 5. This state has recognized the importance of operational
15 efficiency, including through executive initiatives such as the Arizona
16 capacity and efficiency initiative, which emphasizes identifying
17 efficiencies, modernizing operations and improving government performance,
18 recognizing potentially hundreds of millions of dollars in cost savings
19 that should be passed on to taxpayers.
20 6. Temporary restraint on local fee, tax and utility rate increases
21 encourages local governments to prioritize efficiency, innovation and
22 responsible fiscal management, while preserving the ability to meet
23 service demands through improved processes and increased service volume.
24 7. Local governments retain discretion to manage expenditures,
25 staffing, service levels and capital planning within existing fee, tax and
26 utility rate structures during the moratorium period.
27 B. It is the intent of the legislature to:
28 1. Provide temporary relief to residents and businesses by
29 stabilizing locally imposed fees, taxes and utility rates during a period
30 of elevated inflation and economic uncertainty.
31 2. Encourage local governments to pursue efficiencies and
32 modernization before increasing costs borne by residents and businesses.
33 3. Preserve predictability and transparency in local government
34 fee, tax and rate structures by anchoring charges to duly adopted budgets
35 and schedules.
36 4. Respect constitutional limitations, existing contractual
37 obligations and voter-approved authorities, while promoting affordability
38 and economic stability statewide.
