HB2776 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: private property rights; due process State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HB 2776 Introduced by Representative Powell AN ACT amending title 33, chapter 23, article 1, Arizona Revised Statutes, by adding section 33-2502; relating to private property rights. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Title 33, chapter 23, article 1, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended by adding section 33-2502, to read: START_STATUTE33-2502. Laws restricting private property rights prohibited This state and a political subdivision of this state may not adopt a law or implement a policy that deliberately or inadvertently infringes or restricts private property rights without due process.END_STATUTE
