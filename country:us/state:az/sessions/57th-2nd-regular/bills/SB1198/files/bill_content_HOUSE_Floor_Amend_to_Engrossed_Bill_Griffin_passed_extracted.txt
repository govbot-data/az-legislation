ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
SB 1198: beef council; veterinary board; continuation
GRIFFIN FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Removes the following:
a. the continuation of the Arizona State Land Department (ASLD);
b. provisions relating to the Joint Legislative Audit Committee (JLAC)
and the sunset review of the ASLD; and
c. the legislative purpose statement for the ASLD.
