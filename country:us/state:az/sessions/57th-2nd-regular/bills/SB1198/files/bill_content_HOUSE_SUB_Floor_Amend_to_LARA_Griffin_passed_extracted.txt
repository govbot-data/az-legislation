ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
SB 1198: Arizona beef council; continuation
GRIFFIN FLOOR AMENDMENT
Contains the provisions of the LARA Committee amendment and:
1. Continues the State Land Department (SLD) until July 1, 2027.
2. Repeals the SLD on January 1, 2028.
3. Prohibits the Joint Legislative Audit Committee (JLAC) from directing the
Auditor General to conduct a performance audit for SLD.
4. Repeals the above prohibition on JLAC on January 1, 2028.
5. Contains a purpose statement.
