Reference to: printed bill
Amendment drafted by: Leg.Council
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
• Specifies that the Arizona Department of Revenue, when updating the annual tax forms and
instructions, must assume that the Legislature will conform to the U.S. Internal Revenue Code, but
only to the extent those provisions affect federal adjusted gross income or federal taxable income.
