ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
SB 1180: DOR; income tax forms; conformity
OLSON SUBSTITUTE FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Requires the Department of Revenue (DOR) to supplement the income tax
forms and instructions if nonconformity occurs and provides deadlines for
when they are to be published.
2. Includes a citation to the itemized deduction statute for DOR to consider
when prescribing the forms and instructions.
3. Includes addressing the abatement of penalties and interest due to
nonconformity that DOR needs to address in the published forms and
instructions.
4. Includes a legislative intent clause.
5. Makes technical changes.
