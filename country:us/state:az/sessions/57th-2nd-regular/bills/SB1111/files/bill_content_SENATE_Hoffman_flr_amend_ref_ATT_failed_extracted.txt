FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
1. Narrows the authority of a law enforcement agency to use an automated license plate reader to
only detect government vehicles inside property owned, leased or operated by the state, a state
agency, city, town, county, law enforcement agency or political subdivision of the state that is
enclosed by a wall, fence or other permanent barrier and that does not allow the automated
license plate reader to have visual access to the adjacent public rights of way or members of the
general public in any way.
2. Subjects a governmental agency that violates the use of the automated license plate reader to a
civil penalty of between $25,000 and $250,000 for each violation.
3. Specifies that each day a violation continues constitutes a separate offense.
4. Allows a person whose plate data is captured, stored or shared in violation of the use of an
automated license plate reader a private right of action for actual damages, statutory damages or
$1,000 per violation.
5. Redefines automated license plate reader to mean a technology that provides stationary or mobile
automated detection of government vehicle license plates inside property owned, leased or
operated by the state, a state agency, city, town, county, law enforcement agency or political
subdivision of the state that is enclosed by a wall, fence or other permanent barrier and that does
not allow the automated license plate reader to have visual access to the adjacent public rights of
way or members of the general public in any way and that captures data associated with license
plates for official law enforcement purposes.
6. Redefines official law enforcement purposes to mean the identification of government vehicle
license plates inside property owned, leased or operated by the state, a state agency, city, town,
county, law enforcement agency or political subdivision of the state that is enclosed by a wall,
fence or other permanent barrier and that does not allow the automated license plate reader to
have visual access to the adjacent public rights of way or members of the general public in any
way.
