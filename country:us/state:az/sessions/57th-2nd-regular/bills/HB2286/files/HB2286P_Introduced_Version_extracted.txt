HB2286 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: appropriations; Navajo Nation; capital projects State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HB 2286 Introduced by Representative Tsosie AN ACT appropriating monies to the department of transportation. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriations; department of transportation; Navajo Nation; senior center and veterans building; State Route 264; Ganado water pipeline; exemption A. The following sums are appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the department of transportation to distribute to the Navajo division of transportation for the following projects: 1. $600,000 to complete pavement at Ganado senior citizens center and veterans building. 2. $1,700,000 for the State Route 264 milepost 441 turning lane. 3. $340,000 for materials and construction costs for the Ganado waterline project. B. The appropriations made in subsection A of this section are exempt from the provisions of section 35-190, Arizona Revised Statutes, relating to lapsing of appropriations.
