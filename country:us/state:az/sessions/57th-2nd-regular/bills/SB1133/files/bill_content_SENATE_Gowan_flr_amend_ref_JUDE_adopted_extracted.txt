Gowan Floor Amendment
Reference to: JUDICIARY AND ELECTIONS
Committee Amendment
Amendment drafted by: Leg. Council
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
• Specifies that the prescribed exemption from filing an additional financial disclosure statement
applies to statewide and legislative candidates on the effective date and applies to all other
candidates beginning January 1, 2027.
