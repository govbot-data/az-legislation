9 G. The nomination paper shall include the exact manner in which the
10 candidate desires to have the person's name printed on the official ballot
11 and shall be limited to the candidate's surname and given name or names,
12 an abbreviated version of such names or appropriate initials such as "Bob"

14 Nicknames are permissible, but nicknames, abbreviated versions or initials
15 of given names may not suggest reference to professional, fraternal,
16 religious or military titles and may not include a slogan, a promotional
17 word or phrase or any word that does not actually constitute a nickname.
18 No other descriptive name or names shall be printed on the official
19 ballot, except as provided in this section. Candidates' abbreviated names
20 or nicknames may be printed within quotation marks. The candidate's
21 surname shall be printed first, followed by the given name or names.
22 H. Not later than the date of the first petition signature on a
23 nomination petition, a person who may be a candidate for office pursuant
24 to this section shall file a statement of interest with the appropriate
25 filing officer for that office. The statement of interest shall contain
26 the name of the person, the political party, if any, and the name of the
27 office that may be sought. Any nomination petition signatures collected
28 before the date the statement of interest is filed are invalid and subject
29 to challenge. This subsection does not apply to:
30 1. Candidates for elected office for special taxing districts that
31 are established pursuant to title 48.
32 2. Candidates for precinct committeeman.

44 2. The declaration of qualification and eligibility as prescribed
