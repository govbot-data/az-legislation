HB2798 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; Arizona geological survey; study State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HB 2798 Introduced by Representative Carbone AN ACT APPROPRIATING monies to the University of Arizona. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; university of Arizona; study; map; report A. The sum of $_________ is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the university of Arizona for distribution to the Arizona geological survey established by section 27-102, Arizona Revised Statutes. B. The monies appropriated by subsection A of this section shall be used to: 1. Conduct a study of all minerals and metals, including thorium, that are needed for generation III+ and generation IV small modular reactors and advanced nuclear reactors. 2. Create a statewide map of all proven and probable reserves of the minerals and metals identified in paragraph 1 of this subsection. The map shall include the mineral and metal quantities and grades, if known, for each identified proven and probable reserve. C. On or before _________, the Arizona geological survey shall submit a report of the findings of the study conducted pursuant to subsection B of this section to the governor, the president of the senate, the speaker of the house of representatives, the chairperson of the natural resources, energy and water committee of the house of representatives and the chairperson of the natural resources committee of the senate and shall make available a copy of this report on the Arizona geological survey's website.
