ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2918: renewable energy equipment; valuation; depreciation
GRESS FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Extends the deadline, from January 1, 2027 to January 1, 2030, for certain
renewable energy equipment to have their construction initiated before the
prescribed calculation used for determining full cash value of the equipment
is changed to according to the provisions of the bill.

18 electric utility scale that is capable of absorbing energy, storing energy

21 4. "Original cost" means the actual cost, without trending, of

24 5. "Renewable energy and storage equipment" means electric
