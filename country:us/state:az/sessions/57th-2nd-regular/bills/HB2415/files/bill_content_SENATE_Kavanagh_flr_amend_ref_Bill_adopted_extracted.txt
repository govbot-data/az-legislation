Bill Number: H.B. 2415
Kavanagh Floor Amendment
Reference to: House engrossed bill
Amendment drafted by: Leg. Council
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
1. Stipulates that law enforcement agencies and crime laboratories are not required to test or analyze
products to identify the presence of kratom, or to distinguish between natural and synthetic kratom,
if the law enforcement agency or laboratory does not possess the necessary capability to test the
product.
2. Allows the Attorney General to coordinate kratom testing and analysis through a qualified
laboratory if a law enforcement agency or crime laboratory does not have the capability to test or
analyze kratom.
