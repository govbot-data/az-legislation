Fifty-seventh Legislature Public Safety & Law Enforcement
Second Regular Session S.B. 1520
PROPOSED
HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES AMENDMENTS TO S.B. 1520
(Reference to Senate engrossed bill)
1 Strike everything after the enacting clause and insert:
2 "Section 1. Title 41, chapter 4, article 1, Arizona Revised
3 Statutes, is amended by adding section 41-710.04, to read:
4 41-710.04. Arizona fire service station equipment fund
5 A. THE ARIZONA FIRE SERVICE STATION EQUIPMENT FUND IS ESTABLISHED
6 CONSISTING OF LEGISLATIVE APPROPRIATIONS. MONIES IN THE FUND ARE
7 CONTINUOUSLY APPROPRIATED. THE DEPARTMENT SHALL ADMINISTER THE FUND AND
8 MAY ADOPT RULES, POLICIES AND PROCEDURES TO CARRY OUT THE PURPOSES OF THIS
9 SECTION.
10 B. THE DEPARTMENT SHALL GRANT MONIES TO FIRE DISTRICTS, TRIBAL
11 GOVERNMENTS AND MUNICIPALITIES IN THIS STATE TO PROVIDE KITCHEN EQUIPMENT
12 AND SUPPLIES TO FIRE STATIONS. AN APPLICANT SHALL SUBMIT AN APPLICATION
13 FOR A GRANT ON A FORM PRESCRIBED BY THE DEPARTMENT. GRANT MONIES MAY BE
14 USED TO REIMBURSE ELIGIBLE APPLICANTS FOR PURCHASES MADE WITHIN A CALENDAR
15 YEAR OF THE APPLICATION SUBMISSION DEADLINE AS SET BY THE DEPARTMENT.
16 C. AT LEAST TWENTY-FIVE PERCENT OF GRANT MONIES MUST BE AWARDED TO
17 FIRE DISTRICTS.
18 Sec. 2. Appropriation; Arizona fire service station equipment
19 fund; exemption
20 A. The sum of $1,000,000 is appropriated from the state general
21 fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the Arizona fire service station
22 equipment fund established by section 41-710.04, Arizona Revised Statutes,
23 as added by this act.
24 B. The appropriation made in subsection A of this section is exempt
25 from the provisions of section 35-190, Arizona Revised Statutes, relating
26 to lapsing of appropriations."
27 Amend title to conform
