1 The bill as proposed to be amended is reprinted as follows:
2 Section 1. Department of education; review of reporting
3 requirements; recommendations; delayed repeal;
4 definition
5 A. The department of education shall review each requirement
6 prescribed by the Arizona Revised Statutes for public schools in this
7 state to report information to the department and identify whether:
8 1. The reporting requirement has expired or is otherwise obsolete.
9 2. Public schools are required to submit the same information for
10 two or more reporting requirements.
11 B. The department of education shall review each requirement
12 prescribed by the Arizona Revised Statutes for the department to [submit
13 a] report to the legislature and identify whether:
14 1. The reporting requirement has expired or is otherwise obsolete.
15 2. The department is required to submit the same information for
16 two or more reporting requirements.
17 C. On or before December 1, 2026, the department of education shall
18 submit to the speaker of the house of representatives and the president of
19 the senate a report that both:
20 1. Summarizes the reviews required pursuant to subsections A and B
21 of this section.
22 2. Recommends legislative action to consolidate, eliminate or
23 otherwise modify the reporting requirements described in subsections A and
24 B of this section. For the purposes of this paragraph, the department
25 shall recommend the repeal or consolidation of any reporting requirement
26 that is duplicative, expired or otherwise obsolete.
27 D. This section is repealed from and after June 30, 2029.
