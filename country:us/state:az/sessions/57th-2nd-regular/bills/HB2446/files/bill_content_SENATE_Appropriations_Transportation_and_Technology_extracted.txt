25 Sec. 2. Title 28, chapter 14, article 2, Arizona Revised Statutes,
26 is amended by adding section 28-5233, to read:
27 28-5233. Motor carriers; English proficiency required;
28 inspections; definition
29 A. A MOTOR CARRIER IN THIS STATE SHALL DEMONSTRATE ENGLISH
30 PROFICIENCY. ENGLISH PROFICIENCY INCLUDES A MOTOR CARRIER'S ABILITY TO
31 UNDERSTAND AND RESPOND TO VERBAL QUESTIONS AND TO UNDERSTAND HIGHWAY
32 TRAFFIC SIGNS, INCLUDING ELECTRONIC DYNAMIC MESSAGE SIGNS. A SPECIALTY
33 OFFICER OF THE DEPARTMENT OR A LAW ENFORCEMENT OFFICER AUTHORIZED PURSUANT
34 TO SECTION 28-5204 SHALL ISSUE AN OUT-OF-SERVICE ORDER TO A MOTOR CARRIER
35 WHO CANNOT SUFFICIENTLY DEMONSTRATE ENGLISH PROFICIENCY DURING AN
36 INSPECTION.
37 B. ALL ROADSIDE INSPECTIONS SHALL BE CONDUCTED IN ENGLISH AND
38 WITHOUT TOOLS TO FACILITATE COMMUNICATION, INCLUDING INTERPRETERS, CUE
39 CARDS, SMARTPHONE APPLICATIONS OR ON-CALL TELEPHONE INTERPRETATION
40 SERVICES.
