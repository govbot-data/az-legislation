Carroll Floor Amendment
Reference to: House engrossed bill
Amendment drafted by: Leg. Council
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
• Specifies that a wildfire, for the requirement for the Arizona Department of Environmental Quality
to submit an exceptional event demonstration, must be larger than 25,000 acres and be attributable
to smoke or land damage.
