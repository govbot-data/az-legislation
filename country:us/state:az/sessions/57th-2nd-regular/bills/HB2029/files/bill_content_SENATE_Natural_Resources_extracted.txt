2 Section 1. Section 49-1333, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended to
3 read:
4 49-1333. Water conservation grant fund; procedures;
5 reporting; definition
6 A. In compliance with any applicable requirements, an eligible
7 entity as defined in section 49-1301 may apply to the authority for and
8 accept grants from the water conservation grant fund for a water
9 conservation program or project that complies with the requirements of
10 sections 49-1332 and 49-1334. A nongovernment NONGOVERNMENTAL
11 organization that focuses on water conservation or environmental
12 protection may apply to the authority for and accept grants from the water
13 conservation grant fund for a water conservation program or project if it
14 partners with an eligible entity as defined in section 49-1301. An
15 eligible entity may apply to the authority for and accept grants from the
16 water conservation grant fund to distribute rebates for the installation
17 of gray water systems.
18 B. The authority shall:
19 1. Prescribe a simplified form and procedure to apply for and
20 approve assistance.
21 2. Establish by rule criteria that are consistent with this article
22 by which assistance will be awarded.
23 3. Determine the order and priority of water conservation programs
24 or projects assisted under this section based on the merits of the
25 application with respect to the requirements of sections 49-1332 and
26 49-1334.
27 4. Provide that a single water conservation program grant may not
28 exceed $3,000,000, a single water conservation project grant may not
