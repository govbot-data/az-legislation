ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
SB 1200: assured water supply; certificate; model.
S/E: bodies of water; effluent; landscaping
GRIFFIN FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Clarifies that a body of water that is filled and refilled exclusively with
effluent is exempt from prohibition, only if the body of water was constructed
or substantial capital investment was made toward construction prior to
December 31, 2030.
2. Stipulates that a body of water must be constructed in compliance with the
aforementioned requirement and was used to convey noneffluent for
landscape and recreational purposes between June 30, 2023 and June 30,
2026.
3. Modifies the allowed purposes of the water transported through the body of
water from other purposes to recreational purposes off the body of water.
4. Modifies the water that is to be delivered and withdrawn from the body of
water that is to be measured from the body of water from the total volume of
water to the volume of water by type of water.
5. Specifies that the volume of effluent added to the body of water must be equal
to or greater than the amount that is lost to seepage and evaporation.
6. Prohibits the amount of noneffluent delivered to the body of water from
exceeding the total volume withdrawn for landscaping or recreational
purposes.
7. Requires the effluent to be delivered to the end user before commingling with
any other type of water, except as provided under current law.
8. Expands requirements of certain owners or operators of a body of water to
include documentation that the body of water was used to convey noneffluent
for landscape or recreational purposes between June 30, 2023 and June 30,
2026.
9. Clarifies that this legislation does not allow the use of groundwater to:
a. provide the initial fill of a body of water; or
b. meet seepage or evaporative loss of a body of water unless authorized
under current law.
10. Makes technical and conforming changes.
