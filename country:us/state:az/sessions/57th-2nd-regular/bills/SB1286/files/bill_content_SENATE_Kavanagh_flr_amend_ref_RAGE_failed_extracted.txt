Kavanagh Floor Amendment
Reference to: REGULATORY AFFAIRS AND GOVERNMENT
EFFICIENCY Committee Amendment
Amendment drafted by: Leg. Council
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
1. Extends, from 14 days to 30 days, the initial and subsequent time periods that a veterinarian may
prescribe anti-microbial drugs through electronic means.
2. Makes conforming changes.
