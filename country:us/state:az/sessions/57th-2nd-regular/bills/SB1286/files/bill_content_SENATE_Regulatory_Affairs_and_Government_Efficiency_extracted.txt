2 Section 1. Section 32-2240.03, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended
3 to read:
4 32-2240.03. Veterinarian client patient relationship through
5 electronic means; consent; prescriptions; scope
6 of practice
7 A. Notwithstanding any other law and except for an animal that is
8 used in commercial food production that is regulated under title 3, a
9 veterinarian may establish a veterinarian client patient relationship
10 through electronic means if all of the following apply:
11 1. The veterinarian is licensed pursuant to this chapter or holds a
12 permit pursuant to section 32-2217.01.
13 2. The veterinarian obtains informed consent from the client,
14 including an acknowledgement that the standards of care prescribed by this
15 chapter apply to in-person visits and visits through electronic means.
16 The veterinarian shall maintain for at least three years documentation of
17 the consent described in this paragraph.
18 3. The veterinarian provides the client with the veterinarian's
19 name and contact information and secures an alternate means of contacting
20 the client if the electronic means is interrupted. The electronic or
21 written record provided to the client after the telemedicine visit THROUGH
22 ELECTRONIC MEANS must include the veterinarian's license number.
23 4. Before conducting an evaluation of the patient through
24 electronic means, the veterinarian advises the client of all of the
25 following:
26 (a) The veterinarian may ultimately recommend an in-person visit.
27 (b) The veterinarian is prohibited by federal law from prescribing
28 some drugs or medications based only on an electronic examination.
