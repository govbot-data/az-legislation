1 The bill as proposed to be amended is reprinted as follows:
2 Section 1. Title 41, chapter 7, article 12, Arizona Revised
3 Statutes, is amended by adding section 41-1293, to read:
4 41-1293. Freedom of speech monument committee; membership;
5 duties
6 A. THE FREEDOM OF SPEECH MONUMENT COMMITTEE IS ESTABLISHED
7 CONSISTING OF THE FOLLOWING MEMBERS:
8 1. THE PRESIDENT OF THE SENATE [, OR THE PRESIDENT'S DESIGNEE,] WHO
9 SHALL SERVE AS THE CHAIRPERSON OF THE COMMITTEE IN EVEN-NUMBERED YEARS.
10 2. THE SPEAKER OF THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES [, OR THE SPEAKER'S
11 DESIGNEE,] WHO SHALL SERVE AS THE CHAIRPERSON IN ODD-NUMBERED YEARS.
12 3. THE SENATE MAJORITY LEADER [OR THE MAJORITY LEADER'S DESIGNEE].
13 4. THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES MAJORITY LEADER [OR THE MAJORITY
14 LEADER'S DESIGNEE].
15 5. THE SENATE MINORITY LEADER [OR THE MINORITY LEADER'S DESIGNEE].
16 6. THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES MINORITY LEADER [OR THE MINORITY
17 LEADER'S DESIGNEE].
18 7. THE CHIEF JUSTICE OF THE SUPREME COURT OR THE CHIEF JUSTICE'S
19 DESIGNEE.
20 B. THE FREEDOM OF SPEECH MONUMENT COMMITTEE SHALL NOMINATE AND
21 APPROVE TWO INDIVIDUALS FROM DIFFERENT POLITICAL PARTIES EACH YEAR TO BE
22 ADDED TO THE FREEDOM OF SPEECH MONUMENT FOR THEIR CONTRIBUTIONS TO
23 PRESERVING FREEDOM OF SPEECH IN THIS STATE.
24 C. INDIVIDUALS THAT ARE NOMINATED PURSUANT TO SUBSECTION B OF THIS
25 SECTION MUST BE DECEASED FOR AT LEAST FIVE YEARS.
26 D. THE COMMITTEE SHALL MEET ON THE CALL OF THE CHAIRPERSON.

Senate Amendments to S.B. 1663
1 Sec. 2. Freedom of speech monument; procedures; delayed
2 repeal
3 A. Pursuant to section 41-1363, subsection A, Arizona Revised
4 Statutes, this act authorizes the legislative council to provide for the
5 placement of a freedom of speech monument in the governmental mall.
6 B. The procedures prescribed in section 41-1363, subsection B,
7 Arizona Revised Statutes, apply to the placement of the monument.
8 C. Public monies are not authorized for the costs of the placement
9 of the monument. All fundraising and contracts for artistic design and
10 construction of the monument are the sole responsibility of the
11 proponents. This state may not facilitate fundraising or establish a
12 state fund for the deposit of the monies.
13 D. This section is repealed from and after September 30, 2029.
14 Enroll and engross to conform
15 Amend title to conform
