5 duties
6 A. THE FREEDOM OF SPEECH MONUMENT COMMITTEE IS ESTABLISHED

17 DESIGNEE.
18 B. THE FREEDOM OF SPEECH MONUMENT COMMITTEE SHALL NOMINATE AND
19 APPROVE TWO INDIVIDUALS [WHO WERE] FROM DIFFERENT POLITICAL PARTIES [AND
20 WHO WERE RESIDENTS OF THIS STATE] EACH YEAR TO BE ADDED TO THE FREEDOM OF
21 SPEECH MONUMENT FOR THEIR CONTRIBUTIONS TO PRESERVING FREEDOM OF SPEECH IN
22 THIS STATE.

Senate Amendments to S.B. 1663
1 Sec. 2. Freedom of speech monument; procedures; delayed
2 repeal
3 A. Pursuant to section 41-1363, subsection A, Arizona Revised
4 Statutes, this act authorizes the legislative council to provide for the
5 placement of a freedom of speech monument in the governmental mall.
6 B. The procedures prescribed in section 41-1363, subsection B,
7 Arizona Revised Statutes, apply to the placement of the monument.
8 C. Public monies are not authorized for the costs of the placement
9 of the monument. All fundraising and contracts for artistic design and
10 construction of the monument are the sole responsibility of the
11 proponents. This state may not facilitate fundraising or establish a
12 state fund for the deposit of the monies.
13 D. This section is repealed from and after September 30, 2029.
14 Enroll and engross to conform
