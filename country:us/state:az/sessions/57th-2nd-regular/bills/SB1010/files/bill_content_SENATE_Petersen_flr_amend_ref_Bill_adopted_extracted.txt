Petersen Floor Amendment
Reference to: printed bill
Amendment drafted by: Leg. Council
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
1. Renames the Loop 202 as the Charlie Kirk Loop 202 rather than the Charlie Kirk Highway.
2. States that the Legislature intends:
a) to rename the entire Loop 202 as the Charlie Kirk Loop 202; and
b) that the underlying segments of the Loop 202 would retain their names and designations which
include the Red Mountain Freeway, the Santan Freeway and the Congressman Ed Pastor
Freeway.
3. Makes conforming changes.
