19 Sec. 2. Section 41-162, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended to
20 read:
21 41-162. Address confidentiality; duties of secretary of
22 state; application assistant; criminal records
23 check
24 A. On or before December 31, 2012, The secretary of state shall
25 establish the address confidentiality program to allow persons who have
26 been subjected to domestic violence offenses, sexual offenses or stalking
27 to keep their residence addresses confidential and not accessible to the
28 general public. Participants in the program shall receive a substitute
29 address that becomes the participant's lawful address of record.
30 B. The secretary of state shall:
31 1. Designate a substitute address for a program participant that is
32 used by state and local government entities as set forth in this section.
33 2. Receive mail sent to a program participant at a substitute
34 address and forward the mail to the program participant as set forth in
35 paragraph 3 of this subsection.
36 3. Receive first-class, certified or registered mail on behalf of a
37 program participant and forward the mail to the program participant for no
38 charge. The secretary of state may arrange to receive and forward other
39 classes or kinds of mail at the program participant's expense. The
40 secretary of state is not required to track or otherwise maintain records
41 of any mail received on behalf of a program participant unless the mail is
42 certified or registered mail.
43 C. Notwithstanding any other law and except as provided by court
44 rule, a program participant may be served by registered mail or by
45 certified mail, return receipt requested, addressed to the program
46 participant at the program participant's substitute address with any
