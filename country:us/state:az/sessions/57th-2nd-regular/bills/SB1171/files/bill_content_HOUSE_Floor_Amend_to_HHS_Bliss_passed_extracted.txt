FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
SB 1171: AHCCCS; dual enrollment; notice
BLISS FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Adds controlling persons of behavioral health facilities to the list of persons
required to be U.S. citizens or lawful permanent residents and hold a valid
Level 1 fingerprint clearance card as a condition of licensure.
