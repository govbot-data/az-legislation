3 "Section 1. Section 3-1491, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended to
4 read:
5 3-1491. Salt River horse herd; necessary interaction; written
6 authorization; violation; classification;
7 definition
8 A. A person shall not harass, shoot, injure, kill or slaughter a
9 horse that is part of the Salt River horse herd.
10 B. A person shall not interfere with, take, chase, capture or
11 euthanize a horse that is part of the Salt River horse herd without
12 written authorization from either the department or the county
13 sheriff. The department or the county sheriff may provide written
14 authorization pursuant to this subsection only for humane purposes.
15 C. If an incident occurs that requires interaction with the Salt
16 River horse herd in order to protect the safety or health of a horse that
17 is or horses that are part of the Salt River horse herd or the public, a
18 private or public entity may address the incident after obtaining written
19 authorization from either the department or the county sheriff.
20 D. If a horse has any brand or other mark that indicates ownership,
21 the department shall attempt to prove ownership of the horse or the county
22 sheriff shall contact the department to attempt to establish ownership of
23 the horse. If the department cannot prove ownership of the horse, the
24 department or the county sheriff may provide written authorization
25 pursuant to this section.
26 E. This state shall enter into an agreement pursuant to section
27 11-952 with the United States forest service to implement this article or

2 DEPARTMENT SHALL POST THE AGREEMENT ON ITS WEBSITE.
3 F. This state may enter into an agreement with a private entity to
4 address any issue relating to the Salt River horse herd.
5 G. A horse that is part of the Salt River horse herd is not a stray
6 animal as defined in section 3-1401 and is not subject to sections 3-1371

8 H. A person who violates subsection A of this section is guilty of

14 ownership."
