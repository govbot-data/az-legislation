ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
SB 1199: veterinary medical examining board; continuation
REIM FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Clarifies that prohibited actions taken against the Salt River horse herd
must be intentional and also apply to horses that were previously part of the
herd.
2. Prescribes actions relating to the transfer and adoption of unidentified horses
that are not part of the Salt River horse herd.
3. Requires the Arizona Department of Agriculture (Department) to:
a. maintain a genetically viable herd consistent with current standards
and peer reviewed studies; and
b. to make publicly available, via the Department's website, all genetic
assessment data and their corresponding scientific studies.
4. Outlines requirements of the Department and any contractor as it relates to
genetic assessments and herd management.
5. Repeals the section relating to outlined requirements on July 1, 2030.
6. Adds a legislative findings clause.
7. Adds an emergency clause.
8. Makes conforming changes.
