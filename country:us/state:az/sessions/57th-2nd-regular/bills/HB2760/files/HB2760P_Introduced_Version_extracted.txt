HB2760 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; rural transportation fund State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HB 2760 Introduced by Representative Nguyen AN ACT Appropriating monies to the state match ADVANTAGE for rural transportation fund. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; state match advantage for rural transportation fund The sum of $15,000,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the state match advantage for rural transportation fund established by section 28-339, Arizona Revised Statutes.
