ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2402: ambulance services; certificates of necessity
WILLOUGHBY FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Clarifies the ambulances that are responding to a natural disaster, rather than
an emergency, are exempt from statutory regulations relating to emergency
medical services.
2. Revises the information that an ambulance service must provide to DHS
relating to dispatched ambulances.
3. Removes, as a condition for issuing a certificate of necessity for a geographic
area that is not within the service area of a certificate of necessity to a city,
town or fire district or a private ambulance service to provide ambulance
services to that geographic area, the current certificate of necessity holder has
not met the requirements of the certificate for at least 6 months.
4. Adds separate conditions for allowing DHS to issue a certificate of necessity to
a city, town or fire district or private ambulance service to provide ambulance
services to a geographic area, including that a current certificate holder has
not met the certificate conditions for at least 12 months.
