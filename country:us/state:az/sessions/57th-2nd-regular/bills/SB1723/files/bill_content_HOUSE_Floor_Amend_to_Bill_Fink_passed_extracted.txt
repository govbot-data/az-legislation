FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
SB 1723: domestic violence; release conditions
FINK FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Removes all language in the bill which amends current law, relating to awarding
legal decision-making or parenting time in the context of domestic violence.
2. Provides that, after an order-of-protection hearing has occurred, the superior court
in a Title 25 family-law action, may modify the order of protection, but only as it
applies to a child who is both common to the parties and listed in the order of
protection.
