SB1189 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: campaign spending; personal security State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SB 1189 Introduced by Senator Shope AN ACT amending title 16, chapter 6, article 1.3, Arizona Revised Statutes, by ADDING section 16-921.01; relating to CAMPAIGN expenditures. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Title 16, chapter 6, article 1.3, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended by adding section 16-921.01, to read: START_STATUTE16-921.01. Personal security expenditures A candidate committee may lawfully spend campaign monies for the personal SECURITY of the candidate and the candidate's family members and shall report those expenditures as PRESCRIBED by section 16-926. END_STATUTE
