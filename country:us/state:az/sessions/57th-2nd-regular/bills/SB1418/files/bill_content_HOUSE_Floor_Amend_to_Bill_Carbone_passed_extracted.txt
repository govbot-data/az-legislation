FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
SB 1418: corporation commission; small modular reactors
CARBONE FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Clarifies that all prescribed criteria must be met prior to construction of a
small modular reactor (SMR) for the county to be prohibited from regulating
the use or occupation of land.
2. Replaces the entity that a SMR can be colocated with from a large industrial
energy user to an extra high load factor customer.
3. Expands the requirements a SMR must meet to qualify for the exemption
from county regulations.
4. Defines small modular reactor.
5. Removes rulemaking authority of the Corporation Commission as it relates to
implementing this legislation.
6. Makes conforming changes.
