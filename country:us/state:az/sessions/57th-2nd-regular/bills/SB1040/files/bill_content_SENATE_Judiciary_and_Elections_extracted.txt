27 J. The secretary of state shall develop and administer a statewide
28 database of voter registration information that contains the name and
29 registration information of every registered voter in this state. The
30 statewide database is a matter of statewide concern and is not subject to
31 modification or further regulation by a political subdivision. The
32 database shall include an identifier that is unique for each individual
33 voter. The database shall provide for access by voter registration
34 officials and shall allow expedited entry of voter registration
35 information after it is received by county recorders. As a part of the
36 statewide voter registration database, county recorders shall provide for
37 the electronic transmittal of that information to the secretary of state
38 on a real time basis. The secretary of state shall provide for
39 maintenance of the database, including provisions regarding removal of
40 ineligible voters that are consistent with the national voter registration
41 act of 1993 (P.L. 103-31; 107 Stat. 77; 52 United States Code sections
42 20501 through 20511) and the help America vote act of 2002 (P.L. 107-252;
43 116 Stat. 1666; 52 United States Code sections 20901 through 21145),
44 provisions regarding removal of duplicate registrations and provisions to
45 ensure that eligible voters are not removed in error. For the purpose of
46 maintaining compliance with the help America vote act of 2002, each county
47 voter registration system is subject to approval by the secretary of state
48 for compatibility with the statewide voter registration database system.
