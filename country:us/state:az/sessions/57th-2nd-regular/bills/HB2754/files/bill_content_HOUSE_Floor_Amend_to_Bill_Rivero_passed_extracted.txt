ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB2754: commerce authority; board of directors
RIVERO FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Adds, as ex officio members without the power to vote, to the Arizona
Commerce Authority Board of Directors:
a. the ranking minority party member of the Senate Finance Committee,
or its successor committee, or the ranking member's designee; and
b. the ranking minority party member of the House of Representatives
International Trade Committee, or its successor committee, or the
ranking member's designee.
2. Removes the repeal of the Arizona Competes Program's termination of July 1,
2026.
3. Extends the Arizona Competes Program from July 1, 2026, to July 1, 2030.
4. Makes conforming changes.
