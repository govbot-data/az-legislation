SB1605 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; area agencies on aging State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SB 1605 Introduced by Senators Alston: Bravo, Diaz, Epstein, Gabaldón, Gonzales, Kuby, Miranda, Sears, Sundareshan;  Representative Connolly AN ACT Appropriating monies to the department of economic security. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriations; department of economic security; area agencies on aging The following amounts are appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the department of economic security to distribute to area agencies on aging for the following purposes: 1. $5,000,000 for home and community-based services. 2. $5,000,000 for senior housing assistance.
