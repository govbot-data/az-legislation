HB4021 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; medical marijuana; veterans; fees State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HB 4021 Introduced by Representative Mathis AN ACT appropriating monies to the department of health services. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; department of health services; registry identification cards; veterans; exemption A. The sum of $10,000,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the department of health services for the issuance and renewal of registry identification cards for veterans of the United States armed forces pursuant to section 36-2804.02, Arizona Revised Statutes. B. The appropriation made in subsection A of this section is exempt from the provisions of section 35-190, Arizona Revised Statutes, relating to lapsing of appropriations.
