HB2315 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: exchange teachers; technical correction State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HB 2315 Introduced by Representative Gress AN ACT amending section 15-132, Arizona Revised Statutes; relating to exchange teachers. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Section 15-132, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended to read: START_STATUTE15-132. Certification of exchange teachers and professors The state board of education shall issue temporary certificates to exchange teachers and professors of foreign countries and other states whose training and background comply with the rules promulgated adopted by the board for certification and are equivalent to those of the local teacher or PROFESSOR with whom the exchange is made. END_STATUTE
