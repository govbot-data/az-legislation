1 To the [Arizona Department of Transportation] [State Board on Geographic
2 and Historic Names]:
3 Your memorialist respectfully represents:
4 Whereas, Donald J. Trump was the 45th and is the 47th duly elected
5 President of the United States; and
6 Whereas, in the 2024 presidential election, Donald J. Trump won the
7 popular vote, becoming the first Republican nominee for president to do so
8 in 20 years; and
9 Whereas, Donald J. Trump has established the Department of
10 Government Efficiency, which has dismantled government bureaucracy,
11 eliminated excess regulations, cut wasteful expenditures and restructured
12 federal agencies; and
13 Whereas, during his first term in office Donald J. Trump built over
14 400 miles of the world's most robust and advanced border wall, and he has
15 further secured our southern border during his second term; and
16 Whereas, President Donald J. Trump stands for a strong manufacturing
17 base and fair agreements for international trade; and
18 Whereas, President Trump steadfastly supports a strong military and
19 strengthening our national defense; and
20 Whereas, President Trump's energy plan includes several ambitious
21 policy prescriptions designed to establish American energy dominance and
22 to remove regulatory burdens on the use of energy, while at the same time
23 ensuring meaningful environmental protection and continued progress toward
24 cleaner air and water; and
25 Whereas, during his first term in office, President Trump helped
26 pass $3.2 trillion in historic tax relief and reform the tax code; and
27 Whereas, during his first term in office, President Donald J. Trump
28 canceled United States participation in the United Nations Paris Climate

1 Agreement and ended all payments of United States tax dollars to the
2 United Nations Green Climate Fund; and
3 Whereas, during his first term in office, President Trump
4 discontinued the use of "sue and settle" lawsuits that had the effect of
5 imposing government policy on behalf of special interests without
6 congressional authorization or input from the states, other interested
7 parties and the general public; and
8 Whereas, during his first term in office, 4,000,000 jobs were
9 created, more than 400,000 of those in manufacturing, which is the fastest
10 manufacturing growth rate in more than three decades; and
11 Whereas, during his first term in office, President Trump signed the
12 Executive Order on Modernizing the Regulatory Framework for Agricultural
13 Biotechnology Products, which helped bring innovative new technologies to
14 market in American farming and agriculture; and
15 Whereas, President Trump established the Governors' Initiative on
16 Regulatory Innovation to reduce outdated regulations at the state, local
17 and tribal levels; and
18 Whereas, during the third quarter of 2020, the economy grew at a
19 rate of 33.1%, the most rapid GDP growth ever recorded; and
20 Whereas, Donald J. Trump's top priority as president is the best
21 interests of American workers and American families.
22 Wherefore your memorialist, the Senate of the State of Arizona, the House
23 of Representatives concurring, prays:
24 1. That the [Arizona Department of Transportation] [State Board on
25 Geographic and Historic Names] designate State Route 260 as the "Donald J.
26 Trump Highway".
27 2. That the [Arizona Department of Transportation] [State Board on
28 Geographic and Historic Names] approve, place and maintain appropriate
29 signage to identify the Donald J. Trump Highway.
30 3. That the Secretary of State transmit a copy of this Memorial to
31 the [Director] [Chairperson] of the [Arizona Department of Transportation]
32 [State Board on Geographic and Historic Names].
33 Enroll and engross to conform
