A CONCURRENT MEMORIAL

 

urging the state board on geographic and historic names to designate state route 260 as the "donald j. trump highway".

 

 

(TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE)

 

To the State Board on Geographic and Historic Names:

Your memorialist respectfully represents:

Whereas, Donald J. Trump was the 45th and is the 47th duly elected President of the United States; and

Whereas, in the 2024 presidential election, Donald J. Trump won the popular vote, becoming the first Republican nominee for president to do so in 20 years; and

Whereas, Donald J. Trump has established the Department of Government Efficiency, which has dismantled government bureaucracy, eliminated excess regulations, cut wasteful expenditures and restructured federal agencies; and

Whereas, during his first term in office Donald J. Trump built over 400 miles of the world's most robust and advanced border wall, and he has further secured our southern border during his second term; and

Whereas, President Donald J. Trump stands for a strong manufacturing base and fair agreements for international trade; and

Whereas, President Trump steadfastly supports a strong military and strengthening our national defense; and

Whereas, President Trump's energy plan includes several ambitious policy prescriptions designed to establish American energy dominance and to remove regulatory burdens on the use of energy, while at the same time ensuring meaningful environmental protection and continued progress toward cleaner air and water; and

Whereas, during his first term in office, President Trump helped pass $3.2 trillion in historic tax relief and reform the tax code; and

Whereas, during his first term in office, President Donald J. Trump canceled United States participation in the United Nations Paris Climate Agreement and ended all payments of United States tax dollars to the United Nations Green Climate Fund; and

Whereas, during his first term in office, President Trump discontinued the use of "sue and settle" lawsuits that had the effect of imposing government policy on behalf of special interests without congressional authorization or input from the states, other interested parties and the general public; and

Whereas, during his first term in office, 4,000,000 jobs were created, more than 400,000 of those in manufacturing, which is the fastest manufacturing growth rate in more than three decades; and

Whereas, during his first term in office, President Trump signed the Executive Order on Modernizing the Regulatory Framework for Agricultural Biotechnology Products, which helped bring innovative new technologies to market in American farming and agriculture; and

Whereas, President Trump established the Governors' Initiative on Regulatory Innovation to reduce outdated regulations at the state, local and tribal levels; and

Whereas, during the third quarter of 2020, the economy grew at a rate of 33.1%, the most rapid GDP growth ever recorded; and

Whereas, Donald J. Trump's top priority as president is the best interests of American workers and American families.

Wherefore your memorialist, the Senate of the State of Arizona, the House of Representatives concurring, prays:

1. That the State Board on Geographic and Historic Names designate State Route 260 as the "Donald J. Trump Highway".

2. That the State Board on Geographic and Historic Names approve, place and maintain appropriate signage to identify the Donald J. Trump Highway.

3. That the Secretary of State transmit a copy of this Memorial to the chairperson of the State Board on Geographic and Historic Names.
