SB1455 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; State Route 24; extension State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SB 1455 Introduced by Senator Farnsworth AN ACT Appropriating monies to the department of transportation. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; department of transportation; State Route 24; Ironwood Drive; extension The sum of $______ is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the department of transportation for surveying, designing and constructing the extension of State Route 24 east of Ironwood Drive.
