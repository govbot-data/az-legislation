ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2127: bronze star medal license plates
CARTER N FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Establishes the following special plates and funds:
a. Skin Cancer Prevention Special Plate and Fund;
b. Diabetes Awareness Special Plate and Fund;
c. Small Business Advocate Special Plate and Fund;
d. Grand Canyon Preservation Special Plate and Fund;
e. Recycling Refurbished Electronics Special Plate and Fund;
f. Adaptive Sports and Recreation Special Plate and Fund;
g. Blood and Biologics Special Plate and Fund;
h. Astronomy Center Special Plate and Fund;
i. Law Enforcement Charity Organization Special Plate;
j. Law Enforcement Support Special Plate and Fund;
k. Search and Rescue Special Plate and Fund;
l. Returning Warrior Special Plate and Fund;
m. Community Well-Being Special Plate and Fund;
n. Historic Electric Vehicle Special Plate and Fund;
o. Kindness Charity Special Plate and Fund;
p. Elk and Other Wildlife Special Plate and Fund;
q. Youth Education Charity Special Plate and Fund;
r. Police Service Dogs Special Plate and Fund;
s. Golf Tournament Charity Special Plate and Fund;
t. Distracted Driving Special Plate and Fund;
u. Public Education Foundation Special Plate and Fund; and
v. Grand Canyon National Park Special Plate and Fund.
2. Prescribes rules for the implementation of each special plate and fund.
3. Stipulates that ADOT is only required to issue the Bronze Star Medal License
plate, from and after, December 31, 2026.
4. Makes conforming changes.
