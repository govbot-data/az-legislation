Bill Number: H.B. 2033
Diaz Floor Amendment
Reference to: House engrossed bill
Amendment drafted by: Mason Holler
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
• Specifies that a school district or charter school may administer the statewide assessment in the
form of a written test on approval by a vote of the school district governing board or charter school
governing body only for an assessment that is administered to students in grades three through
eight.
