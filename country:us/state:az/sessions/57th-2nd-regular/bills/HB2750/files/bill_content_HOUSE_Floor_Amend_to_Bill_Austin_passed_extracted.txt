HOUSE FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
Bill Number: HB 2750
Floor Amendment
Austin
1. Increases the number of members of the Arizona-Sonora Trade Commission
(Commission) appointed by the President of the Senate to three, rather than
two.
2. Increases the number of Commission members appointed by the House of
Representatives to three, rather than two.
3. Directs the leader of the Senate minority party to appoint two Commission
members who have knowledge of or any involvement in an organization that
promotes Sonoran affairs or who have interest in the well-being of trade
relations between the state and Sonora, Mexico.
4. Directs the leader of the House of Representatives minority party to appoint
two Commission members who have knowledge of or any involvement in an
organization that promotes Sonoran affairs or who have interest in the well-
being of trade relations between the state and Sonora, Mexico.
