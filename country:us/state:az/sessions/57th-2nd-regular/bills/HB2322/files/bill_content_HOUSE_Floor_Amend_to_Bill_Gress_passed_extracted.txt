ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2322: DCS; investigations; interviews; recording
GRESS FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Requires DCS to apply for a grant if federal legislation is enacted that
provides grants to states for the recording of child welfare interviews.
2. Requires DCS to apply for a grant until it is awarded and implement the
audio and video requirements if a grant is received.
3. Makes a technical change.
