HB 2720: prostitution; assessment; anti-human trafficking fund
BLISS FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Permits persons convicted of prostitution, who were sex trafficking victims, to
seal all records related to their arrest, conviction and sentence.
2. Excludes the sealed records, of a sex trafficking victim convicted of
prostitution, from being used by the courts as a historical prior or for other
such purposes.
