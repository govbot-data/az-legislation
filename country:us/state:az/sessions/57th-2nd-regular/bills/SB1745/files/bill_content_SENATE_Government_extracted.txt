7 A. NOTWITHSTANDING ANY OTHER LAW, ORDINANCE, CHARTER PROVISION,

12 TRANSACTION PRIVILEGE TAX CLASSIFICATION.
13 B. EXCEPT AS PROVIDED IN SUBSECTION D OF THIS SECTION, THE TAX RATE

16 CLASSIFICATIONS, COMPONENTS OR ORDINANCES UNDER WHICH THE TRANSACTION
17 PRIVILEGE TAX OR EXCISE TAX IS IMPOSED.
18 C. A CITY OR TOWN MAY NOT ADOPT A NEW TRANSACTION PRIVILEGE TAX OR
19 EXCISE TAX, INCREASE AN EXISTING TAX RATE, EXPAND A TAX BASE OR IMPOSE A

21 PRIVILEGE TAX CLASSIFICATION TO EXCEED THE LIMIT PRESCRIBED BY SUBSECTION
22 A OF THIS SECTION UNLESS THE NEW TRANSACTION PRIVILEGE TAX OR EXCISE TAX
23 IS APPROVED PURSUANT TO SUBSECTION D OF THIS SECTION.
24 D. NOTWITHSTANDING ANY OTHER LAW, ORDINANCE, CHARTER PROVISION,
25 RESOLUTION OR SPECIAL ACT, A CITY OR TOWN SHALL SUBMIT ANY PROPOSED
