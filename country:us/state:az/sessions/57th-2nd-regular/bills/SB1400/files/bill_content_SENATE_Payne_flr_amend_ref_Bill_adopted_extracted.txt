FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
1. Allows a law enforcement agency (LEA) to establish a crisis response services program, rather
than a peer support counseling program, to provide support to public safety employees who have
been in or exposed to a traumatic event or an emotional experience during employment.
2. Exempts, from being confidential and privileged from disclosure, crisis response services
communication if:
a) the designated person waives the person's right to confidentiality;
b) any communications or advice given within the program indicates clear and present danger to
the designated person or to any other person;
c) the communication or advice is made during the course of a criminal investigation;
d) the designated person violates any of the agency's policies and the violation amounts to a
violation of laws that is normally enforced by a law enforcement agency; or
e) the designated person who received crisis response services voluntarily testifies, in which case
the critical incident stress management team member or peer support team member may be
compelled to testify on the same subject.
3. Requires a crisis response services designated person, rather than a peer support participant, to
hold a privilege from disclosure of any crisis response services communication, rather than of any
peer support communication, as prescribed.
4. Allows a wellness program to support the mental health and well-being of the law enforcement
agency's employees to include crisis response services, rather than crisis counseling.
5. Exempts prescribed proceedings and records from being confidential and privileged from
disclosure if:
a) a circumstance occurs in which the designated person waives the person's right to
confidentiality;
b) any communications or advice given within the program indicates clear and present danger to
the designated person or to any other person;
c) the communication or advice is made during the course of a criminal investigation;
d) the designated person violates any of the law enforcement agency's policies and the violation
amounts to a violation of laws that is normally enforced by a law enforcement agency; or
e) the designated person who received crisis response services voluntarily testifies, in which case
the critical incident stress management team member or peer support team member may be
compelled to testify on the same subject.
6. Defines terms.
7. Removes defined terms.
8. Makes technical and conforming changes.
