2 Section 1. Section 36-504.01, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended
3 to read:
4 36-504.01. Guardians and agents; rights in proceedings
5 Guardians and agents who have decisional authority to make personal,
6 medical and treatment decisions for a patient pursuant to an order of the
7 court or pursuant to a validly executed mental health power of attorney in
8 which the principal has been found incapable of giving informed consent
9 have the following rights in any proceedings under this article regarding
10 involuntary treatment of the patient:
11 1. To be notified of any petition for treatment, motion for amended
12 court order, application for continued court-ordered RENEWAL OF THE COURT
13 ORDER FOR treatment, APPLICATION FOR CONVERSION TO AN ORDER FOR CONTINUING
14 COURT-ORDERED TREATMENT and request for judicial review.
15 2. If allowed by the court, to provide the court with the
16 guardian's or agent's position regarding the relief being sought in any of
17 the proceedings set forth in paragraph 1 of this section and to provide
18 the court with any relevant information to help the court make a
19 determination.
20 3. To provide relevant information to any agency providing
21 inpatient or outpatient screening, evaluation or treatment to the patient.
22 4. When appropriate, to participate in treatment and discharge
23 planning with the inpatient or outpatient treatment providers.
