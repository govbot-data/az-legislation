Bill Number: S.B. 1244
Angius Floor Amendment
Reference to: JUDICIARY AND ELECTIONS
Committee Amendment
Amendment drafted by: Leg. Concil
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
1. Specifies that the court must appoint an attorney to represent a patient when an application is filed
to continue court-ordered treatment, if the patient does not already have an attorney.
2. Outlines annual reporting requirements for continuing court-ordered treatment and requires that a
copy of the report be provided to the patient, patient's attorney and guardian.
3. Specifies that if the annual report supports the continuation of the order for treatment, the court
may order that continuing treatment remain in place without a hearing.
4. Stipulates that if a patient objects to an order for continuing treatment, the treatment agency, rather
than the patient, has the burden of proving by clear and convincing evidence that the order for
continuing treatment is necessary.
5. Removes the specification that the process to convert a court order for treatment to an order for
continuing treatment must follow the current process prescribed for applications to renew court-
ordered treatment.
6. Grants a patient the right to request an independent evaluation on objection to an order for
continuing court-ordered treatment.
7. Adds a delayed effective date of January 1, 2027.
