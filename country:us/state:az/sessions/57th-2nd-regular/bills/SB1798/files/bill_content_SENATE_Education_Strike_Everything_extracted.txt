4 15-120.08. High schools; free application for federal student
5 aid requirements; designated employees;
6 definition
7 A. BEGINNING IN THE 2027-2028 SCHOOL YEAR, EACH HIGH SCHOOL IN THIS
8 STATE SHALL:
9 1. DESIGNATE AT LEAST ONE SCHOOL EMPLOYEE AS THE SCHOOL'S POINT OF
10 CONTACT FOR THE FREE APPLICATION FOR FEDERAL STUDENT AID. EACH DESIGNATED
11 EMPLOYEE SHALL:
12 (a) COMPLETE AN INITIAL ORIENTATION PROGRAM FOR THE FREE
13 APPLICATION FOR FEDERAL STUDENT AID THAT IS APPROVED OR RECOGNIZED BY
14 EITHER THE ARIZONA BOARD OF REGENTS OR A FREE APPLICATION FOR FEDERAL
15 STUDENT AID SUPPORT PARTNER. THE DESIGNATED EMPLOYEE MAY RECEIVE
16 CONTINUING EDUCATION CREDITS FOR:
17 (i) COMPLETING THE ORIENTATION PROGRAM REQUIRED BY THIS
18 SUBDIVISION.
19 (ii) COMPLETING ANY TRAINING AND EDUCATION AFTER THE ORIENTATION
20 PROGRAM TO LEARN ABOUT UPDATES TO FEDERAL POLICIES AND PROCESSES RELATING
21 TO THE FREE APPLICATION FOR FEDERAL STUDENT AID.
22 (b) PROVIDE INFORMATION ABOUT THE FREE APPLICATION FOR FEDERAL
23 STUDENT AID, INCLUDING INFORMATION ABOUT AVAILABLE RESOURCES AND
24 ASSISTANCE, TO STUDENTS AND FAMILIES OF STUDENTS.
25 2. ANNUALLY REPORT TO THE ARIZONA BOARD OF REGENTS THE NAME AND
26 CONTACT INFORMATION OF EACH EMPLOYEE WHO IS DESIGNATED PURSUANT TO
27 PARAGRAPH 1 OF THIS SUBSECTION.
28 3. INCLUDE THE NAME AND CONTACT INFORMATION OF EACH EMPLOYEE WHO IS
29 DESIGNATED PURSUANT TO PARAGRAPH 1 OF THIS SUBSECTION IN THE STUDENT
30 HANDBOOK AND IN AT LEAST ONE COMMUNICATION FROM THE SCHOOL TO STUDENTS AND
31 PARENTS OF STUDENTS.
32 4. POST THE NAME AND CONTACT INFORMATION OF EACH EMPLOYEE WHO IS
33 DESIGNATED PURSUANT TO PARAGRAPH 1 OF THIS SUBSECTION ON THE SCHOOL'S
34 WEBSITE.
35 5. DEVELOP AND IMPLEMENT A FREE APPLICATION FOR FEDERAL STUDENT AID
36 AWARENESS STRATEGY THAT IS ALIGNED WITH EDUCATION AND CAREER ACTION PLAN
37 REQUIREMENTS ADOPTED BY THE STATE BOARD OF EDUCATION. THE STRATEGY THAT
38 IS DEVELOPED PURSUANT TO THIS PARAGRAPH MUST PROVIDE, THROUGH THE SCHOOL'S
39 MESSAGING SYSTEM, SOCIAL MEDIA ACCOUNTS, EMAIL ACCOUNTS AND STUDENT
