SB1272 - 572R - S Ver Senate Engrossed appropriation; Douglas port of entry State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SENATE BILL 1272 AN ACT appropriating monies to the state treasurer. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1.1. Appropriation; state treasurer; city of Douglas; Douglas port of entry A. The sum of $5,000,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the state treasurer for distribution to the city of Douglas for the state match of the Douglas port of entry. B. The monies appropriated in subsection A of this section may be expended only to the extent that the general services administration provides an award of $678,000,000 for the Douglas port of entry.
