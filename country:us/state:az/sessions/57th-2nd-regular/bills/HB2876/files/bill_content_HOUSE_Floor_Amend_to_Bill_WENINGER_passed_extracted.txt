ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2876: public bodies; executive sessions; agenda
WENINGER FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Prohibits a public body from placing any proposal to impose or increase a tax
rate, assessment or fee in Arizona on the consent agenda for a meeting of the
public body.
2. Directs a public body to place an item that was discussed or voted on in
executive session on an action agenda and not on a consent agenda.
