31 B. The study committee shall meet at the call of the
32 cochairpersons.
33 C. Members of the study committee are not eligible to receive
34 compensation but are eligible for reimbursement of expenses pursuant to
35 title 38, chapter 4, article 2, Arizona Revised Statutes.
36 D. The study committee shall:
37 1. Conduct a comprehensive survey and research study to assess the
38 availability, capacity and geographic distribution of licensed and
39 unlicensed programs that offer substance use disorder specialty care in
40 this state and evaluate the economic impact, effectiveness and value of
41 implementing statewide minimum standards for evidence-based care.
42 2. Identify gaps in access to and implementation of evidence-based
43 biopsychosocial treatments, workforce capacity needs and resources
44 necessary to support high-quality care delivery across urban and rural
45 settings, including medically supported withdrawal services, medications
46 for opioid use disorder, licensed clinical providers, care coordination
47 and recovery supports.
