27 B. The study committee shall meet at the call of the

29 C. Members of the study committee are not eligible to receive

32 D. The study committee shall:
33 1. Conduct a comprehensive survey and research study to assess the
34 availability, capacity and geographic distribution of licensed and
35 unlicensed programs that offer substance use disorder specialty care in
36 this state and evaluate the economic impact, effectiveness and value of
37 implementing statewide minimum standards for evidence-based care.
38 2. Identify gaps in access to and implementation of evidence-based
39 biopsychosocial treatments, workforce capacity needs and resources
40 necessary to support high-quality care delivery across urban and rural
41 settings, including medically supported withdrawal services, medications
42 for opioid use disorder, licensed clinical providers, care coordination
43 and recovery supports.
44 3. Review state laws, rules and oversight structures governing
45 substance use disorder treatment programs, identify regulatory loopholes
46 that allow unlicensed or nonstandard programs to operate without
47 sufficient clinical oversight and processes that enable fraud, waste and
