FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
SB 1167: cities; towns; counties; posting; website
BLACKMAN FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Allows a municipality or county to satisfy their statutory requirement for
advertising, publishing or printing by posting the notice on a newspaper's
official website, in addition to the municipality or county's official website.
2. Requires the municipality or county to electronically provide the newspaper
with notice of the posting at least one full business day before the required
notice date in order to satisfy the posting requirements through the outlined
process.
3. Directs the municipality or county to post the notice on their official website
by the required notice date.
