1 The bill as proposed to be amended is reprinted as follows:
2 Section 1. Title 9, chapter 4, article 8, Arizona Revised Statutes,
3 is amended by adding section 9-500.54, to read:
4 9-500.54. Notices; posting requirements; official website;
5 applicability
6 A. NOTWITHSTANDING ANY OTHER LAW, A CITY OR TOWN MAY SATISFY ANY
7 STATUTORY REQUIREMENT FOR ADVERTISING, PUBLISHING OR PRINTING REQUIRED BY
8 THIS TITLE BY POSTING THE ADVERTISEMENT, PUBLICATION OR PRINTING ON THE
9 OFFICIAL WEBSITE OF THE CITY OR TOWN.
10 B. A CITY OR TOWN THAT POSTS PUBLIC NOTICES ON THE OFFICIAL WEBSITE
11 OF THE CITY OR TOWN SHALL INCLUDE A PUBLIC NOTICES LINK ON THE HOME PAGE
12 OF THE WEBSITE OF THE CITY OR TOWN THAT LINKS TO ALL STATUTORILY REQUIRED
13 ADVERTISING, PUBLISHING OR PRINTING, LISTED BY CATEGORY.
14 C. THIS SECTION DOES NOT APPLY TO PUBLISHING A NOTICE OF A PROPOSED
15 CHARTER AS REQUIRED BY SECTION 9-282.
16 Sec. 2. Title 11, chapter 2, article 4, Arizona Revised Statutes,
17 is amended by adding section 11-269.31, to read:
18 11-269.31. Notices; posting requirements; official website
19 A. NOTWITHSTANDING ANY OTHER LAW, A COUNTY MAY SATISFY ANY
20 STATUTORY REQUIREMENT FOR ADVERTISING, PUBLISHING OR PRINTING REQUIRED BY
21 THIS TITLE BY POSTING THE ADVERTISEMENT, PUBLICATION OR PRINTING ON THE
22 OFFICIAL WEBSITE OF THE COUNTY.
23 B. A COUNTY THAT POSTS PUBLIC NOTICES ON THE OFFICIAL WEBSITE OF
24 THE COUNTY SHALL INCLUDE A PUBLIC NOTICES LINK ON THE HOME PAGE OF THE
25 WEBSITE OF THE COUNTY THAT LINKS TO ALL STATUTORILY REQUIRED ADVERTISING,
26 PUBLISHING OR PRINTING, LISTED BY CATEGORY.
