HB2237 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; Apache Junction; tourism; recreation State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HB 2237 Introduced by Representative Blackman AN ACT Appropriating monies to the state treasurer. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; state treasurer; Apache Junction; Superstition trails The sum of $4,500,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the state treasurer for distribution to the city of Apache Junction for development and construction of enhancements to Superstition trails and a visitor gateway.
