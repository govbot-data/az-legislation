SB1598 - 572R - S Ver Senate Engrossed appropriation; schools; community gardens State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SENATE BILL 1598 AN ACT appropriating monies to the department of education. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; department of education; community gardens The sum of $500,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the department of education to award grants pursuant to title 41, chapter 24, Arizona Revised Statutes, to school districts and charter schools to build community gardens.
