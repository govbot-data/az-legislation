SB1598 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; schools; community gardens State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SB 1598 Introduced by Senator Alston: Representatives Connolly, Sandoval AN ACT appropriating monies to the department of education. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; department of education; community gardens The sum of $500,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the department of education to award grants pursuant to title 41, chapter 24, Arizona Revised Statutes, to school districts and charter schools to build community gardens.
