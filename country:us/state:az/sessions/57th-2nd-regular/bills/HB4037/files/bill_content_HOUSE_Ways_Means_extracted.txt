16 Sec. 3. Title 43, chapter 10, article 5, Arizona Revised Statutes,
17 is amended by adding section 43-1073.02, to read:
18 43-1073.02. Education opportunity tax credit; rules;
19 definition
20 A. FOR TAXABLE YEARS BEGINNING FROM AND AFTER DECEMBER 31, 2025, A
21 CREDIT IS ALLOWED AGAINST THE TAXES IMPOSED BY THIS CHAPTER FOR EACH
22 QUALIFYING CHILD OF A TAXPAYER AS PROVIDED BY THIS SECTION.
23 B. THE AMOUNT OF THE CREDIT IS:
24 1. FOR A QUALIFYING CHILD WHO WAS NOT ENROLLED IN A PUBLIC
25 SCHOOL[,] [AND] DID NOT PARTICIPATE IN AN ARIZONA EMPOWERMENT SCHOLARSHIP
26 ACCOUNT PURSUANT TO SECTION 15-2402 [AND DID NOT RECEIVE AN EDUCATIONAL
27 SCHOLARSHIP OR TUITION GRANT FROM A SCHOOL TUITION ORGANIZATION PURSUANT
28 TO CHAPTER 15 OR 16 OF THIS TITLE] DURING THE TAXABLE YEAR, EQUAL TO
29 EIGHTY PERCENT OF THE AMOUNT OF THE BASE SUPPORT LEVEL AS DETERMINED BY
30 SECTION 15-943 FOR THE QUALIFYING CHILD OF THE TAXPAYER.
31 2. FOR A QUALIFYING CHILD WHO WAS [EITHER] ENROLLED IN A PUBLIC
32 SCHOOL[,] [OR] PARTICIPATED IN AN ARIZONA EMPOWERMENT SCHOLARSHIP ACCOUNT
33 PURSUANT TO SECTION 15-2402 [OR RECEIVED AN EDUCATIONAL SCHOLARSHIP OR
34 TUITION GRANT FROM A SCHOOL TUITION ORGANIZATION PURSUANT TO CHAPTER 15 OR
35 16 OF THIS TITLE] FOR NOT MORE THAN FIFTY PERCENT OF THE INSTRUCTIONAL
36 DAYS OF THE TAXABLE YEAR, EQUAL TO FORTY PERCENT OF THE AMOUNT OF THE BASE
37 SUPPORT LEVEL AS DETERMINED BY SECTION 15-943 FOR THE QUALIFYING CHILD OF
38 THE TAXPAYER.
39 C. IF THE ALLOWABLE AMOUNT UNDER SUBSECTION B OF THIS SECTION
40 EXCEEDS THE TAXES OTHERWISE DUE UNDER THIS TITLE FOR THE TAXABLE YEAR,
41 AFTER APPLYING ALL OTHER CREDITS TO WHICH THE CLAIMANT MAY BE ENTITLED AND
42 AFTER ANY SETOFF UNDER SECTION 42-1122, THE EXCESS AMOUNT SHALL BE PAID IN
43 THE SAME MANNER AS A REFUND UNDER SECTION 42-1118.
44 D. THE DEPARTMENT OF REVENUE SHALL ADOPT RULES AND PRESCRIBE FORMS
45 TO ADMINISTER THIS SECTION AND MAY CONSULT WITH THE DEPARTMENT OF
46 EDUCATION TO DETERMINE WHETHER A QUALIFYING CHILD OF A TAXPAYER WHO IS
47 CLAIMING THE CREDIT UNDER THIS SECTION WAS ENROLLED IN A PUBLIC SCHOOL OR
