HOUSE FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
Bill Number: SB 1193
Floor Amendment
Contreras P
The P. Contreras amendment dated 3/23/2026 at 11:33am:
1. Prohibits DHS from selling or disclosing any personal identifying
information of any individual who DHS licenses or certifies for
public health or safety or professions and occupations
2. Clarifies the section does not prohibit DHS from sharing
information relating to the application for licensure or the licensure
of an emergency medical care technician with another regulatory
entity including a commission that administers a licensure
compact.
