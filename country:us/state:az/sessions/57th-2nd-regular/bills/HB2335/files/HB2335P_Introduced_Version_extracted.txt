HB2335 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: health professionals; patients; vaccination status State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HB 2335 Introduced by Representatives Heap: Fink AN ACT Amending title 32, chapter 32, article 1, Arizona Revised Statutes, by adding section 32-3230.03; relating to health professionals. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Title 32, chapter 32, article 1, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended by adding section 32-3230.03, to read: START_STATUTE32-3230.03. Vaccination status; unprofessional conduct It is an act of unprofessional conduct for a health professional to refuse to provide medical care to a person based on the person's vaccination status.END_STATUTE
