HB2225 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; northern Arizona; training academy State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HB 2225 Introduced by Representatives Nguyen: Bliss AN ACT Appropriating monies to state treasurer. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; state treasurer; Yavapai county; northern Arizona regional training academy; exemption A. The sum of $10,000,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the state treasurer to distribute to Yavapai county for capital costs for a northern Arizona regional training academy. B. The appropriation made in subsection A of this section is exempt from the provisions of section 35-190, Arizona Revised Statutes, relating to lapsing of appropriations, until June 30, 2029.
