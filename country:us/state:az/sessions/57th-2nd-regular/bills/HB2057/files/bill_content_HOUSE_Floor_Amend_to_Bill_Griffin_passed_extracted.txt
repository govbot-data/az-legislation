2 Section 1. Section 28-2448, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended to
3 read:
4 28-2448. Arizona centennial special plates; fund
5 A. The department shall issue Arizona centennial special
6 plates. The department shall use thirty-two thousand dollars $32,000 from
7 the state highway fund, established by section 28-6991, to implement this
8 section. The office of tourism shall design the Arizona centennial
9 special plates. The design and color of the Arizona centennial special
10 plates are subject to the approval of the department. The director of the
11 department of transportation may allow a request for Arizona centennial
12 special plates to be combined with a request for personalized special
13 plates. If the director of the department of transportation allows such a
14 combination, the request shall be in a form prescribed by the director of
15 the department of transportation and is subject to the fees for the
16 personalized special plates in addition to the fees required for the
17 Arizona centennial special plates.
18 B. Of the twenty-five dollar $25 fee required by section 28-2402
19 for the original special plates and for renewal of special plates, eight
20 dollars $8 is a special plate administration fee and seventeen dollars $17
21 is an annual donation.
22 C. NOTWITHSTANDING SECTION 28-2402, THE FEE FOR ARIZONA CENTENNIAL
23 SPECIAL PLATES AND FOR RENEWAL OF ARIZONA CENTENNIAL SPECIAL PLATES IS $20
24 IF THE ARIZONA CENTENNIAL SPECIAL PLATES ARE ISSUED TO A [COMMERCIAL]
25 VEHICLE IN A FLEET AS DEFINED IN SECTION 28-2201 AND REGISTERED PURSUANT
26 TO ARTICLE 6 OF THIS CHAPTER. OF THE $20 FEE REQUIRED BY THIS SUBSECTION,
27 $8 IS A SPECIAL PLATE ADMINISTRATION FEE AND $12 IS AN ANNUAL DONATION.
