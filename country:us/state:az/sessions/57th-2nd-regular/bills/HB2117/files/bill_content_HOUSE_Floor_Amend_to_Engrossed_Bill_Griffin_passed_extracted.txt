57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2117: natural resource conservation board; fund
GRIFFIN FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Provides parameters for defining of geographic natural resources areas.
2. States each board member may hold office until their successor is appointed.

1 41-6013. Environmental special plate fund; distribution
2 A. The environmental special plate fund is established and is
3 administered by the board consisting of monies received pursuant to
4 section 28-2413.
5 B. Subject to legislative appropriation, the board shall distribute
6 $5,000 NOT MORE THAN $10,000 annually to each natural resource
7 conservation district with an established education center for the purpose
8 of developing and implementing an environmental education program that is
9 conducted in a balanced manner, that is based on current scientific
10 information and that includes a discussion of economic and social
11 implications.
12 C. The fund established in this section is exempt from the
13 provisions of section 35-190 relating to lapsing of appropriations. On
14 notice from the board, the state treasurer shall invest and divest monies
15 in the fund as provided by section 35-313, and monies earned from
16 investment shall be credited to the fund.
17 D. The appropriated monies shall only be used for the designated
18 purposes specified in statute.
19 Sec. 4. Retention of members
20 Notwithstanding section 41-6011, Arizona Revised Statutes, as
21 amended by this act, all persons serving as members of the state natural
22 resource conservation board on the effective date of this act may continue
23 to serve until the expiration of their normal terms. All subsequent
24 appointments shall be as prescribed by statute.
