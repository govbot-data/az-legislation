HB2739 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: landlord tenant; senior rental limits State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HB 2739 Introduced by Representatives Crews: Garcia, Mathis, Peshlakai, Simacek AN ACT amending title 33, chapter 10, article 1, Arizona Revised Statutes, by adding section 33-1309; relating to the Arizona residential landlord and tenant act. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Title 33, chapter 10, article 1, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended by adding section 33-1309, to read: START_STATUTE33-1309. Senior rent increase limitation A landlord may not increase rent more than three percent annually for a tenant who is sixty-five years of age or older.END_STATUTE
