57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
SB 1242: mental health; hearings; audiovisual technology
BLISS FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Modifies the definition of a mental health treatment agency.
2. Makes further technical changes and revisions relating to behavioral health
facilities.
3. Contains a retroactivity clause.
