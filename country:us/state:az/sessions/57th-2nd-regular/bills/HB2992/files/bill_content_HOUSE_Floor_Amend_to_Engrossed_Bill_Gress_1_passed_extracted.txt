2 Section 1. Child sexual abuse and assault awareness and
3 prevention pilot program; contract; provider;
4 parental exemption; reporting requirements;
5 delayed repeal
6 A. The child sexual abuse and assault awareness and prevention
7 pilot program is established in the department of education. On or before
8 July 1, 2027, the department shall select six public schools to
9 participate in the pilot program during the 2027-2028 school year. The
10 department of education may consult with the department of child safety
11 and organizations that are operated by survivors of grooming or human
12 trafficking, or both, in developing the pilot program established by this
13 subsection. The pilot program must include:
14 1. For teachers, at least four hours of in-person, evidence-based
15 instructional modules that may include the following:
16 (a) Training regarding the prevention and identification of, and
17 responses to, child sexual abuse and assault, including exploitation,
18 grooming, grooming with the intent to exploit and technology-facilitated
19 abuse.
20 (b) Resources to further student, teacher and parental awareness
21 and prevention of child sexual abuse and assault, including exploitation,
22 grooming, grooming with the intent to exploit and technology-facilitated
23 abuse.
24 2. For students, at least three hours of age-appropriate,
25 in-person, evidence-based instruction and age-appropriate educational
26 materials that are designed for children in kindergarten programs or any
27 of grades one through twelve regarding child sexual abuse and assault
