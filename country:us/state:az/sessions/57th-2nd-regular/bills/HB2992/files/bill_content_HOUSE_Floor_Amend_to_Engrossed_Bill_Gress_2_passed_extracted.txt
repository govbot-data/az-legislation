ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2992: pilot program; sexual abuse prevention
GRESS FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Requires, rather than allows, the Arizona Department of Education (ADE) to
consult with the Department of Child Safety and organizations operated by
survivors of grooming or human trafficking.
2. Instructs ADE to select one or more organizations to provide the instructional
modules for teachers or the instruction for students, or both.
3. Outlines criteria an organization must meet to be selected by ADE to provide
the instructional modules for teachers or the instruction for students, or both.
4. Directs ADE, beginning the general effective date, to gather stakeholder input
to develop a curriculum for the Child Sexual Abuse and Assault Awareness
Pilot Program (Pilot Program), subject to prescribed stakeholder input
requirements.
5. Requires ADE to select an organization to develop the curriculum for the Pilot
Program, coordinate stakeholder input and ensure the curriculum meets
specified requirements.
6. Details requirements for ADE when selecting the organization to develop the
curriculum.
7. Subjects the curriculum developed by the organization to approval by the State
Board of Education and ADE.
