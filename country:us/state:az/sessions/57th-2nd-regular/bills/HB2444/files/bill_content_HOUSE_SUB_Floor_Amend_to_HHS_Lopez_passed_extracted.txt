FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2444: pharmacists; independent testing; treatment
LOPEZ FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Changes the age of a person that a pharmacist may independently initiate
treatment on from 6 years of age to 12.
2. Prohibits an employer from overruling a pharmacist's decision to not
independently initiate treatment for the authorized conditions.
3. Requires a pharmacist to maintain professional liability insurance if
independently initiating treatment for the authorized conditions.
4. Establishes the Independent Testing and Treatment Advisory Committee
under the Arizona State Board of Pharmacy to assist in developing written
protocols and outlines membership, compensation and protocol review
requirements.
5. Outlines requirements for a pharmacist to initiate treatment for human
immunodeficiency virus preexposure prophylaxis and postexposure
prophylaxis.
6. Defines preexposure prophylaxis and postexposure prophylaxis.
