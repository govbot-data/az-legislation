9 A. PURSUANT TO A STATEWIDE WRITTEN PROTOCOL APPROVED BY THE BOARD,
10 A PHARMACIST MAY INDEPENDENTLY ORDER, PERFORM AND INTERPRET TESTS THAT ARE
11 AUTHORIZED BY THE UNITED STATES FOOD AND DRUG ADMINISTRATION AND WAIVED
12 UNDER THE CLINICAL LABORATORY IMPROVEMENT AMENDMENTS OF 1988 (P.L.
13 100-578; 102 STAT. 2903; 42 UNITED STATES CODE SECTION 201). A PHARMACIST
14 MAY INDEPENDENTLY INITIATE TREATMENT TO ELIGIBLE PERSONS WHO ARE AT LEAST
15 TWELVE YEARS OF AGE OR THE AGE AUTHORIZED BY THE TREATMENT, WHICHEVER AGE
16 IS OLDER, AND WHO HAVE TEST RESULTS THAT INDICATE THE NEED FOR TREATMENT,
17 BY A TEST THAT IS AUTHORIZED BY THE UNITED STATES FOOD AND DRUG
18 ADMINISTRATION AND WAIVED UNDER THE CLINICAL LABORATORY IMPROVEMENT
19 AMENDMENTS OF 1988, FOR ANY OF THE FOLLOWING:
20 1. INFLUENZA.
21 2. GROUP A STREPTOCOCCUS PHARYNGITIS.
22 3. SARS-COV-2 OR ANY OTHER CORONAVIRUS RESPIRATORY ILLNESS.
23 4. A CONDITION RELATED TO AN EMERGING OR EXISTING PUBLIC HEALTH
24 THREAT IDENTIFIED BY THE DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH SERVICES FOR WHICH A
25 STATEWIDE STANDING ORDER, RULE OR EXECUTIVE ORDER IS ISSUED.
26 B. WHEN DEVELOPING THE STATEWIDE WRITTEN PROTOCOL, THE BOARD SHALL

17 32-1979.06. Human immunodeficiency virus prevention;
18 preexposure prophylaxis; postexposure
19 prophylaxis; definitions
20 A. IN ADDITION TO THE AUTHORITY PROVIDED IN SECTION 32-1979.04, A
21 PHARMACIST WHO IS LICENSED PURSUANT TO THIS CHAPTER MAY INITIATE AND
22 DISPENSE HUMAN IMMUNODEFICIENCY VIRUS PREEXPOSURE PROPHYLAXIS AND
23 POSTEXPOSURE PROPHYLAXIS PURSUANT TO THE STATEWIDE WRITTEN PROTOCOL
24 [ADOPTED] [APPROVED] BY THE BOARD.
25 B. BEFORE INITIATING A PREEXPOSURE PROPHYLAXIS, A PHARMACIST SHALL:
26 1. CONDUCT OR OBTAIN A CLINICAL LABORATORY IMPROVEMENT
27 AMENDMENTS-WAIVED HUMAN IMMUNODEFICIENCY VIRUS TEST AND CONFIRM A NEGATIVE
28 TEST RESULT.
29 2. ASSESS THE PATIENT FOR CONTRAINDICATIONS, DRUG INTERACTIONS AND
30 CLINICAL ELIGIBILITY CONSISTENT WITH THE CURRENT GUIDELINES OF THE UNITED
31 STATES CENTERS FOR DISEASE CONTROL AND PREVENTION.
32 C. BEFORE INITIATING A POSTEXPOSURE PROPHYLAXIS, A PHARMACIST
33 SHALL:
34 1. DETERMINE THAT THE PATIENT PRESENTS WITHIN SEVENTY-TWO HOURS
35 AFTER A POTENTIAL EXPOSURE TO THE HUMAN IMMUNODEFICIENCY VIRUS.
36 2. CONDUCT OR OBTAIN A HUMAN IMMUNODEFICIENCY VIRUS TEST IF
37 FEASIBLE UNDER THE STATEWIDE WRITTEN PROTOCOL.
38 3. ASSESS THE PATIENT FOR CONTRAINDICATIONS, DRUG INTERACTIONS AND
39 CLINICAL ELIGIBILITY CONSISTENT WITH THE CURRENT GUIDELINES OF THE UNITED
40 STATES CENTERS FOR DISEASE CONTROL AND PREVENTION.
41 D. A PHARMACIST WHO INITIATES THERAPY PURSUANT TO THIS SECTION:
42 1. MAY DISPENSE NOT MORE THAN A THIRTY-DAY SUPPLY OF PREEXPOSURE
43 PROPHYLAXIS.
44 2. MAY DISPENSE A COMPLETE TWENTY-EIGHT-DAY COURSE OF POSTEXPOSURE
45 PROPHYLAXIS.
46 3. SHALL PROVIDE COUNSELING REGARDING MEDICATION ADHERENCE,
47 POTENTIAL SIDE EFFECTS AND PREVENTION MEASURES.
