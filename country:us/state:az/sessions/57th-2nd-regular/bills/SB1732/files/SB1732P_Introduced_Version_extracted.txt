SB1732 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; city of Page; roundabout State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SB 1732 Introduced by Senators Hatathlie: Gonzales, Kuby AN ACT appropriating monies to the department of transportation. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; department of transportation; design and construction of roundabout The sum of $3,000,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the department of transportation to distribute to the city of Page to design and construct a roundabout on South Lake Powell Boulevard.
