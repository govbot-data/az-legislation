HB2500 - 572R - H Ver House Engrossed supplemental appropriation; ESA administration State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HOUSE BILL 2500 AN ACT Appropriating monies to the superintendent of public instruction. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Supplemental appropriation; superintendent of public instruction; Arizona empowerment scholarship account program; administration In addition to the appropriation made by Laws 2025, chapter 233, section 31, the sum of $1,000,000 and 12 FTE positions are appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2025-2026 to the superintendent of public instruction to administer the Arizona empowerment scholarship account program established by section 15-2402, Arizona Revised Statutes.
