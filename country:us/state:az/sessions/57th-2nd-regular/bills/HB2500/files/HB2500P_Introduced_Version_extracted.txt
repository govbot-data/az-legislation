HB2500 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: supplemental appropriation; ESA administration State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HB 2500 Introduced by Representatives Gress: Carbone AN ACT Appropriating monies to the superintendent of public instruction. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Supplemental appropriation; superintendent of public instruction; Arizona empowerment scholarship account program; administration In addition to the appropriation made by Laws 2025, chapter 233, section 31, the sum of $1,000,000 and 12 FTE positions are appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2025-2026 to the superintendent of public instruction to administer the Arizona empowerment scholarship account program established by section 15-2402, Arizona Revised Statutes.
