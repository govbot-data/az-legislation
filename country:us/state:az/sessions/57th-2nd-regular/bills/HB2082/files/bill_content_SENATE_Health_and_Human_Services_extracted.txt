4 36-121. Childhood cancer and rare childhood disease research
5 fund
6 A. The childhood cancer and rare childhood disease research fund is
7 established consisting of monies received pursuant to section 28-2442,
8 LEGISLATIVE APPROPRIATIONS, GIFTS, DONATIONS AND FEDERAL GRANTS. The
9 director shall administer the fund. Not more than ten per cent PERCENT of
10 monies deposited in the fund annually shall be used for the cost of
11 administering the fund. The first thirty-two thousand dollars $32,000
12 received PURSUANT TO SECTION 28-2442 shall be reimbursed to the person or
13 entity that paid the implementation fee. Monies in the fund are
14 continuously appropriated. The director shall annually distribute monies
15 in the fund.
16 B. [SUBJECT TO LEGISLATIVE APPROPRIATION OR THE AWARD OF FEDERAL
17 GRANTS, OR A COMBINATION OF APPROPRIATIONS AND GRANTS, IN AN AMOUNT OF AT
18 LEAST $5,000,000 FOR THE PURPOSES OF CHILDHOOD CANCER AND RARE CHILDHOOD
19 DISEASE RESEARCH,] the director shall allocate monies from the fund to
20 health care providers and research institutions that are located in this
21 state, that are nonprofit organizations and that are engaged in phase I
22 clinical trials relating to research on pediatric cancer or other rare
23 pediatric diseases. The monies may be used in a collaborative study or
24 research program with other facilities outside of this state. Nonprofit
25 organizations receiving monies from the fund shall use the monies for the
26 purposes prescribed in this subsection even if the monies are aggregated
27 with other monies FOR THE GRANTS AWARDED BY THE CHILDHOOD CANCER AND RARE
28 CHILDHOOD DISEASE RESEARCH COMMISSION ESTABLISHED BY SECTION 36-121.01.

19 Sec. 2. Title 36, chapter 1, article 1, Arizona Revised Statutes,
20 is amended by adding section 36-121.01, to read:
21 36-121.01. Childhood cancer and rare childhood disease
22 research commission; membership; reimbursement;
23 conflict of interest; annual report
24 A. [SUBJECT TO THE CONDITIONS SPECIFIED IN SECTION 36-121,
25 SUBSECTION B,] THE CHILDHOOD CANCER AND RARE CHILDHOOD DISEASE RESEARCH
26 COMMISSION IS ESTABLISHED CONSISTING OF AT LEAST THE FOLLOWING MEMBERS WHO
27 ARE APPOINTED BY THE DIRECTOR:
28 1. ONE MEMBER WHO IS A SURVIVOR OF CHILDHOOD CANCER OR A CAREGIVER
29 TO INDIVIDUALS WHO HAVE CHILDHOOD CANCER.
30 2. ONE MEMBER WHO IS A SURVIVOR OF A RARE CHILDHOOD DISEASE OR A
31 CAREGIVER TO INDIVIDUALS WHO HAVE RARE CHILDHOOD DISEASES.
32 3. FOUR HEALTH PROFESSIONALS WHO PRACTICE IN THE FIELD OF EITHER
33 PEDIATRIC CANCER OR RARE CHILDHOOD DISEASES.
34 4. ONE MEMBER WHO REPRESENTS A NATIONWIDE PEDIATRIC CANCER ADVOCACY
35 NONPROFIT ORGANIZATION.
36 B. COMMISSION MEMBERS SHALL SERVE FIVE-YEAR TERMS AND SHALL
37 ANNUALLY ELECT A CHAIRPERSON FROM THE MEMBERSHIP. COMMISSION MEMBERS ARE
38 NOT ELIGIBLE TO RECEIVE COMPENSATION BUT ARE ELIGIBLE FOR REIMBURSEMENT OF
39 EXPENSES PURSUANT TO TITLE 38, CHAPTER 4, ARTICLE 2.
40 C. THE COMMISSION SHALL[:
41 1. ESTABLISH CRITERIA FOR THE CLINICAL TRIALS THAT QUALIFY TO
42 RECEIVE RESEARCH GRANTS.
43 2. OVERSEE THE APPLICATION PROCESS AND REVIEW APPLICATIONS FOR THE
44 CLINICAL TRIAL RESEARCH GRANTS TO ASSIST THE DIRECTOR IN SELECTING THE
45 MOST CREDIBLE CLINICAL TRIALS TO WHICH TO AWARD THE RESEARCH GRANTS.
46 3. ENSURE THAT ALL COMMISSION MEETINGS ARE OPEN TO THE PUBLIC AND
47 ALLOW FOR PUBLIC TESTIMONY.
