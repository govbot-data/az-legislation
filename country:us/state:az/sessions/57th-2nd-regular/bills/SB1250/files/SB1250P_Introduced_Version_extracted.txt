SB1250 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; State Route 87 State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SB 1250 Introduced by Senator Shope AN ACT Appropriating monies to the department of transportation. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; department of transportation; State Route 87 turn lane The sum of $6,406,300 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the department of transportation to construct a left turn lane at the intersection of southbound State Route 87 and East Malcolm X Street.
