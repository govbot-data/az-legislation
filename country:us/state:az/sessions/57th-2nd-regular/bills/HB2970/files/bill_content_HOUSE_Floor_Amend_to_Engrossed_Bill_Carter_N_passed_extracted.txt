ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2970: fraudulent schemes; artifices; jurisdiction
CARTER N FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Specifies that in a prosecution for fraudulent schemes and artifices, the state
is only required to establish that one of the acts which constitute the
violation occurred within Arizona or one of its subdivisions.
