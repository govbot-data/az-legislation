2 Section 1. Title 20, chapter 4, article 3, Arizona Revised
3 Statutes, is amended by adding section 20-841.14, to read:
4 20-841.14. Breast examinations; cost sharing; exception;
5 definitions
6 A. A HOSPITAL SERVICE CORPORATION OR MEDICAL SERVICE CORPORATION
7 THAT ISSUES, AMENDS, DELIVERS OR RENEWS A SUBSCRIPTION CONTRACT ON OR
8 AFTER JANUARY 1, 2027 MAY NOT IMPOSE COST SHARING REQUIREMENTS FOR
9 [DIAGNOSTIC BREAST EXAMINATIONS AND SUPPLEMENTAL BREAST EXAMINATIONS]
10 [PREVENTATIVE SCREENING SERVICES FOR BREAST CANCER OR FOR ADDITIONAL
11 SCREENING SERVICES REQUIRED TO COMPLETE THE SCREENING CONTINUUM].
12 B. IF A SUBSCRIPTION CONTRACT INCLUDES A HIGH DEDUCTIBLE HEALTH
13 PLAN AS DEFINED IN 26 UNITED STATES CODE SECTION 223(c)(2)(A), SUBSECTION
14 A OF THIS SECTION APPLIES AFTER THE DEDUCTIBLE IS MET, EXCEPT THAT IF THE
15 ITEMS OR SERVICES ARE PREVENTIVE AS PRESCRIBED IN 26 UNITED STATES CODE
16 SECTION 223(c)(2)(C), SUBSECTION A OF THIS SECTION APPLIES REGARDLESS OF
17 WHETHER THE MINIMUM DEDUCTIBLE UNDER 26 UNITED STATES CODE SECTION 223 HAS
18 BEEN MET.
19 C. FOR THE PURPOSES OF THIS SECTION:
20 [1. "ADDITIONAL SCREENING SERVICES" INCLUDES A DIAGNOSTIC BREAST
21 EXAMINATION AND A SUPPLEMENTAL BREAST EXAMINATION.]
22 [1.][2.] "COST SHARING" MEANS A DEDUCTIBLE, COINSURANCE, COPAYMENT
23 OR MAXIMUM LIMITATION ON THE APPLICATION OF THE DEDUCTIBLE, COINSURANCE,
24 COPAYMENT OR SIMILAR OUT-OF-POCKET EXPENSE.
25 [2.][3.] "DIAGNOSTIC BREAST EXAMINATION":
26 (a) MEANS A MEDICALLY NECESSARY AND APPROPRIATE EXAMINATION OF THE
27 BREAST IN ACCORDANCE WITH THE NATIONAL COMPREHENSIVE CANCER NETWORK
28 GUIDELINES.
