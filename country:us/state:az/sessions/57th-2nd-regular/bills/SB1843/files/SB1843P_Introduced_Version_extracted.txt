SB1843 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: management; state properties; 2026-2027 State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SB 1843 Introduced by Senators Farnsworth: Kavanagh (with permission of Committee on Rules) AN ACT establishing rental rates for state-owned buildings; relating to the management of state properties. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Rental rates; state-owned buildings; fiscal year 2026-2027 Notwithstanding section 41-792.01, subsection D, Arizona Revised Statutes, the capital outlay stabilization fund rental rates for state-owned buildings in fiscal year 2026-2027 are $17.87 per square foot for office space and $6.43 per square foot for storage space.
