Bill Number: H.B. 2032
Angius Floor Amendment
Reference to: House engrossed bill
Amendment drafted by: Leg. Council
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
1. Prohibits any testing window for the administration of the statewide assessment from being longer
than six, rather than four, consecutive school weeks.
2. Prohibits the statewide assessment testing window for students in grades three through eight from
beginning before the second week, rather than the fourth week, of April.
