SB1245 - 572R - S Ver Senate Engrossed appropriation; ADOT; Rodeo Road State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SENATE BILL 1245 AN ACT Appropriating monies to the department of TRANSPORTATION. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; department of transportation; Rodeo Road; pavement replacement; trail connector project The sum of $2,457,100 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the department of transportation to distribute to the city of Williams for the Rodeo Road pavement replacement and trail connector project.
