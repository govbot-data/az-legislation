HB2287 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; N9073 Rockfall/Roadway project State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HB 2287 Introduced by Representative Tsosie AN ACT appropriating monies to the department of transportation. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; department of transportation; N9073 Rockfall/Roadway project; exemption A. The sum of $4,000,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the department of transportation to distribute to the Navajo department of transportation for engineering services and early-stage construction of mitigations for the N9073 Rockfall/Roadway project. B. The appropriation made in subsection A of this section is exempt from the provisions of section 35-190, Arizona Revised Statutes, relating to lapsing of appropriations.
