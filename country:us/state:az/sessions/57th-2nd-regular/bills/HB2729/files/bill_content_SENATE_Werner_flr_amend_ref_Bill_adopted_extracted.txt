Werner Floor Amendment
Reference to: House engrossed bill
Amendment drafted by: Leg. Council
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
1. Continues the Department of Economic Security, the Board of Occupational Therapy Examiners
and the Arizona Regulatory Board of Physician Assistants for four years.
2. Continues the Arizona State Board of Pharmacy for six years.
