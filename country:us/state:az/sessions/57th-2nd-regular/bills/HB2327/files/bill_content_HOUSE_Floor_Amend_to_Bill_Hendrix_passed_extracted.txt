ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2327: identifying information; records; confidentiality
HENDRIX FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Specifies that the definition of identifying information does not include the
following:
a. a parcel number;
b. the legal description of a property;
c. a property address; or
d. any other property identifier.
