17 36-2903.19. Presumptive eligibility; limits; standards;
18 notification; training
19 A. THE ADMINISTRATION SHALL REQUEST APPROVAL FROM THE CENTERS FOR
20 MEDICARE AND MEDICAID SERVICES FOR A SECTION 1115 WAIVER TO ALLOW THE
21 ADMINISTRATION TO ELIMINATE MANDATORY HOSPITAL PRESUMPTIVE ELIGIBILITY AND
22 RESTRICT PRESUMPTIVE ELIGIBILITY DETERMINATIONS TO CHILDREN AND PREGNANT
23 WOMEN ELIGIBILITY GROUPS. IF APPROVAL FOR THE SECTION 1115 WAIVER IS
24 DENIED, THE ADMINISTRATION SHALL RESUBMIT A SUBSEQUENT REQUEST FOR
25 APPROVAL WITHIN TWELVE MONTHS AFTER EACH DENIAL.
26 B. UNLESS REQUIRED BY FEDERAL LAW, THE ADMINISTRATION MAY NOT
27 DESIGNATE ITSELF AS A QUALIFIED HEALTH ENTITY FOR THE PURPOSE OF MAKING
28 PRESUMPTIVE ELIGIBILITY DETERMINATIONS OR FOR ANY PURPOSE NOT EXPRESSLY
29 AUTHORIZED BY STATE LAW.
30 C. WHEN MAKING PRESUMPTIVE ELIGIBILITY DETERMINATIONS, A QUALIFIED
31 HOSPITAL SHALL DO ALL OF THE FOLLOWING:
32 1. NOTIFY THE ADMINISTRATION OF EACH PRESUMPTIVE ELIGIBILITY
33 DETERMINATION WITHIN FIVE WORKING DAYS AFTER THE DATE THE DETERMINATION IS
34 MADE.
35 2. ASSIST INDIVIDUALS WHO ARE DETERMINED PRESUMPTIVELY ELIGIBLE
36 UNDER THE SYSTEM WITH COMPLETING AND SUBMITTING A FULL APPLICATION FOR
37 SYSTEM ELIGIBILITY.
38 3. NOTIFY EACH APPLICANT IN WRITING AND ON ALL RELEVANT FORMS WITH
39 PLAIN LANGUAGE AND LARGE PRINT THAT IF THE APPLICANT DOES NOT FILE A FULL
40 APPLICATION FOR SYSTEM ELIGIBILITY WITH THE ADMINISTRATION BEFORE THE LAST
41 DAY OF THE FOLLOWING MONTH, PRESUMPTIVE ELIGIBILITY COVERAGE WILL END ON
42 THE LAST DAY OF THE FOLLOWING MONTH.
43 4. NOTIFY EACH APPLICANT THAT IF THE APPLICANT FILES A FULL
44 APPLICATION FOR SYSTEM ELIGIBILITY WITH THE ADMINISTRATION BEFORE THE LAST
45 DAY OF THE FOLLOWING MONTH, PRESUMPTIVE ELIGIBILITY COVERAGE WILL CONTINUE
46 UNTIL AN ELIGIBILITY DETERMINATION IS MADE ON THE APPLICATION THAT IS
47 FILED.
