HB2892 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; rural bus soil stabilization State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HB 2892 Introduced by Representative Tsosie AN ACT Appropriating monies to the Department of TRANSPORTATION. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; department of transportation; Apache county; rural school bus route enhancement and soil stabilization project The sum of $665,500 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the department of transportation to distribute to Apache county for the rural school bus route enhancement and soil stabilization project.
