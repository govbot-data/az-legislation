ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
SB 1335: water banking; annual report; posting
GRIFFIN FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Expands requirements of the ADWR Director (Director) when issuing
groundwater savings credits to include:
a. utilizing of a notice of intent that pledges groundwater savings credits
to a designation of assured water supply within two years after the
date the credits are issued; and
b. allowing the holder of an Irrigation Grandfathered Right (IGFR), if
receiving written confirmation of relinquishment of the IGFR, to
continue to irrigate the land consistent with a temporary permit to
irrigate with groundwater issued by the Director.
2. Allows a person that applies to relinquish an IGFR in exchange for
groundwater savings credits to accompany the application with a request
that the Director issue a temporary permit to irrigate with groundwater on
relinquishment of the IGFR.
3. Requires the Director issue to the holder of the groundwater savings credit a
temporary permit to irrigate with groundwater, if certain conditions are met.
4. Outlines requirements of a holder of a temporary permit to irrigate with
groundwater.
5. Requires the Director, upon application from a holder of groundwater savings
credits issued prior to the effective date of this legislation, to issue a holder of
groundwater savings credits a temporary permit to irrigate with groundwater
that allows the use of groundwater subject to existing regulations.
6. Prohibits the Director from issuing a temporary permit to irrigate with
groundwater to a holder of groundwater savings credits that were issued
prior to the effective date of this legislation if any outlined criteria are met.
7. Repeals the retroactive issuing ability of the Director on January 1, 2029.
8. Makes technical and conforming changes.
