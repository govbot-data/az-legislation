2 "Section 1. Section 41-1733, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended
3 to read:
4 41-1733. School safety interoperability fund; school safety
5 program; annual report; testing
6 A. The school safety interoperability fund is established
7 consisting of monies appropriated to the fund by the legislature. The
8 department of administration shall administer the fund. Monies in the
9 fund are continuously appropriated. Monies in the fund shall be
10 distributed to the sheriff of a county or a city or town police department
11 that establishes a school safety program and may be used only for a school
12 safety program that meets all of the following:
13 1. Encompasses schools throughout this state.
14 2. In a school safety program county, CITY OR TOWN, enables the
15 deployment of a secure, multimedia data communications system to a user
16 base consisting of public safety agencies and public schools providing
17 instruction in any combination of kindergarten programs and grades one
18 through twelve.
19 3. Provides a communications solution environment that allows for:
20 (a) Identifying system users' identity, location and operational
21 status during an incident.
22 (b) Secure text messaging and file sharing to all users involved in
23 DURING an incident.
24 (c) Secure sharing of collaborative maps, building floor plans and
25 images between schools and public safety agencies.
26 (d) Integrating manually activated panic alarm systems that, when
27 activated, establish direct collaboration between schools and public
28 safety agencies.
29 (e) Using multiple forms of real-time communications and
30 information collaboration, including voice and, IF FEASIBLE, full-motion
31 video sharing, during an incident.
32 4. Is capable of being deployed to end users on existing
33 communications assets owned by participating entities.
34 5. Allows each participating entity to maintain discretionary
35 real-time control of all communications assets owned or operated by the
36 entity.
37 6. Encrypts all media communications.
38 7. Is certified under the United States department of homeland
39 security safety act as qualified anti-terrorism technology.
