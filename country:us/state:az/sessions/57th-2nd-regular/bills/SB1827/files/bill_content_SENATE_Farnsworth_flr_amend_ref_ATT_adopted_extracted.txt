Amendment drafted by: Leg. Council
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
• Removes the requirement that the Office of Advanced Air Mobility contract with qualified public
or private entities for services relating to submissions to the Federal Aviation Administration of
flight plans or related items for the use of advanced air mobility in Arizona.
