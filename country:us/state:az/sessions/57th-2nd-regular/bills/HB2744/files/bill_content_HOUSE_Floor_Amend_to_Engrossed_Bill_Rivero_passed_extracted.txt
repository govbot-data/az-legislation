ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2744: industrial commission; wages; adjudication
RIVERO FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Removes the ICA's authority to enforce their determinations in an overtime
wage adjudication case.
2. Modifies the definition of overtime wage.
3. Makes a clarifying change.

2 Section 1. Title 23, chapter 1, article 1, Arizona Revised

4 23-111. Commission; investigation; overtime wage; employers;
5 rules; definitions
6 A. THE COMMISSION MAY INVESTIGATE AND ADJUDICATE MATTERS THAT ARE
7 RELATED TO OVERTIME WAGE PAY VIOLATIONS.
8 B. AN EMPLOYEE SHALL FILE A COMPLAINT WITH THE COMMISSION NOT MORE
9 THAN ONE YEAR AFTER THE ALLEGED VIOLATION OCCURRED.
10 C. THE COMMISSION SHALL ADOPT RULES THAT PROVIDE ALL OF THE
11 FOLLOWING:
12 1. THE PROCESS ON HOW AN EMPLOYEE OR THE EMPLOYEE'S AUTHORIZED
13 REPRESENTATIVE SHALL FILE A COMPLAINT.
14 2. HOW AN EMPLOYER WILL RECEIVE NOTICE OF THE COMPLAINT FROM THE
15 COMMISSION.
16 3. THE TIME FRAMES RELATED TO RESPONSE PERIODS.
17 4. THE COMMISSION'S INVESTIGATION PROCEDURES.
18 D. THE COMMISSION MAY DO ANY OF THE FOLLOWING IN AN OVERTIME WAGE
19 ADJUDICATION CASE:
20 1. ADMINISTER OATHS AND AFFIRMATIONS.
21 2. ISSUE SUBPOENAS.
22 3. COMPEL THE ATTENDANCE OF WITNESSES.
23 4. COMPEL THE PRODUCTION OF RECORDS.
24 5. CONDUCT HEARINGS UNDER OATH.
25 6. ISSUE DETERMINATIONS [PURSUANT TO SECTION 44-1201] THAT INCLUDE
26 AN ORDER FOR THE PAYMENT OF WAGES THAT ARE OWED, INCLUDING INTEREST.
27 [7. ENFORCE OF THE COMMISSION'S DETERMINATIONS.]

House Amendments to H.B. 2744
1 E. THIS SECTION DOES NOT PREVENT AN EMPLOYEE FROM SEEKING OTHER
2 REMEDIES AT LAW.
3 F. FOR THE PURPOSES OF THIS SECTION:
4 1. "COMMISSION" MEANS THE INDUSTRIAL COMMISSION OF ARIZONA.
5 2. "EMPLOYEE" HAS THE SAME MEANING PRESCRIBED IN SECTION 23-493.
6 3. "OVERTIME WAGE":
7 (a) MEANS WAGES THAT ARE OWED FOR HOURS WORKED IN EXCESS OF THE
8 STATUTORY WORKWEEK AS DEFINED IN 29 CODE OF FEDERAL REGULATIONS SECTION
9 778.105.
10 (b) INCLUDES PREMIUM PAY [AS REQUIRED BY STATE LAW] [AS AGREED ON
11 BY THE EMPLOYEE AND EMPLOYER, BUT NOT LESS THAN ONE HUNDRED FIFTY PERCENT
12 OF THE BASE RATE OF PAY].
