ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2140: bullion depository; state monies; treasurer
FINK FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Stikes the requirement for the Department of Insurance and Financial
Institutions to adopt rules for the program.
2. Requires the State Treasurer to have ultimate operational control of the
Arizona Bullion Depository at all times.
