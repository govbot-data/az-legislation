ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2056: appropriation; brackish groundwater; feasibility study
GRIFFIN FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Removes a requirement that the study include the cost to treat and transport
any water and dispose of any brine. (Sec. 1)
2. Modifies the locations the study must be conducted in by:
a. removing Gila Bend and the West Salt River valley; and
b. adding Willcox and the Hualapai valley. (Sec. 1)
3. Requires ADWR consult with the State Land Department, local officials,
political subdivisions and property owners in or around each studied area to
identify information regarding the feasibility of constructing and operating
brackish groundwater recovery and treatment facilities. (Sec. 1)
4. Makes conforming changes. (Sec. 1)
