ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2772: license; driver; nonoperating; medical indicia
POWELL FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Requires the Arizona Department of Transportation (ADOT) to provide on
each driver license or nonoperating identification license (license) a space
where a licensee may indicate the licensee suffers from some type of adverse
medical condition using a medical indicator, rather than a medical code.
2. Removes the requirement for ADOT to prescribe by rule, a medical code to
identify the medical conditions using a system of numerals or letters
commonly accepted by the medical profession.
3. Eliminates the ability for a person, to affirmatively request in writing, that
they want the medical code as part of the computer record.
4. Tasks a person, who indicates that they have some type of adverse medical
condition using a medical indicator on their license, with affirming to ADOT
at each license renewal, that the indicator remains in effect for the licensee.
5. Mandates ADOT with providing space on a license to indicate an advanced
health care directive including a health care power of attorney, a living will
or a mental health care power of attorney.
6. Repeals the rules and requirement for ADOT to prescribe an orange symbol
to indicate a prehospital medical care directive on a license.
7. Prevents ADOT from maintaining any document relating to the adverse
medical condition in their computer records after issuing the license, except
for the purpose of indicating the symbol on the license.
8. Exempts ADOT from liability for damages from the use of indicator
information provided on a license, rather than the medical code or
prehospital medical care directive information.
9. Adds any civilian bystanders who make a good faith effort to identify a
patient and who rely on a medical indicator, rather than an orange symbol,
from exemption of liability.
10. Redefines medical or disability information.
11. Contains a legislative intent clause and a delayed effective date of January 1,
2027.
12. Makes conforming changes.
