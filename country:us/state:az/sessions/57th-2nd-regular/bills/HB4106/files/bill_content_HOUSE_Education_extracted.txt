4 CHAPTER 20
5 ONE ARIZONA SERVICE FELLOWSHIP PROGRAM
6 ARTICLE 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS
7 15-2501. Definitions
8 IN THIS CHAPTER, UNLESS THE CONTEXT OTHERWISE REQUIRES:
9 1. "EDUCATION EXPENSE" MEANS:
10 (a) TUITION OR STUDENT FEES AT AN INSTITUTION OF HIGHER EDUCATION
11 THAT PARTICIPATES IN THE FEDERAL STUDENT ASSISTANCE PROGRAMS UNDER TITLE
12 IV OF THE HIGHER EDUCATION ACT OF 1965 (20 UNITED STATES CODE SECTION 1070
13 ET SEQ.)
14 (b) REPAYMENT OF A STUDENT LOAN.
15 (c) OTHER COSTS OF ATTENDING AN INSTITUTION OF HIGHER EDUCATION AS
16 DETERMINED BY THE INSTITUTION OF HIGHER EDUCATION FOR A DEGREE OR
17 CERTIFICATE PROGRAM, INCLUDING:
18 (i) BOOKS.
19 (ii) SUPPLIES.
20 (iii) TRANSPORTATION.
21 (iv) ROOM AND BOARD.
22 2. "ELIGIBLE RECIPIENT" MEANS AN INDIVIDUAL WHO BOTH:
23 (a) IS A RESIDENT OF THIS STATE.
24 (b) SUCCESSFULLY COMPLETES A FELLOWSHIP UNDER THE PROGRAM
25 ESTABLISHED BY THIS CHAPTER.
26 3. "FEDERAL REQUIREMENTS FOR THE AMERICORPS PROGRAM" MEANS:
27 (a) RELEVANT PROVISIONS OF:

24 (a) AGREES TO SUPERVISE A PARTICIPANT FOR THE TOTAL NUMBER OF HOURS
25 OUTLINED IN AN AGREEMENT WITH THE GOVERNOR'S OFFICE OF YOUTH, FAITH AND
26 FAMILY.
27 (b) AGREES TO PROVIDE THE GOVERNOR'S OFFICE OF YOUTH, FAITH AND
28 FAMILY WITH A MATCHING STIPEND AMOUNT.
29 (c) PROVIDES A VALUABLE SERVICE TO THE COMMUNITY, AS DETERMINED BY
30 THE GOVERNOR'S OFFICE OF YOUTH, FAITH AND FAMILY.
31 8. "SUPERVISE" MEANS THE ACT OF OVERSEEING THE WORK OF A
32 PARTICIPANT, INCLUDING SOME COMPONENT OF IN-PERSON INTERACTION.
33 9. "THIRD-PARTY ADMINISTRATOR" MEANS AN ENTITY THAT:
34 (a) ENTERS INTO AN AGREEMENT WITH THE GOVERNOR'S OFFICE OF YOUTH,
35 FAITH AND FAMILY.
36 (b) IS A NONPROFIT ORGANIZATION OR A SUBSIDIARY OR AFFILIATE OF AN
37 INSTITUTION OF HIGHER EDUCATION.
38 (c) HAS EXPERIENCE MANAGING PROGRAMS AND MONIES.
39 (d) OPERATES UNDER THE DIRECTION OF THE GOVERNOR'S OFFICE OF YOUTH,
40 FAITH AND FAMILY FOR THE PURPOSES OF THE PROGRAM.
41 10. "TUITION AWARD" MEANS AN AMOUNT OF MONIES TO BE USED FOR AN
42 EDUCATION EXPENSE.
43 15-2502. One Arizona service fellowship program;
44 administration; duties; powers; rulemaking
45 A. THE ONE ARIZONA SERVICE FELLOWSHIP PROGRAM IS ESTABLISHED TO
46 PROVIDE MEANINGFUL SERVICE OPPORTUNITIES TO YOUNG ADULTS IN THIS STATE TO:

30 QUALIFIED PARTNER ORGANIZATIONS.
31 7. SHALL DETERMINE THE METRICS OF SUCCESS OF THE PROGRAM, INCLUDING
32 METRICS RELATING TO WHETHER AN ELIGIBLE RECIPIENT:
33 (i) MATRICULATES AT AN INSTITUTION OF HIGHER EDUCATION AFTER
34 COMPLETING A ONE ARIZONA SERVICE FELLOWSHIP.
35 (ii) GRADUATES FROM, OR OTHERWISE COMPLETES A PROGRAM AT, AN
36 INSTITUTION OF HIGHER EDUCATION.
37 8. SHALL MEASURE THE SUCCESS OF THE PROGRAM ACCORDING TO THE
38 METRICS DETERMINED UNDER THIS SUBSECTION.
39 9. SHALL COORDINATE WITH INSTITUTIONS OF HIGHER EDUCATION TO:
40 (i) CONNECT ELIGIBLE RECIPIENTS WITH ADDITIONAL EDUCATIONAL,
41 TRAINING, CERTIFICATION AND APPRENTICESHIP OPPORTUNITIES.
42 (ii) EXPLORE OPTIONS TO AWARD ELIGIBLE RECIPIENTS WITH ACADEMIC
43 CREDIT FOR COMPLETING A ONE ARIZONA SERVICE FELLOWSHIP, IN ADDITION TO
44 STIPENDS AND TUITION AWARDS.
45 10. MAY SOLICIT PRIVATE DONATIONS TO SUPPLEMENT THE PROGRAM,

6 15-2503. Agreements; participants; qualified partner
7 organizations
8 A. BEFORE A PARTICIPANT BEGINS PROVIDING SERVICE THROUGH THE
9 PROGRAM, THE GOVERNOR'S OFFICE OF YOUTH, FAITH AND FAMILY OR THIRD-PARTY
10 ADMINISTRATOR SHALL ENTER INTO AN AGREEMENT WITH THE PARTICIPANT THAT
11 OUTLINES THE MUTUAL EXPECTATIONS OF THE PROGRAM AND THE PARTICIPANT. THE
12 AGREEMENT SHALL DETAIL THE REQUIREMENTS OF THE PARTICIPANT, INCLUDING:
13 1. THE TOTAL NUMBER OF HOURS OF SERVICE REQUIRED UNDER THE
14 AGREEMENT.
15 2. THE EXACT STIPEND AMOUNT PROMISED TO THE PARTICIPANT IN
16 CONSIDERATION OF SERVICE AS DESCRIBED IN SECTION 15-2504.
17 3. THE EXACT TUITION AWARD AMOUNT PROMISED TO THE PARTICIPANT ON
18 SUCCESSFUL COMPLETION OF A FELLOWSHIP, AS DESCRIBED IN SECTION 15-2505.
19 4. QUALIFICATIONS FOR AND ACCEPTABLE USES OF THE TUITION AWARD, AS
20 DESCRIBED IN SECTION 15-2505.
21 5. THE CIRCUMSTANCES UNDER WHICH THE AGREEMENT MAY BE AMENDED,
22 INCLUDING FOR PARTICIPANT HARDSHIP OR COMPELLING PERSONAL CIRCUMSTANCE.
23 B. BEFORE A QUALIFIED PARTNER ORGANIZATION ACCEPTS SERVICE FROM A
24 PARTICIPANT, THE GOVERNOR'S OFFICE OF YOUTH, FAITH AND FAMILY OR
25 THIRD-PARTY ADMINISTRATOR SHALL ENTER INTO AN AGREEMENT WITH THE QUALIFIED
26 PARTNER ORGANIZATION THAT OUTLINES THE MUTUAL EXPECTATIONS OF THE PROGRAM
27 AND QUALIFIED PARTNER ORGANIZATION, INCLUDING THE EXACT AMOUNT OF MATCHING
28 MONIES THAT THE QUALIFIED PARTNER ORGANIZATION MUST PROVIDE TO THE
29 GOVERNOR'S OFFICE OF YOUTH, FAITH AND FAMILY TO CONTRIBUTE TO A
30 PARTICIPANT'S STIPEND. A QUALIFIED PARTNER ORGANIZATION SHALL AGREE TO
31 CONTRIBUTE [AN AMOUNT THAT IS EQUAL TO] AT LEAST [$5 PER HOUR TO A] [FIFTY
32 PERCENT OF THE MINIMUM WAGE ESTABLISHED PURSUANT TO SECTION 23-363
33 MULTIPLIED BY THE TOTAL NUMBER OF HOURS OF SERVICE REQUIRED UNDER THE
34 AGREEMENT FOR THE] PARTICIPANT'S STIPEND.
35 C. THE AGREEMENT DESCRIBED IN SUBSECTION A OF THIS SECTION MAY
36 INCLUDE A PROVISION THAT THE PROGRAM IS REDUCING THE QUALIFIED PARTNER
37 ORGANIZATION'S MATCHING MONIES REQUIREMENT DUE TO THE RECEIPT OF PRIVATE
38 DONATIONS AS DESCRIBED IN SECTION 15-2504, SUBSECTION C, PARAGRAPH 2,
39 SUBDIVISION (b).
40 15-2504. Stipends
41 A. THE GOVERNOR'S OFFICE OF YOUTH, FAITH AND FAMILY MAY ISSUE, AND
42 A PARTICIPANT MAY RECEIVE, A STIPEND FOR PARTICIPATING IN THE PROGRAM.
43 B. THE GOVERNOR'S OFFICE OF YOUTH, FAITH AND FAMILY OR THIRD-PARTY
44 ADMINISTRATOR SHALL ESTABLISH THE EXACT STIPEND FOR A PARTICIPANT ON A
45 CASE-BY-CASE BASIS IN AN AGREEMENT DESCRIBED IN SECTION 15-2503 BASED ON:
46 1. AVAILABLE PROGRAM MONIES.
47 2. ANY MATCHING MONIES PROVIDED BY EITHER:

19 YOUTH, FAITH AND FAMILY, WITH PREFERENCE GOING TO QUALIFIED PARTNER
20 ORGANIZATIONS THAT OFFER TO PROVIDE A LARGER STIPEND.
21 4. DISBURSE THE STIPEND TO A PARTICIPANT IN INSTALLMENTS AT LEAST

23 15-2505. Tuition awards
24 A. THE GOVERNOR'S OFFICE OF YOUTH, FAITH AND FAMILY SHALL:
25 1. PROVIDE A TUITION AWARD TO EACH ELIGIBLE RECIPIENT ON THE
26 SUCCESSFUL COMPLETION OF A FELLOWSHIP.
27 2. ESTABLISH THE EXACT TUITION AWARD FOR EACH ELIGIBLE RECIPIENT ON
28 A CASE-BY-CASE BASIS IN AN AGREEMENT DESCRIBED IN SECTION 15-2503 BASED ON
29 FEDERAL REQUIREMENTS FOR THE AMERICORPS PROGRAM, INCLUDING:
30 (a) A MAXIMUM TUITION AWARD FOR ONE THOUSAND SEVEN HUNDRED HOURS OF
31 SERVICE DURING A ONE-YEAR PERIOD.
32 (b) A REDUCED TUITION AWARD FOR A REDUCED NUMBER OF HOURS OF
33 SERVICE DURING A ONE-YEAR PERIOD.
34 B. AN ELIGIBLE RECIPIENT MAY USE A TUITION AWARD:
35 1. FOR ANY ELIGIBLE EDUCATION EXPENSE.
36 2. OVER A SEVEN-YEAR PERIOD BEGINNING THE DAY ON WHICH THE ELIGIBLE
37 RECIPIENT RECEIVES THE TUITION AWARD.
38 3. SUBJECT TO THE REQUIREMENTS OF SUBSECTION C OF THIS SECTION.
39 C. IF THE PROGRAM USES STATE MONIES TO SUPPLEMENT A TUITION AWARD:
40 1. THE GOVERNOR'S OFFICE OF YOUTH, FAITH AND FAMILY OR THIRD-PARTY
41 ADMINISTRATOR SHALL DETAIL THAT INFORMATION IN AN AGREEMENT DESCRIBED IN
42 SECTION 15-2503.
43 2. AN ELIGIBLE RECIPIENT MAY USE THE STATE-FUNDED PORTION OF THE
44 TUITION AWARD ONLY AFTER THE ELIGIBLE RECIPIENT HAS EXHAUSTED ANY
45 SCHOLARSHIP OR EDUCATION GRANT MONIES OR OTHER FINANCIAL AID.
