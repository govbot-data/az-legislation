HB2403 - 572R - H Ver House Engrossed appropriation; AHCCCS; provider increase State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HOUSE BILL 2403 AN ACT appropriating monies to the Arizona health care cost containment system. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1.1. Appropriation; Arizona health care cost containment system; provider increases The sum of $2,500,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the Arizona health care cost containment system for provider increases for home and community based services providers for the elderly and persons with physical disabilities.
