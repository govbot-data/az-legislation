15 OR COMPLAINT AGAINST THE PERSON WAS FILED AND THE CHARGES WERE DISMISSED,
16 THE PERSON WAS FOUND NOT GUILTY OR THE PERSON'S CONVICTION WAS VACATED,
17 EXCEPT THAT IF THE COMPLAINT WAS FILED IN A JUSTICE COURT AND A SUBSEQUENT
18 INFORMATION WAS FILED, THE PETITION MUST BE FILED IN THE SUPERIOR COURT.
19 3. THE COURT IN WHICH THE PERSON HAD AN INITIAL APPEARANCE IF
20 CHARGES WERE NOT FILED.
21 4. THE SUPERIOR COURT IN THE COUNTY WHERE A PERSON WAS ARRESTED IF
22 THE PERSON DID NOT HAVE AN INITIAL APPEARANCE AND NO CHARGES WERE FILED.
23 D. THE COURT SHALL REQUIRE THE PETITIONER TO ATTEST TO THE
24 FOLLOWING AND SHALL DENY A PETITION FOR EXPUNGEMENT IF THE COURT FINDS
25 THAT EITHER OF THE FOLLOWING APPLIES:
26 1. THE PETITIONER HAS PENDING OR UNRESOLVED MATTERS IN ANY COURT OR
27 JURISDICTION IN THIS STATE OR ANOTHER STATE.
28 2. THE PETITIONER HAS NOT PAID FULL RESTITUTION OR OTHER
29 COURT-ORDERED MONETARY OBLIGATIONS.
30 E. THE COURT MAY NOT GRANT OR DENY A PETITION TO EXPUNGE A PERSON'S
31 CASE RECORDS UNTIL SIXTY CALENDAR DAYS AFTER THE COURT RECEIVES THE
32 PETITION UNLESS THE COURT RECEIVES NOTICE THAT BOTH THE PROSECUTOR AND ALL
33 VICTIMS WHO HAVE MADE A REQUEST FOR POSTCONVICTION NOTICE DO NOT OBJECT TO
34 THE PETITION. UNLESS THE PETITIONER, PROSECUTOR OR VICTIM REQUESTS A
35 HEARING, THE COURT MAY GRANT OR DENY A PETITION TO EXPUNGE CASE RECORDS
36 WITHOUT A HEARING. THE COURT MAY DISMISS A PETITION THAT DOES NOT MEET
37 THE REQUIREMENTS PRESCRIBED IN THIS SECTION WITHOUT A HEARING. THE COURT
38 SHALL GRANT THE PETITION IF THE COURT DETERMINES THAT GRANTING THE
39 PETITION IS IN THE BEST INTERESTS OF THE PETITIONER AND THE PUBLIC'S
40 SAFETY. THE COURT SHALL PROVIDE A COPY OF THE PETITION TO EXPUNGE CASE
41 RECORDS TO THE PROSECUTOR. THE PROSECUTOR MAY RESPOND TO THE PETITION AND
42 REQUEST A HEARING. THE VICTIM HAS A RIGHT TO BE PRESENT AND HEARD AT ANY
43 PROCEEDING IN WHICH THE DEFENDANT HAS FILED A PETITION TO EXPUNGE CASE
44 RECORDS. IF THE VICTIM HAS MADE A REQUEST FOR POSTCONVICTION NOTICE, THE
45 PROSECUTOR SHALL PROVIDE THE VICTIM WITH NOTICE OF THE DEFENDANT'S
46 POSITION AND OF THE VICTIM'S RIGHTS UNDER THIS SECTION.

7 Sec. 2. Section 22-125, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended to
8 read:
9 22-125. Justice of the peace compensation; judicial
10 productivity credits; annual report; definitions
11 A. The annual salary of each justice of the peace is determined by
12 the total judicial productivity credits of each court as reported in
13 statistics compiled by the Arizona supreme court.
14 B. Judicial productivity credits shall be determined according to
15 the following formula:
16 1. All civil filings divided by ten equals __________ judicial
17 productivity credits.
18 2. All felony counts filed divided by ten equals __________
19 judicial productivity credits.
20 3. Except for counts described in paragraph 4 of this subsection,
21 all misdemeanor counts filed divided by ten equals __________ judicial
22 productivity credits.
23 4. All counts filed that allege a violation of title 5, chapter 3,
24 article 10, title 28, chapter 4, article 3 or section 28-8282 divided by
25 five equals __________ judicial productivity credits.
26 5. All civil traffic counts filed divided by sixty equals
27 ___________ judicial productivity credits.
28 6. All petitions for a protective order filed divided by five
29 equals ___________ judicial productivity credits.
30 7. All civil marijuana counts filed divided by sixty equals
31 ___________ judicial productivity credits.
32 8. All petitions to expunge filed pursuant to section 13-922 OR
33 36-2862 divided by sixty equals ___________ judicial productivity credits.
34 C. Beginning January 1, 2018, the Arizona supreme court shall
35 annually perform the calculations required by subsection B of this section
36 for each justice court for the previous twelve-month period ending on June
37 30 to determine the total judicial productivity credits. The Arizona
38 supreme court shall report the total judicial productivity credits for
39 each justice court to the applicable board of supervisors within one
40 hundred twenty days after the end of each twelve-month period. Any
41 adjustment to the salary of a justice of the peace is effective on the
42 following January 1.
43 D. Each justice of the peace shall be paid a percentage of the
44 salary of a superior court judge based on the following schedule:
45 1. Five hundred or more judicial productivity credits equals
46 seventy percent.
