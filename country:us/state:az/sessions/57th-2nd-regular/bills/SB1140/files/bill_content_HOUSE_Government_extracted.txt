2 Section 1. Title 13, chapter 9, Arizona Revised Statutes, is
3 amended by adding section 13-922, to read:
4 13-922. Misdemeanor records; expungement
5 A. NOTWITHSTANDING SECTION 8-348 OR 13-905, A PERSON MAY FILE A
6 PETITION WITH THE COURT FOR AN EXPUNGEMENT OF THE PERSON'S RECORD AS
7 FOLLOWS:
8 1. THREE YEARS AFTER THE DATE OF THE PERSON'S ARREST IF THE PERSON
9 WAS ARRESTED FOR A MISDEMEANOR OFFENSE AND NO CHARGES WERE FILED.
10 2. THREE YEARS AFTER THE DATE OF THE DISMISSAL IF THE PERSON WAS
11 CHARGED WITH A MISDEMEANOR OFFENSE AND THE CHARGE WAS SUBSEQUENTLY
12 DISMISSED.
13 3. THREE YEARS AFTER THE DATE OF THE NOT GUILTY VERDICT IF THE
14 PERSON WAS CHARGED WITH A MISDEMEANOR OFFENSE AND THE CHARGE RESULTED IN A
15 NOT GUILTY VERDICT.
16 4. FIVE YEARS AFTER THE DATE OF THE FULFILLMENT OF THE CONDITIONS
17 OF THE PERSON'S PROBATION OR SENTENCE AND DISCHARGE BY THE COURT IF THE
18 PERSON WAS CONVICTED OF A MISDEMEANOR OFFENSE.
19 B. THE PETITION MUST INCLUDE THE FOLLOWING:
20 1. WHETHER THE PETITIONER:
21 (a) WAS ARRESTED FOR A MISDEMEANOR OFFENSE AND NO CHARGES WERE
22 FILED.
23 (b) WAS CHARGED WITH A MISDEMEANOR OFFENSE AND THE CHARGE WAS
24 SUBSEQUENTLY DISMISSED OR RESULTED IN A NOT GUILTY VERDICT.
25 (c) WAS CONVICTED OF A MISDEMEANOR OFFENSE AND HAS FULFILLED THE
26 CONDITIONS OF SENTENCE OR ANY TERM OF PROBATION.

1 (d) HAS ANY ADDITIONAL ARRESTS OR CONVICTIONS.
2 (e) HAS OBTAINED A PRIOR EXPUNGEMENT UNDER THIS SECTION.
3 2. ANY WRITTEN RECOMMENDATION IN SUPPORT OF EXPUNGEMENT THAT IS
4 PROVIDED BY A THIRD PARTY.
5 C. THE PERSON SHALL FILE A PETITION TO EXPUNGE ALL CASE RECORDS IN
6 ONE OF THE FOLLOWING:
7 1. THE COURT IN WHICH THE PERSON WAS CONVICTED OF AN OFFENSE.
8 2. THE COURT IN WHICH AN INDICTMENT, INFORMATION, CRIMINAL CITATION
9 OR COMPLAINT AGAINST THE PERSON WAS FILED AND THE CHARGES WERE DISMISSED,
10 THE PERSON WAS FOUND NOT GUILTY OR THE PERSON'S CONVICTION WAS VACATED,
11 EXCEPT THAT IF THE COMPLAINT WAS FILED IN A JUSTICE COURT AND A SUBSEQUENT
12 INFORMATION WAS FILED, THE PETITION MUST BE FILED IN THE SUPERIOR COURT.
13 3. THE COURT IN WHICH THE PERSON HAD AN INITIAL APPEARANCE IF
14 CHARGES WERE NOT FILED.
15 4. THE SUPERIOR COURT IN THE COUNTY WHERE A PERSON WAS ARRESTED IF
16 THE PERSON DID NOT HAVE AN INITIAL APPEARANCE AND NO CHARGES WERE FILED.
17 D. THE COURT SHALL REQUIRE THE PETITIONER TO ATTEST TO THE
18 FOLLOWING AND SHALL DENY A PETITION FOR EXPUNGEMENT IF THE COURT FINDS
19 THAT EITHER OF THE FOLLOWING APPLIES:
20 1. THE PETITIONER HAS PENDING OR UNRESOLVED MATTERS IN ANY COURT OR
21 JURISDICTION IN THIS STATE OR ANOTHER STATE.
22 2. THE PETITIONER HAS NOT PAID FULL RESTITUTION OR OTHER
23 COURT-ORDERED MONETARY OBLIGATIONS.
24 E. THE COURT MAY NOT GRANT OR DENY A PETITION TO EXPUNGE A PERSON'S
25 CASE RECORDS UNTIL SIXTY CALENDAR DAYS AFTER THE COURT RECEIVES THE
26 PETITION UNLESS THE COURT RECEIVES NOTICE THAT BOTH THE PROSECUTOR AND ALL
27 VICTIMS WHO HAVE MADE A REQUEST FOR POSTCONVICTION NOTICE DO NOT OBJECT TO
28 THE PETITION. UNLESS THE PETITIONER, PROSECUTOR OR VICTIM REQUESTS A
29 HEARING, THE COURT MAY GRANT OR DENY A PETITION TO EXPUNGE CASE RECORDS
30 WITHOUT A HEARING. THE COURT MAY DISMISS A PETITION THAT DOES NOT MEET
31 THE REQUIREMENTS PRESCRIBED IN THIS SECTION WITHOUT A HEARING. THE COURT
32 SHALL GRANT THE PETITION IF THE COURT DETERMINES THAT GRANTING THE
33 PETITION IS IN THE BEST INTERESTS OF THE PETITIONER AND THE PUBLIC'S
34 SAFETY. THE COURT SHALL PROVIDE A COPY OF THE PETITION TO EXPUNGE CASE
35 RECORDS TO THE PROSECUTOR. THE PROSECUTOR MAY RESPOND TO THE PETITION AND
36 REQUEST A HEARING. THE VICTIM HAS A RIGHT TO BE PRESENT AND HEARD AT ANY
37 PROCEEDING IN WHICH THE DEFENDANT HAS FILED A PETITION TO EXPUNGE CASE
38 RECORDS. IF THE VICTIM HAS MADE A REQUEST FOR POSTCONVICTION NOTICE, THE
39 PROSECUTOR SHALL PROVIDE THE VICTIM WITH NOTICE OF THE DEFENDANT'S
40 POSITION AND OF THE VICTIM'S RIGHTS UNDER THIS SECTION.
41 F. IF THE COURT GRANTS THE PETITION FOR EXPUNGEMENT, ALL OF THE
42 FOLLOWING APPLY:
43 1. THE COURT SHALL:
44 (a) ISSUE A SIGNED ORDER EXPUNGING THE RECORD.

1 Sec. 2. Section 22-125, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended to
2 read:
3 22-125. Justice of the peace compensation; judicial
4 productivity credits; annual report; definitions
5 A. The annual salary of each justice of the peace is determined by
6 the total judicial productivity credits of each court as reported in
7 statistics compiled by the Arizona supreme court.
8 B. Judicial productivity credits shall be determined according to
9 the following formula:
10 1. All civil filings divided by ten equals __________ judicial
11 productivity credits.
12 2. All felony counts filed divided by ten equals __________
13 judicial productivity credits.
14 3. Except for counts described in paragraph 4 of this subsection,
15 all misdemeanor counts filed divided by ten equals __________ judicial
16 productivity credits.
17 4. All counts filed that allege a violation of title 5, chapter 3,
18 article 10, title 28, chapter 4, article 3 or section 28-8282 divided by
19 five equals __________ judicial productivity credits.
20 5. All civil traffic counts filed divided by sixty equals
21 ___________ judicial productivity credits.
22 6. All petitions for a protective order filed divided by five
23 equals ___________ judicial productivity credits.
24 7. All civil marijuana counts filed divided by sixty equals
25 ___________ judicial productivity credits.
26 8. All petitions to expunge filed pursuant to section 13-922 OR
27 36-2862 divided by sixty equals ___________ judicial productivity credits.
28 C. Beginning January 1, 2018, the Arizona supreme court shall
29 annually perform the calculations required by subsection B of this section
30 for each justice court for the previous twelve-month period ending on June
31 30 to determine the total judicial productivity credits. The Arizona
32 supreme court shall report the total judicial productivity credits for
33 each justice court to the applicable board of supervisors within one
34 hundred twenty days after the end of each twelve-month period. Any
35 adjustment to the salary of a justice of the peace is effective on the
36 following January 1.
37 D. Each justice of the peace shall be paid a percentage of the
38 salary of a superior court judge based on the following schedule:
39 1. Five hundred or more judicial productivity credits equals
40 seventy percent.
41 2. Two hundred or more but less than five hundred judicial
42 productivity credits equals sixty-five percent.
43 3. One hundred fifty or more but less than two hundred judicial
44 productivity credits equals fifty-five percent.
45 4. One hundred or more but less than one hundred fifty judicial
46 productivity credits equals fifty percent.
