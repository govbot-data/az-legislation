ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB2257: HOV lane; blood transport vehicles
BIASIUCCI FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Changes the percentages, from 65% to 55%, rather than from 65% to 50%, of
monies collected from the numbering of watercraft fees to be distributed into
the Watercraft Licensing Fund and from 35% to 45%, rather than from 35%
to 50%, into the State Lake Improvement Fund (SLIF) and Law Enforcement
and Boating Safety Fund (LEBSF).
2. Modifies the distribution of collected monies for the SLIF and LEBSF, from
15% to 10% for SLIF and from 85% to 90% for LEBSF.
3. Removes ADOT's reporting requirement on expenditures made from the
Watercraft Licensing Fund.
4. Distributes LEBSF monies as follows:
a. 49.22% to Mohave County;
b. 12.5% to La Paz County;
c. 10.45% to Coconino County;
d. 9.65% to Maricopa County;
e. 9.3% to Yuma County;
f. 7.35% to Gila County;
g. 1.03% to Apache County; and
h. 0.5% to Navajo County.
5. Removes the requirement that, to distribute LEBSF monies to counties, the
State Treasurer must use the Arizona Department of Game and Fish
(AZGFD) Commission's allocation formula.
6. Eliminates statutorily prescribed items that the AZGFD Commission may
use in determining the allocation formula.
7. Makes technical and conforming changes.
