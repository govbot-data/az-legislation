15 9-467. Building permits; issuance; distribution of copies;
16 state preemption; utilities; subsequent owner;
17 limitation; definitions
18 A. Any municipality requiring the issuance of a building permit
19 shall transmit one copy of the permit to the county assessor and one copy
20 to the director of the department of revenue. Permit copies shall provide
21 the permit number, issue date and parcel number. On the issuance of the
22 certificate of occupancy or the certificate of completion or on the
23 expiration or cancellation of the permit, the assessor and the department
24 of revenue shall be notified in writing or in electronic format of the
25 permit number, parcel number, issue date and completion date.
26 B. The regulation of a utility provider's authority to operate and
27 serve customers is a matter of statewide concern. The regulation of
28 building permits as it relates to a building permit applicant's ability to
29 use a utility provider that is capable and authorized to provide utility
30 service is allowed solely in accordance with subsections C and D of this
31 section. A building permit applicant's ability to use a utility provider
32 that is capable and authorized to provide utility service is not subject
33 to further regulation by a municipality.
34 C. A municipality requiring the issuance of a building permit may
35 not deny a permit application based on the utility provider proposed to
36 provide utility service to the project.
37 D. A municipality issuing a building permit shall ensure that all
38 applicable permits and associated fees assessed on a building permit
39 applicant contain requirements and amounts that do not exceed the
40 requirements and amounts for use of other utility providers and do not
41 have the effect of restricting a THE permit applicant's ability to use
42 the services of a utility provider that is capable and authorized to
43 provide utility service.
