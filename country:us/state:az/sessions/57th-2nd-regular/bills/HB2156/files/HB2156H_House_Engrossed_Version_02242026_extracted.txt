HB2156 - 572R - H Ver House Engrossed livestock compensation fund; appropriation State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HOUSE BILL 2156 AN ACT Appropriating monies to the livestock compensation fund. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1.1. Appropriation; livestock compensation fund The sum of $250,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the livestock compensation fund established by section 17-493, Arizona Revised Statutes.
