ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2023: land divisions; disclosure affidavit; recording
GRIFFIN FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Expands the information required in an affidavit of disclosure to include
whether the property is or is not located in a basin or subbasin from which
groundwater may be transported away. (Sec. 1)
