SB1156 - 572R - S Ver Senate Engrossed appropriation; short-term detention holds State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SENATE BILL 1156 AN ACT Appropriating monies to the department of public safety. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; department of public safety; state detention centers The sum of $20,000,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the department of public safety to reimburse cities, towns and counties in this state for costs associated with short-term detention holds for unauthorized aliens.
