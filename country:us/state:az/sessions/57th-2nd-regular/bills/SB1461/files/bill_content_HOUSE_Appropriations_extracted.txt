1 The bill as proposed to be amended is reprinted as follows:
2 Section 1. Appropriation; Arizona health care cost
3 containment system; allied health care workforce
4 development program; definition
5 A. The sum of [$5,000,000] [$1,000,000] is appropriated from the
6 state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the Arizona health care
7 cost containment system to establish an allied health care workforce
8 development program to develop the allied health care workforce in this
9 state.
10 B. The Arizona health care cost containment system shall distribute
11 the monies to a nonprofit organization that meets all of the following:
12 1. Is solely dedicated to training allied health care workers
13 through direct programs and education partners.
14 2. Has established partnerships with employers, education
15 institutions and public agencies.
16 3. Has graduated at least seven thousand students before the
17 effective date of this section.
18 4. Has maintained an active presence in at least eight states.
19 5. Is able to demonstrate the capacity to graduate a minimum of one
20 thousand students through the allied health care workforce development
21 program established pursuant to subsection A of this section.
22 C. For the purposes of this section, "allied health care worker"
23 means an individual who contributes to the delivery of health or related
24 services in roles that are distinct from physicians and registered nurses
25 and that typically require specialized training beyond a high school
26 diploma but less than a bachelor's degree.
