ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
SB 1713: AHCCCS; procurement; contracting
LOPEZ FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Requires each pharmacy where testing and treatment are being provided to
ensure that there is an area to provide privacy for patients.
2. Requires all Advisory Committee members to be appointed within 60 days
after the general effective date.
3. Clarifies that if some Advisory Committee members are not appointed within
60 days, the members who have been appointed can still meet and make
recommendations to the Pharmacy Board on the statewide written protocols.
4. Requires the Pharmacy Board to provide a copy of the statewide written
protocols to the chairpersons of the Health & Human Services Committee of
the House of Representatives and Arizona State Senate, or their successor
committees and post the protocols on its public website.
5. Makes technical changes.
