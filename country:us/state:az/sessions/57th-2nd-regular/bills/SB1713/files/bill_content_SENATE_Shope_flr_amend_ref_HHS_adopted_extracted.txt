FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
1. Delays, from October 1, 2026, to October 1, 2027, the deadline for the Arizona Health Care Cost
Containment System to implement the Arizona hybrid procurement model.
2. Makes technical changes.

5 36-2978. Definitions
6 IN THIS ARTICLE, UNLESS THE CONTEXT OTHERWISE REQUIRES:
7 1. "ADMINISTRATION" MEANS THE ARIZONA HEALTH CARE COST CONTAINMENT
8 SYSTEM ADMINISTRATION.
9 2. "ARIZONA HYBRID MODEL" MEANS A PERFORMANCE-BASED PROCUREMENT AND
10 CONTRACT LIFE CYCLE FRAMEWORK THAT EVALUATES CONTRACTORS BASED ON
11 OBJECTIVE, MEASURABLE OPERATIONAL PERFORMANCE OVER A DEFINED MULTI-YEAR
12 PERIOD, SUBJECT TO THRESHOLD OPERATIONAL READINESS REQUIREMENTS.
13 3. "CONTRACTING DETERMINATION" MEANS A DETERMINATION BY THE
14 ADMINISTRATION REGARDING A CONTRACTOR'S ELIGIBILITY, SELECTION,
15 CONTINUATION, EXPANSION, LIMITATION, SUSPENSION OR TERMINATION UNDER THIS
16 ARTICLE.

5 36-2978.02. Arizona hybrid model; performance-based
6 procurement framework; implementation
7 A. THE ARIZONA HYBRID MODEL SHALL INCLUDE ONLY THE FOLLOWING:
8 1. A PERFORMANCE-BASED SELECTION AND RETENTION OF CONTRACTORS USING
9 DEMONSTRATED PERFORMANCE AGAINST A PERFORMANCE RUBRIC AND THE OPERATIONAL
10 FACTORS.
11 2. AN EVALUATION OF PERFORMANCE OVER AN INITIAL DEFINED PERFORMANCE
12 AND OPERATIONS PHASE LASTING FOUR YEARS. THE RESULTS OF THE PERFORMANCE
13 AND OPERATIONS PHASE CONSTITUTE THE PRIMARY AND CONTROLLING BASIS FOR THE
14 ADMINISTRATION'S DECISIONS REGARDING CONTRACT CONTINUATION, EXPANSION,
15 LIMITATION, SUSPENSION AND TERMINATION. AFTER COMPLETING THE INITIAL
16 PERFORMANCE AND OPERATIONS PHASE, CONTRACTORS REMAIN SUBJECT TO ONGOING
17 PERFORMANCE REVIEW, MONITORING AND ENFORCEMENT BASED ON THE PERFORMANCE
18 RUBRIC AND OPERATIONAL FACTORS.
19 3. EVALUATION STANDARDS THAT ARE OBJECTIVE, MEASURABLE, PUBLISHED
20 IN ADVANCE AND APPLIED UNIFORMLY AND THAT DO NOT INCLUDE SUBJECTIVE,
21 NARRATIVE-BASED OR DISCRETIONARY EVALUATION CRITERIA.
22 4. THE ESTABLISHMENT AND ENFORCEMENT OF MINIMUM ENROLLMENT
23 VIABILITY STANDARDS AND USE OF A SINGLE MEDICAID MANAGED CARE CONTRACT
24 FRAMEWORK FOR ALL CONTRACTORS THAT SERVICE A PROGRAM, INCLUDING UNIFORM
25 PERFORMANCE EXPECTATIONS, EVALUATION STANDARDS AND RENEWAL MECHANISMS TO
26 ELIMINATE DUPLICATIVE PROCUREMENTS AND ASSOCIATED ADMINISTRATIVE BURDEN.
27 B. NOTWITHSTANDING TITLE 41, CHAPTERS 6 AND 23 OR ANY OTHER LAW TO
28 THE CONTRARY, THE ADMINISTRATION SHALL USE A MULTI-PHASE PROCUREMENT AND
29 PERFORMANCE STRUCTURE TO IMPLEMENT THE ARIZONA HYBRID MODEL, CONSISTING OF
30 ALL OF THE FOLLOWING:
31 1. ON OR BEFORE OCTOBER 1, {{2026}} {{2027}}, THE ADMINISTRATION
32 SHALL DO BOTH OF THE FOLLOWING AND MAKE THE INFORMATION PUBLICLY
33 AVAILABLE:

37 Sec. 3. Legislative findings; intent
38 A. The legislature finds that the Arizona health care cost
39 containment system should operate as a national leader in the
40 administration of medicaid managed care contracting and that competition
41 is best achieved through demonstrated performance rather than speculative
42 or proposal-based projections.

44 primarily on subjective or narrative evaluation criteria increase

46 for providers, members and this state.
