5 32-1979.06. Human immunodeficiency virus prevention;
6 preexposure prophylaxis; postexposure
7 prophylaxis; definitions
8 A. IN ADDITION TO THE AUTHORITY PROVIDED IN SECTION 32-1979.04, A
9 PHARMACIST WHO IS LICENSED PURSUANT TO THIS CHAPTER MAY INITIATE AND
10 DISPENSE HUMAN IMMUNODEFICIENCY VIRUS PREEXPOSURE PROPHYLAXIS AND
11 POSTEXPOSURE PROPHYLAXIS PURSUANT TO THE STATEWIDE WRITTEN PROTOCOL
12 APPROVED BY THE BOARD.
13 B. BEFORE INITIATING A PREEXPOSURE PROPHYLAXIS, A PHARMACIST SHALL:
14 1. CONDUCT OR OBTAIN A CLINICAL LABORATORY IMPROVEMENT
15 AMENDMENTS-WAIVED HUMAN IMMUNODEFICIENCY VIRUS TEST AND CONFIRM A NEGATIVE
16 TEST RESULT.
17 2. ASSESS THE PATIENT FOR CONTRAINDICATIONS, DRUG INTERACTIONS AND
18 CLINICAL ELIGIBILITY CONSISTENT WITH THE CURRENT GUIDELINES OF THE UNITED
19 STATES CENTERS FOR DISEASE CONTROL AND PREVENTION.
20 C. BEFORE INITIATING A POSTEXPOSURE PROPHYLAXIS, A PHARMACIST
21 SHALL:
22 1. DETERMINE THAT THE PATIENT PRESENTS WITHIN SEVENTY-TWO HOURS
23 AFTER A POTENTIAL EXPOSURE TO THE HUMAN IMMUNODEFICIENCY VIRUS.
24 2. CONDUCT OR OBTAIN A HUMAN IMMUNODEFICIENCY VIRUS TEST IF
25 FEASIBLE UNDER THE STATEWIDE WRITTEN PROTOCOL.
26 3. ASSESS THE PATIENT FOR CONTRAINDICATIONS, DRUG INTERACTIONS AND
27 CLINICAL ELIGIBILITY CONSISTENT WITH THE CURRENT GUIDELINES OF THE UNITED
28 STATES CENTERS FOR DISEASE CONTROL AND PREVENTION.
29 D. A PHARMACIST WHO INITIATES THERAPY PURSUANT TO THIS SECTION:
30 1. MAY DISPENSE NOT MORE THAN A THIRTY-DAY SUPPLY OF PREEXPOSURE
31 PROPHYLAXIS.
32 2. MAY DISPENSE A COMPLETE TWENTY-EIGHT-DAY COURSE OF POSTEXPOSURE
33 PROPHYLAXIS.
34 3. SHALL PROVIDE COUNSELING REGARDING MEDICATION ADHERENCE,
35 POTENTIAL SIDE EFFECTS AND PREVENTION MEASURES.
36 4. SHALL PROVIDE THE PATIENT WITH A WRITTEN REFERRAL TO A PRIMARY
37 CARE PROVIDER OR OTHER APPROPRIATE HEALTH CARE PROVIDER FOR FOLLOW-UP
38 CARE.
39 5. SHALL NOTIFY THE PATIENT'S PRIMARY CARE PROVIDER OF THE SERVICES
40 PROVIDED IF THE PATIENT CONSENTS TO THE NOTIFICATION.
41 E. THE STATEWIDE WRITTEN PROTOCOL APPROVED BY THE BOARD SHALL
42 ADDRESS, AT A MINIMUM, THE FOLLOWING:
43 1. MINIMUM TRAINING REQUIREMENTS FOR PHARMACISTS WHO INITIATE
44 THERAPY PURSUANT TO THIS SECTION.
45 2. DOCUMENTATION AND RECORDS RETENTION REQUIREMENTS.
