Kavanagh ADD COW Floor Amendment
Reference to: House engrossed bill
Amendment drafted by: Leg. Council
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
1. Requires the State Treasurer to only invest in safe investments that prioritize safety and principal
preservation before high returns, liquidity and yield.
2. Prohibits the State Treasurer from:
a) using any insider information obtained while performing official duties to knowingly benefit
another party or for any personal gain or for the personal gain of any family member of the
State Treasurer; and
b) investing in speculative investments.
3. Specifies that the interests of the State Treasurer's office must take precedence over any personal
interests of the State Treasurer.
4. Requires the State Treasurer to establish and oversee an investment advisory team and prescribes
investment advisory team membership.
5. Requires the investment advisory team to meet as necessary to review and make appropriate
recommendations to the State Board of Investment pertaining to investment performance and
strategies and prioritizing safety and principal preservation before liquidity and yield.
6. Adds, to the statutory restrictions on the State Treasurer's investment of public monies, a
prohibition against investments in real estate using permanent land funds or any fund administered
by the State Treasurer.
7. Requires the State Treasurer to manage the Local Government Investment Pool by using only
employees of the State Treasurer’s Office and allows the State Treasurer to confer with the State
Board of Investment as necessary.
8. Allows the State Treasurer to enter into a contract with a third party as a contingency if the State
Treasurer is unable to conduct their fiduciary duty only for the time period that the State Treasurer's
Office is unable to conduct such fiduciary duty.
9. Defines terms.
10. Makes technical changes.
