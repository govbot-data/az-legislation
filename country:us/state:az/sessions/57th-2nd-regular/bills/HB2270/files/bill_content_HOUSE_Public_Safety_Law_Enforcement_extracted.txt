10 Sec. 2. Section 11-441, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended to
11 read:
12 11-441. Powers and duties
13 A. The sheriff shall:
14 1. Preserve the peace.
15 2. Arrest and take before the nearest magistrate for examination
16 all persons who attempt to commit or who have committed a public offense.
17 3. Prevent and suppress all affrays, breaches of the peace, riots
18 and insurrections that may come to the knowledge of the sheriff.
19 4. Attend all courts, except justice and municipal courts, when an
20 element of danger is anticipated and attendance is requested by the
21 presiding judge and obey lawful orders and directions issued by the judge.
22 5. Take charge of and keep the county jail, including a county jail
23 under the jurisdiction of a county jail district, and the prisoners in the
24 county jail.
25 6. Endorse on all process and notices the year, month, day, hour
26 and minute of reception and issue to the person delivering the process or
27 notice, on payment of fees, a certificate showing the names of the
28 parties, the title of the paper and the time of reception.
29 7. Serve process and notices in the manner prescribed by law and
30 certify under the sheriff's hand on the process or notices the manner and
31 time of service, or if the sheriff fails to make service, the reasons for
32 failure, and return them without delay. When returnable to another
33 county, the sheriff may enclose such process or notices in an envelope,
34 addressed to the officer from whom received, and deposit the envelope
35 postage prepaid in the post office. The return of the sheriff is prima
36 facie evidence of the facts stated in the return.
37 8. Secure, as soon as possible, the home of a deceased person
38 located outside the boundaries of an incorporated city or town if the
39 sheriff is unable to determine or locate the heirs or executor of the
40 deceased person.
41 B. The sheriff, in executing the duties prescribed in subsection A,
42 paragraphs 1 through 4 of this section, may command the aid of as many
43 inhabitants of the county as the sheriff deems necessary.
44 C. The sheriff shall conduct or coordinate within the county search
45 or rescue operations involving the life or health of any person or may
46 assist in such operations in another county at the request of that
