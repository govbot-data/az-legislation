ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
SB 1336: state land department; continuation; oversight
GRIFFIN FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Defines relevant terms.
2. Repeals the definition of mineral in current law.
3. Allows the State Land Commissioner (Commissioner) to renew an existing
production lease without public auction if the Commissioner determines
outlined criteria are met.
4. Requires a production lease renewed by the Commissioner pursuant to this
legislation to provide for a royalty rate that is adjusted according to a
market-based indexing mechanism adopted by the Commissioner.
5. Prescribes eligible characteristics of the indexing mechanism.
6. Requires the Commissioner to review the indexed royalty rate for each
renewed production lease not more than once every five years.
7. Expands the permitted minerals, to include state land reserved minerals,
that are eligible to an applicant of an exploration permit.
8. Limits the number of contiguous sections an exploration permit may apply to,
to that of nine sections.
9. Moves, from the State Land Department (SLD) to the Commissioner, the
authority to stamp each application that meets the approval requirements.
10. Designates an application for an exploration permit for a specific commodity
type as having priority over any other application for an exploration permit.
11. Modifies the definition of exploration.
12. Asserts that an exploration permit: shall be for a term of five years after the
date of issuance, can be renewed for one additional five-year period and
cannot exceed an aggregate of ten years after the date of issuance.
13. Outlines the rights given to a permittee.
14. Provides a timeline, of within 60 days before the expiration of the initial five-
year period of an exploration permit, for the filing of an application for
renewal of the exploration permit.
15. Transfers and renumbers the section of current law relating to mineral leases
on state land.

16. Modifies when the Commissioner is allowed to require an applicant for an
exploration permit to file a surety bond, from prior to the issuance of the
permit to in conjunction with issuing the permit.
17. Clarifies that a mineral permit of common variety right of entry permit that
the SLD issued before the effective date of this legislation may, at the choice
of the permittee:
a. continue in effect until the initial expiration date stated in the existing
permit; or
b. be surrendered at any time before the expiration date and remain
subject to replacement with a five-year permit.
18. Specifies the SLD's authority as the primary oversight of all lands owned by
this state, and timber, stone, gravel and other products of such lands, except
lands under the specific use and control of state institutions and the products
of such lands.
19. Mandates the SLD cooperate and seek the advice of the President of the
Arizona Senate and the Speaker of the Arizona House of Representatives on
the communication in matters involving:
a. a federal land withdrawal or reservation; or
b. the federal government's acquisition of non-federally controlled land.
20. Modifies powers and duties of the SLD.
21. Removes the establishment of the State Land Oversight Board.
22. Requires the SLD establish a procedure and criteria that the Commissioner
must use for determining whether to initiate the sale of state lands by the
Commissioner's own initiative and to post the procedure and criteria on the
SLD's website.
23. Mandates the Commissioner hire third-party contractors to appraise and
prepare for public auction.
24. Allows the SLD utilize SLD staff or a third-party contractor to satisfy notice
requirements.
25. Clarifies the above requirements to not preclude the Commissioner from
selling state lands that have not been identified for sale in the Five-Year
Disposition Plan or restrict the use of lands sold to the uses identified in the
Conceptual Land Use Plan.
26. Outlines requirements of the SLD relating to lease renewals, permit holdover
provisions and special land use permits.
27. Transfers authority for the creation of the Conceptual Land Use Plan from
the Commissioner to the SLD.
28. Includes approval of the Conceptual Land Use Plan as a requirement of the
SLD.

29. Expands characteristics to prioritize in the creation of the Conceptual Land
Use Plan to include promoting economic growth in the rural areas of the
state.
30. Requires the Commissioner issue a written order approving each Conceptual
Land Use Plan and revision.
31. Includes the Arizona Geological Survey (AZGS) in the parties to be consulted
for state trust land in the Conceptual Land Use Plan.
32. Requires the Conceptual Land Use Plan, the Commissioner's written
approval of each Conceptual Land Use Plan and any revisions on the SLD's
website.
33. Requires the Commissioner use both SLD staff and private consultants in the
creation of the Conceptual Land Use Plans.
34. Specifies that a five-year disposition plan must be created for all state trust
land in each county.
35. Modifies the definition of Conceptual Land Use Plan.
36. Modifies what the Trust Land Management Fund (Fund) consists of.
37. Expands uses of monies in the Fund.
38. Establishes the Joint Legislative Ad Hoc Committee on State Land Oversight
(Committee).
39. Outlines the Committee's membership and duties.
40. Requires the SLD and Commissioner, upon request of the cochairpersons of
the Committee, to provide any requested information and appear and testify.
41. Repeals the Committee on January 1, 2027.
42. Requires the SLD, before July 1, 2027, collaborate with stakeholders and
members of the Committee to develop procedures and criteria for
Commissioner initiated sales of state land.
43. Repeals the above requirement on January 1, 2029.
44. Outlines requirements of the Commissioner that must be completed within
two years of the effective date of this legislation.
45. Establishes a Biosolids Study Committee (Study Committee)
46. Outlines the Study Committee's membership, duties, abilities and reporting
requirements.
47. Repeals the Study Committee on July 1, 2030.
48. Modifies the legislative findings and purpose sections.
49. Makes technical and conforming changes.
