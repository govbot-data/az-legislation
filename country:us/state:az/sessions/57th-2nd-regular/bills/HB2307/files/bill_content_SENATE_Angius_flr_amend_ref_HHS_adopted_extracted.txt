Bill Number: H.B. 2307
Angius Floor Amendment
Reference to: HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES
Committee amendment
Amendment drafted by: Leg. Council
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
1. Requires the Department of Health Services (DHS) to pay all costs of nonpsychiatric medical
services provided to a dangerous, incompetent and nonrestorable defendant placed at the Arizona
State Hospital (ASH) forensic campus, rather than the defendant's originating county.
2. Reduces, from three to one, the maximum number of defendants who may be placed at ASH and
requires the defendant to originate from a county with a population of less than 400,000 persons.
3. Requires, the Superintendent of ASH to inform each county's presiding judge, county attorney and
chairperson of the board of supervisors that ASH may not accept additional defendants after
reaching, or when expected to reach, its maximum funded capacity.
4. Specifies that DHS is not required to reimburse a county for costs associated with deferred ASH
admission for a person found guilty except insane if the deferral is due to current placement of a
dangerous, incompetent and nonrestorable defendant.
5. Adds two representatives of a statewide association representing county supervisors to the
membership of the Study Committee on Secure State Mental Health Facilities and modifies
recommendation requirements.
