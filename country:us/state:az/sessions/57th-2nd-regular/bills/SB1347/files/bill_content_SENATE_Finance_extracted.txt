2 Section 1. Title 20, chapter 4, article 3, Arizona Revised
3 Statutes, is amended by adding section 20-841.14, to read:
4 20-841.14. Fertility preservation coverage; requirements;
5 religious employer; exemption; definitions
6 A. A HOSPITAL SERVICE CORPORATION OR MEDICAL SERVICE CORPORATION
7 THAT ISSUES, AMENDS OR RENEWS A SUBSCRIPTION CONTRACT ON OR AFTER JANUARY
8 1, 2027 SHALL PROVIDE COVERAGE FOR STANDARD FERTILITY PRESERVATION
9 SERVICES TO A SUBSCRIBER WHO IS WITHIN REPRODUCTIVE AGE, WHO IS DIAGNOSED
10 WITH CANCER AND WHOSE MEDICALLY NECESSARY TREATMENT [MAY] [IS LIKELY TO]
11 DIRECTLY OR INDIRECTLY CAUSE IATROGENIC INFERTILITY.
12 B. A HOSPITAL SERVICE CORPORATION OR MEDICAL SERVICE CORPORATION
13 MAY NOT REQUIRE PREAUTHORIZATION FOR STANDARD FERTILITY PRESERVATION
14 SERVICES. A SUBSCRIPTION CONTRACT MAY CONTAIN PROVISIONS FOR MAXIMUM
15 BENEFITS AND MAY REQUIRE THAT STANDARD FERTILITY PRESERVATION SERVICES BE
16 SUBJECT TO THE SAME DEDUCTIBLES, COPAYMENTS, COINSURANCE AND REASONABLE
17 LIMITATIONS AND EXCLUSIONS THAT ARE CONSISTENT WITH THIS SECTION.
18 C. A RELIGIOUS EMPLOYER MAY SUBMIT A WRITTEN REQUEST FOR AN
19 EXEMPTION FROM THE REQUIREMENTS OF THIS SECTION TO A HOSPITAL SERVICE
20 CORPORATION OR MEDICAL SERVICE CORPORATION, AND THE HOSPITAL SERVICE
21 CORPORATION OR MEDICAL SERVICE CORPORATION SHALL GRANT THE EXEMPTION IF
22 THE COVERAGE REQUIRED BY THIS SECTION CONFLICTS WITH THE EMPLOYER'S
23 RELIGIOUS BELIEFS. A RELIGIOUS EMPLOYER THAT OBTAINS AN EXEMPTION SHALL
24 PROVIDE WRITTEN NOTICE OF THE EXEMPTION TO PROSPECTIVE SUBSCRIBERS.
25 D. THIS SECTION DOES NOT PREVENT A SUBSCRIBER FROM PURCHASING A
26 SUPPLEMENTAL INSURANCE POLICY THAT COVERS STANDARD FERTILITY PRESERVATION
27 SERVICES AT THE SUBSCRIBER'S OWN EXPENSE.
28 E. FOR THE PURPOSES OF THIS SECTION:

Senate Amendments to S.B. 1347
1 1. "IATROGENIC INFERTILITY" MEANS AN IMPAIRMENT OF FERTILITY THAT
2 IS CAUSED DIRECTLY OR INDIRECTLY BY SURGERY, CHEMOTHERAPY, RADIATION OR
3 OTHER MEDICAL TREATMENT THAT HAS A POTENTIAL SIDE EFFECT OF IMPAIRED
4 FERTILITY AS ESTABLISHED BY THE AMERICAN SOCIETY OF CLINICAL ONCOLOGY OR
5 THE AMERICAN SOCIETY FOR REPRODUCTIVE MEDICINE.
6 2. "RELIGIOUS EMPLOYER" MEANS AN EMPLOYER THAT BOTH:
7 (a) IS A CHURCH, A CONVENTION OR ASSOCIATION OF CHURCHES OR AN
8 ELEMENTARY OR SECONDARY SCHOOL THAT IS CONTROLLED, OPERATED OR PRINCIPALLY
9 SUPPORTED BY A CHURCH OR BY A CONVENTION OR ASSOCIATION OF CHURCHES.
10 (b) QUALIFIES AS A TAX-EXEMPT ORGANIZATION UNDER SECTION 501(c)(3)
11 OF THE UNITED STATES INTERNAL REVENUE CODE.
12 3. "REPRODUCTIVE AGE" MEANS THE AGE RANGE IN WHICH AN INDIVIDUAL IS
13 DEEMED FERTILE AS ESTABLISHED BY THE AMERICAN SOCIETY OF CLINICAL ONCOLOGY
14 OR THE AMERICAN SOCIETY FOR REPRODUCTIVE MEDICINE.
15 4. "STANDARD FERTILITY PRESERVATION SERVICES":
16 (a) MEANS:
17 (i) OOCYTE AND SPERM PRESERVATION PROCEDURES THAT ARE CONSISTENT
18 WITH ESTABLISHED MEDICAL PRACTICES OR PROFESSIONAL GUIDELINES PUBLISHED BY
19 THE AMERICAN SOCIETY OF CLINICAL ONCOLOGY OR THE AMERICAN SOCIETY FOR
20 REPRODUCTIVE MEDICINE.
21 (ii) THE STORAGE FOR AT LEAST THREE [PLAN] YEARS OF PRESERVED
22 OVARIAN TISSUE, SPERM AND OOCYTE.
23 (b) INCLUDES CRYOPRESERVATION OF ANY OF THE FOLLOWING:
24 (i) OVARIAN TISSUE.
25 (ii) SPERM.
26 (iii) OOCYTE.
