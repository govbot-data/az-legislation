HB2783 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: labor organizations; elections; recertification requirement State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HB 2783 Introduced by Representative Carter N AN ACT amending title 23, chapter 8, article 1, arizona revised statutes, by adding section 23-1308; relating to labor relations. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Title 23, chapter 8, article 1, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended by adding section 23-1308, to read: START_STATUTE23-1308. Labor organization; recertification; annual basis; requirement; definition a. A labor organization that is organized in this state shall recertify its status as a labor organization on an annual basis. b. For the purposes of this section, "recertify" means the process where employees in an established bargaining agreement participate in a formal vote to reaffirm whether the employees desire for their current labor organization to act as their exclusive bargaining representative. END_STATUTE
