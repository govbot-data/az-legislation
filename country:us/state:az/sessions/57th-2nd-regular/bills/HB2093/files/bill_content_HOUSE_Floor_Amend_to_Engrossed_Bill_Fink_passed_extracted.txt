ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2093: mental health instruction requirement; repeal
FINK FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Delays the repeal of statute that requires the State Board of Education to
require that health education instruction include mental health instruction to
January 1, 2037.
2. Deletes language that requires mental health instruction incorporate the
multiple dimensions of health by including mental health and the relationship
of physical and mental health to enhance student understanding, social and
emotional learning, attitudes and behavior that promote health and well-
being.
