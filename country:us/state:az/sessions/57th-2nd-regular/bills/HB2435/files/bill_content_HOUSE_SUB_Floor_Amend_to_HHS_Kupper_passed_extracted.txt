ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2435: internationally trained physicians; licensure
KUPPER FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Incorporates the HHS Committee amendment.
2. Defines the phrase indirect supervision with direct supervision immediately
available to mean the supervising physician is physically present within the
hospital or other eligible entity site of patient care and is immediately available
to provide direct supervision of the provisional licensee.
