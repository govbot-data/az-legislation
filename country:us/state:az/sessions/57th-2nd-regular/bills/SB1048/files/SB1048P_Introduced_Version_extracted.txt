SB1048 - 572R - I Ver PREFILED    DEC 19 2025 REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; juvenile services; Coconino county State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SB 1048 Introduced by Senator Rogers AN ACT Appropriating monies to the department of administration. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; department of administration; Coconino county; juvenile services The sum of $36,000,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the department of administration for distribution to Coconino county for eighty percent of the total cost of constructing and purchasing equipment for a new juvenile court services facility and converting the existing juvenile detention center into a detox, sobriety and crisis recovery center.
