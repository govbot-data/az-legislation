ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HCR 2051: ballot measures; circulators; revenue; disclosure
CARBONE FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Removes the requirement for a paid circulator to verbally disclose their first
name upon approaching a potential petition signer.
2. Strikes the requirement for a paid circulator to have their first name and last
initial clearly displayed on a badge when approaching a potential petition
signer.
