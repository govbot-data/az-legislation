HB2519 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; water conservation grant fund State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HB 2519 Introduced by Representatives Stahl Hamilton: Garcia, Gutierrez, Sandoval, Simacek, Travers, Villegas; Senators Gabaldón, Ortiz AN ACT appropriating monies to the water conservation grant fund. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; water conservation grant fund The sum of $100,000,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the water conservation grant fund established by section 49-1331, Arizona Revised Statutes.
