LIVINGSTON FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Grants political subdivisions, beginning January 1, 2027, through January 1,
2030, the authority to consolidate existing municipal street lighting
improvement districts into a single municipal improvement district.
2. Requires the political subdivision to provide a specified notice to property
owners and allow for public comment regarding the consolidation at least 60
days prior to adopting a resolution to consolidate.
3. Directs the governing body of the political subdivision to submit a copy of the
resolution to the county recorder upon approval to consolidate the lighting
districts.
4. Specifies any municipal street lighting improvement district, including
districts that are established beginning January 1, 2027, through January 1,
2030, is eligible for consolidation by the political subdivision.
