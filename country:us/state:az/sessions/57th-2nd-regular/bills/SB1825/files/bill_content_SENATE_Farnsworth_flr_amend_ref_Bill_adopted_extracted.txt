Bill Number: S.B. 1825
Farnsworth Floor Amendment
Reference to: printed bill
Amendment drafted by: Leg. Council
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
• Requires the county board of supervisors to fill a vacancy for the office of precinct committeeman
within 30 days, rather than 5 days, after the complete and correct list of names is submitted.
