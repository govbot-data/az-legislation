4 40-113. Integrated resource plan; third-party evaluation;
5 definitions
6 A. THE COMMISSION SHALL REQUIRE AN ELECTRIC PUBLIC SERVICE
7 CORPORATION IN THIS STATE TO PREPARE AND SUBMIT AN INTEGRATED RESOURCE
8 PLAN TO THE COMMISSION FOR REVIEW AND APPROVAL AT LEAST ONCE EVERY THREE
9 YEARS. THE PLAN SHALL:
10 1. PROJECT ENERGY DEMAND FOR THE NEXT FIFTEEN YEARS, INCLUDING AT
11 LEAST THREE SCENARIOS THAT PROJECT LOW, MEDIUM AND HIGH LOAD GROWTH
12 SCENARIOS.
13 2. SUMMARIZE AND QUANTIFY ALL EXISTING ELECTRIC GENERATION PLANTS
14 THE PUBLIC SERVICE CORPORATION USES TO MEET EXISTING AND FUTURE DEMANDS,
15 LESS ANY PLANNED RETIREMENTS, AND IDENTIFY EACH ELECTRIC GENERATION PLANT
16 THAT IS SCHEDULED TO RETIRE OR THAT THE COMMISSION HAS APPROVED TO RETIRE
17 WITHIN THE NEXT FIFTEEN YEARS.
18 3. IDENTIFY ALL ELECTRIC GENERATION PLANTS THAT ARE NOT YET
19 CONSTRUCTED OR THAT ARE NOT CURRENTLY OWNED, OPERATED OR USED BY THE
20 PUBLIC SERVICE CORPORATION AND THAT COULD BE CONSTRUCTED OR COULD BE
21 PURCHASED, OPERATED OR OTHERWISE USED OR RELIED ON TO MEET THE DIFFERENCE
22 BETWEEN EXISTING GENERATING CAPACITY AND FUTURE ENERGY DEMAND IN THE NEXT
23 FIFTEEN YEARS.
24 4. ANALYZE THE TOTAL ESTIMATED CAPITAL AND OPERATING EXPENDITURES
25 FOR EACH ELECTRIC GENERATION PLANT, COMBINATION OF PLANTS AND COMBINATION
26 OF OWNERSHIP STRUCTURES THAT ARE IDENTIFIED PURSUANT TO PARAGRAPH 3 OF
27 THIS SUBSECTION OVER THE USEFUL LIFE OF EACH PLANT OR COMBINATION OF
28 PLANTS.

1 2. "RATEPAYER IMPACT MEASURE TEST" MEANS A TEST THAT MEASURES THE
2 CHANGE IN CUSTOMER BILLS OR RATES DUE TO CHANGES IN THE UTILITY'S CAPITAL
3 AND OPERATING EXPENSES CAUSED BY THE UTILITY'S INVESTMENT OR PROCUREMENT
4 DECISIONS IN A PARTICULAR ELECTRIC GENERATION PLANT, COMBINATION OF PLANTS

6 40-114. Request for proposals; requirements
7 [A. IN ADDITION TO ANY OTHER REQUIREMENTS IMPOSED BY THE COMMISSION
8 BY RULE, BEFORE ISSUING A REQUEST FOR PROPOSALS FOR THE GENERATION OF
9 ELECTRICITY, AN ELECTRIC PUBLIC SERVICE CORPORATION SHALL PROVIDE THE
10 REQUEST FOR PROPOSALS TO THE COMMISSION FOR APPROVAL.
11 B. A REQUEST FOR PROPOSALS SHALL BE COMPETITIVE, TECHNOLOGY
12 AGNOSTIC AND OPEN TO ALL TYPES OF GENERATION AND MAY NOT INCLUDE ANY
13 CRITERIA THAT WOULD DISCRIMINATE AGAINST A RESOURCE ON THE BASIS OF CARBON
14 EMISSIONS.
15 C. TO THE MAXIMUM DEGREE PRACTICABLE, A REQUEST FOR PROPOSALS SHALL
16 PRIORITIZE RELIABILITY AND AFFORDABILITY AND BE DESIGNED TO RESULT IN
17 CONTRACTS FOR RESOURCES THAT PROVIDE RELIABLE ELECTRICITY AT THE LOWEST
18 COST.
19 D. THE COMMISSION SHALL APPOINT AN INDEPENDENT MONITOR TO OVERSEE
20 EACH REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL TO ENSURE COMPLIANCE WITH THE REQUIREMENTS OF
21 THIS SECTION AND ANY OTHER REQUIREMENTS IMPOSED BY THE COMMISSION BY RULE.
22 E. AN ELECTRIC PUBLIC SERVICE CORPORATION SHALL REPORT TO THE
23 COMMISSION THE RESULT OF A REQUEST FOR PROPOSALS INCLUDING THE RESOURCES
24 THE ELECTRIC SERVICE CORPORATION CONTRACTED AND EVIDENCE SUPPORTING THAT
25 RESOURCE OR THE RESOURCES BEING SELECTED INSTEAD OF OTHER PROPOSED
26 RESOURCES.]
