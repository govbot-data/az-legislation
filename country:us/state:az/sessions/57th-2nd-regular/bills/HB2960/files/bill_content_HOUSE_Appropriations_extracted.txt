8 A. THE VETERANS' SPECIALTY COURT GRANT PROGRAM IS ESTABLISHED FOR
9 MUNICIPALITIES AND COUNTIES IN THIS STATE TO ESTABLISH A VETERANS' COURT
10 PROGRAM. THE [ATTORNEY GENERAL] [ADMINISTRATIVE OFFICE OF THE COURTS]
11 SHALL ADMINISTER THE VETERANS' SPECIALTY COURT GRANT PROGRAM. THE
12 [ATTORNEY GENERAL] [SUPREME COURT] SHALL ADOPT RULES [AS NECESSARY] AND
13 ESTABLISH [THE] CRITERIA [NECESSARY] TO IMPLEMENT THIS SECTION. [ANY
14 VETERANS' COURT PROGRAM ESTABLISHED PURSUANT TO THIS SECTION MAY NOT
15 DUPLICATE, DISRUPT OR INTERFERE WITH A VETERANS COURT ESTABLISHED PURSUANT
16 TO SECTION 22-601 OR ANY OTHER COURT PROGRAMS FOR VETERANS.][GRANT MONIES
17 MAY BE USED TO ESTABLISH NEW VETERANS' COURT PROGRAMS AND TO EXPAND AND
18 ENHANCE EXISTING VETERANS' COURT PROGRAMS ESTABLISHED PURSUANT TO SECTION
19 22-601.]
20 B. THE VETERANS' SPECIALTY COURT GRANT PROGRAM SHALL:
21 1. SUPPORT THE REHABILITATION AND REINTEGRATION OF VETERANS AS
22 CONTRIBUTING MEMBERS OF THEIR COMMUNITIES.
23 2. PROMOTE COLLABORATION AMONG PROSECUTORS, DEFENSE COUNSELS,
24 JUDGES, COMMUNITY-BASED SUPPORT ORGANIZATIONS IN THIS STATE, THE UNITED
25 STATES DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS AND THE DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS'
26 SERVICES.

9 C. THE VETERANS' TREATMENT COURT FUND IS ESTABLISHED CONSISTING OF
10 LEGISLATIVE APPROPRIATIONS. THE [ATTORNEY GENERAL] [ADMINISTRATIVE OFFICE
11 OF THE COURTS] SHALL ADMINISTER THE FUND. MONIES IN THE FUND ARE
12 CONTINUOUSLY APPROPRIATED. THE FUND MAY BE USED TO PROVIDE MONIES TO
13 MUNICIPALITIES AND COUNTIES IN THIS STATE TO ESTABLISH A VETERANS' COURT
14 PROGRAM PURSUANT TO THIS SECTION [AND TO EXPAND AND ENHANCE EXISTING
15 VETERANS' COURT PROGRAMS ESTABLISHED PURSUANT TO SECTION 22-601].
16 APPLICANTS SHALL SUBMIT AN APPLICATION ON A FORM PRESCRIBED BY THE
17 [ATTORNEY GENERAL] [ADMINISTRATIVE OFFICE OF THE COURTS]. FUND MONIES
18 SHALL BE AWARDED ANNUALLY TO GRANTEES AND SHALL BE USED FOR ANY RELEVANT
19 ADMINISTRATIVE COSTS, INCLUDING HIRING PERSONNEL TO ESTABLISH, MAINTAIN OR
20 ENHANCE A VETERANS' COURT PROGRAM.
21 D. THE [ATTORNEY GENERAL] [ADMINISTRATIVE OFFICE OF THE COURTS]
22 SHALL COLLABORATE WITH COUNTY ATTORNEYS, MUNICIPAL PROSECUTORS, PUBLIC
23 DEFENDERS [, THE ADMINISTRATIVE OFFICE OF THE COURTS] AND ANY RELEVANT
24 STAKEHOLDERS TO ESTABLISH THE BEST PRACTICES AND STANDARDS FOR THE
25 ADMINISTRATION OF VETERANS' COURT PROGRAMS AND DATA COLLECTION IN THIS
26 STATE.
27 E. ON OR BEFORE DECEMBER 31 OF EACH YEAR, EACH GRANTEE SHALL SUBMIT
28 A REPORT TO THE [ATTORNEY GENERAL] [ADMINISTRATIVE OFFICE OF THE COURTS]
29 THAT INCLUDES ALL OF THE FOLLOWING:
30 1. THE NUMBER OF VETERANS SERVED, SEPARATED BY TYPE OF OFFENSE,
31 DEMOGRAPHICS AND MILITARY SERVICE HISTORY.
32 2. ARREST AND RECIDIVISM RATES AMONG VETERANS' COURT PROGRAM
33 PARTICIPANTS.
34 3. VETERANS' COURT PROGRAM COMPLETION RATES AND PARTICIPANT
35 OUTCOMES.
36 4. USE OF MENTAL HEALTH AND SUBSTANCE ABUSE TREATMENT SERVICES.
37 5. RECOMMENDATIONS FOR VETERANS' COURT PROGRAM AND VETERANS'
38 SPECIALTY COURT GRANT PROGRAM IMPROVEMENT.
39 F. ON OR BEFORE MARCH 1 OF EACH YEAR, THE [ATTORNEY GENERAL]
40 [ADMINISTRATIVE OFFICE OF THE COURTS] SHALL SUBMIT A COMPREHENSIVE ANNUAL
41 REPORT TO THE GOVERNOR, THE PRESIDENT OF THE SENATE AND THE SPEAKER OF THE
42 HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES THAT ANALYZES THE DATA SUBMITTED PURSUANT TO

45 1. STATEWIDE DATA ON VETERANS' COURT PROGRAMS THAT INCORPORATE
