SB1022 - 572R - I Ver PREFILED    DEC 09 2025 REFERENCE TITLE: public records; exemption; photos State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SB 1022 Introduced by Senator Kavanagh AN ACT amending title 39, chapter 1, article 2, Arizona Revised Statutes, by adding section 39-130; relating to public records. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Title 39, chapter 1, article 2, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended by adding section 39-130, to read: START_STATUTE39-130. Booking photographs; photo enforcement system recordings; nondisclosure; definitions A. This chapter does not require the disclosure of a booking photograph or a photograph, video or other digital recording of a person that is used as part of a photo enforcement system. B. For the purposes of this section: 1. "Booking photograph" has the same meaning prescribed in section 44-7901. 2. "Photo enforcement system" has the same meaning prescribed in section 28-601. END_STATUTE
