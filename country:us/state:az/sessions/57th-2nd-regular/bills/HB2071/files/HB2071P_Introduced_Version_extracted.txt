HB2071 - 572R - I Ver PREFILED    DEC 16 2025 REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; Apache Junction; rural development State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HB 2071 Introduced by Representative Blackman AN ACT Appropriating monies to the state treasurer. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; state treasurer; city of Apache Junction The following amounts are appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the state treasurer to distribute to the city of Apache Junction for the following purposes: 1. $5,000,000 for support of a rural small business and workforce hub. 2. $6,000,000 for support of rural infrastructure revitalization. 3. $4,500,000 for development of a rural health and community services facility.
