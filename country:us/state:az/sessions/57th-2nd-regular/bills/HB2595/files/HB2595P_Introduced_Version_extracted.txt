HB2595 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; Coconino county; juvenile services State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HB 2595 Introduced by Representatives Peshlakai: Austin, Cavero, Connolly, Liguori, Márquez, Sandoval, Villegas AN ACT Appropriating monies to the attorney general. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; attorney general; Coconino county; juvenile services The sum of $45,000,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the attorney general for distribution to Coconino county for the purpose of constructing and purchasing equipment for a new juvenile court services facility and converting the existing juvenile detention center into a detox, sobriety and crisis recovery center.
