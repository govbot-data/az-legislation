4 41-4259. Duty to register; foreign adversary principles;
5 lobbyists; violation; classification; enforcement;
6 fees; definitions
7 A. NOTWITHSTANDING ANY OTHER LAW, A FOREIGN ADVERSARY PRINCIPAL
8 SHALL SUBMIT TO THE DEPARTMENT A COPY OF THE FOREIGN ADVERSARY PRINCIPAL'S
9 LOBBYING REGISTRATION AND A LIST OF THE FOREIGN ADVERSARY PRINCIPAL'S
10 CURRENT LOBBYISTS PURSUANT TO SECTION 41-1232.
11 B. EACH FOREIGN ADVERSARY PRINCIPAL SHALL REREGISTER NOT LATER THAN
12 5:00 P.M. ON THE SECOND MONDAY IN JANUARY OF EACH ODD-NUMBERED YEAR UNLESS
13 AT THAT TIME THE FOREIGN ADVERSARY PRINCIPAL NO LONGER ENGAGES, EMPLOYS,
14 RETAINS OR USES ANY LOBBYIST. BEGINNING DECEMBER 1 OF EACH EVEN-NUMBERED
15 YEAR, A FOREIGN ADVERSARY PRINCIPAL SHALL FILE ITS REGISTRATION AT ANY
16 TIME. EACH FOREIGN ADVERSARY PRINCIPAL SHALL AMEND ITS REGISTRATION
17 STATEMENT WITHIN FIVE BUSINESS DAYS AFTER ANY CHANGE IN THE INFORMATION
18 REQUIRED BY SUBSECTION A OF THIS SECTION.
19 C. THE DEPARTMENT SHALL POST PUBLICLY ON ITS WEBSITE A DATABASE OF
20 ALL FOREIGN PRINCIPALS AND EACH FOREIGN ADVERSARY PRINCIPAL'S LOBBYISTS IN
21 THIS STATE.
22 D. A PERSON WHO IS A DESIGNATED LOBBYIST, LOBBYIST FOR COMPENSATION
23 OR AUTHORIZED LOBBYIST FOR A FOREIGN ADVERSARY PRINCIPAL IN THIS STATE
24 SHALL DISCLOSE THAT FACT TO ALL OF THE FOLLOWING:
25 1. ANY LEGISLATOR THE PERSON IS LOBBYING FOR THE FIRST TIME OR ON
26 ANY SUBSEQUENT REQUEST OF A LEGISLATOR.

2 PERSON IS LOBBYING FOR THE PROCUREMENT OF MATERIALS, SERVICES OR

19 H. THE DEPARTMENT MAY ADOPT RULES, DEVELOP FORMS AND IMPLEMENT
20 PROCEDURES AS NECESSARY TO IMPLEMENT THIS SECTION.
21 I. EACH FOREIGN ADVERSARY PRINCIPAL THAT REGISTERS A LOBBYIST FOR
22 COMPENSATION OR A DESIGNATED LOBBYIST WHO RECEIVES COMPENSATION FOR
23 LOBBYING FROM THE FOREIGN PRINCIPAL, AT THE TIME OF REGISTERING OR
24 REREGISTERING, SHALL PAY A REGISTRATION OR REREGISTRATION FEE SET BY THE
25 DEPARTMENT. THE DEPARTMENT SHALL DEPOSIT, PURSUANT TO SECTIONS 35-146 AND
26 35-147, REGISTRATION AND REREGISTRATION FEES COLLECTED IN THE FOREIGN
27 PRINCIPAL LOBBYIST REGISTRATION FUND ESTABLISHED BY SECTION 41-4260.
28 J. FOR THE PURPOSES OF THIS SECTION:
29 1. "AUTHORIZED LOBBYIST" HAS THE SAME MEANING PRESCRIBED IN SECTION
30 41-1231.
31 2. "DESIGNATED LOBBYIST" HAS THE SAME MEANING PRESCRIBED IN SECTION
32 41-1231.
33 3. "FOREIGN ADVERSARY PRINCIPAL" MEANS ANY OF THE FOLLOWING:
34 (a) A GOVERNMENT OF A FOREIGN ADVERSARY NATION.
35 (b) A FOREIGN POLITICAL PARTY OF A FOREIGN ADVERSARY NATION.
36 (c) A FOREIGN PERSON AS DEFINED IN 31 CODE OF FEDERAL REGULATIONS
37 SECTION 802.221 WHEN APPLIED TO A FOREIGN ADVERSARY NATION.
38 (d) A PARTNERSHIP, ASSOCIATION, CORPORATION, ORGANIZATION OR OTHER
39 COMBINATION OF PERSONS ORGANIZED UNDER THE LAWS OF, OR HAVING ITS
40 PRINCIPAL PLACE OF BUSINESS IN, A FOREIGN ADVERSARY NATION.
41 (e) A PARTNERSHIP, ASSOCIATION, CORPORATION, ORGANIZATION OR OTHER
42 COMBINATION OF PERSONS THAT IS AT LEAST THIRTY PERCENT OWNED BY A
43 PARTNERSHIP, ASSOCIATION, CORPORATION, ORGANIZATION OR OTHER COMBINATION
44 OF PERSONS ORGANIZED UNDER THE LAWS OF, OR HAVING ITS PRINCIPAL PLACE OF
45 BUSINESS IN, A FOREIGN ADVERSARY NATION.
