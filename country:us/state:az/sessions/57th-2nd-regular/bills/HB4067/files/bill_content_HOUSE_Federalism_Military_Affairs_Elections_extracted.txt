2 Section 1. Section 16-583, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended to
3 read:
4 16-583. Voter not on precinct register; inactive voter list;
5 procedure
6 A. On or before election day, the county recorder shall provide to
7 each precinct the names of electors on the inactive voter list BY WAY OF A
8 PRECINCT REGISTER, A SIGNATURE ROSTER OR AN ELECTRONIC POLLBOOK. If a
9 person whose name is not on the precinct register appears at a polling
10 place OR OTHER VOTING LOCATION, an election official shall determine
11 whether the person is on the inactive voter list. If the person is on the
12 inactive voter list, the registrant, on affirmation by the registrant
13 before an election official at the polling place OR OTHER VOTING LOCATION
14 that the registrant continues to reside at the address indicated on the
15 inactive voter list, shall be permitted ALLOWED to vote at that polling
16 place OR OTHER VOTING LOCATION. The elector's name shall be entered on a
17 separate signature roster page at the end of the signature roster, and
18 voters' names shall be numbered consecutively. If the registrant IS AT A
19 POLLING PLACE AND THE REGISTRANT indicates that the registrant lives at a
20 new residence, the election official shall direct the registrant to the
21 polling place for the new address.
22 B. IN ADDITION TO LISTING THE INACTIVE VOTERS ON OR WITH THE
23 PRECINCT REGISTER, SIGNATURE ROSTER OR ELECTRONIC POLLBOOK, THE COUNTY
24 RECORDER [SHALL] [MAY] INCLUDE THE FOLLOWING INFORMATION IN OR WITH THE
25 PRECINCT REGISTER, SIGNATURE ROSTER OR ELECTRONIC POLLBOOK FOR EACH VOTER
26 WHOSE REGISTRATION STATUS IS AS FOLLOWS:
27 1. "NOT REGISTERED" STATUS FOR A REGISTRANT WHO SUBMITTED A STATE
28 VOTER REGISTRATION FORM WITHOUT SATISFACTORY EVIDENCE OF CITIZENSHIP, THE
29 COUNTY RECORDER HAS NOT FOUND SATISFACTORY EVIDENCE OF CITIZENSHIP FOR
30 THAT REGISTRANT THROUGH GOVERNMENTAL DATABASES AND THE COUNTY RECORDER HAS
31 MAILED A NOTICE TO THAT REGISTRANT THAT INFORMS THE REGISTRANT THAT
32 SATISFACTORY EVIDENCE OF CITIZENSHIP IS REQUIRED FOR REGISTRATION.
33 2. "SUSPENSE" STATUS FOR A REGISTRANT:

17 4. "CANCELED" STATUS FOR A REGISTRANT WHO:

21 (c) IS DECLARED INCAPACITATED.
22 (d) MOVES OUT OF THE JURISDICTION.
23 (e) SELF-REPORTED AS A NONCITIZEN OR NONRESIDENT OF THE

30 from inactive status all electors who voted pursuant to subsection A OF
31 THIS SECTION, shall place the electors' names back on the general register
32 and shall return the electors' status to active.
