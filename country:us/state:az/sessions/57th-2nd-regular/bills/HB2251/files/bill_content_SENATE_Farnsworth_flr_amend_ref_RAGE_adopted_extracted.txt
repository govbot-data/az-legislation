Farnsworth Floor Amendment
Reference to: REGULATORY AFFAIRS AND
GOVERNMENT EFFICIENCY Committee Amendment
Amendment drafted by: Leg. Council
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
1. Transfers the regulatory oversight of licensed midwives from the Department of Health Services
(DHS) to the Naturopathic Physicians Medical Board (Board).
2. Adds one licensed midwife who is appointed by the Governor as a member of the Board.
3. Requires, beginning July 1, 2027, all monies deposited in the Board Fund for naturopathic
physician licensing and regulation to be only used for licensing and regulating naturopathic
physicians.
4. Requires, beginning July 1, 2027, all monies deposited in the Board Fund for midwife licensing
and regulation to be only used for licensing and regulating midwives.
5. Requires the Board to establish a separate account for monies transferred to the Board Fund for
midwife licensing and regulation.
6. Applies the statutory investigative and disciplinary procedures prescribed for naturopathic
physicians to licensed midwives.
7. Removes the statutory cap on midwife licensure and application fees.
8. Specifies that the Midwifery Advisory Committee is established under the Board, rather than under
DHS.
9. States that the Midwifery Advisory Committee's duties are at the discretion of the Board.
10. Outlines succession procedures and expenditure authority relating to the transfer of the regulatory
oversight of licensed midwifes from DHS to the Board.
11. Makes technical and conforming changes.
