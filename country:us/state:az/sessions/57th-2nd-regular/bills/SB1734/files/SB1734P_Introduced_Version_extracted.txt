SB1734 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; Dennehotso pedestrian path State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SB 1734 Introduced by Senators Hatathlie: Gonzales, Kuby AN ACT Appropriating monies to the department of administration. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; department of administration; Dennehotso pedestrian path The sum of $5,900,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the department of administration to distribute to the Navajo Nation for surveying, designing, constructing and lighting of a pedestrian path in Dennehotso along U.S. Route 160.
