2 Section 1. Title 13, chapter 6, Arizona Revised Statutes, is
3 amended by adding section 13-604.01, to read:
4 13-604.01. Class 4, 5 or 6 felony; misdemeanor designation;
5 definition
6 A. NOTWITHSTANDING ANY OTHER PROVISION OF THIS TITLE, IF A PERSON
7 IS CONVICTED OF A CLASS 4, 5 OR 6 FELONY NOT INVOLVING A DANGEROUS
8 OFFENSE, THE COURT MAY ENTER JUDGMENT OF CONVICTION FOR A CLASS 1
9 MISDEMEANOR IF THE COURT DETERMINES ALL OF THE FOLLOWING:
10 1. THE PERSON HAS NOT PREVIOUSLY BEEN CONVICTED OF ANY FELONY
11 OFFENSE.
12 2. THE OFFENSE DID NOT INVOLVE A VICTIM.
13 3. THE PERSON HAS COMPLETED ALL OF THE TERMS AND CONDITIONS IMPOSED
14 BY THE COURT FOR THE CONVICTION, INCLUDING FULL PAYMENT OF ALL MONETARY
15 OBLIGATIONS.
16 4. AT LEAST FIVE YEARS HAVE PASSED SINCE THE [PERSON WAS CONVICTED]
17 [COMPLETION OF THE PERSON'S SENTENCE], AND THE PERSON HAS NOT BEEN
18 CONVICTED OF A SUBSEQUENT MISDEMEANOR OFFENSE INVOLVING A VICTIM OR A
19 SUBSEQUENT FELONY OFFENSE DURING THAT TIME.
