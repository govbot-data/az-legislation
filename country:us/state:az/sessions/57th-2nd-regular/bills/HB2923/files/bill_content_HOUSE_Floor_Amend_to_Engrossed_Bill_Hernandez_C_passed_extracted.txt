HOUSE FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
Bill Number: HB 2923
Floor Amendment
Hernandez C
The C Hernandez Amendment dated 3/2/2026 at 9:56am:
1. Mandates before rendering an opinion regarding the patient's ability of
accept and follow with recommended treatment voluntarily the medical
director of the agency make a good faith effort to contact
2. Adds that if the patient is under a guardianship, within 3 days after
receiving a request for judicial review the medical director of the agency must
notify the guardian that a request for judicial review has been presented to
the agency and is being filed with the court for further action
a. Clarifies if the hearing is set on request of judicial review, mandates
the court notify the guardian of the time and place of the hearing
b. Allows the guardian to submit additional materials for the court to
consider and can participate in any hearing scheduled on the request
of judicial review
c. States a copy of any additional materials submitted to the court be
given to the other parities by the party submitting the materials
d. Allows the court to older an independent examination of the patient
3. Requires the patient be informed at the time the patient makes the request of
their right to consult an attorney
a. Asserts the patient be allowed to consult an attorney to assist in
preparation of petition for the writ of habeas corpus and to present the
patient in the hearing
b. States if the patient is not represented by an attorney the reviewing
court must appoint an attorney to assist the patient in preparation of a
petition and to represent the patient in the hearing
4. Adds and effective date from and after December 31, 2026
Amendment explanation prepared by M. Bearden
Phone Number 6-5848
mb
3/2/2026

5. Modifies the form to add the date the request was received be on the
completed form
6. Clarifies it is within 3 business days and allows the medical directors
designee to deliver the request and other information.
7. Strikes
a. Prohibition that judicial review is not available for an issue that has
an administrative remedy per rules adopted by AHCCCS
b. Stipulates that within 3 days after receiving a request for judicial
review, the medical director of the agency must notify the following
that a request for judicial review has been presented to the mental
health treatment agency and is being filed with the court for further
action:
i. the guardian of the patient.
ii. any relative or victim of the patient who has filed a demand for
notice and any other person found by a court to have a
legitimate reason for receiving notice per statute
iii. if the patient is undergoing treatment as the result of a petition
filed by a prosecuting agency, to the prosecuting agency that
filed the petition.
c. Requires the court notify any person entitled to notice of the time and
place of the hearing regardless of whether the court sets a hearing
d. States any person who receives notice can submit additional materials
for the court to consider and can participate in any hearing scheduled
on the request for judicial review
i. States any party submitting materials to the court must also
provide it to the other parties
e. Allows the court to order an independent examination of the patient
