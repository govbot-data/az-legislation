Bill Number: HB 2923
Bravo Floor Amendment
Reference to: House engrossed bill
Amendment drafted by: Leg. Council
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
• Extends, from three to seven business days, the deadline for the medical director of a mental health
treatment agency to deliver a request for judicial review of a patient to the court.
