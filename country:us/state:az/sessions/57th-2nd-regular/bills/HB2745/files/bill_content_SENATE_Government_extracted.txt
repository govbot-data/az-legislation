2 Section 1. Section 41-1153, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended to
3 read:
4 41-1153. Disobedience of subpoena as legislative contempt
5 A. If a witness neglects or refuses to obey a legislative subpoena,
6 or, appearing, neglects or refuses to testify, the senate or the house
7 may, by resolution entered in the journal, commit him UNLESS ANOTHER
8 PROCEDURE IS PROVIDED BY THE RULES OF EITHER HOUSE OF THE LEGISLATURE, THE
9 WITNESS MAY BE COMMITTED for contempt BY EITHER OF THE FOLLOWING:
10 1. AFTER THE WITNESS IS GIVEN NOTICE AND AN OPPORTUNITY FOR A
11 HEARING TO PRESENT EVIDENCE, AT THE DISCRETION OF THE CHAIRPERSON, TO SHOW
12 CAUSE AS TO WHY THE WITNESS IS NOT IN CONTEMPT, AN ORDER OF CONTEMPT BY
13 ANY COMMITTEE OR THE PRESIDENT OF THE SENATE OR SPEAKER OF THE HOUSE OF
14 REPRESENTATIVES THAT FINDS THAT THE WITNESS HAD KNOWLEDGE OF THE SUBPOENA,
15 HAD THE ABILITY TO COMPLY WITH THE SUBPOENA AND FAILED TO COMPLY WITH THE
16 SUBPOENA.
17 2. A RESOLUTION ENTERED IN THE JOURNAL BY THE SENATE OR THE HOUSE
18 OF REPRESENTATIVES.
19 B. A witness neglecting WHO NEGLECTS or refusing REFUSES to attend
20 in obedience to a subpoena may be arrested by the sergeant-at-arms [OR A
21 COUNTY SHERIFF] and brought before the senate or house upon OF
22 REPRESENTATIVES ON authority of EITHER OF THE FOLLOWING:
23 1. A copy of the ORDER OF CONTEMPT THAT IS SIGNED BY THE
24 CHAIRPERSON OR THE PRESIDENT OF THE SENATE OR THE SPEAKER OF THE HOUSE OF
25 REPRESENTATIVES AND THAT IS COUNTERSIGNED BY THE SECRETARY OF THE SENATE
26 OR CHIEF CLERK OF THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES.
