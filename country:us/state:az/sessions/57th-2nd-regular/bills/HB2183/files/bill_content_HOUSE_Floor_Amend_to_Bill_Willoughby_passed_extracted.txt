2 Section 1. Emergency medicine study committee; membership;
3 duties; reports; delayed repeal
4 A. The emergency medicine study committee is established consisting
5 of the following members:
6 1. Three members of the house of representatives, not more than two
7 of whom are members of the same political party and who are appointed by
8 the speaker of the house of representatives. The speaker of the house of
9 representatives shall designate one of these members to serve as
10 chairperson of the study committee.
11 2. Three members of the senate, not more than two of whom are
12 members of the same political party and who are appointed by the president
13 of the senate.
14 3. The director of the department of health services or the
15 director's designee.
16 4. Two individuals who operate an emergency department, one in a
17 county with a population of less than five hundred thousand persons and
18 one in a county with a population of five hundred thousand or more
19 persons, who are appointed by the speaker of the house of representatives.
20 5. [Two] [Three] physicians who are licensed in this state
21 [pursuant to title 32, chapter 13 or 17, Arizona Revised Statutes], [and
22 who are appointed by the president of the senate as follows:]

20 B. The study committee shall do all of the following:
21 1. Review the delivery of the emergency medical services system in
22 this state and obstacles facing the delivery and sustainability of the
23 emergency medical services system in this state.
24 2. Analyze the emergency medicine capacity in this state, including
25 factors impacting emergency medical service delivery and adequacy in rural
26 and urban areas of this state.
27 3. Review the impact of uncompensated care on the financial
28 viability of the practice of emergency medicine in this state.
29 4. Identify factors that impact the stability of entities that
30 deliver emergency medicine in this state.
31 5. Solicit input from patients, health care providers and
32 stakeholders to gather information regarding service delivery and
33 financial viability of emergency medicine in this state.
34 C. The study committee may hold hearings, conduct fact-finding
35 tours, request data from the department of health services and take
36 testimony from witnesses who may assist the study committee in fulfilling
37 its responsibilities. All hearings of the study committee shall be open
38 to the public.
39 D. The legislature and the department of health services shall
40 provide staff and support services to the study committee.
41 E. Study committee members are not eligible to receive compensation
42 but are eligible for reimbursement of expenses pursuant to title 38,
43 chapter 4, article 2, Arizona Revised Statutes.
