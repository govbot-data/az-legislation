FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
SB 1552: ADOT; revisions.
BIASIUCCI FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Requires a contract, between a law enforcement agency and a business that
uses a camera system that identifies motor vehicles, to prohibit the business
from selling or disseminating any data collected by the camera system to
third parties.
2. Clarifies that evidence-based psychotherapy is an alternative form of
treatment, rather than an alternative form of alcohol or other drug screening
education.
3. Makes a heading change under Title 44.
4. Makes technical and conforming changes.
