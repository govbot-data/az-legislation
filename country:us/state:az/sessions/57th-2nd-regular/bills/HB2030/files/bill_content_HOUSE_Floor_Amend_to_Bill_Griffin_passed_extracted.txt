ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2030: water conservation grant fund; education
GRIFFIN FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Reinserts education programs as eligible for monies in the water conservation
grant fund, under the condition that WIFA prioritize on-the-ground projects
over educational programs.

4 49-1332. Water conservation grant fund; purposes
5 A. Monies in the water conservation grant fund must facilitate
6 voluntary water conservation programs or projects that are expected to
7 result in at least one of the following:
8 1. Long-term reductions in water use.
9 2. Improvements in water use efficiency.
10 3. Improvements in water reliability.
11 B. Monies in the water conservation grant fund may be used for the
12 following:
13 1. Education and research programs on how to reduce water
14 consumption, increase water efficiency or increase water reuse.
15 2. 1. Programs and projects for rainwater harvesting, gray water
16 systems, efficiency upgrades, installing drought-resistant landscaping,
17 turf removal and other practices to reduce water use.
18 3. 2. Programs or projects to promote groundwater recharge and
19 improved aquifer health.
20 4. 3. Programs or projects to improve groundwater conservation and
21 surface water flows.
22 5. 4. Landscape watershed protection, restoration and
23 rehabilitation, including through green infrastructure and low-impact
24 development to conserve or augment water supplies.
25 6. 5. Projects facilitating coordinated water management,
26 including groundwater storage and recovery.
27 7. 6. Programs or projects to reduce structural water overuse
28 issues.
