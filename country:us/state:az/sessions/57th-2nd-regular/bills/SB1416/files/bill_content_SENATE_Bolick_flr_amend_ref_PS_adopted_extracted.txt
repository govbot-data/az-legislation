Bolick Floor Amendment
Reference to: PUBLIC SAFETY Committee amendment
Amendment drafted by: Leg. Council
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
1. Requires each law enforcement agency to conduct training once every two years, rather than
biannually, regarding:
a) the mandatory reporting requirements for a missing, kidnapped or runaway child; and
b) the Seek and Find Alert Notification System.
2. Specifics that an LEA must document all decision regarding the provision of outlined information
to media outlets and social media platforms in accordance with the agency's documentation
policies.
3. Makes technical changes.
