ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 4109: safety; school procedures; criminal classification
BIASIUCCI FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Stipulates the public safety policy must require the superintendent to provide
written notification as specified to students' parents and school employees
within 24 hours of any incident involving life-threatening violence, a threat of
life-threatening violence or a threat of violence involving a deadly weapon or
dangerous instrument that occurs in connection with the school district.
2. Asserts the written notification requirements do not require or authorize the
superintendent to disclose personally identifiable information about a student
involved in an incident.
3. Details notification requirements for a superintendent if a law enforcement
officer who responds to an incident notifies the school district that disclosure
might materially compromise a pending criminal investigation or prosecution.
4. Authorizes a superintendent to designate school administrators at each school
campus who are responsible for confiscating any dangerous instrument or
deadly weapon possessed by a person on school property as prescribed.
5. Removes the felonies for a superintendent and instead makes a superintendent
who knowingly or intentionally violates the written notification requirements
without good cause guilty of a class 1 misdemeanor.
6. Removes the felonies for school district governing board members and instead
makes governing board members guilty of a class 1 misdemeanor if the
governing board violates public safety policy requirements as outlined.
7. Allows a school district governing board to satisfy public safety policy
requirements by adopting an emergency response plan that includes the public
safety policy requirements.
8. Requires each school district to annually compile and post a public safety
report on its website that includes specified information.
9. Specifies a school district is not required to include any information from any
school year that begins before the general effective date in the required annual
public safety report.
10. Adds legislative findings.
11. Cites this legislation as Michael's Law.
