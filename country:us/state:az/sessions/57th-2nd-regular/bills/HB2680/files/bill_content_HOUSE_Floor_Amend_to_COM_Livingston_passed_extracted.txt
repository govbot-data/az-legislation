FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2680: worker's compensation; notice; fraud; commission
LIVINGSTON FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Removes language requiring specified information be included in a certificate
of insurance.
2. Requires, for any construction related workers' compensation policy with zero
estimated exposure, any certificate of insurance to contain a specified
statement.
3. Applies the zero estimated exposure policy requirements to zero estimated
exposure polices and any related certificates of insurance, rather than to
contracts, entered into or prepared on July 1, 2027.
4. Includes a conditional enactment clause.
