SB1088 - 572R - I Ver PREFILED    JAN 05 2026 REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; Arizona homeland security; cybersecurity State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SB 1088 Introduced by Senator Carroll AN ACT Appropriating monies to the arizona department of homeland security. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; Arizona department of homeland security; cybersecurity programs The sum of $2,500,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the Arizona department of homeland security for cybersecurity programs. Of the appropriation made in this section, $500,000 shall be used for generative artificial intelligence cybersecurity programs and $2,000,000 shall be used to modernize the statewide VPN security network with a zero trust network access solution.
