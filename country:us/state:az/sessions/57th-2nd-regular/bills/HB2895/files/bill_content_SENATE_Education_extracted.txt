4 15-701.05. Native American language courses; high school
5 graduation requirement
6 [A.] IF THE STATE BOARD OF EDUCATION OR A SCHOOL DISTRICT OR
7 CHARTER SCHOOL INCLUDES IN THE MINIMUM COURSE OF STUDY FOR THE GRADUATION
8 OF STUDENTS FROM HIGH SCHOOL PRESCRIBED PURSUANT TO SECTION 15-701.01 A
9 REQUIREMENT THAT STUDENTS RECEIVE INSTRUCTION IN A WORLD LANGUAGE, THE
10 STATE BOARD OF EDUCATION, SCHOOL DISTRICT OR CHARTER SCHOOL SHALL ALLOW
11 STUDENTS TO SATISFY THE REQUIREMENT BY DEMONSTRATING PROFICIENCY IN A
12 NATIVE AMERICAN LANGUAGE.
13 [B. THIS SECTION DOES NOT REQUIRE A SCHOOL DISTRICT OR CHARTER
14 SCHOOL TO OFFER A NATIVE AMERICAN LANGUAGE COURSE.
15 C. THE STATE BOARD OF EDUCATION MAY ESTABLISH CRITERIA FOR
16 EVALUATING THE NATIVE AMERICAN LANGUAGE PROFICIENCY OF STUDENTS.]
