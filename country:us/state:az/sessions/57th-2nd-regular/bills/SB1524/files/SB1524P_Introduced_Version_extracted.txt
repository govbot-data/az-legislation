SB1524 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; Navajo Route 8070; improvements State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SB 1524 Introduced by Senators Hatathlie: Epstein, Miranda AN ACT Appropriating monies to the department of TRANSPORTATION. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; department of transportation; Navajo Route 8070; exemption a. The sum of $3,200,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the department of transportation to grade, improve drainage, gravel and stabilize Navajo Route 8070. B. The appropriation made in subsection A of this section is exempt from the provisions of section 35-190, Arizona Revised Statutes, relating to lapsing of appropriations.
