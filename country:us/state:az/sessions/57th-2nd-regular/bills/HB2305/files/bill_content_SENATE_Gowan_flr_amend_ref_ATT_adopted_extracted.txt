Amendment drafted by: Kaytie Sherman
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
1. Specifies that the Heavy-duty Truck Towing Study Committee (Study Committee) is established
to consider only private property towing practices.
2. Modifies Study Committee membership by removing the member who represents the trucking
industry who is appointed by the President of the Senate.
3. Limits the Study Committee's duties to only reviewing the outlined practices that pertain to towing
vehicles that weigh at least 26,000 pounds from private property.
4. Makes technical and conforming changes.
