ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB2305: private towing carriers; regulation; rates
BIASIUCCI FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Clarifies that the regulation of towing rates only apply to motor vehicles
towed from private property by private towing carriers.
2. Applies the rate for towing, transporting, impounding or storing a motor
vehicle for a city, town or county that does not regulate the rate for private
towing vehicles from private property as of January 1, 2026.
3. Directs a city, town or county, by January 1, 2027, that has regulated the
rate for private towing vehicles from private property to update the rate for
private towing vehicles to the market standards or the rates and fees as
prescribed.
4. Requires that signs on restricted parking posted in a private parking area
must contain the rights of the owner of the vehicle.
5. Asserts that any private towing carriers that tows a vehicle, rather than any
person towing a vehicle, must notify the appropriate law enforcement agency.
6. Tasks a private towing carrier to release an impounded vehicle to the owner
who makes arrangements, rather than makes satisfactory arrangements, to
pay.
7. Subjects a private parking facility owner that intentionally, knowingly or
recklessly violates rules on private towing to violation damages.
8. Restricts a private towing carrier from requiring the immediate payment as a
condition of releasing a vehicle.
9. Mandates that the regulation on private towing from private property must
be enforced by a city's, town's or county's law enforcement agency.
10. Prohibits a private towing carrier from paying or agreeing to pay a private
property owner or an employee of the owner from which a vehicle is towed.
11. Restricts a private property owner or an owner's employee from which a
vehicle is towed from accepting or agreeing to accept any money, fees,
commissions, credits, gifts, gratuities or other compensation for the provided
towing service.
12. Excludes a motor vehicle dealership from the definition of a private towing
carrier.

13. Establishes the Heavy-Duty Truck Towing Study Committee (Study
Committee)
14. Outlines the Study Committee's membership.
15. Lists the Study Committee's duties.

the Study Committee's activities and recommendations to the Governor, the

to provide a copy to the Secretary of State.
17. Repeals the Study Committee on December 31, 2027.
