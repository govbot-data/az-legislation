SB 1445: water quality; testing; on-site
BLISS FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Prohibits ADEQ from requiring the minimum storage capacity for a
community water system to be greater than 1,000 gallons if serving 25 or
fewer connections and in a county with a population less than 400,000
persons.
2. Makes technical and conforming changes.
3. Contains the language of the NREW Committee amendment.
