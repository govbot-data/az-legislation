Amendment drafted by: Leg. Council
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
1. Specifies that a person commits unlawful alerting of another person to avoid arrest if the person
hinders, delays or prevents the lawful arrest of a specific person, rather than any person, by
knowingly communicating prescribed information.
2. Specifies that unlawful alerting only applies to a real-time, imminent effort by law enforcement to
execute an arrest, rather than an ongoing effort by law enforcement.
3. Makes technical changes.
