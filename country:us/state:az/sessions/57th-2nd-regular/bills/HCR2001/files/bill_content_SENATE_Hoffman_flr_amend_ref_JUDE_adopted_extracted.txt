Hoffman Floor Amendment
Reference to: JUDICIARY AND ELECTIONS
Committee amendment
Amendment drafted by: Grey Gartin
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
1. Requires the Legislature to appropriate monies necessary to implement the requirements of this
resolution.
2. Applies the requirements outlined in this resolution to elections that take place on or after January
1, 2028,
3. Specifies that voters must be provided the option for on-site ballot tabulation in order to prevent
administrative delays and ensure timely results on election day.
4. Makes technical and clarifying changes.
