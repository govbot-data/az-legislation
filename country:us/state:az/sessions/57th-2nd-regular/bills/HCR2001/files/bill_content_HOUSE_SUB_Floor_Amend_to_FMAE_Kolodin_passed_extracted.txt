ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HCR2001: citizenship; identification; contributions; early voting
KOLODIN FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Requires that only citizens of the U.S. be eligible to register and vote once for
each available office or ballot measure, in any primary, general or municipal
election in Arizona, rather than in any public election in Arizona.
2. Strikes language that prohibits contributions from a foreign corporation to
any Arizona candidate or ballot measure.
3. Prohibits a foreign national from making any contribution or expenditure for
the purpose of influencing any Arizona election.
4. Prohibits a person from knowingly accepting from a foreign national any
contribution or expenditure for the purpose of influencing any Arizona
election.
5. Clarifies that all qualified electors must be required to provide a government
issued identification concurrent with casting a ballot.
6. Requires that acceptable identification be made available to qualified electors
solely at the state's expense, rather than free of charge.
7. Requires early voting, if authorized by law, to end no later than 7 p.m. on the
Friday prior to the election, rather than the Tuesday general election.
8. Allows early ballots delivered through the U.S. Postal Service to be accepted
until the closing of polls on election day.
9. Prohibits votes from being cast or accepted after poll closing times on election
day, rather than after poll closing times on general election day.
10. Specifies that a qualified elector who is standing in line by the time polls
close must be permitted to vote.
11. Strikes language from the underlying resolution outlining that all qualified
electors must have the right to vote in-person on election day at conveniently
located polling places.
12. Allows all qualified electors to be offered an opportunity to receive a mail-in
ballot, provided:
a. the qualified elector has made an affirmative request confirming a
specific mail address prior to each biennial general election;
