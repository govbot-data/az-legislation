1 Sec. 3. Legislative findings
2 The legislature finds that:
3 1. It is in the best interest of this state to ensure that the
4 family court is equipped to hear and evaluate safety-related evidence that
5 is necessary to protect children.
6 2. It is important to remove procedural barriers to the submission
7 of evidence in a family law matter that unfairly silences protective
8 parents or serves to deny the admission of critical evidence related to a
9 child's safety and well-being.
10 3. It is in the best interest of this state to allow the court to
11 make determinations regarding the credibility of evidence but remove the
12 ability of a court to reject relevant evidence based solely on the form of
13 the evidence of procedural formalities.

15 Amend title to conform
