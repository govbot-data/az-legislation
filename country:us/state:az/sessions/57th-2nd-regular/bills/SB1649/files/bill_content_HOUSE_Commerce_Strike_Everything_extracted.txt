HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES AMENDMENTS TO S.B. 1649
(Reference to Senate engrossed bill)
1 Strike everything after the enacting clause and insert:
2 "Section 1. Title 23, chapter 1, article 1, Arizona Revised
3 Statutes, is amended by adding section 23-111, to read:
4 23-111. Industrial commission of Arizona; department of
5 economic security public assistance; list of
6 employees who receive public assistance; employer
7 list; public posting
8 A. ON OR BEFORE JANUARY 1, 2027 AND EACH YEAR THEREAFTER, THE
9 INDUSTRIAL COMMISSION OF ARIZONA AND THE DEPARTMENT OF ECONOMIC SECURITY
10 SHALL PUBLISH ON THE INDUSTRIAL COMMISSION'S AND THE DEPARTMENT OF
11 ECONOMIC SECURITY'S PUBLICLY ACCESSIBLE WEBSITES A LIST OF THE TOP FIFTY
12 EMPLOYERS IN THIS STATE WITH THE HIGHEST NUMBER OF EMPLOYEES WHO RECEIVE
13 MEDICAID OR OTHER PUBLIC ASSISTANCE. THE REPORT MUST INCLUDE:
14 1. THE NAME AND ADDRESS OF THE EMPLOYER.
15 2. THE SIZE OF THE EMPLOYER.
16 3. THE NUMBER OF PUBLIC ASSISTANCE PROGRAM BENEFICIARIES WHO ARE AN
17 EMPLOYEE OF THAT EMPLOYER AND THE TYPE OF ASSISTANCE EACH EMPLOYEE
18 RECEIVES.
19 4. THE NUMBER OF PUBLIC ASSISTANCE BENEFICIARIES WHO ARE A SPOUSE
20 OR DEPENDENT OF AN EMPLOYEE OF THE EMPLOYER AND THE TYPE OF ASSISTANCE
21 EACH SPOUSE OR DEPENDENT RECEIVES.
22 5. IF AVAILABLE, THE PUBLIC WELFARE PROGRAM COSTS TO THIS STATE FOR
23 EMPLOYED INDIVIDUALS AND ENROLLED DEPENDENTS.
24 6. IF AN EMPLOYER OFFERS HEALTH BENEFITS TO THE EMPLOYER'S
25 EMPLOYEES, THE NUMBER OF EMPLOYEES WHO RECEIVE PUBLIC ASSISTANCE.
26 B. THE LIST MAY NOT INCLUDE CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION THAT INCLUDES
27 THE NAMES OF THE INDIVIDUALS OR BENEFICIARIES WHO RECEIVE PUBLIC
28 ASSISTANCE.
29 C. THE DEPARTMENT OF ECONOMIC SECURITY SHALL COMPILE AND SEND ALL
30 DATA THAT IS RELATED TO SUBSECTION A OF THIS SECTION TO THE INDUSTRIAL
31 COMMISSION OF ARIZONA."
32 Amend title to conform
