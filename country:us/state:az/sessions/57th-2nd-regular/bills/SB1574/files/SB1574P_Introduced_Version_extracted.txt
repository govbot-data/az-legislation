SB1574 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: immunization rates; disclosure; public schools State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SB 1574 Introduced by Senator Epstein AN ACT amending title 15, chapter 8, article 6, Arizona Revised Statutes, by adding section 15-875; relating to school immunization. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Title 15, chapter 8, article 6, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended by adding section 15-875, to read: START_STATUTE15-875. Immunization rates; disclosure requirements A. On request by a pupil's parent, a school district or charter school shall provide the most recent aggregated immunization rates for the school, as reported to the department of health services pursuant to section 15-874. B. This section does not require a school district or charter school to release the personally identifiable information of any pupil. END_STATUTE
