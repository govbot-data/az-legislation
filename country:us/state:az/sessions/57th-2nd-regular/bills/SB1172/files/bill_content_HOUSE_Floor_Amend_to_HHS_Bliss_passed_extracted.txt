8 A. IF THE DEPARTMENT DENIES AN APPLICATION FOR CREDENTIALING OR
9 RECREDENTIALING, THE DEPARTMENT SHALL GIVE THE APPLICANT AN OPPORTUNITY TO
10 CORRECT ANY DEFICIENCIES WITH THE APPLICATION THAT LED TO THE INITIAL
11 DENIAL. THE APPLICANT HAS FOURTEEN BUSINESS DAYS AFTER RECEIVING THE
12 NOTICE OF DENIAL FROM THE DEPARTMENT TO CORRECT THE DEFICIENCIES. IF THE
13 APPLICANT NOTIFIES THE DEPARTMENT IN WRITING WITHIN THE FOURTEEN-DAY
14 PERIOD THAT THE DEFICIENCIES HAVE BEEN CORRECTED, THE DEPARTMENT SHALL
15 REEVALUATE THE CREDENTIALING OR RECREDENTIALING APPLICATION.
16 B. IF AFTER REEVALUATION THE DEPARTMENT DENIES THE APPLICATION AND
17 DOES NOT INITIATE A CONTRACT [ACTION] WITHIN FOURTEEN BUSINESS DAYS, THE
18 APPLICANT MAY APPEAL THE DECISION PURSUANT TO ARTICLE 3 OF THIS CHAPTER.
