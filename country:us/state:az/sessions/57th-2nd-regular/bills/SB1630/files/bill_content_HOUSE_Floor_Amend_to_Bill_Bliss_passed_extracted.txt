ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
SB 1630: home; community-based services; mental illness
BLISS FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Renames the HCBS Program for SMI adults the SMI Enhanced Residential
Treatment Program which is a three-year pilot program.
2. Contains enrollment, waitlist management and stakeholder workgroup
provisions.
3. Outlines a grievance and appeal process and provider requirements.
4. Defines terms.
5. Makes technical and conforming changes.
