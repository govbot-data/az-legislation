2 Section 1. Title 36, chapter 29, Arizona Revised Statutes, is
3 amended by adding article 3.1, to read:
4 ARTICLE 3.1. HOME AND COMMUNITY-BASED SERVICES FOR PERSONS
5 WITH SERIOUS MENTAL ILLNESS
6 36-2979. Definitions
7 IN THIS ARTICLE, UNLESS THE CONTEXT OTHERWISE REQUIRES:
8 1. "ADMINISTRATION" MEANS THE ARIZONA HEALTH CARE COST CONTAINMENT
9 SYSTEM ADMINISTRATION.
10 2. "BEHAVIORAL HEALTH RESIDENTIAL FACILITY" MEANS A HEALTH CARE
11 INSTITUTION THAT IS LICENSED PURSUANT TO THIS TITLE TO PROVIDE LEVEL I OR
12 II BEHAVIORAL HEALTH RESIDENTIAL SERVICES.
13 3. "HIGH-ACUITY SERIOUSLY MENTALLY ILL INDIVIDUAL" MEANS A PERSON
14 WHO MEETS A SERIOUS MENTAL ILLNESS [LONG-TERM] LEVEL OF CARE AND ONE OR
15 MORE PRIORITY CRITERIA LISTED IN SECTION 36-2979.04, SUBSECTION B.
16 4. "HOME AND COMMUNITY-BASED SERVICES":
17 (a) MEANS SERVICES AUTHORIZED UNDER ONE OR MORE MEDICAID STATE PLAN
18 AUTHORITIES, WAIVERS OR DEMONSTRATION AUTHORITIES, INCLUDING THOSE
19 AUTHORIZED UNDER 42 UNITED STATES CODE SECTION 1315 OR 1396n, THAT SUPPORT
20 INDIVIDUALS IN THE COMMUNITY AS AN ALTERNATIVE TO INSTITUTIONALIZATION.
21 (b) INCLUDES SERVICES THAT PROVIDE ASSISTANCE WITH ACTIVITIES OF
22 DAILY LIVING, MEDICATION ADMINISTRATION, SUPERVISION AND STRUCTURED
23 SUPPORTS COMPARABLE TO ASSISTED LIVING MODELS.
24 5. "MEMBER" MEANS A PERSON WHO IS ENROLLED IN THE PROGRAM.
25 6. "PROGRAM" MEANS THE HOME AND COMMUNITY-BASED SERVICE PROGRAM FOR
26 ADULTS WHO ARE SERIOUSLY MENTALLY ILL.
27 7. "QUALIFIED INCOME TRUST" MEANS A TRUST AS DESCRIBED IN 42 UNITED
28 STATES CODE SECTION 1396p(d)(4)(B).

6 36-2979.01. Home and community-based service program for
7 adults who are seriously mentally ill; request
8 for federal approval; stakeholder workgroup;
9 semiannual implementation updates
10 A. THE HOME AND COMMUNITY-BASED SERVICE PROGRAM FOR ADULTS WHO ARE
11 SERIOUSLY MENTALLY ILL IS ESTABLISHED. THE ADMINISTRATION SHALL DEVELOP
12 AND REQUEST AUTHORITY FROM THE CENTERS FOR MEDICARE AND MEDICAID SERVICES
13 TO IMPLEMENT A DEDICATED HOME AND COMMUNITY-BASED SERVICES BENEFIT FOR
14 ADULTS WHO ARE DETERMINED TO BE SERIOUSLY MENTALLY ILL.
15 B. [THROUGH THE SUBMISSION OF THE FIRST ANNUAL REPORT,] THE
16 ADMINISTRATION SHALL CONVENE A STAKEHOLDER WORKGROUP THAT INCLUDES
17 REPRESENTATIVES FROM BEHAVIORAL HEALTH PROVIDERS, FAMILY MEMBERS,
18 CAREGIVERS AND GUARDIANS OF INDIVIDUALS WHO ARE SERIOUSLY MENTALLY ILL,
19 COUNTIES, TRIBAL NATIONS AND COMMUNITY ORGANIZATIONS TO ADVISE ON PROGRAM
20 DESIGN AND IMPLEMENTATION. STAKEHOLDER INPUT SHALL SPECIFICALLY ADDRESS
21 THE NEEDS OF INDIVIDUALS WHO REQUIRE COMPLEX MEDICATION MANAGEMENT,
22 ENHANCED MONITORING AND STRUCTURED COMMUNITY-BASED SUPPORTS, INCLUDING
23 ASSISTED LIVING-TYPE MODELS, TO PROMOTE MEDICATION CONTINUITY AND SAFETY.
24 C. THE ADMINISTRATION SHALL REQUEST APPROVAL FROM THE CENTERS FOR
25 MEDICARE AND MEDICAID SERVICES FOR THE PROGRAM NOT LATER THAN JULY 1, 2027
26 AND SHALL BEGIN IMPLEMENTING THE PROGRAM NOT LATER THAN ONE YEAR AFTER THE
27 APPROVAL BY THE CENTERS FOR MEDICARE AND MEDICAID SERVICES.
28 D. UNTIL THE PROGRAM IS IMPLEMENTED, THE ADMINISTRATION SHALL
29 PROVIDE [QUARTERLY] [SEMIANNUAL] IMPLEMENTATION UPDATES TO THE PRESIDENT
30 OF THE SENATE, THE SPEAKER OF THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES AND THE
31 CHAIRPERSONS OF THE SENATE AND HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES HEALTH AND HUMAN
32 SERVICES COMMITTEES, OR THEIR SUCCESSOR COMMITTEES.
33 36-2979.02. Eligibility; financial eligibility; serious
34 mental illness long-term level of care;
35 criteria
36 A. TO BE ELIGIBLE FOR THE PROGRAM, AN INDIVIDUAL MUST MEET ALL OF
37 THE FOLLOWING:
38 1. HAVE BEEN DETERMINED TO BE SERIOUSLY MENTALLY ILL.
39 2. MEET THE SERIOUS MENTAL ILLNESS [LONG-TERM] LEVEL OF CARE
40 ADOPTED BY THE ADMINISTRATION PURSUANT TO SUBSECTION C OF THIS SECTION.
41 3. REQUIRE HOME AND COMMUNITY-BASED SERVICES IN ORDER TO AVOID
42 PLACEMENT IN A BEHAVIORAL HEALTH RESIDENTIAL FACILITY OR PSYCHIATRIC
43 INSTITUTION.
44 4. MEET THE FINANCIAL ELIGIBILITY REQUIREMENTS PURSUANT TO
45 SUBSECTION B OF THIS SECTION.

1 B. AN INDIVIDUAL WHO MEETS THE SERIOUS MENTAL ILLNESS [LONG-TERM]
2 LEVEL OF CARE ADOPTED BY THE ADMINISTRATION PURSUANT TO SUBSECTION C OF
3 THIS SECTION IS FINANCIALLY ELIGIBLE FOR THE PROGRAM IF THE INDIVIDUAL'S

5 SECURITY INCOME BENEFIT RATE. AN INDIVIDUAL WHOSE INCOME EXCEEDS THIS

7 ELIGIBILITY. RESOURCE AND SPOUSAL IMPOVERISHMENT STANDARDS SHALL BE THE
8 SAME AS REQUIRED PURSUANT TO ARTICLE 2 OF THIS CHAPTER FOR ENROLLEES IN
9 THE ARIZONA LONG-TERM CARE SYSTEM HOME AND COMMUNITY-BASED SERVICES.
10 C. THE ADMINISTRATION SHALL ADOPT A SERIOUS MENTAL ILLNESS
11 [LONG-TERM] LEVEL OF CARE SPECIFIC TO INDIVIDUALS WHO ARE SERIOUSLY
12 MENTALLY ILL THAT IS BASED ON BEHAVIORAL, FUNCTIONAL AND SAFETY CRITERIA,
13 WHICH MAY INCLUDE ANY OF THE FOLLOWING:
14 1. CURRENT OR RECENT COURT-ORDERED TREATMENT.
15 2. LEGAL GUARDIANSHIP DUE TO PSYCHIATRIC INCAPACITY.
16 3. RECENT DISCHARGE FROM A JAIL OR PRISON, THE STATE HOSPITAL OR A
17 BEHAVIORAL HEALTH RESIDENTIAL FACILITY.
18 4. REPEATED PSYCHIATRIC HOSPITALIZATIONS OR CRISIS EPISODES.
19 5. IMPAIRED JUDGMENT, DISORGANIZATION OR INABILITY TO PERFORM
20 ACTIVITIES OF DAILY LIVING DUE TO PSYCHIATRIC SYMPTOMS.
21 6. DOCUMENTED SAFETY RISKS, INCLUDING ELOPEMENT, FIRE OR WATER
22 MISUSE, AGGRESSION, DELUSIONAL BEHAVIOR OR EXPLOITATION RISK.
23 7. HOMELESSNESS OR IMMINENT RISK OF HOMELESSNESS.
24 8. HIGH-INTENSITY OR COMPLEX PSYCHOTROPIC MEDICATION REGIMENS
25 REQUIRING ENHANCED MONITORING TO ENSURE ADHERENCE AND TO IDENTIFY
26 POTENTIAL ADVERSE EFFECTS.
27 9. CLINICAL NEEDS THAT NECESSITATE STRUCTURED COMMUNITY-BASED
28 SUPPORTS, INCLUDING ASSISTED LIVING-TYPE SUPPORTS, TO MAINTAIN CONTINUITY
29 OF MEDICATION THERAPY, SUCH AS THOSE AT ELEVATED RISK OF RELAPSE,
30 DECOMPENSATION OR HOSPITALIZATION RELATED TO MEDICATION NONADHERENCE.
31 D. THE SERIOUS MENTAL ILLNESS [LONG-TERM] LEVEL OF CARE ADOPTED
32 PURSUANT TO SUBSECTION C OF THIS SECTION MAY NOT REQUIRE A NURSING
33 FACILITY LEVEL OF CARE OR PHYSICAL DISABILITY OR PHYSICAL IMPAIRMENT
34 CRITERIA AND MAY NOT USE A PREADMISSION SCREENING TOOL.
35 E. THE ADMINISTRATION MAY REFINE THE ASSESSMENT CRITERIA AND
36 PROCESSES THROUGH RULE OR POLICY, CONSISTENT WITH LEGISLATIVE INTENT,
37 INCLUDING ADJUSTMENT TO ASSESSMENT TOOLS, THRESHOLDS OR PROCESSES.
38 36-2979.03. Covered services; modification
39 A. SUBJECT TO THE APPROVAL OF THE CENTERS FOR MEDICARE AND MEDICAID
40 SERVICES, THE ADMINISTRATION SHALL PROVIDE HOME AND COMMUNITY-BASED
41 SERVICES THAT ARE COMPARABLE TO THOSE AUTHORIZED UNDER ARTICLE 2 OF THIS
42 CHAPTER, WITH MODIFICATIONS APPROPRIATE FOR BEHAVIORAL HEALTH NEEDS. THE
43 HOME AND COMMUNITY-BASED SERVICES SHALL INCLUDE:
44 1. ATTENDANT CARE AND PERSONAL CARE.
45 2. HABILITATION WITH BEHAVIOR MANAGEMENT.
46 3. ADULT DAY HEALTH CARE WITH A BEHAVIORAL HEALTH FOCUS.

5 36-2979.05. Provider requirements; enhanced reimbursement
6 rates
7 A. PROVIDERS SHALL DOCUMENT BEHAVIORAL INTERVENTIONS, CRISIS
8 SUPPORTS AND STAFFING ADJUSTMENTS BEFORE INITIATING DISCHARGE.
9 B. A PROVIDER THAT RECEIVES REIMBURSEMENT UNDER THE PROGRAM SHALL
10 IMPLEMENT EVICTION-PREVENTION PROTOCOLS AND OBTAIN REGIONAL BEHAVIORAL
11 HEALTH AUTHORITY APPROVAL BEFORE ISSUING NONEMERGENCY NOTICES TO VACATE.
12 C. THE ADMINISTRATION MAY ADOPT ENHANCED REIMBURSEMENT RATES FOR
13 HIGH-ACUITY SERIOUSLY MENTALLY ILL INDIVIDUALS WHO ARE RECEIVING HOME AND
14 COMMUNITY-BASED SERVICES.
15 D. THIS SECTION DOES NOT LIMIT THE ADMINISTRATION'S AUTHORITY TO
16 ENSURE THE HEALTH AND SAFETY OF PARTICIPANTS.
17 E. THIS SECTION DOES NOT REQUIRE A PROVIDER TO CONTINUE SERVICES
18 WHEN IMMEDIATE AND DOCUMENTED RISKS TO HEALTH OR SAFETY CANNOT BE
19 MITIGATED THROUGH REASONABLE CLINICAL INTERVENTIONS.
