SCR1007 - 572R - I Ver PREFILED    JAN 06 2026 REFERENCE TITLE: technical correction; racing commission; prohibition State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SCR 1007 Introduced by Senator Finchem A CONCURRENT RESOLUTION enacting and ordering the submission to the people of a measure relating to horse and dog racing. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it resolved by the Senate of the State of Arizona, the House of Representatives concurring: 1. Under the power of the referendum, as vested in the legislature, the following measure, relating to horse and dog racing, is enacted to become valid as a law if approved by the voters and on proclamation of the Governor: AN ACT AMENDING SECTION 5-103.01, ARIZONA REVISED STATUTES; RELATING TO HORSE AND DOG RACING. Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Section 5-103.01, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended to read: START_STATUTE5-103.01. Prohibited activities An employee or appointee of the commission may not: 1. Enter into any business dealing, venture or contract with an owner or lessee of a race track racetrack or any permittee. 2. Be employed in any capacity by any race track or permittee. 3. Participate as an owner-trainer, trainer or jockey in any racing meeting conducted in this state. END_STATUTE 2. The Secretary of State shall submit this proposition to the voters at the next general election as provided by article IV, part 1, section 1, Constitution of Arizona.
