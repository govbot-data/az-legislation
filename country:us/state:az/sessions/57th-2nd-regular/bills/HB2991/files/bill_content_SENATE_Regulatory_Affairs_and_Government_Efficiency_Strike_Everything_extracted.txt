4 41-191.14. Technology content protection for minors grant
5 fund; attorney general; annual report
6 A. THE TECHNOLOGY CONTENT PROTECTION FOR MINORS GRANT FUND IS
7 ESTABLISHED CONSISTING OF MONIES FROM CIVIL PENALTIES, ATTORNEY FEES AND
8 LITIGATION COSTS THAT ARE RECOVERED BY THE ATTORNEY GENERAL PURSUANT TO
9 SECTION 44-8047. THE ATTORNEY GENERAL SHALL ADMINISTER THE FUND. MONIES
10 IN THE FUND ARE CONTINUOUSLY APPROPRIATED AND ARE EXEMPT FROM THE
11 PROVISIONS OF SECTION 35-190 RELATING TO LAPSING OF APPROPRIATIONS.
12 B. MONIES IN THE FUND MAY NOT BE TRANSFERRED TO OR OTHERWISE MADE
13 AVAILABLE TO ANY OTHER STATE FUND OR ACCOUNT AND MAY NOT BE USED FOR
14 OPERATING EXPENSES OF THE ATTORNEY GENERAL'S OFFICE OR STAFF SALARIES.
15 C. THE ATTORNEY GENERAL SHALL USE THE MONIES IN THE FUND SOLELY TO
16 AWARD GRANTS TO NONPROFIT ORGANIZATIONS FOR THE FOLLOWING:
17 1. OUTREACH PROGRAMS THAT PROVIDE EDUCATION TO PARENTS, GUARDIANS
18 AND MINORS ABOUT ONLINE SAFETY, DIGITAL LITERACY AND THE EFFECTS OF SOCIAL
19 MEDIA ON THE MENTAL HEALTH OF MINORS.
20 2. PROGRAMS THAT PROVIDE RESOURCES OR SERVICES TO MINORS WHO HAVE
21 EXPERIENCED HARM AS A RESULT OF VIOLATIONS OF CHAPTER 42 OF THIS TITLE.
22 3. RESEARCH ON THE EFFECTS OF SOCIAL MEDIA ON THE MENTAL AND
23 PHYSICAL HEALTH OF MINORS.
24 4. LAW ENFORCEMENT PROGRAMS THAT INVESTIGATE VIOLATIONS OF CHAPTER
25 42 OF THIS TITLE AND TECHNOLOGY-FACILITATED HARM TO MINORS.
26 5. PROGRAMS THAT PROVIDE MENTAL HEALTH COUNSELING, TREATMENT OR
27 SUPPORT SERVICES TO MINORS WHO HAVE EXPERIENCED PSYCHOLOGICAL OR EMOTIONAL
28 HARM AS A RESULT OF USING SOCIAL MEDIA AND VIOLATIONS OF CHAPTER 42 OF
29 THIS TITLE.
30 6. PROGRAMS THAT PROVIDE PREVENTION EDUCATION, VICTIM SERVICES OR
31 LAW ENFORCEMENT SUPPORT RELATED TO THE HUMAN TRAFFICKING OR SEXUAL
32 EXPLOITATION OF MINORS FACILITATED THROUGH SOCIAL MEDIA PLATFORMS.
33 D. THE ATTORNEY GENERAL SHALL ESTABLISH GRANT ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA,
34 APPLICATION PROCEDURES AND REPORTING REQUIREMENTS FOR RECIPIENTS OF GRANTS
35 AWARDED PURSUANT TO THIS SECTION SUBJECT TO JOINT LEGISLATIVE BUDGET
36 COMMITTEE APPROVAL.

15 44-8041. Definitions
16 IN THIS CHAPTER, UNLESS THE CONTEXT OTHERWISE REQUIRES:
17 1. "ANONYMOUS AGE VERIFICATION" MEANS A COMMERCIALLY REASONABLE
18 METHOD THAT IS USED BY A GOVERNMENT AGENCY OR A BUSINESS FOR THE PURPOSE
19 OF AGE VERIFICATION THAT IS CONDUCTED BY A NONGOVERNMENTAL, INDEPENDENT
20 THIRD PARTY THAT IS ORGANIZED UNDER THE LAWS OF ANY STATE OF THE UNITED
21 STATES THAT BOTH:
22 (a) HAS ITS PRINCIPAL PLACE OF BUSINESS IN ANY STATE OF THE UNITED
23 STATES.
24 (b) IS NOT OWNED OR CONTROLLED BY A COMPANY THAT IS FORMED IN A
25 FOREIGN COUNTRY, A GOVERNMENT OF A FOREIGN COUNTRY OR ANY OTHER ENTITY
26 FORMED IN A FOREIGN COUNTRY.
27 2. "APPLICATION" MEANS A SOFTWARE APPLICATION OR ELECTRONIC SERVICE
28 THAT A USER MAY RUN OR DIRECT ON A COMPUTER, A MOBILE DEVICE OR ANY OTHER
29 GENERAL PURPOSE COMPUTING DEVICE.
30 3. "APPLICATION STORE" MEANS A PUBLICLY AVAILABLE WEBSITE, SOFTWARE
31 APPLICATION, ELECTRONIC SERVICE OR PLATFORM THAT DISTRIBUTES AND
32 FACILITATES THE DOWNLOAD OF APPLICATIONS FROM THIRD-PARTY DEVELOPERS TO
33 USERS OF A COMPUTER, A MOBILE DEVICE OR ANY OTHER GENERAL PURPOSE
34 COMPUTING DEVICE.
35 4. "CHILD" MEANS AN INDIVIDUAL WHO IS LESS THAN SIXTEEN YEARS OF
36 AGE.
37 5. "COMMERCIAL ENTITY" INCLUDES A CORPORATION, A LIMITED LIABILITY
38 COMPANY, A PARTNERSHIP, A LIMITED PARTNERSHIP, A SOLE PROPRIETORSHIP AND
39 ANY OTHER LEGALLY RECOGNIZED ENTITY.
40 6. "COVERED COMPANY" MEANS A PERSON THAT OWNS, CONTROLS OR OPERATES
41 AN APPLICATION STORE OR OPERATING SYSTEM THAT SERVICES CUSTOMERS IN THIS
42 STATE.

17 44-8044. Age signal conflicts
18 A. IF A DEVELOPER DETERMINES THAT AN INTERNAL AGE SIGNAL CONFLICTS
19 WITH THE AGE SIGNAL FROM A COVERED COMPANY, THE DEVELOPER MAY RELY ON THE
20 AGE SIGNAL FROM THE COVERED COMPANY UNLESS THE DEVELOPER HAS CLEAR AND
21 CONVINCING EVIDENCE THAT AN INTERNAL AGE SIGNAL IS MORE ACCURATE. IF THE
22 DEVELOPER HAS CLEAR AND CONVINCING EVIDENCE THAT THE DEVELOPER'S INTERNAL
23 AGE SIGNAL IS MORE ACCURATE, INCLUDING IF THE USER PROVIDED PROOF OF AGE
24 TO THE DEVELOPER, THE DEVELOPER SHALL USE THE DEVELOPER'S INTERNAL SIGNAL.
25 B. IF APPLICABLE AND TECHNICALLY FEASIBLE, A DEVELOPER SHALL
26 TRANSMIT THE CONFLICTING INTERNAL AGE SIGNAL BACK TO A COVERED COMPANY
27 THROUGH A MECHANISM THAT IS PROVIDED BY THE COVERED COMPANY IF THE
28 DEVELOPER HAS CLEAR AND CONVINCING EVIDENCE THAT AN INTERNAL AGE SIGNAL IS
29 MORE ACCURATE.
30 C. ON RECEIPT OF A CONFLICTING INTERNAL AGE SIGNAL FROM A
31 DEVELOPER, THE COVERED COMPANY SHALL TAKE STEPS TO REVERIFY THE USER'S AGE
32 AND UPDATE THE AGE SIGNAL TO PROVIDE DEVELOPERS WITH THE NEWLY VERIFIED
33 AGE.
34 44-8045. Material that is harmful to minors; age
35 verification; personal identifying information
36 protection
37 IF AN APPLICATION CONTAINS A SUBSTANTIAL PORTION OF MATERIAL THAT IS
38 HARMFUL TO MINORS, A COMMERCIAL ENTITY THAT KNOWINGLY AND INTENTIONALLY
39 PUBLISHES OR DISTRIBUTES MATERIAL THAT IS HARMFUL TO MINORS ON THE
40 APPLICATION SHALL DO ALL OF THE FOLLOWING:
41 1. USE EITHER ANONYMOUS AGE VERIFICATION OR STANDARD AGE
42 VERIFICATION TO VERIFY THAT THE PERSON ATTEMPTING TO ACCESS THE MATERIAL
43 THAT IS HARMFUL TO MINORS IS AT LEAST EIGHTEEN YEARS OF AGE.
44 2. PREVENT ACCESS TO THE MATERIAL THAT IS HARMFUL TO MINORS BY A
45 PERSON WHO IS LESS THAN EIGHTEEN YEARS OF AGE.
46 3. OFFER ANONYMOUS AGE VERIFICATION AND STANDARD AGE VERIFICATION
47 AND ALLOW A PERSON ATTEMPTING TO ACCESS THE MATERIAL THAT IS HARMFUL TO
48 MINORS TO SELECT WHICH METHOD WILL BE USED TO VERIFY THE PERSON'S AGE.
