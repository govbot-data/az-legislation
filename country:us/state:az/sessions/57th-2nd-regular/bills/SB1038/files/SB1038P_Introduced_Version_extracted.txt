SB1038 - 572R - I Ver PREFILED    DEC 19 2025 REFERENCE TITLE: cast vote record; public record State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SB 1038 Introduced by Senators Finchem: Rogers AN ACT amending title 16, chapter 4, article 1, Arizona Revised Statutes, by adding section 16-407.04; relating to the conduct of elections. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Title 16, chapter 4, article 1, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended by adding section 16-407.04, to read: START_STATUTE16-407.04. Voter list; cast vote record; public record A. Notwithstanding any other law, within one hour after the CLOSING of the polls for each election, The county recorder shall make available as a public record the following: 1. The name, voter identification number and political party registration, if any, of every person who voted in that election. 2. The cast vote record in a sortable format. B. The county recorder shall not randomize or otherwise alter the original files. C. The information and records prescribed by subsection A of this section constitute a public record.END_STATUTE
