Bill Number: H.B. 2249
Mesnard ADD COW Floor Amendment
Reference to: Senate engrossed House bill
Amendment drafted by: Mason Holler
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
1. Specifies that a governmental entity or institution employee who is found liable of facilitating,
encouraging or coercing a minor child to withhold information from the child's parent is personally
liable in an amount of $20,000 for each instance in which information was withheld from the
parent.
2. Removes the specification that, if an act that violates the Parents' Bill of Rights occurred before
the general effective date and the person continues to violate the Parents' Bill of Rights after the
general effective date, each act that occurs after the general effective date constitutes a separate
violation for the purposes of bringing suit for a violation.
3. Determines that, within six months of the general effective date, the state, a political subdivision
or governmental entity or institution has an affirmative duty to investigate and discover all
previous instances in which it or its employees facilitated, encouraged or coerced a minor child to
withhold information from the child's parent before the effective date.
4. Specifies that the state, a political subdivision or a governmental entity or institution or an
employee of such entities is civilly liable to the parent of a minor child for any violation that was
not disclosed only if the violation was actually known or would have been discovered with
reasonable diligence and investigation.
5. Defines distinct act or omission, instance and withhold for the purposes of bringing suit for a
violation of the Parents' Bill of Rights as outlined.
6. Makes technical and conforming changes.
