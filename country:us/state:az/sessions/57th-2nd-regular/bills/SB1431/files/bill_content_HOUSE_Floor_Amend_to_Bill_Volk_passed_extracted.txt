ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
SB 1431: municipalities; planned communities; design; prohibition
VOLK FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Adds that the prohibited home design requirements do not supersede:
a. applicable building codes or fire codes, including specifications of
building materials related to durability or suitability for weather; and
b. requirements for walls that are located on a road that is designed as an
arterial road or a collector street as designated by an adopted municipal
general plan.
