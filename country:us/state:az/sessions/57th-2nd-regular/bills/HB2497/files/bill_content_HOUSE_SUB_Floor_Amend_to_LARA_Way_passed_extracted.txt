HB 2497: right to hunt; fish; harvest
WAY FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Removes the Legislature as an entity prohibited by this act from enacting or
adopting unreasonable restrictions related to hunting and fishing. (Sec. 1)
2. Makes additional technical changes. (Sec. 1)

4 17-106. Right to hunt, fish and harvest wildlife; public
5 policy
6 A. THE CITIZENS OF THIS STATE HAVE A RIGHT TO LAWFULLY HUNT, FISH

8 B. AS PRESCRIBED IN SECTION 17-102, WILDLIFE IS THE PROPERTY OF

10 STATE.
11 C. THE LEGISLATURE HAS EXCLUSIVE AUTHORITY TO REGULATE THE MANNER,
12 METHODS AND SEASONS FOR HUNTING, FISHING AND HARVESTING WILDLIFE. THE
13 LEGISLATURE MAY DELEGATE THE RULEMAKING AUTHORITY TO THE [GAME AND FISH]
14 COMMISSION AS PROVIDED IN THIS TITLE.
15 D. [THE LEGISLATURE AND] THE [ARIZONA GAME AND FISH] COMMISSION
