2 Section 1. Title 46, chapter 2, article 2, Arizona Revised
3 Statutes, is amended by adding section 46-232, to read:
4 46-232. Payment error rate; reduction; quarterly reports;
5 requirements; auditor general
6 A. ON OR BEFORE DECEMBER 30, 2030, THE DEPARTMENT SHALL REDUCE THE
7 SUPPLEMENTAL NUTRITION ASSISTANCE PROGRAM'S PAYMENT ERROR RATE AS REPORTED
8 BY THE UNITED STATES DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE TO NOT MORE THAN THREE
9 PERCENT.
10 B. BEGINNING IN FISCAL YEAR [2027-2028] [2026-2027] AND EACH FISCAL
11 YEAR THEREAFTER, THE DEPARTMENT SHALL SUBMIT [AN ANNUAL] [A QUARTERLY]
12 REPORT TO THE LEGISLATURE [BY DECEMBER 31] [WITHIN THIRTY DAYS AFTER THE
13 END OF THE QUARTER] THAT DETAILS THE DEPARTMENT'S [MONTHLY] PROGRESS
14 TOWARDS REDUCING THE PAYMENT ERROR RATE AND THAT INCLUDES STRATEGIES AND
15 BARRIERS THAT MAY BE PRESENT IN REDUCING THE PAYMENT ERROR RATE.
16 C. IF THE DEPARTMENT FAILS TO MEET ANNUAL INTERIM TARGETS
17 ESTABLISHED BY RULE OR THE FINAL TARGET AS PRESCRIBED IN SUBSECTION A OF
18 THIS SECTION, THE DEPARTMENT SHALL:
19 1. SUBMIT A CORRECTIVE ACTION PLAN TO THE LEGISLATURE WITHIN SIXTY
20 DAYS THAT INCLUDES AN ANALYSIS OF WHY THE TARGETS WERE NOT MET AND
21 TIMELINES FOR CORRECTING THE PAYMENT ERROR RATE.
22 2. PAY FIFTY PERCENT OF ANY FEDERAL LIABILITIES THAT MAY BE IMPOSED
23 DUE TO THE EXCESS PAYMENT ERROR RATES. THE REMAINING FEDERAL LIABILITIES
24 SHALL BE PAID FROM THE STATE GENERAL FUND.
25 3. IMPLEMENT A CORRECTIVE PLAN [UNDER THE OVERSIGHT OF THE AUDITOR
26 GENERAL]. IF THE DEPARTMENT FAILS TO COMPLY WITH THE CORRECTIVE PLAN, THE
27 DEPARTMENT'S ADMINISTRATIVE FUNDING SHALL BE REDUCED BY TEN PERCENT UNTIL
28 RESOLVED.

Senate Amendments to S.B. 1333
1 D. ON OR BEFORE [DECEMBER 30, 2031] [NOVEMBER 15, 2027], THE
2 AUDITOR GENERAL SHALL COMPLETE A [FORENSIC] [SPECIAL] AUDIT. THE
3 [FORENSIC] [SPECIAL] AUDIT SHALL DETERMINE WHAT FACTORS CONTRIBUTED TO THE
4 PAYMENT ERROR RATE AND SHALL INCLUDE RECOMMENDATIONS TO REDUCE THE PAYMENT
5 ERROR RATE. THE DEPARTMENT SHALL IMPLEMENT THE RECOMMENDATIONS WITHIN
6 TWELVE MONTHS AFTER RECEIVING THE RECOMMENDATIONS FROM THE AUDITOR GENERAL
7 UNLESS THE RECOMMENDATIONS ARE WAIVED BY THE JOINT LEGISLATIVE AUDIT
8 COMMITTEE. [THE AUDITOR GENERAL MAY REQUEST THAT THE DEPARTMENT SUBMIT A
9 WRITTEN STATUS REPORT ON THE DEPARTMENT'S IMPLEMENTATION OF THE SPECIAL
10 AUDIT RECOMMENDATIONS.]
11 E. IF THE DEPARTMENT CORRECTS THE PAYMENT ERROR RATE AHEAD OF
12 SCHEDULE, THE LEGISLATURE MAY ALLOCATE ADDITIONAL FUNDING FOR PROGRAM
13 IMPROVEMENTS.
14 Sec. 2. Delayed repeal
15 Section 46-232, Arizona Revised Statutes, as added by this act, is
16 repealed from and after December 31, 2032.
17 Sec. 3. Short title
18 This act may be cited as the "Oh SNAP Act".
