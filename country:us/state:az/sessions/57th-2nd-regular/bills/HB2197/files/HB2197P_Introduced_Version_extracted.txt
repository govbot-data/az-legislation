HB2197 - 572R - I Ver PREFILED    JAN 09 2026 REFERENCE TITLE: unlawful camping; stock; wildlife; access State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HB 2197 Introduced by Representative Bliss AN ACT amending section 17-308, Arizona Revised Statutes; RELATING to water access. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Section 17-308, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended to read: START_STATUTE17-308. Unlawful camping It is unlawful for a person to camp within one-fourth mile of a natural water hole containing water or a man-made watering facility containing water in such a place that wildlife or domestic stock will be denied access to the only reasonably available water. END_STATUTE
