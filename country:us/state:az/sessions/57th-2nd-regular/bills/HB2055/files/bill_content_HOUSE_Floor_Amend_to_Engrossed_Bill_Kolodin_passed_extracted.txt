ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2055: brackish groundwater recovery program
KOLODIN FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Removes the requirement that WIFA adopt rules to implement this act.
2. Removes the condition that WIFA establish minimum criteria that a project
must meet to qualify for matching monies from the Brackish Groundwater
Recovery Program Fund.
3. Makes conforming changes.
