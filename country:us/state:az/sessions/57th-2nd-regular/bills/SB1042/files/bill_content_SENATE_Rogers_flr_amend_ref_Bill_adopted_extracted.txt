Rogers Floor Amendment

Amendment drafted by: Molly Graver
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
1. Adds stablecoin to the definition of virtual currency.
2. Defines stablecoin.
3. Renames this legislation as the Arizona Strategic Digital Asset Reserve Act.

1 The bill as proposed to be amended is reprinted as follows:
2 Section 1. Title 35, chapter 2, Arizona Revised Statutes, is
3 amended by adding article 2.2, to read:
4 ARTICLE 2.2. [ARIZONA] STRATEGIC [BITCOIN][DIGITAL ASSET] RESERVE
5 35-331. Public monies; investment; virtual currency;
6 reserves; definitions
7 A. IN ADDITION TO THE INVESTMENT AUTHORITY IN ARTICLES 2 AND 2.1 OF
8 THIS CHAPTER, A PUBLIC FUND MAY INVEST NOT MORE THAN TEN PERCENT OF THE
9 PUBLIC MONIES UNDER ITS CONTROL IN VIRTUAL CURRENCY HOLDINGS.
10 B. IF THE UNITED STATES SECRETARY OF THE TREASURY ESTABLISHES A
11 STRATEGIC BITCOIN RESERVE FOR THE STORAGE OF GOVERNMENT BITCOIN HOLDINGS,
12 A PUBLIC FUND MAY STORE THE VIRTUAL CURRENCY HOLDINGS OF THE PUBLIC FUND
13 IN A SECURE SEGREGATED ACCOUNT WITHIN THE STRATEGIC BITCOIN RESERVE.
14 C. FOR THE PURPOSES OF THIS SECTION:
15 1. "PUBLIC FUND" MEANS THE STATE TREASURER OR A RETIREMENT SYSTEM.
16 2. "RETIREMENT SYSTEM" MEANS A RETIREMENT PLAN OR SYSTEM THAT IS
17 ESTABLISHED BY OR PURSUANT TO TITLE 38.
18 3. "VIRTUAL CURRENCY" MEANS A DIGITAL REPRESENTATION OF VALUE THAT
19 FUNCTIONS AS A MEDIUM OF EXCHANGE, A UNIT OF ACCOUNT AND A STORE OF VALUE
20 [OTHER THAN A REPRESENTATION OF THE UNITED STATES DOLLAR OR A FOREIGN
21 CURRENCY] [AND INCLUDES STABLECOINS. VIRTUAL CURRENCY DOES NOT INCLUDE
22 LEGAL TENDER ISSUED BY THE UNITED STATES OR A FOREIGN GOVERNMENT].
23 [4. "STABLECOIN" MEANS A PAYMENT STABLECOIN AS DEFINED IN THE
24 GUIDING AND ESTABLISHING NATIONAL INNOVATION FOR U.S. STABLECOINS (GENIUS)
25 ACT OF 2025 (P.L. 119-27) AND THAT IS ISSUED IN COMPLIANCE WITH ALL
26 APPLICABLE FEDERAL LAWS GOVERNING SUCH ISSUANCE.]
27 Sec. 2. Short title
28 This act may be cited as the "Arizona Strategic [Bitcoin] [Digital
29 Asset] Reserve Act".
