2 Section 1. Title 40, chapter 2, article 6.2, Arizona Revised
3 Statutes, is amended by adding section 40-360.14, to read:
4 40-360.14. Exemption; new plants colocated with extra high
5 load factor customers
6 [A.] NOTWITHSTANDING SECTIONS 40-360.03 AND 40-360.07, A UTILITY,
7 AFTER PROVIDING THIRTY DAYS' WRITTEN NOTICE TO THE COMMISSION AND HOLDING
8 AT LEAST ONE PUBLIC COMMENT SESSION IN THE COUNTY WHERE THE RELEVANT PLANT
9 IS LOCATED, MAY CONSTRUCT A NEW PLANT WITHOUT FILING FOR OR RECEIVING A
10 CERTIFICATE OF ENVIRONMENTAL COMPATIBILITY FOR THE NEW PLANT IF ALL OF THE
11 FOLLOWING CONDITIONS ARE MET:
12 1. THE NEW PLANT IS COLOCATED WITH AN EXTRA HIGH LOAD FACTOR
13 CUSTOMER AND THE EXTRA HIGH LOAD FACTOR CUSTOMER HAS RECEIVED ALL
14 APPLICABLE ZONING ENTITLEMENTS.
15 2. THE UTILITY IS A PUBLIC SERVICE CORPORATION OR PUBLIC POWER
16 ENTITY DOING BUSINESS IN THIS STATE.
17 [B. THE COMMISSION SHALL ADOPT RULES TO IMPLEMENT THIS SECTION THAT
18 INCLUDE A DEFINITION FOR EACH OF THE FOLLOWING TERMS:
19 1. COLOCATED WITH. THE COMMISSION'S DEFINITION MUST SPECIFY ONE OR
20 BOTH OF THE FOLLOWING:
21 (a) THE DISTANCE IN FEET OR PARCELS BETWEEN THE SITE OF AN EXTRA
22 HIGH LOAD FACTOR CUSTOMER AND THE SITE OF A PLANT.
23 (b) THE QUALITY OR NATURE OF THE ELECTRICAL INTERCONNECTION BETWEEN
24 THE EXTRA HIGH LOAD FACTOR CUSTOMER AND THE PLANT.
25 2. EXTRA HIGH LOAD FACTOR CUSTOMER. THE COMMISSION'S DEFINITION:
26 (a) MUST SPECIFY THE PEAK OR AVERAGE ANNUAL OR MONTHLY ENERGY
27 DEMAND OF THE USER THAT IS MEASURED IN MEGAWATTS OR MEGAWATT HOURS.
