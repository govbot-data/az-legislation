20 Sec. 2. Section 28-3154, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended to read:
21 28-3154. Instruction permit for a class D or G license
22 A. A person who is at least fifteen years and six months of age may
23 apply to the department for an instruction permit for a class D or G
24 license. The department may issue an instruction permit to the applicant
25 after the applicant successfully passes all parts of the examination other
26 than the driving test.
27 B. The instruction permit entitles the permittee to drive a motor
28 vehicle requiring a class D or G license on the public highways for twelve
29 EIGHTEEN months when both of the following conditions are met:
30 1. The permittee has the permit in the permittee's immediate
31 possession.
32 2. The permittee is accompanied by a person who has a class A, B, C or
33 D license, who is at least twenty-one years of age and who occupies a seat
34 beside the permittee.
35 C. A permittee may not drive a motor vehicle while using a wireless
36 communication device for any reason except during an emergency in which
37 stopping the motor vehicle is impossible or will create an additional
38 emergency or safety hazard. A peace officer may not stop or issue a citation
39 to a person operating a motor vehicle on a highway in this state for a
40 violation of this subsection unless the peace officer has reasonable cause to
41 believe there is another alleged violation of a motor vehicle law of this
42 state.
