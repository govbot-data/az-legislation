SB1063 - 572R - I Ver PREFILED    DEC 19 2025 REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; Route 70; safety improvements State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SB 1063 Introduced by Senator Rogers AN ACT Appropriating monies to the department of transportation. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; department of transportation; U.S. Route 70; safety improvements The sum of $10,400,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the department of transportation for safety improvements on U.S. Route 70 between Mile Post 255 and Mile Post 301.
