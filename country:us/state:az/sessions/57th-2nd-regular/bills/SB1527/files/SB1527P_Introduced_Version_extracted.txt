SB1527 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: technical correction; ALTCS insurance; exemption State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SB 1527 Introduced by Senator Hatathlie: Representative Sandoval AN ACT amending section 36-2949, Arizona Revised Statutes; relating to the arizona long-term care system. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Section 36-2949, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended to read: START_STATUTE36-2949. Exemption from insurance law To the extent that services are provided pursuant to this article, a provider or program contractor is not subject to the provisions of title 20. END_STATUTE
