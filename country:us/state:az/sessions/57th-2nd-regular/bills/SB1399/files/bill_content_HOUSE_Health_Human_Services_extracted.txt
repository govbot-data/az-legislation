2 Section 1. Title 36, chapter 29, article 1, Arizona Revised
3 Statutes, is amended by adding section 36-2904.01, to read:
4 36-2904.01. Prepaid capitated contracts; expenditures; annual
5 report
6 ON OR BEFORE DECEMBER 1 OF EACH YEAR, THE ADMINISTRATION SHALL
7 ANNUALLY REPORT TO THE JOINT LEGISLATIVE BUDGET COMMITTEE, THE GOVERNOR,
8 THE PRESIDENT OF THE SENATE, THE SPEAKER OF THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
9 AND THE MEMBERS OF THE SENATE AND HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES HEALTH AND
10 HUMAN SERVICES COMMITTEES, OR THEIR SUCCESSOR COMMITTEES, IN AGGREGATE FOR
11 ALL NONGOVERNMENT ENTITIES THAT HAVE A PREPAID CAPITATED CONTRACT WITH THE
12 ADMINISTRATION, THE PERCENTAGE OF MONIES IN THE PREVIOUS CONTRACT YEAR:
13 1. THAT WAS SPENT ON DIRECT PAYMENTS TO PROVIDERS FOR SERVICES.
14 2. THAT WAS SPENT ON ADMINISTRATIVE COSTS FOR EACH OF THE
15 FOLLOWING:
16 (a) HEALTH CARE QUALITY IMPROVEMENT ADMINISTRATIVE ACTIVITIES.
17 (b) PROGRAM INTEGRITY, INCLUDING FRAUD, WASTE AND ABUSE PREVENTION
18 EXPENSES.
19 (c) TAXES, LICENSES AND REGULATORY FEES.
20 (d) COMMUNITY REINVESTMENT.
21 (e) ALL OTHER ADMINISTRATIVE ACTIVITIES.

2 Statutes, is amended by adding section 36-2930.07, to read:
3 36-2930.07. Managed care organizations; high-volume service
4 providers; termination without cause; written
5 notice; determination of network adequacy;
6 definitions
7 [A. BEFORE A MANAGED CARE ORGANIZATION MAY TERMINATE A HIGH-VOLUME
8 SERVICE PROVIDER'S CONTRACT WITHOUT CAUSE, THE MANAGED CARE ORGANIZATION
9 SHALL SUBMIT WRITTEN NOTICE TO THE ADMINISTRATION AT LEAST NINETY DAYS
10 BEFORE THE PROPOSED EFFECTIVE DATE OF THE TERMINATION.
11 B. THE NOTICE TO THE ADMINISTRATION PURSUANT TO SUBSECTION A OF
12 THIS SECTION SHALL INCLUDE ALL OF THE FOLLOWING:
13 1. DOCUMENTATION SHOWING THAT THE SERVICE PROVIDER IS A HIGH-VOLUME
14 SERVICE PROVIDER, INCLUDING THE SPECIFIC DATA SOURCES, CALCULATION
15 METHODOLOGY AND METRICS USED.
16 2. A NETWORK ADEQUACY STUDY PERFORMED BY THE MANAGED CARE
17 ORGANIZATION THAT EVALUATES AND DOCUMENTS, AT A MINIMUM:
18 (a) CURRENT AND PROJECTED POSTTERMINATION SERVICE
19 PROVIDER-TO-ENROLLEE RATIOS BY SERVICE PROVIDER TYPE AND GEOGRAPHIC
20 SERVICE AREA.
21 (b) CURRENT APPOINTMENT WAIT TIME PERFORMANCE FOR THE AFFECTED
22 SERVICES.
23 (c) THE PATIENT VOLUME AND GEOGRAPHIC DISTRIBUTION OF THE AFFECTED
24 SERVICES.
25 (d) THE IMPACT ON MEMBERS WHO ARE RECEIVING BEHAVIORAL HEALTH
26 SERVICES ASSOCIATED WITH THE MEMBER'S DISABILITY.
27 (e) THE CUMULATIVE EFFECT OF ALL PENDING OR RECENTLY COMPLETED
28 WITHOUT-CAUSE TERMINATIONS OF HIGH-VOLUME SERVICE PROVIDERS BY THE MANAGED
29 CARE ORGANIZATION.
30 (f) ANY ADDITIONAL FACTORS THE MANAGED CARE ORGANIZATION OR THE
31 ADMINISTRATION IDENTIFIES AS RELEVANT TO NETWORK ADEQUACY.
32 3. THE MANAGED CARE ORGANIZATION'S PRELIMINARY ASSESSMENT OF THE
33 IMPACT OF THE TERMINATION ON NETWORK ADEQUACY.
34 4. ANY MITIGATION MEASURES THE MANAGED CARE ORGANIZATION IS
35 PROPOSING.
36 C. IF THERE IS A DISCREPANCY BETWEEN THE MANAGED CARE ORGANIZATION
37 AND THE SERVICE PROVIDER OF WHETHER THE SERVICE PROVIDER IS A HIGH-VOLUME
38 SERVICE PROVIDER, THE MANAGED CARE ORGANIZATION SHALL NOTIFY THE
39 ADMINISTRATION AND PROVIDE DOCUMENTATION SUPPORTING THE MANAGED CARE
40 ORGANIZATION'S DECISION NOT TO FILE THE WRITTEN NOTICE PURSUANT TO
41 SUBSECTION A OF THIS SECTION. THE ADMINISTRATION SHALL REVIEW THE
42 DOCUMENTATION AND DECIDE WHETHER THE MANAGED CARE ORGANIZATION IS REQUIRED
43 TO FILE WRITTEN NOTICE PURSUANT TO SUBSECTION A OF THIS SECTION. THE
44 ADMINISTRATION SHALL NOTIFY THE SERVICE PROVIDER OF THE DECISION.
45 D. A MANAGED CARE ORGANIZATION MAY NOT TERMINATE A HIGH-VOLUME
46 SERVICE PROVIDER WITHOUT CAUSE UNTIL THE ADMINISTRATION HAS REVIEWED THE
47 MANAGED CARE ORGANIZATION'S NETWORK ADEQUACY STUDY AND HAS PROVIDED
-2-

1 WRITTEN CONFIRMATION THAT APPLICABLE NETWORK ADEQUACY STANDARDS WILL
2 CONTINUE TO BE MET AFTER THE TERMINATION OF THE HIGH-VOLUME SERVICE
3 PROVIDER. THE ADMINISTRATION SHALL COMPLETE ITS REVIEW OF THE NETWORK
4 ADEQUACY STUDY WITHIN TEN BUSINESS DAYS AFTER RECEIVING THE NOTICE
5 PURSUANT TO SUBSECTION A OF THIS SECTION.
6 E. IF THE ADMINISTRATION AGREES WITH THE MANAGED CARE
7 ORGANIZATION'S DECISION BASED ON THE FINDINGS PROVIDED, THE ADMINISTRATION
8 SHALL BOTH:
9 1. POST THE MANAGED CARE ORGANIZATION'S COMPLETE NETWORK ADEQUACY
10 STUDY, INCLUDING METHODOLOGY, DATA SOURCES, METRICS AND FINDINGS, AND THE
11 ADMINISTRATION'S DETERMINATION ON THE ADMINISTRATION'S PUBLIC WEBSITE AND
12 SEND A COPY TO THE CHAIRPERSONS OF THE SENATE AND HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
13 HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES COMMITTEES, OR THEIR SUCCESSOR COMMITTEES, AND
14 THE GOVERNOR'S OFFICE.
15 2. PROVIDE WRITTEN NOTICE OF THE ADMINISTRATION'S DETERMINATION TO
16 THE MANAGED CARE ORGANIZATION.
17 F. IF THE ADMINISTRATION DETERMINES THAT NETWORK ADEQUACY STANDARDS
18 WOULD NOT BE MET, THE MANAGED CARE ORGANIZATION MAY NOT PROCEED WITH THE
19 HIGH-VOLUME SERVICE PROVIDER'S TERMINATION UNLESS THE MANAGED CARE
20 ORGANIZATION DEMONSTRATES TO THE ADMINISTRATION'S SATISFACTION THAT
21 NETWORK ADEQUACY STANDARDS WILL BE MET.
22 G. IF A MANAGED CARE ORGANIZATION DECLINES TO CONTRACT WITH A
23 SERVICE PROVIDER OR POTENTIAL SERVICE PROVIDER DUE TO A DETERMINATION OF
24 NETWORK ADEQUACY, THE MANAGED CARE ORGANIZATION SHALL COMPLETE AND SEND TO
25 THE ADMINISTRATION A NETWORK ADEQUACY STUDY, INCLUDING THE SPECIFIC DATA
26 SOURCES, CALCULATION METHODOLOGY AND METRICS USED TO SUPPORT THE DENIAL
27 DECISION. THE STUDY MUST INCLUDE, AT A MINIMUM:
28 1. SERVICE PROVIDER-TO-ENROLLEE RATIOS BY SERVICE PROVIDER TYPE.
29 2. CURRENT APPOINTMENT WAIT TIME PERFORMANCE FOR THE SERVICES THAT
30 THE APPLICANT WAS TO PROVIDE.
31 3. ALL PENDING OR RECENTLY COMPLETED TERMINATIONS OF HIGH-VOLUME
32 SERVICE PROVIDERS WITHOUT CAUSE IN THE GEOGRAPHIC AREA THAT THE APPLICANT
33 WOULD HAVE SERVED.
34 4. ANY ADDITIONAL FACTORS THE MANAGED CARE ORGANIZATION OR THE
35 ADMINISTRATION IDENTIFIES AS RELEVANT TO NETWORK ADEQUACY.
36 H. SUBSECTION G OF THIS SECTION APPLIES ONLY TO HIGH-VOLUME SERVICE
37 PROVIDERS.
38 I. THE ADMINISTRATION SHALL POST THE MANAGED CARE ORGANIZATION'S
39 COMPLETE NETWORK ADEQUACY STUDY SUBMITTED PURSUANT TO SUBSECTION G OF THIS
40 SECTION ON THE ADMINISTRATION'S PUBLIC WEBSITE AND SEND A COPY TO THE
41 CHAIRPERSONS OF THE SENATE AND HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES HEALTH AND HUMAN
42 SERVICES COMMITTEES, OR THEIR SUCCESSOR COMMITTEES, AND THE GOVERNOR'S
43 OFFICE.

1 J. FOR THE PURPOSES OF THIS SECTION:
2 1. "HIGH-VOLUME SERVICE PROVIDER" MEANS A SERVICE PROVIDER THAT
3 MEETS EITHER OF THE FOLLOWING:
4 (a) DELIVERED AT LEAST TEN PERCENT OF ANY SPECIFIC SERVICE FOR A
5 MANAGED CARE ORGANIZATION IN THE PRECEDING STATE FISCAL YEAR.
6 (b) EMPLOYS MORE THAN TEN PERCENT OF THE ACTIVELY LICENSED
7 BEHAVIORAL HEALTH PROVIDERS IN THIS STATE.
8 2. "MANAGED CARE ORGANIZATION" MEANS A CONTRACTOR THAT HAS A
9 PREPAID CAPITATED CONTRACT WITH THE ADMINISTRATION OR A REGIONAL
10 BEHAVIORAL HEALTH AUTHORITY.
11 3. "SERVICE PROVIDER" MEANS AN ORGANIZATION OR MENTAL HEALTH
12 PROFESSIONAL THAT MEETS THE CRITERIA ESTABLISHED BY THE ADMINISTRATION AND
13 THAT HAS A CONTRACT WITH THE ADMINISTRATION OR A REGIONAL BEHAVIORAL
14 HEALTH AUTHORITY.]>>
