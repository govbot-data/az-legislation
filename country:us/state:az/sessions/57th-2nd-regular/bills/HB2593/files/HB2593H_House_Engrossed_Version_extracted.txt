HB2593 - 572R - H Ver House Engrossed appropriation; psychiatry access lines State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HOUSE BILL 2593 AN ACT Appropriating monies to the University of Arizona. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; university of Arizona; perinatal and pediatric psychiatry access lines; exemption A. The sum of $1,500,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the university of Arizona for direct costs of the perinatal and pediatric psychiatry access line. B. The appropriation made in subsection A of this section is exempt from the provisions of section 35-190, Arizona Revised Statutes, relating to lapsing of appropriations.
