SB1454 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: technical correction; bulk food; applicability State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SB 1454 Introduced by Senator Farnsworth AN ACT amending section 36-972, Arizona Revised Statutes; relating to pure food control. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Section 36-972, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended to read: START_STATUTE36-972. Applicability of article This article applies to any person who displays,  or sells or offers for sale at retail any bulk food. END_STATUTE
