ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
SB 1497: school districts; insurance quotes
GRESS FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Deletes language that excludes a school district that participates in a self-
insurance program that meets specified criteria from the prescribed school
district self-insurance program quote requirement.
