COMMITTEE ON REGULATORY AFFAIRS AND GOVERNMENT EFFICIENCY
SENATE AMENDMENTS TO S.B. 1366
(Reference to printed bill)
1 Strike everything after the enacting clause and insert:
2 "Section 1. Public property towing and impound practices
3 study committee; membership; duties; report;
4 delayed repeal
5 A. The public property towing and impound practices study committee
6 is established consisting of the following members:
7 1. Two members of the senate who are appointed by the president of
8 the senate. The president of the senate shall designate one of these
9 members to serve as cochairperson of the committee.
10 2. Two members of the house of representatives who are appointed by
11 the speaker of the house of representatives. The speaker of the house of
12 representatives shall designate one of these members to serve as
13 cochairperson of the committee.
14 3. One member who represents the public property towing industry
15 and who is appointed by the president of the senate.
16 4. One member who represents a local public safety agency and who
17 is appointed by the governor.
18 5. One member who represents the department of public safety and
19 who is appointed by the governor.
20 B. Committee members are not eligible to receive compensation, but
21 members are eligible for reimbursement of expenses under title 38, chapter
22 4, article 2, Arizona Revised Statutes.
23 C. The committee shall:
24 1. Review current fees, rates, fines and administrative charges for
25 the department of public safety initiated accident, arrest, breakdown,
26 disabled and abandoned vehicles tows.
27 2. Examine the qualifications and operational standards for public
28 property towing companies to be considered qualified for
29 government-authorized towing of vehicles.
30 3. Evaluate minimum insurance requirements and background check
31 standards for towing company owners and operators.
32 4. Review the impact of the laws of this state and rules and
33 department of public safety policies relating to public property towing.
34 D. The committee may:
35 1. Request information and data from state and local agencies
36 regarding towing contracts and impound statistics.
37 2. Hold public hearings to receive testimony from industry experts,
38 law enforcement agencies and the public.

5 report to the secretary of state.
6 F. This section is repealed from and after September 30, 2027."
