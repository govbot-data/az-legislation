FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2136: civil terrorism; disorderly conduct; subversion
WAY FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Defines intent to coerce or intimidate a civilian population and intent to
subvert the government.
2. Outlines what acts and evidence may be used to establish when subversion is
occurring.
3. Asserts that advocacy, protest, dissent or political opposition without intent
to use unlawful force does not constitute subversion.
