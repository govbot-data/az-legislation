ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2136: civil terrorism; disorderly conduct; subversion
KOLODIN FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Redefines the elements of civil terrorism and subversion.
2. Strikes the enhanced penalty for the offence of disorderly conduct.
3. Removes riot, felony disorderly conduct and subversion from the list of predicate
offenses for racketeering charges.
4. Adds language specifying that the civil terrorism and subversion statutes are to be
construed by any trier of fact as being written to maximally safeguard the rights of
the people under the U.S. and Arizona constitutions.
