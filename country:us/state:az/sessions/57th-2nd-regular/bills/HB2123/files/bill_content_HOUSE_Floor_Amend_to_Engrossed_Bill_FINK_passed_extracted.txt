ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2123: bullion depository; establishment
FINK FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Stipulates that the State Treasurer must implement and discharge the duties
for the depository's administration in a manner consistent with a prudent
investor.
2. Redefines transactional gold or silver.
3. Defines additional terms.
4. Makes technical, clarifying and conforming changes.

18 [1.] [3.] "BULLION":
19 (a) MEANS REFINED PRECIOUS GOLD METAL AND SILVER METAL THAT IS:

21 (ii) VALUED PRIMARILY BASED ON THE METAL CONTENT OF THE GOLD OR
22 SILVER AND NOT ON THE FORM OR FUNCTION OF THE GOLD OR SILVER.

24 SUBDIVISION:
25 (i) "GOLD COIN" MEANS GOLD METAL THAT IS IN THE SHAPE OF A BAR OR

1 6-1802. Arizona bullion depository; administration;
2 electronic payment system; annual report
3 A. THE ARIZONA BULLION DEPOSITORY IS ESTABLISHED. THE STATE
4 TREASURER SHALL ADMINISTER THE DEPOSITORY. THE DEPOSITORY SERVES AS THE
5 CUSTODIAN, GUARDIAN AND ADMINISTRATOR OF BULLION AND SPECIE THAT MAY BE
6 TRANSFERRED TO OR OTHERWISE ACQUIRED BY THIS STATE OR RESIDENTS OF THIS
7 STATE. THE DEPOSITORY SHALL PROVIDE A SECURE LOCATION FOR STORING
8 BULLION. [RESIDENTS OF THIS STATE] [AN ACCOUNT HOLDER] MAY USE THE
9 DEPOSITORY TO DEPOSIT BULLION FOR ELECTRONIC USE.
10 B. THE STATE TREASURER MAY CONTRACT WITH A THIRD PARTY TO
11 ADMINISTER THE DAILY OPERATIONS OF THE DEPOSITORY [AND IMPLEMENT THE
12 DEPOSITORY SECURITY, STORAGE, TRANSACTIONAL AND ADMINISTRATIVE PROCEDURES
13 IN ACCORDANCE WITH THIS CHAPTER AND RULES ADOPTED PURSUANT TO THIS
14 CHAPTER].
15 C. THE STATE TREASURER SHALL CONTRACT WITH A THIRD-PARTY VENDOR IN
16 THIS STATE TO AUTHORIZE AND APPROVE ONE OR MORE ELECTRONIC PAYMENT
17 SYSTEMS. THE THIRD-PARTY VENDOR MUST COMPLY WITH ALL STATE AND FEDERAL
18 FINANCIAL LAWS. THE INFORMATION OF RESIDENTS OF THIS STATE WHO USE THE
19 ELECTRONIC PAYMENT SYSTEM MAY NOT BE USED IN ANY SOCIAL CREDIT SCORING
20 SYSTEM.
21 D. FOR THE PURPOSES OF SUBSECTION B AND C OF THIS SECTION, THE
22 THIRD PARTY SHALL PROVIDE VAULT FACILITIES WITHIN THE UNITED STATES FOR
23 THE STORAGE OF BULLION THAT:
24 1. COMPLIES WITH THE REQUIREMENTS PRESCRIBED BY THE LONDON BULLION
25 MARKET ASSOCIATION OR AN EQUIVALENT BEST PRACTICES GUIDELINES.
26 2. PROVIDES ACCOUNTS THAT DO BOTH OF THE FOLLOWING:
27 (a) HOLD BULLION.
28 (b) ALLOW ACCOUNT HOLDERS TO BUY, SELL, SAVE OR SPEND BULLION.
29 E. THE DEPOSITORY SHALL ENSURE THAT EACH DEPOSIT MADE IN THE
30 DEPOSITORY IS INSURED UNDER AN ALL-RISK INSURANCE POLICY THAT IS ISSUED BY
31 A NONGOVERNMENTAL OPERATED INSURER FOR ONE HUNDRED PERCENT OF THE FULL
32 REPLACEMENT VALUE OF THE DEPOSIT.
33 F. ON OR BEFORE JUNE 30 OF EACH YEAR, THE STATE TREASURER SHALL
34 SUBMIT A REPORT TO THE GOVERNOR, THE PRESIDENT OF THE SENATE AND THE
35 SPEAKER OF THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES THAT INCLUDES ALL OF THE
36 FOLLOWING:
37 1. THE STATUS AND OPERATIONS OF THE DEPOSITORY.
38 2. THE IMPLEMENTATION AND USAGE OF THE ELECTRONIC PAYMENT SYSTEM.
39 3. THE ECONOMIC IMPACT OF RECOGNIZING GOLD AND SILVER AS LEGAL
40 TENDER.
41 6-1803. Standard; prudent investor
42 THE STATE TREASURER [MAY ADOPT RULES AS NECESSARY TO IMPLEMENT THIS
43 CHAPTER. THE RULES MUST:
44 1. ENSURE THE SECURITY OF THE SPECIE, BULLION, TRANSACTIONAL
45 CURRENCY, TRANSACTIONS AND RELATED DATA.
46 2. PREVENT FRAUD.

6 6-1804. Legal tender; gold and silver specie
7 A. GOLD AND SILVER SPECIE ARE RECOGNIZED AS A LEGAL TENDER IN THIS
8 STATE AND MAY BE USED FOR THE PAYMENT OF EITHER OF THE FOLLOWING:
9 1. IF MUTUALLY AGREED ON, DEBTS BETWEEN PRIVATE PARTIES.
10 2. IF THIS STATE OR A POLITICAL SUBDIVISION OF THIS STATE AGREES TO
11 ACCEPT SPECIE AS A FORM OF PAYMENT OF TAXES, FEES OR OTHER OBLIGATIONS
12 OWED TO THIS STATE OR A POLITICAL SUBDIVISION OF THIS STATE.
13 B. THE PURCHASE, SALE OR EXCHANGE OF ANY TYPE OF SPECIE OR BULLION
14 DOES NOT CREATE ANY TAX LIABILITY. EXCHANGING ONE FORM OF LEGAL TENDER
15 FOR ANOTHER FORM OF LEGAL TENDER DOES NOT CREATE TAX LIABILITY.
16 C. A PERSON OR ENTITY IN THIS STATE MAY NOT BE COMPELLED TO ACCEPT
17 GOLD OR SILVER SPECIE AS A FORM OF PAYMENT EXCEPT AS PROVIDED IN THIS
18 SECTION.
