FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
1. Specifies that the prohibition against operating a commercial vehicle without evidence of legal
presence applies to a person who is issued a nondomiciled commercial driver license.
2. Modifies the evidence an outlined person may provide or which may be requested by a law
enforcement officer to include evidence of lawful immigration status and to remove a commercial
driver license that is issued by another state or a foreign jurisdiction.
3. Requires a law enforcement officer or law enforcement agency, if an outlined person operating a
commercial vehicle fails to provide the prescribed provide evidence, to:
a) impose an outlined civil penalty;
b) issue a citation; and
c) report the violation to the jurisdiction that issued the person's commercial driver license and
the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration.
4. Allows, rather than requires, a law enforcement agency to impound a commercial motor vehicle
and the vehicle's contents if the vehicle is operated by a person who fails to provide the prescribed
evidence.
