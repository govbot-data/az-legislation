FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB4018: technical correction; public records
S/E: Sheriff; exclusive authority; auxiliary
MARSHALL FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Allows the sheriff to request and use the aid of volunteer posse and reserve
organizations located in the county for duties lawfully undertaken by the
sheriff pursuant to and consistent with the constitution and laws of this state.
2. Removes affiliated auxiliaries from the organizations subject to a sheriff's
regulatory authority.
3. Clarifies the sheriff has exclusive authority to regulate the organization of,
and equipment used by, posse or reserve organizations that are activated by
the sheriff and acting within the sheriff's authority and written policy in
support of the sheriff's office.
