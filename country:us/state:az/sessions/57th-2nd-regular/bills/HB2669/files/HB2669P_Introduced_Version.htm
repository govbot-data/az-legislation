AN ACT

 

amending title 40, chapter 4, article 3, Arizona Revised Statutes, by adding section 40-850; relating to railroads.

 

 

(TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE)

 

Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona:

Section 1. Title 40, chapter 4, article 3, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended by adding section 40-850, to read:

START_STATUTE40-850. Railroad; train length

A railroad operating in this state may not run or be allowed to run on any part of a main track or branch line a train that exceeds eight thousand feet in length. END_STATUTE
