HOUSE FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
Bill Number: HB 2321
Floor Amendment
Contreras P
The P. Contreras amendment dated 2/18/2026 at 7:09pm:
1. Clarifies that it is within 30 days after a child is adjudicated a dependent child
and is placed in the care of DCS, that the department place a security freeze
on their record or credit report
2. Mandates the freeze stay on the child report until they reach 16 years of age.
3. Stipulates when a child reaches 16 years of age the child can elect to have the
freeze remain or removed
4. Requires DCS to notify the parent or guardian of a child when the freeze has
been placed on the child's record or credit report and must notify the parent or
guardian the freeze is on the child's account when they leave the custody of
DCS
5. Asserts the notification include information on the process for removing the
security freeze when the child leaves the care of DCS
6. Appropriates $100,000 and one FTE from the state general fund in FY27 to
DCS to assist with placing and removing security freezes on the record or credit
report of dependent children to assist with any credit fraud issues related to
the credit of a dependent child who is in the care of DCS
7. States it is the legislatures intent that the one FTE position be considered
ongoing in future years.
