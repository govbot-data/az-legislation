2 Section 1. Section 42-13302, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended

4 42-13302. Determining limited value in cases of
5 modifications, omissions, changes and splits,
6 subdivisions and consolidations; definitions
7 A. In the following circumstances the limited property value shall
8 be established at a level or percentage of full cash value that is
9 comparable to that of other properties of the same or a similar use or
10 classification:
11 1. Property that was erroneously totally or partially omitted from
12 the property tax rolls in the preceding tax year, except as a result of
13 this section.
14 2. Property for which a change in physical, objectively verifiable
15 use has occurred on the property since the preceding tax year. For the
16 purposes of this paragraph and section 42-16002:
17 (a) A change in the occupant or classification of a single-family
18 residence is not a change in use, in and of itself.
19 (b) A change in the classification of a guest ranch as defined in
20 section 42-13551 is not a change in use, in and of itself.
21 3. Property that has been modified by construction, destruction or
22 demolition since the preceding valuation year such that the total value of
23 the modification is equal to or greater than fifteen percent of the full
24 cash value.
25 4. Property that has been split, subdivided or consolidated from
26 January 1 through September 30 of the valuation year, except for cases
27 that result from an action initiated by a governmental entity AS PROVIDED
28 IN SUBSECTIONS B AND C OF THIS SECTION.
