HB2931 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: civil rights advisory board; continuation State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HB 2931 Introduced by Representatives Travers: Contreras L, Volk AN ACT REPEALING SECTION 41-3026.11, ARIZONA REVISED STATUTES; AMENDING TITLE 41, CHAPTER 27, ARTICLE 2, ARIZONA REVISED STATUTES, BY ADDING SECTION 41-3034.11; RELATING TO THE arizona civil rights advisory board. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Repeal Section 41-3026.11, Arizona Revised Statutes, is repealed. Sec. 2. Title 41, chapter 27, article 2, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended by adding section 41-3034.11, to read: START_STATUTE41-3034.11. Arizona civil rights advisory board; termination July 1, 2034 A. The Arizona civil rights advisory board terminates on July 1, 2034. B. Title 41, chapter 9, article 1 and this section are repealed on January 1, 2035.END_STATUTE Sec. 3. Purpose Pursuant to section 41-2955, subsection B, Arizona Revised Statutes, the legislature continues the Arizona civil rights advisory board to conduct hearings and investigations regarding charges of alleged discrimination as prescribed by state law and to perform those other functions as prescribed by law. Sec. 4. Retroactivity Sections 1 and 2 of this act apply retroactively to from and after July 1, 2026.
