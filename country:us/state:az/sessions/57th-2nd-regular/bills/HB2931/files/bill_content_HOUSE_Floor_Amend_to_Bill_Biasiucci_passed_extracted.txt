ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2931: civil rights advisory board; continuation
BIASIUCCI FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Separates the enabling statute for the Civil Rights Advisory Board from the
Civil Rights Division of the Department of Law.
2. Decreases the continuation length for the Board from 2035 to 2031.
