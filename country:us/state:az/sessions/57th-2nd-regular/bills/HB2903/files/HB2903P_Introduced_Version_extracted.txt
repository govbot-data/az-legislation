HB2903 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: social credit; use; prohibition State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HB 2903 Introduced by Representative Montenegro AN ACT amending title 6, chapter 2, article 1, Arizona Revised Statutes, by adding section 6-194; relating to banks and financial institutions. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Title 6, chapter 2, article 1, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended by adding section 6-194, to read: START_STATUTE6-194. Social credit score; lending; prohibition This state may not require a bank or financial institution to use a social credit score when the bank or financial institution evaluates whether to lend money to a customer. END_STATUTE
