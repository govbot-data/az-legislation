ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2382: school district superintendents; secondary employment
GRESS FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Defines secondary employment to mean any work, service or business activity
for which an individual receives monetary or other compensation as an
employee, contractor, business owner or business partner.
2. Excludes from secondary employment:
a. the discharge of an individual's duties as school district superintendent;
and
b. passive investment activity.
