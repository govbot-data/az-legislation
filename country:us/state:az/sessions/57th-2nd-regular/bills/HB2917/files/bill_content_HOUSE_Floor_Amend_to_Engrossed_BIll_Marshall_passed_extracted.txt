FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2917: firefighter cancer registry
MARSHALL FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Requires DHS to obtain written consent before collecting specified
information from a firefighter who is eligible to be included in the firefighter
cancer registry, or their authorized designee if the firefighter is deceased.
2. Allows, instead of requires, a firefighter diagnosed with cancer while
employed as a firefighter to participate in the firefighter cancer registry.
