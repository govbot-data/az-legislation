HB2632 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: landlords; tenant's marijuana use State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HB 2632 Introduced by Representatives Austin: Garcia, Sandoval, Villegas AN ACT amending title 33, chapter 10, article 1, Arizona Revised Statutes, by adding section 33-1317.01; relating to residential landlords and tenants. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Title 33, chapter 10, article 1, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended by adding section 33-1317.01, to read: START_STATUTE33-1317.01. Tenant use of marijuana; rental agreement; termination prohibited A landlord may not terminate a tenant's rental agreement because the tenant uses marijuana. END_STATUTE
