Werner Floor Amendment
Reference to: HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES
Committee amendment
Amendment drafted by: Leg. Council
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
1. Requires the Department of Child Safety (DCS) to submit the results of random quarterly drug
screenings of group foster home employees to the chairpersons of the Health and Human Services
Committees of the Senate and House of Representatives, or their successor committees.
2. Requires DCS, rather than the Department of Health Services, to develop and implement outlined
rules, policies and procedures for juvenile group homes relating to safety and technology protocols,
inspections and resident privacy.
3. Establishes minimum security standard requirements for juvenile group homes.
