FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2611: group homes; safety; training; rights
BLACKMAN FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Modifies statute and provisions relating to punishing a child for reporting a
violation of their personal rights as outlined.
2. Grants a child in foster care or kinship foster care the right to report a
violation of statutorily prescribed personal rights without fear of interference,
coercion, intimidation or retaliation.
3. Removes the exemption for punishing a child for reporting a violation of their
personal rights.
4. Prohibits a foster parent, kinship caregiver, foster home, group foster home,
group home or an employee, contractor or agent of a home or facility where a
child under the Arizona Department of Child Safety (DCS) is placed from
threatening or causing retaliatory action against a child who reports or
participates in an investigation of a suspected violation of their statutorily
prescribed rights.
5. Specifies that the protection right applies to a child who participated in an
inquiry or investigation of a violation of their statutorily prescribed personal
rights, who, in good faith or with reasonable belief that the report was true.
6. Defines retaliatory action.
7. Removes the prohibition of group foster home employees from carrying guns.
