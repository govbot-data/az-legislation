Bill Number: HB 2673
Floor Amendment
Hernandez C
The C Hernandez Amendment dated 3/3/2026 at 12:24pm:
1. Creates a study committee on inmate mental health services
2. Outlines the membership:
a. Appointed by Speaker
i. 2 members of the House who are from different political parties.
One designated as cochair
ii. 1 member who represents the regional behavioral health
authority
iii. 1 member with a family member who is currently in jail or prison
and who has a mental health diagnosis
iv. 1 member who is a prosecutor with a city, town or county or this
state and who prosecutes defendants with mental illness
v. 1 member who represents a law enforcement agency from a
county with a population of less than 1 million persons
b. Appointed by Senate President
i. 2 members of the Senate who are from different political parties.
One designated as cochair
ii. 1 member who is employed by a special health care district
iii. 1 member who is an attorney with experience representing
mentally ill defendants who are in jail or who are sentenced to
prison
iv. 1 member who represents a law enforcement agency from a
county with a population of one million persons or more
c. Director of the state department of corrections or the director's designee
d. Director of AHCCCS or the director's designee
3. Requires the committee meet at least once each year at a date and time
determined by the committee
4. States the members are not eligible for compensation but are eligible for
reimbursement
5. Outlines the committee must:
