8 Sec. 3. Section 32-1158.02, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended to
9 read:
10 32-1158.02. Residential construction contracts
11 A. For residential repair or replacement of damage resulting
12 directly from a catastrophic storm in a specific area that is designated
13 by an insurer, in addition to the requirements of section 32-1158, a
14 licensed contractor who enters into a contract for the repair or
15 replacement of a residential roof or other repair or replacement within
16 the scope of the regulation of this chapter with a purchaser who resides
17 in the residence or who intends to reside in the residence after the
18 completion of the contract shall include IN THE CONTRACT at least the
19 following provisions:
20 1. A statement in at least ten-point bold type in substantially the
21 following form: "You may cancel this contract at any time within
22 seventy-two hours after you have been notified that your insurer has
23 denied your claim to pay for the goods and services to be provided under
24 this contract."
25 2. A statement in at least ten-point bold type in substantially the
26 following form: "You may cancel this contract at any time, for any
27 reason, within four business days after signing this contract."
28 3. A copy of a repair estimate that contains the following
29 disclosures:
30 (a) A precise description and location of all damage claimed on the
31 repair estimate.
32 (b) For roofing repair or replacement, a detailed description of
33 the work to be done, including the square footage of the repair area or
34 the replacement area.
35 (c) If the damaged areas are not included in the repair estimate, a
36 specification of those areas and any reason for their exclusion from the
37 repair estimate.
38 (d) Whether or not the property was inspected before the
39 preparation of the estimate and the nature of that inspection,
40 specifically whether the roof was physically accessed.
41 (e) That a contractor has made no assurances that the claimed loss
42 will be covered by an insurance policy.
43 (f) That the policyholder is responsible for payment for any work
44 performed if the insurer should deny payment or coverage for any part of
45 the loss.
