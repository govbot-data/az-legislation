21 (i) A licensed attorney-at-law who is qualified to practice law in
22 this state.
23 (ii) A salaried employee of an insurer or of a managing general
24 agent. For the purposes of this item, "salaried employee" means an
25 employee whose compensation is not contingent on the outcome of a claim

10 32-1158.02. Residential construction contracts
11 A. For residential repair or replacement of damage resulting
12 directly from a catastrophic storm in a specific area that is designated
13 by an insurer, in addition to the requirements of section 32-1158, a
14 licensed contractor who enters into a contract for the repair or
15 replacement of a residential roof or other repair or replacement within
16 the scope of the regulation of this chapter with a purchaser who resides
17 in the residence or who intends to reside in the residence after the
18 completion of the contract shall include IN THE CONTRACT at least the

20 1. A statement in at least ten-point bold type in substantially the
21 following form: "You may cancel this contract at any time within

24 this contract."

29 disclosures:
30 (a) A precise description and location of all damage claimed on the
