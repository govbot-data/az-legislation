4 13-3730. Unlawful patient brokering; classification;
5 definitions
6 A. It is unlawful for a person, including a health care provider,
7 behavioral health professional, health care institution or sober living
8 home, to offer, pay, solicit or receive any commission, bonus, rebate,
9 kickback or bribe, directly or indirectly, in cash or in kind, or engage
10 in any split-fee arrangement, in any form whatsoever, in return for
11 either:
12 1. Referring patients or clients to or from a sober living home or
13 a substance use disorder treatment facility.
14 2. Accepting or acknowledging the enrollment of a patient or client
15 for substance use disorder services at a sober living home.
16 B. EXCEPT AS OTHERWISE EXPRESSLY ALLOWED BY FEDERAL LAW, IT IS
17 UNLAWFUL FOR A HEALTH CARE PROVIDER, HEALTH CARE INSTITUTION OR DRUG
18 MANUFACTURER TO:
19 1. PAY, DIRECTLY OR INDIRECTLY, THE PREMIUMS OR SUBSCRIPTION FEES
20 FOR AN ENROLLEE UNDER ANY HEALTH BENEFITS PLAN ISSUED BY A HEALTH CARE
21 INSURER IN THIS STATE.
22 2. STEER, ADVISE, RECOMMEND OR ENCOURAGE AN ENROLLEE TO TERMINATE
23 COVERAGE UNDER AN EXISTING HEALTH BENEFITS PLAN OR TO CHANGE A HEALTH
24 BENEFITS PLAN DUE TO ANY HEALTH STATUS-RELATED FACTOR OF THE ENROLLEE
25 UNLESS THE PERSON STEERING, ADVISING, RECOMMENDING OR ENCOURAGING THE
26 ENROLLEE IS A LICENSED INSURANCE PRODUCER IN THIS STATE.
27 B. C. A violation of SUBSECTION A OR B OF this section is:
28 1. A class 3 felony if the consideration has a value of $1,000 or
29 more.
30 2. A class 4 felony if the consideration has a value of more than
31 $100 but less than $1,000.
32 3. A class 6 felony if the consideration has a value of $100 or
33 less.
