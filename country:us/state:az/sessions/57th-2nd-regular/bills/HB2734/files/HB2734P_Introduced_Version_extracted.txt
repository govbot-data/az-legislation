HB2734 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: employer requirement; dependent care benefits State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HB 2734 Introduced by Representative De Los Santos AN ACT amending title 23, chapter 2, article 1, arizona revised statutes, by adding section 23-207; relating to employment practices. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Title 23, chapter 2, article 1, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended by adding section 23-207, to read: START_STATUTE23-207. Dependent care assistance program; employer mandate All employers in this state shall offer to its employees a dependent care assistance program as described in 26 United States Code section 129. END_STATUTE Sec. 2. Effective date This act is effective from and after December 31, 2027.
