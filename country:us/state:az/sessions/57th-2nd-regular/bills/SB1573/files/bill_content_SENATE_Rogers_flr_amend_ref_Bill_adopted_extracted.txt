Rogers Floor Amendment
Reference to: printed bill
Amendment drafted by: Leg. Council
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
1. Specifies that a court is prohibited from relying on any religious sectarian law as controlling or
persuasive authority.
2. Removes language stating that a decision or ratification of a private agreement by a judge who
relies on sectarian law is void, constitutes appealable error and is grounds for impeachment.
3. Modifies the definition of religious sectarian law by removing the specific inclusion of Sharia
law, Canon law, Halacha and Karma.
4. Modifies the legislative findings clause.
5. Makes technical and conforming changes.
