ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
SB 1326: victims' rights; attorney fees; costs
BLACKMAN FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Specifies a mens rea of intentionally, knowingly or with gross negligence for
the violation of a victim's rights.
2. Specifies that the court's ordering of a party only applies when the party
violates, rather than violates or exacerbates a violation of, a victim's rights.
3. Mirrors the expansion of recoverable expenses for victim rights into the child
safety title for juvenile offenders (A.R.S. Title 8).
