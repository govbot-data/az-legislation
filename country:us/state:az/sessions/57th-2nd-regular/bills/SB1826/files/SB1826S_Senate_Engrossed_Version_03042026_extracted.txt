SB1826 - 572R - S Ver Senate Engrossed appropriation; wayfinding signs; rural communities (now:  advanced air mobility; appropriation) State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SENATE BILL 1826 AN ACT Appropriating monies to the office of advanced air mobility. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1.1. Appropriation; office of advanced air mobility; operations and support The sum of $500,000 is appropriated from the advanced air mobility fund established by section 28-8604, Arizona Revised Statutes, in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the office of advanced air mobility for operations and support. Sec. 2. Conditional enactment This act does not become effective unless Senate Bill 1827, fifty-seventh legislature, second regular session, relating to aviation, becomes law.
