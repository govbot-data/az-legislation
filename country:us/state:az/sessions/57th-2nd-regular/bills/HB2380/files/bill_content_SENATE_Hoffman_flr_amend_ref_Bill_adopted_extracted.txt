Hoffman Floor Amendment

Amendment drafted by: Leg. Council
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
1. Replaces the requirement for a school district governing board (governing board) with a student
count of more than 5,000 students to provide the public with access to a live video feed of each
meeting and online access to video recordings of each meeting for at least five years after the date
of the meeting with a requirement for a governing board to provide such materials if:
a) the school district has a student count of more than 250 students; or
b) the school district has a student count of 250 or fewer students and has previously provided the
public with a live video feed of a regular governing board meeting.
2. Makes technical changes.
