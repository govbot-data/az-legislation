HB2893 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; Navajo Nation; transportation projects State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HB 2893 Introduced by Representative Tsosie AN ACT Appropriating monies to the department of transportation. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; department of transportation; Navajo Nation division of transportation; transportation projects The following amounts are appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the department of transportation to distribute to the Navajo Nation division of transportation for the following projects: 1. $32,056,900 for graveling and chip sealing and raising the roadway of and for drainage improvements to Navajo Route 8090. 2. $3,184,300 for grading, graveling and stabilizing and for drainage improvements to Navajo Route 8070. 3. $788,300 for grading, graveling and stabilizing and for drainage improvements to Navajo Route 8009.
