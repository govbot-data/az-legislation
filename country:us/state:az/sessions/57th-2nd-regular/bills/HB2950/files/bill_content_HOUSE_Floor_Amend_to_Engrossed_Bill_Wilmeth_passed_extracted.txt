Majority Research Staff
HB 2950: tourism improvement areas; municipalities; counties
WILMETH FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Amends the petition signature requirements to form a tourism improvement
area.
2. Deems a person who signed the petition as having joined the improvement
area.
3. Provides for allowing a person who did not sign the petition to join the
improvement area.
4. Includes notification requirements for a destination marketing organization
and the Treasurer.
5. Makes clarifying changes.

8 IN THIS CHAPTER, UNLESS THE CONTEXT OTHERWISE REQUIRES:
9 1. "BOARD" MEANS THE BOARD OF SUPERVISORS OF THE COUNTY.

11 ORGANIZATION OR GOVERNMENTAL UNIT THAT IS DESIGNATED BY THE GOVERNING BODY
12 AS BEING RESPONSIBLE FOR THE TOURISM PROMOTION AND MARKETING OF THE
13 MUNICIPALITY OR COUNTY.
14 3. "GOVERNING BODY" MEANS THE GOVERNING BODY OR BOARD THAT BY LAW
15 IS CONSTITUTED AS THE LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT OF THE MUNICIPALITY OR
16 COUNTY.
17 4. "LODGING BUSINESS":
18 (a) MEANS ANY BUSINESS PROVIDING TRANSIENT LODGING AS PRESCRIBED BY
19 SECTION 42-5070.
20 (b) INCLUDES A PROPERTY THAT BEGINS PROVIDING TRANSIENT LODGING AS
21 PRESCRIBED BY SECTION 42-5070 AFTER THE ESTABLISHMENT OF A TOURISM
22 IMPROVEMENT AREA THAT INCLUDES THE PROPERTY.
23 5. "LODGING BUSINESS ASSESSMENT" MEANS A LEVY ON ROOM RENTALS AT A
24 BUSINESS THAT IS CLASSIFIED UNDER SECTION 42-5070.
25 6. "LODGING BUSINESS OWNER" MEANS A PERSON RECOGNIZED AS THE OWNER
26 OF A LODGING BUSINESS THAT IS SUBJECT TO A LODGING BUSINESS ASSESSMENT.
27 7. "MUNICIPALITY" MEANS A CITY OR TOWN.

1 48-6508. Renewal; dissolution; remaining revenue
2 A. AN ESTABLISHED TOURISM IMPROVEMENT AREA MAY BE RENEWED FOR A NEW
3 TERM. A RENEWED TOURISM IMPROVEMENT AREA MAY ENGAGE IN DIFFERENT TOURISM
4 IMPROVEMENT AREA ACTIVITIES THAN THE ORIGINAL TOURISM IMPROVEMENT AREA.
5 ON RENEWAL OF A TOURISM IMPROVEMENT AREA, THE GOVERNING BODY SHALL
6 TRANSFER ANY REMAINING MONIES HELD BY THE TOURISM IMPROVEMENT AREA TO THE
7 RENEWED TOURISM IMPROVEMENT AREA.
8 B. DURING THE TERM OF THE TOURISM IMPROVEMENT AREA, THERE SHALL BE
9 A SIXTY-DAY PERIOD EACH YEAR IN WHICH LODGING BUSINESS OWNERS THAT HAVE
10 PAID LODGING BUSINESS ASSESSMENTS MAY REQUEST DISSOLUTION OF THE TOURISM
11 IMPROVEMENT AREA. THE INITIAL SIXTY-DAY PERIOD SHALL BEGIN ONE YEAR AFTER
12 THE DATE OF ESTABLISHMENT OF THE TOURISM IMPROVEMENT AREA. ON RECEIPT OF
13 A PETITION SUBMITTED BY THE LODGING BUSINESS OWNERS THAT REPRESENT FIFTY
14 PERCENT OR MORE OF THE TOTAL [NUMBER OF ASSESSED] ROOMS WITHIN THE TOURISM
15 IMPROVEMENT AREA, THE GOVERNING BODY SHALL PASS A RESOLUTION OF INTENTION
16 TO DISSOLVE THE TOURISM IMPROVEMENT AREA. THE GOVERNING BODY SHALL HOLD A
17 HEARING ON THE PETITION TO DISSOLVE THE TOURISM IMPROVEMENT AREA AND SHALL
18 PROVIDE NOTICE OF THE HEARING PURSUANT TO THE NOTICE REQUIREMENTS OF
19 SECTION 48-6504.
20 C. THE GOVERNING BODY MAY ADOPT A RESOLUTION TO DISSOLVE A TOURISM
21 IMPROVEMENT AREA. THE GOVERNING BODY MAY ONLY ADOPT A RESOLUTION TO
22 DISSOLVE A TOURISM IMPROVEMENT AREA WHEN THE TOURISM IMPROVEMENT AREA HAS
23 SATISFIED ALL DEBTS OF THE TOURISM IMPROVEMENT AREA. THE GOVERNING BODY
24 MAY ALSO DISSOLVE A TOURISM IMPROVEMENT AREA IF THE GOVERNING BODY FINDS

6 The legislature finds that:
7 1. Many tourism and lodging businesses located and operating in

9 destinations in other states in attracting overnight visitation.
10 2. Tourism improvement areas will help municipalities within this
11 state to grow leisure, convention and event visitor demand by promoting
12 tourism, business activities, economic growth and employment within this
13 state.
14 3. Because additional services and activities will provide direct
15 benefits to the lodging businesses [that choose to participate] within a
16 tourism improvement area, the most equitable method of financing tourism
17 improvement area activities is to levy a lodging business assessment on
18 lodging businesses that will benefit from a tourism improvement area.
19 4. Tourism and lodging demand generated by a tourism improvement
20 area will increase revenue for this state.
21 5. Tourism lodging business owners should be encouraged to create a
