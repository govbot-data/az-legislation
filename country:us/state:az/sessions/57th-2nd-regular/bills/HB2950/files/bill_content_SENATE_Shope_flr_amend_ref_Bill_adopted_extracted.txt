Bill Number: H.B. 2950
Shope Floor Amendment
Reference to: House engrossed bill
Amendment drafted by: Leg. Council
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
1. Removes the requirement for the destination marking organization that manages a tourism
improvement area to establish lodging business assessments on lodging businesses within the area
and removes the authority for the organization to levy the assessments.
2. Allows a city, town or county governing body to approve a lodging business assessment rate on
presentation of a tourism improvement area formation petition that meets outlined requirements.
3. Requires the lodging business assessment to be levied 90 days after the governing body approves
the formation petition, rather than 90 days after the lodging business owners' board is established.
4. Specifies that a lodging business owner within a tourism improvement area who did not sign the
formation petition is not a tourism improvement area participant.
5. Removes the specification that any lodging business that commences operations during a tourism
improvement area's term is subject to a lodging business assessment.
6. Stipulates that a city, town or county governing body may amend a tourism improvement area's
plan only after approval by a majority of the lodging business owners' board.
7. Specifies that the signatures of two lodging business owners that are required to be included in a
tourism improvement area formation petition must be from lodging business owners that agreed
to participate in the area.
8. Makes technical and conforming changes.

7 Sec. 2. Legislative findings
8 The legislature finds that:
9 1. Many tourism and lodging businesses located and operating in
10 municipalities and counties in this state struggle to compete with
11 destinations in other states in attracting overnight visitation.
12 2. Tourism improvement areas will help municipalities within this
13 state to grow leisure, convention and event visitor demand by promoting
14 tourism, business activities, economic growth and employment within this
15 state.
16 3. Because additional services and activities will provide direct
17 benefits to the lodging businesses that choose to participate within a
18 tourism improvement area, the most equitable method of financing tourism
19 improvement area activities is to levy a lodging business assessment on
20 lodging businesses that will benefit from a tourism improvement area.
21 4. Tourism and lodging demand generated by a tourism improvement
22 area will increase revenue for this state.
23 5. Tourism lodging business owners should be encouraged to create a
24 tourism improvement area to enhance the local tourism business climate.
25 6. Tourism improvement area activities are intended to supplement
26 and not replace existing governmental activities and services.
