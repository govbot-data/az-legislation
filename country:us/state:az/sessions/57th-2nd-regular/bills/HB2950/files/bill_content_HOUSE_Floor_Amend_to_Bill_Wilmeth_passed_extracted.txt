FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2950: tourism improvement areas; municipalities; counties
WILMETH FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Revises the contents of the tourism improvement plan.
2. Requires the owner's board to submit the approved tourism improvement area
map to the Department of Revenue.
3. Requires the governing board to provide a notice of the approved tourism
improvement area plan amendment to the Department of Revenue if the plan
amendment includes a new or increased assessment.
4. Includes a process allowing a lodging business to withdraw from the tourism
improvement area.
5. Exempts a lodging business that commences operations during the tourism
improvement area's term from the lodging business assessment if the business
has withdrawn from the tourism improvement area.
6. Clarifies when a lodging business is subject to the lodging business
assessment.
7. Makes clarifying changes relating to calculating the lodging business
assessment.
