Bill Number: H.B. 2592
Farnsworth Floor Amendment
Reference to: House engrossed bill
Amendment drafted by: Liam Maher
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
1. Removes the requirement that the Legislature approve any emergency or temporary rule that
specifically regulates artificial intelligence systems or computational resources.
2. Makes conforming changes.

Senate Amendments to H.B. 2592
1 1. "ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM" MEANS ANY MACHINE
2 LEARNING-BASED SYSTEM THAT CAN, FOR A GIVEN SET OF OBJECTIVES, GENERATE
3 OUTPUTS SUCH AS CONTENT, PREDICTIONS, RECOMMENDATIONS OR DECISIONS
4 INFLUENCING PHYSICAL OR VIRTUAL ENVIRONMENTS.
5 2. "COMPUTATIONAL RESOURCE" MEANS ANY TOOL, TECHNOLOGY, SYSTEM OR
6 INFRASTRUCTURE, WHETHER DIGITAL, ANALOG OR QUANTUM, THAT FACILITATES
7 COMPUTATION, DATA PROCESSING, STORAGE, TRANSMISSION, MANIPULATION OR USE
8 OF INFORMATION.
9 3. "CONTENT" MEANS ANY DIGITALLY GENERATED, MANIPULATED OR
10 SYNTHESIZED TEXT, IMAGE, AUDIO RECORDING, VIDEO RECORDING,
11 THREE-DIMENSIONAL MODEL OR INTERACTIVE ELEMENT, OR ANY COMBINATION OF
12 THESE, THAT CAN BE PRODUCED AS OUTPUT BY AN ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE
13 SYSTEM.
