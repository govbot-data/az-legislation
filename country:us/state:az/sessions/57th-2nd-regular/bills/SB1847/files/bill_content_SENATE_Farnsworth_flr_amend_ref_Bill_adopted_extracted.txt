FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
1. Specifies that, of the two full-time equivalent positions (FTEs) appropriated to the Arizona
Department of Agriculture (AZDA) from the Commercial Feed Trust Fund, one FTE is for
expenses associated with screwworm response and one FTE is for expenses related to a foreign
animal disease or pest, a U.S. Department of Agriculture Program disease, a zoonotic disease or
any other emerging animal disease responses.
2. Allows AZDA to conduct inspections as part of the response at the border if the border opens for
live cattle imports and the State Veterinarian can oversee the screwworm response.
3. Allows, rather than requires, the Arizona Department of Corrections, Rehabilitation and Reentry
to prioritize inmates closest to reentry for participation in the Braille Transcription Program.
4. Increases the lump sum appropriation to the Arizona Criminal Justice Commission (ACJC) by
$32,500 and adds the Peace Officer Training Equipment Fund as a funding source for ACJC.
5. Lowers the Department of Economic Security's appropriated FTE positions by 187 FTEs.
6. Removes the requirement for $2,000,000 from the FY 2026 appropriation to the School Safety
Program to be used for the School Safety and Threat Identification Pilot Program.
7. Adds water facilities to the list of eligible recipients to whom the Arizona Department of Homeland
Security can award a cyber security grant.
8. Increases the Arizona Vehicle Theft Task Force appropriation under the Department of Insurance
and Financial Institutions (DIFI) appropriation by $100,000 and decreases the Automobile Theft
Authority operating budget by $100,000.
9. Increases the appropriated FTEs to DIFI by 9 FTEs.
10. Requires the monies appropriated to the Arizona Vehicle Theft Task Force to be used to pay for
100 percent of overtime expenditures for city, town and county sworn officers who participate in
the Task Force.
11. Stipulates that, if any monies remain from the appropriation for the probation electronic
monitoring system operating costs, the Administrative Office of the Courts can use the remaining
monies to operate the system for other violent offender populations.
