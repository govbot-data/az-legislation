HB2596 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; tribal shelter beds State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HB 2596 Introduced by Representatives Peshlakai: Austin, Cavero, Connolly, Liguori, Márquez, Sandoval, Villegas AN ACT appropriating monies to the department of economic security. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; department of economic security; tribal domestic violence shelter beds; exemption A. The sum of $5,000,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the department of economic security to distribute to tribal governments in this state for additional domestic violence shelter beds. B. The appropriation made in subsection A of this section is exempt from the provisions of section 35-190, Arizona Revised Statutes, relating to lapsing of appropriations.
