SB1091 - 572R - I Ver PREFILED    JAN 05 2026 REFERENCE TITLE: public offices; holidays; technical correction State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SB 1091 Introduced by Senator Finchem AN ACT amending section 1-302, Arizona Revised Statutes; relating to holidays. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Section 1-302, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended to read: START_STATUTE1-302. Closing of offices and courts; transaction of certain judicial business A. Public offices shall not be open, and no court of justice shall be open or any judicial business transacted on a legal holiday, except for the following purposes: 1. To give upon on its request, instructions to a jury deliberating on its the jury's verdict. 2. To receive a verdict or discharge a jury. 3. For the exercise of the powers of a magistrate in a criminal action or in a proceeding of a criminal nature. B. Injunctions, attachments, process for claim and delivery and writs of prohibition may be issued and served on any day. END_STATUTE
