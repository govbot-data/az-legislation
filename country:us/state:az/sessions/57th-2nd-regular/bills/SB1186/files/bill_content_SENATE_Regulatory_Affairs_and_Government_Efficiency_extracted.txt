2 Section 1. Title 41, chapter 23, article 1, Arizona Revised
3 Statutes, is amended by adding section 41-2505, to read:
4 41-2505. State contracts; request for proposals; governor;
5 donations; disclosure
6 NOTWITHSTANDING ANY OTHER LAW, A COMPANY [THAT CURRENTLY HAS A STATE
7 CONTRACT WITH ANY STATE AGENCY OR] THAT RESPONDS TO A REQUEST FOR
8 PROPOSALS SHALL DISCLOSE ANYTHING OF VALUE THAT THE COMPANY, ITS OFFICERS
9 OR DIRECTORS OR ANY OF THEIR FAMILY MEMBERS HAVE PROVIDED, DIRECTLY OR
10 INDIRECTLY, DURING THE PRECEDING FIVE YEARS TO ANY OF THE FOLLOWING:
11 1. THE GOVERNOR.
12 2. ANY ENTITY THAT IS ESTABLISHED, FINANCED, MAINTAINED OR
13 CONTROLLED BY THE GOVERNOR OR AN AGENT OF THE GOVERNOR, INCLUDING A
14 CAMPAIGN COMMITTEE, A JOINT FUNDRAISING COMMITTEE OR AN INAUGURAL FUND.
15 3. AN ENTITY THAT ADVOCATES FOR THE ELECTION OF THE GOVERNOR OR FOR
16 THE DEFEAT OF AN ELECTORAL OPPONENT OF THE GOVERNOR, INCLUDING A POLITICAL
17 COMMITTEE OR OTHER NONPROFIT ORGANIZATION THAT MAKES ANY INDEPENDENT
18 EXPENDITURES.
