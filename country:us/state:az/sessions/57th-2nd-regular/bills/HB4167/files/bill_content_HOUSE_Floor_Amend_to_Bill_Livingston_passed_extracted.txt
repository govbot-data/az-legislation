ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 4167: state properties; management; 2026-2027
LIVINGSTON FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Authorizes a commemorative plaque that recognizes the service of the Arizona
America 250 Commission.
2. Specifies that the procedures in statute relating to monuments and memorials
apply to the establishment of the commemorative plaque.
3. Asserts that all funding and contracts related to the commemorative plaque are
the proponents' sole responsibility.
4. Prohibits the use of public monies for the cost of the commemorative plaque and
Arizona from facilitating fundraising for the deposit of the monies.
5. Repeals the legislation on October 1, 2029.
