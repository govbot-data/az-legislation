FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2050: radiologic technologists; radiologist assistants
BLISS FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Allows DHS to approve a satisfactory standard for a school of radiologic
technology that is accredited as recognized by the U.S. Department of
Education.
2. Adds a legislative intent clause.
3. Makes technical changes.

39 E. A radiologist assistant shall not interpret images, make
