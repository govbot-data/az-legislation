FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2793: annexation of territory; procedures
CARBONE FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Requires municipalities to provide notice as prescribed in statute relating to
the deletion of an annexed area from fire district territory if any part or all of
the territory of a fire district is part of the territory that is proposed to be
annexed.
