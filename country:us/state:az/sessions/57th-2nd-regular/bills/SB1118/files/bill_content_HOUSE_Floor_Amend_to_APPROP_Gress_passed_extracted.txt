FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
SB 1118: rural hospitals; radiation protection grants
S/E: historic neighborhoods; housing; zoning
GRESS FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Removes the provisions relating to the building form and dimensional
standards for municipal middle housing developed.
2. Removes demolished structures on sites that are designated as historic by the
municipality from areas where middle housing cannot be developed unless
the demolition was necessary due to conditions that constitute a danger to
public health, safety and welfare.
