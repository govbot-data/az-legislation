2 Section 1. Appropriation; department of health services;
3 rural hospitals; radiation protection systems;
4 grants; definitions
5 A. The sum of [$________] [$3,000,000] is appropriated from the
6 state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the department of health
7 services to establish and administer a grant program to assist rural
8 hospitals with the costs of acquiring and installing radiation protection
9 systems in [cardiac catheterization] procedure rooms in which ionizing
10 radiation procedures occur.
11 B. For the purposes of this section:
12 1. "Radiation protection system" means a shielding system that is
13 designed to protect against scatter or direct ionizing radiation exposure,
14 that provides protection equal to or greater than the shielding
15 effectiveness of a .25 millimeter lead-equivalent apron and that includes
16 real-time dosimetry to measure radiation exposure to health professionals
17 throughout an ionizing radiation medical procedure.
18 2. "Rural hospital" means a licensed hospital that is located in a
19 county with a population of less than one million persons.
