Bill Number: H.B. 2428
Shope Floor Amendment
Reference to: House engrossed bill
Amendment drafted by: Leg. Council
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
1. Specifies that an applicant's election to participate in any program that results in the certification
of emission reduction credits (ERCs) by a county is voluntary.
2. Prohibits a control officer, if after the general effective date, participation of an on-road vehicle
fleet in the ERC program becomes mandatory pursuant to an act of the Arizona Legislature,
administrative rule or State Implementation Plan commitment, from certifying any new surplus
ERCs that are mobile sources within the county's jurisdiction.
3. Specifies that only mobile source fleets with a base of operations in a nonattainment area are
eligible for certification of ERCs by a county.
4. Makes technical changes.
