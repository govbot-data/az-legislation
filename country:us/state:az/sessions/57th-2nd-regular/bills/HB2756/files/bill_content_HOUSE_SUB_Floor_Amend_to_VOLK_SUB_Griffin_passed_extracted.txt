ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2756: utilities; high load factor customers
GRIFFIN FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Defines new extra high load factor customers. (Sec. 1, 2)
2. Modifies the rules that can be adopted by the Arizona Corporation
Commission (ACC) as it relates to governing the terms of service for extra
high load factor customers. (Sec. 2)
3. Removes the requirement that the ACC conduct a workshop to determine
certain factor relating to extra high load factor customer's customer class or
rates.
4. Expands the legislative findings section. (Sec. 4)
