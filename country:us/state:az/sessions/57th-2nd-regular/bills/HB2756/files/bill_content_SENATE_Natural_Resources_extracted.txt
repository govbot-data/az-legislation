5 Sec. 3. Cost-of-service studies; exemption
6 Within one hundred eighty days after the effective date of this act:
7 1. An electric public service corporation operating in this state
8 shall file with the corporation commission a cost-of-service study that
9 shows the cost allocation of serving new extra high load factor customers.
10 This paragraph does not apply to member-owned electric cooperatives.
11 2. A public power entity that services more than one million
12 connections in this state shall file with the governing body of the public
13 power entity a cost-of-service study that shows the cost allocation of
14 serving new extra high load factor customers.
15 Sec. 4. Legislative findings
16 The legislature finds that this act:
17 1. Is necessary pursuant to article XV, section 3, Constitution of
18 Arizona, "for the convenience, comfort, and safety, and the preservation
19 of the health, of patrons of such corporations".
20 2. Does not attempt to set or establish any specific rates or
21 charges to be made or collected by public service corporations or require
22 the corporation commission to set or adopt any specific rates or charges
23 to be made or collected by a public service corporation.
