SB 1429: board of directors; commerce authority.
S/E: ballot measures; circulators; disclosure
KOLODIN FLOOR AMENDMENT
Requires a circulator of an initiative or referendum petition to state whether the
circulator is paid, a volunteer or an out-of-state resident by checking all applicable
lines regarding the status of the circulator, rather than the appropriate line, on an
initiative or referendum petition sheet prior to circulating the petition for
signatures.
