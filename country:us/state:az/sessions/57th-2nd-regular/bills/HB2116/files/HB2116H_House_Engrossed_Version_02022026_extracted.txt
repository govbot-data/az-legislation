HB2116 - 572R - H Ver House Engrossed appropriation; Colorado River litigation fund State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HOUSE BILL 2116 AN ACT Appropriating monies to the Colorado River litigation fund. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; Colorado River litigation fund The sum of $1,000,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the Colorado River litigation fund established by section 45-119, Arizona Revised Statutes.
