SB1041 - 572R - S Ver Senate Engrossed appropriation; Arizona trail fund State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SENATE BILL 1041 AN ACT Appropriating monies to the Arizona trail fund. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1.1. Appropriation; Arizona trail fund The sum of $100,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the Arizona trail fund established by section 41-511.15, Arizona Revised Statutes.
