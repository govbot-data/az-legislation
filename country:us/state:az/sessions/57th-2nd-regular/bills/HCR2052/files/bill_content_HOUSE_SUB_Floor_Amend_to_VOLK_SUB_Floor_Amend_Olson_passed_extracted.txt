HCR2052: rates; fees; taxes; increases; moratorium
OLSON FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. States that the moratorium on municipal and county fees, taxes and utility
rates does not apply to ad valorem taxes or special assessments levied to pay
for:
a. the principal, interest and redemption charges on bonded indebtedness
and other lawful long-term obligations;
b. property improvement assessment districts, improvement districts and
other special purpose districts; and
c. the support of a school district. (Sec. 1, 3)
2. Excepts a municipality that has not raised utility rates by twelve percent or
more in the prior four fiscal years from the moratorium but only allows an
increase of more than that of inflation determined by the consumer price
index. (Sec. 1)
3. Allows a municipality to adopt, impose or collect a new or increased rate or
fee to fund the acquisition and delivery of new or additional water resources.
(Sec. 1)

36 Sec. 6. Legislative findings and intent
37 A. The legislature finds that:
38 1. Arizona residents and businesses continue to
39 experience elevated inflation and rising costs, including
40 housing, utilities, construction, labor and essential
41 services, placing increased pressure on household budgets and
42 economic activity.
43 2. Local government taxes, fees and utility rates
44 materially affect affordability, business formation, housing
45 development and the overall cost of living throughout this
46 state.

17 6. Temporary restraint on local fee, tax and utility

19 efficiency, innovation and responsible fiscal management,
20 while preserving the ability to meet service demands through
21 improved processes and increased service volume.
22 7. Local governments retain discretion to manage
23 expenditures, staffing, service levels and capital planning
24 within existing fee, tax and utility rate structures during
25 the moratorium period.
26 B. It is the intent of the legislature to:
27 1. Provide temporary relief to residents and businesses
28 by stabilizing locally imposed fees, taxes and utility rates
29 during a period of elevated inflation and economic
30 uncertainty.
31 2. Encourage local governments to pursue efficiencies
32 and modernization before increasing costs borne by residents
33 and businesses.
34 3. Preserve predictability and transparency in local
35 government fee, tax and rate structures by anchoring charges
36 to duly adopted budgets and schedules.
37 4. Respect constitutional limitations, existing
38 contractual obligations and voter-approved authorities, while
39 promoting affordability and economic stability statewide.
