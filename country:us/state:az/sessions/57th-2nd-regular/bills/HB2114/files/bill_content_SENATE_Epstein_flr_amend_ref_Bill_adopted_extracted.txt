Bill Number: H.B. 2114
Epstein Floor Amendment
Reference to: House engrossed bill
Amendment drafted by: Leg. Council
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
1. Specifies ADOT may renew a registration for a motorcycle only if at least one of the registered
owners of the motorcycle is licensed to legally operate a motorcycle in Arizona.
2. Stipulates that, if the registered owner of a motorcycle is a trust, a trustee named in the trust must
be legally licensed to operate a motorcycle to renew a registration.
3. Applies the prescribed requirements only to motorcycle registration renewals, rather than to initial
issuances of registrations and renewals.
4. Exempts motorcycles owned by a business from prescribed requirements.
5. Contains a delayed effective date of January 1, 2027.
