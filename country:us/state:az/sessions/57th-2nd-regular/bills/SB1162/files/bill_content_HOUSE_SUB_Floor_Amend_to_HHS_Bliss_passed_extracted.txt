ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
SB 1162: health care institutions; licensing; compliance
BLISS FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Incorporates the HHS Committee amendment with revisions.
2. Revises the definition of behavioral health technician.
3. Allows a behavioral health technician to provide services consistent with the
individual's training, delegation and supervision, as outlined.

31 CONSISTENT WITH THE INDIVIDUAL'S DEMONSTRATED TRAINING AND COMPETENCE AND

34 36-444.01. Qualifications; standards
35 [A. A BEHAVIORAL HEALTH TECHNICIAN SHALL:
36 1. BE AT LEAST EIGHTEEN YEARS OF AGE.
37 2. POSSESS A MINIMUM OF A HIGH SCHOOL DIPLOMA OR THE EQUIVALENT.
38 3. SUCCESSFULLY COMPLETE ANY REQUIRED BACKGROUND CHECKS.
39 B. BEFORE PROVIDING SUPERVISED DIRECT SERVICES, A BEHAVIORAL HEALTH

43 3. PROFESSIONAL BOUNDARIES AND ETHICS.
44 4. CRISIS RESPONSE AND DE-ESCALATION TECHNIQUES AND SKILLS.
