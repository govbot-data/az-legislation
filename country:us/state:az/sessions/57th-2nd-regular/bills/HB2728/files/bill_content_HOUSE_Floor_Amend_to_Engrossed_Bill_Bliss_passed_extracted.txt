FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2728: department of economic security; continuation
BLISS FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Outlines evaluation and verification requirements for DES to confirm
eligibility for SNAP benefits.
2. Contains provisions relating to hospital reporting of patient immigration
status.
3. Contains provisions relating to the SNAP payment error rate.
