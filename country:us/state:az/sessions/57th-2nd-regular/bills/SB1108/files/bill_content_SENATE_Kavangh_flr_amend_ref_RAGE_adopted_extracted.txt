8 44-7952. Cash transactions; mandatory rounding of transaction
9 totals; notice; enforcement
10 A. IF ONE-CENT COINS ARE NOT AVAILABLE OR ARE NOT USED BY A SELLER
11 AT A POINT OF SALE, THE SELLER SHALL APPLY SWEDISH ROUNDING TO A TOTAL
12 TRANSACTION AMOUNT THAT ROUNDS THE TOTAL TRANSACTION AMOUNT TO THE NEAREST
13 FIVE-CENT INCREMENT. IF A TOTAL TRANSACTION AMOUNT ENDS:

1 1. IN ONE OR TWO CENTS, THE TOTAL TRANSACTION AMOUNT IS ROUNDED
2 DOWN TO THE NEAREST MULTIPLE OF FIVE CENTS.
3 2. IN THREE OR FOUR CENTS, THE TOTAL TRANSACTION AMOUNT IS ROUNDED
4 UP TO THE NEAREST MULTIPLE OF FIVE CENTS.

11 C. SWEDISH ROUNDING DOES NOT APPLY TO[:
12 1. THE SALE OF AN INDIVIDUAL ITEM.
13 2. A TRANSACTION THAT IS PAID BY A CREDIT CARD, DEBIT CARD,

19 THAT IS VISIBLE TO CUSTOMERS AT THE POINT OF SALE STATING "CASH
20 TRANSACTIONS ARE ROUNDED TO THE NEAREST FIVE-CENT INCREMENT PURSUANT TO
21 STATE LAW".
22 E. A SELLER MAY NOT APPLY A ROUNDING METHOD OTHER THAN SWEDISH
23 ROUNDING.
24 [F. A SELLER SHALL CALCULATE AND REMIT ALL TAXES AND FEES THAT ARE
