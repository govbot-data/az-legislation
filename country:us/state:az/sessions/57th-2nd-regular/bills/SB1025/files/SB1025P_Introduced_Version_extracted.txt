SB1025 - 572R - I Ver PREFILED    DEC 11 2025 REFERENCE TITLE: science curricula; evolution; intelligent design State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SB 1025 Introduced by Senator Farnsworth AN ACT amending title 15, chapter 7, article 1, Arizona Revised Statutes, by adding section 15-709; relating to school curricula. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Title 15, chapter 7, article 1, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended by adding section 15-709, to read: START_STATUTE15-709. Science curricula; instructional requirements Notwithstanding any other law, a public school may provide instruction in evolution by natural selection only if the school provides concurrent instruction in intelligent design. END_STATUTE
