22 IN THIS CHAPTER, UNLESS THE CONTEXT OTHERWISE REQUIRES:
23 1. "ANONYMOUS AGE VERIFICATION" MEANS A COMMERCIALLY REASONABLE
24 METHOD THAT IS USED BY A GOVERNMENT AGENCY OR A BUSINESS FOR THE PURPOSE
25 OF AGE VERIFICATION THAT IS CONDUCTED BY A NONGOVERNMENTAL, INDEPENDENT
26 THIRD PARTY THAT IS ORGANIZED UNDER THE LAWS OF ANY STATE OF THE UNITED
27 STATES THAT BOTH:
28 (a) HAS ITS PRINCIPAL PLACE OF BUSINESS IN ANY STATE OF THE UNITED

34 THAT A USER MAY RUN OR DIRECT ON A COMPUTER, A MOBILE DEVICE OR ANY OTHER
35 GENERAL PURPOSE COMPUTING DEVICE.

37 APPLICATION, ELECTRONIC SERVICE OR PLATFORM THAT DISTRIBUTES AND
38 FACILITATES THE DOWNLOAD OF APPLICATIONS FROM THIRD-PARTY DEVELOPERS TO
39 USERS OF A COMPUTER, A MOBILE DEVICE OR ANY OTHER GENERAL PURPOSE
40 COMPUTING DEVICE.

42 AGE.
43 5. "COMMERCIAL ENTITY" INCLUDES A CORPORATION, A LIMITED LIABILITY
44 COMPANY, A PARTNERSHIP, A LIMITED PARTNERSHIP, A SOLE PROPRIETORSHIP AND

19 44-8044. Age signal conflicts
20 A. IF A DEVELOPER DETERMINES THAT AN INTERNAL AGE SIGNAL CONFLICTS
21 WITH THE AGE SIGNAL FROM A COVERED COMPANY, THE DEVELOPER MAY RELY ON THE
22 AGE SIGNAL FROM THE COVERED COMPANY UNLESS THE DEVELOPER HAS CLEAR AND
23 CONVINCING EVIDENCE THAT AN INTERNAL AGE SIGNAL IS MORE ACCURATE. IF THE
24 DEVELOPER HAS CLEAR AND CONVINCING EVIDENCE THAT THE DEVELOPER'S INTERNAL
25 AGE SIGNAL IS MORE ACCURATE, INCLUDING IF THE USER PROVIDED PROOF OF AGE
26 TO THE DEVELOPER, THE DEVELOPER SHALL USE THE DEVELOPER'S INTERNAL SIGNAL.
27 B. IF APPLICABLE AND TECHNICALLY FEASIBLE, A DEVELOPER SHALL
28 TRANSMIT THE CONFLICTING INTERNAL AGE SIGNAL BACK TO A COVERED COMPANY
29 THROUGH A MECHANISM THAT IS PROVIDED BY THE COVERED COMPANY IF THE
30 DEVELOPER HAS CLEAR AND CONVINCING EVIDENCE THAT AN INTERNAL AGE SIGNAL IS
31 MORE ACCURATE.
32 C. ON RECEIPT OF A CONFLICTING INTERNAL AGE SIGNAL FROM A
33 DEVELOPER, THE COVERED COMPANY SHALL TAKE STEPS TO REVERIFY THE USER'S AGE
34 AND UPDATE THE AGE SIGNAL TO PROVIDE DEVELOPERS WITH THE NEWLY VERIFIED
35 AGE.
