2 Section 1. Section 5-101.01, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended
3 to read:
4 5-101.01. Division of racing; director; qualifications; term;
5 deputy director; conflict of interest
6 A. There is established a division of racing within the department
7 of gaming.
8 B. The director shall administer the division. To be eligible for
9 appointment as director, a person must have a minimum of five years of
10 experience in business and administration and shall not have a financial
11 interest in a racetrack or in the racing industry in this state during
12 that person's appointment.
13 C. The director may establish the position of deputy director of
14 the division.
15 D. The position of deputy director, if applicable, is exempt from
16 title 41, chapter 4, articles 5 and 6. The deputy director, if
17 applicable, is eligible to receive compensation pursuant to section
18 38-611.
19 E. The provisions of Title 38, chapter 3, article 8, relating to
20 conflict of interest, apply APPLIES to the director and all other
21 employees of the department.
22 F. Neither the director, any employee of the department nor any
23 member of the immediate family of the director or other employee of the
24 department may:
25 1. Have any pecuniary interest in a racetrack in this state or in
26 any stable, compound or farm licensed under this chapter.
27 2. Wager money at a racetrack enclosure or additional wagering
28 facility in this state or wager money on the results of any race held at a
29 racetrack enclosure in this state.
30 3. Hold more than a five percent interest in any entity doing
31 business with a racetrack in this state.
32 4. Have any interest, whether direct or indirect, in a license
33 issued pursuant to this chapter or in a licensee, facility or entity that
34 is involved in any way with pari-mutuel wagering. For the purposes of
35 this paragraph, "interest" includes employment.
