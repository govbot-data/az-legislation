SB1738 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: Coconino county; juvenile services; appropriation State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SB 1738 Introduced by Senators Hatathlie: Gonzales, Kuby AN ACT Appropriating monies to the attorney general. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; attorney general; Coconino county; juvenile services; exemption A. The sum of $20,000,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the attorney general to distribute to Coconino county for the purpose of constructing and purchasing equipment for a new juvenile court services facility and converting the existing juvenile detention center into a detox, sobriety and crisis recovery center. B. The appropriation made in subsection A of this section is exempt from the provisions of section 35-190, Arizona Revised Statutes, relating to lapsing of appropriations.
