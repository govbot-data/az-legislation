ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2147: deer; taking; landowner permits
GRIFFIN FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Modifies the conditions that the Game and Fish Commission may use in
determining the necessity for issuing a landowner permit for the taking of
deer. (Sec. 1)
2. Prohibits a person from transferring a landowner permit. (Sec. 1)

4 17-321. Private lands; taking deer; landowner permits
5 A. THE DEPARTMENT SHALL ISSUE A LANDOWNER PERMIT FOR THE TAKING OF
6 DEER ON PRIVATE LANDS.
7 B. THE DEPARTMENT SHALL ISSUE A LANDOWNER PERMIT IF THE COMMISSION
8 DETERMINES THAT THE PERMIT IS NECESSARY [FOR WILDLIFE POPULATION
9 MANAGEMENT OR] TO REDUCE CONFLICTS BETWEEN HUMANS AND WILDLIFE [AND TO
10 PROVIDE SPORT HUNTING OPPORTUNITIES CONSISTENT WITH RULES ADOPTED BY THE
11 DEPARTMENT] [OR BETWEEN PROPERTY AND WILDLIFE].
12 C. [THE DEPARTMENT MAY NOT ISSUE A LANDOWNER PERMIT PURSUANT TO
13 THIS SECTION FOR DEER IF THE TOTAL NUMBER OF PERMITS ISSUED FOR DEER
14 EXCEEDS TEN PERCENT OF THE TOTAL NUMBER OF TAGS ISSUED FOR DEER THROUGH
15 THE GENERAL LOTTERY DRAW DURING THE CALENDAR YEAR] [A PERSON MAY NOT
16 TRANSFER A LANDOWNER PERMIT TO ANY OTHER PERSON].
