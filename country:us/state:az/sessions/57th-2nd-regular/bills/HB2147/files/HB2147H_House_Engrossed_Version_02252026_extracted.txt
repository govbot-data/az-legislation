HB2147 - 572R - H Ver House Engrossed deer; taking; landowner permits State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HOUSE BILL 2147 AN ACT Amending title 17, chapter 3, ARTICLE 1, Arizona Revised Statutes, by adding section 17-321; RELATING to the taking of wildlife. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1.1. Title 17, chapter 3, article 1, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended by adding section 17-321, to read: START_STATUTE17-321. Private lands; taking deer; landowner permits A. The department shall issue a landowner permit for the taking of deer on private lands. B. The department shall issue a landowner permit if the commission determines that the permit is necessary FOR wildlife population management or to reduce conflicts between humans and wildlife or BETWEEN property and wildlife. C. A person may not transfer a landowner permit to any other person. END_STATUTE
