SB1654 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: appropriations; end-point monitoring; elections State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SB 1654 Introduced by Senator Finchem AN ACT appropriating monies to the state treasurer. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; state treasurer; end-point monitoring of elections and nefarious signal interception The sum of $100,000 is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the state treasurer to reimburse counties for end-point monitoring of elections and nefarious signal interception.
