FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
1. Requires a defamation action involving a defamatory statement that is published on the internet to
be commenced within one year of the date that the person removes the defamatory statement from
the internet site or internet-based platform where the person originally published the defamatory
statement.
2. Defines internet-based platform as:
a) a digital service that facilitates user interaction, content creation or information exchange;
b) a social media service;
c) a content sharing service; or
d) a cloud based service.
3. Removes the specification that a plaintiff's showing in certain defamation cases is subject to
appellate review.
4. Makes technical changes.
