HB2971 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: occupational licensing; fee deferment State of Arizona House of Representatives Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 HB 2971 Introduced by Representative Carter N AN ACT Amending title 32, chapter 43, ARTICLE 1, Arizona Revised Statutes, by adding section 32-4305; relating to fees. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Title 32, chapter 43, article 1, Arizona Revised Statutes, is amended by adding section 32-4305, to read: START_STATUTE32-4305. Initial license, registration and certification fees; deferment Notwithstanding any other law, a person shall pay the initial license, registration or certification fee for any profession or occupation that is licensed, registered or certified pursuant to this title at the beginning of the second year the person is licensed, registered or certified in this state. END_STATUTE
