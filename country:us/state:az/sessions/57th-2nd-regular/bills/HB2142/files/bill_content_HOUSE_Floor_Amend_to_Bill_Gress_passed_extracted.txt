HB 2142: ADE; school safety; center; programs
GRESS FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Allows the School Safety Center (Center) to annually use up to $6,500,000,
rather than up to 10%, of the monies appropriated for the School Safety
Program.
2. Expands the duties of the Center to include:
a. providing training related to prevention measures that promote school
safety;
b. researching and evaluating prevention measures that promote school
safety;
c. adopting guidelines for threat vulnerability assessments, rather than
threat prevention and vulnerability mitigation;
d. adopting guidelines for coordination between schools, specified state
agencies and any local law enforcement agency, emergency medical services
provider or fire department that provides services to schools; and
e. providing technical assistance to state and local law enforcement agencies
to enhance each agency's ability to respond to online threats against
schools.
3. Establishes the 17-member School Safety Center Council (Council) to support
the Center.
4. Directs Council members to select a chairperson from among its members each
calendar year.
5. States Council members are not eligible to receive compensation but are
eligible for reimbursement of expenses as authorized by statute.
6. Details the terms of Council members.
7. Requires the Arizona Department of Education to provide meeting space and
administrative support to the Council.
