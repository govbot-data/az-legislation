ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
SB 1670: municipalities; counties; occupation; licensure; prohibition
WENINGER FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Removes municipal and county authority to require a person to be licensed
and issue a journeyman license for specified occupations.

2 Section 1. Title 9, chapter 4, article 8, Arizona Revised Statutes,
3 is amended by adding section 9-500.54, to read:
4 9-500.54. State preemption; contractors; journeymen;
5 licensure; prohibition; definitions
6 A. THE LICENSURE OF PERSONS SUBJECT TO TITLE 32, CHAPTER 10 IS OF
7 STATEWIDE CONCERN. THE LICENSURE OF PERSONS SUBJECT TO TITLE 32, CHAPTER
8 10 IS NOT SUBJECT TO FURTHER REGULATION BY A MUNICIPALITY EXCEPT AS
9 PROVIDED IN THIS SECTION.
10 B. A MUNICIPALITY MAY NOT REQUIRE A PERSON TO OBTAIN A JOURNEYMAN
11 LICENSE IF THE PERSON IS SUBJECT TO LICENSURE PURSUANT TO TITLE 32,
12 CHAPTER 10.
13 [C. A MUNICIPALITY MAY REQUIRE A PERSON TO BE LICENSED AND MAY
14 ISSUE A JOURNEYMAN LICENSE FOR THE FOLLOWING OCCUPATIONS:
15 1. PLUMBING.
16 2. PIPE FITTING.
17 3. MECHANICAL TRADES.
18 4. HVAC INSTALLATION.
19 5. ELECTRICAL OR ALARM SYSTEM TRADES.
20 D.][C.] FOR THE PURPOSES OF THIS SECTION:
21 1. "LICENSURE" MEANS ANY TRAINING, EDUCATION, TEST, CERTIFICATION,
22 REGISTRATION OR LICENSE THAT IS REQUIRED FOR A PERSON TO PERFORM AN
23 OCCUPATION IN ADDITION TO ANY ASSOCIATED LICENSING FEE.
24 2. "OCCUPATION" MEANS A PAID JOB, PROFESSION, WORK, LINE OF WORK,
25 TRADE, EMPLOYMENT, POSITION, POST, CAREER, FIELD, VOCATION OR CRAFT.
