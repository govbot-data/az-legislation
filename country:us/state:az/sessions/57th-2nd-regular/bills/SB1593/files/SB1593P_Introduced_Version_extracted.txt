SB1593 - 572R - I Ver REFERENCE TITLE: appropriation; adult protective services State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SB 1593 Introduced by Senator Alston: Representative Connolly AN ACT appropriating monies to the department of economic security. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; department of economic security; adult protective services In addition to any other appropriations made in fiscal year 2026-2027, the sum of $3,000,000 and 43 FTE positions are appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the department of economic security for adult protective services.
