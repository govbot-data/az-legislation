20 Sec. 2. Legislative intent and purpose
21 A. The legislature intends that the vision of the technology first
22 study committee is to:
23 1. Increase assistive technology awareness.
24 2. Identify technological solutions.
25 3. Facilitate assistive technology access for all Arizonans with
26 developmental disabilities.
27 B. The purpose of the technology first study committee is to
28 discover and recommend systemic changes regarding assistive technology and
29 other technological solutions to better serve individuals with
30 disabilities across Arizona.
31 Sec. 3. Short title
32 This act may be cited as the "Technology First Study Committee".
