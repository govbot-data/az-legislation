HOUSE FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
Bill Number: HB 2746
Floor Amendment
Austin
1. Adds, to the Study Committee on Trade Offices, the ranking minority party
members (ranking members) of the following committees, or their successor
committees, or the ranking members' designees:
a. the House of Representatives (House) International Trade Committee;
b. the House Commerce Committee;
c. the House Government Committee; and
d. the House Appropriations Committee.
