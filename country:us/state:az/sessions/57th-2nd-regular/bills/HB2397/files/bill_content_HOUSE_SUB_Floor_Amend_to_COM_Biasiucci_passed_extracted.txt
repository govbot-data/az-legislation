ARIZONA HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
57th Legislature, 2nd Regular Session
Majority Research Staff
HB 2397: homeowners' associations; property covenants; disclosures
BIASIUCCI SUBSTITUTE FLOOR AMENDMENT
1. Removes language which added stipulations for enforcing private covenants.
2. Changes the time frame for submitting the specified information to the
purchaser to 10 days after acceptance of the purchaser's offer to purchase.
3. Removes the ability of the unit owner to request the required information be
provided on listing the unit for sale.
4. Revises the specified information that must be submitted to the purchaser.
5. Includes, to the specified information, a statement notifying the purchaser that
the unit is subject to each association's resale disclosure fee, if the unit is
governed by multiple associations.
6. Removes the purchaser's ability to propose a cure to address any concerns
identified or withdraw the offer without penalty or loss of earnest money after
acceptance of the reported information.
7. Specifies all specified information that is submitted to the purchaser must be
based on the good faith reliance on association records or information, without
the need for independent investigation or validation.
8. Removes the requirement for the association to provide to an escrow agent any
required information that is relevant to the close of the escrow at no cost.
9. Strikes language regarding the nominal fee charged to purchasers for updating
the new owner information in the association records.
10. Omits the declarant's requirement, for initial sales of a condominium unit from
the declarant, to provide to any purchaser the prescribed documents relating
to the disclosure report.
11. Removes language relating to mandatory assessment restrictions and
emergency spending stipulations for planned communities.
12. Mirrors the revisions made to the Condominium statutes within the Planned
Community statutes.
13. Makes clarifying changes.
