Ortiz Floor Amendment

Amendment drafted by: Leg. Council
FLOOR AMENDMENT EXPLANATION
1. Caps, at five percent of the total number of single-family residences in any census tract, the total
number of single-family residences located in Arizona that may be purchased by a corporation or
a limited liability company (LLC) or an affiliate of a corporation or an LLC in that census tract.
2. Caps, at 50 units per calendar year, the total number of single-family residences in Arizona that
may be purchased by a corporation or LLC.
3. Prohibits a corporation or LLC from owning more than 2,000 single-family residences in Arizona.
4. Outlines requirements for a corporation or LLC registering with the Securities Division of the
Arizona Corporation Commission (ACC) before purchasing a single-family residence in Arizona.
5. Exempts, from the registration requirements:
a) a corporation or LLC or an affiliate of a corporation or LLC that owns less than 10
single-family residences in Arizona;
b) a governmental entity;
c) any 501(c)(3) nonprofit;
d) a land trust;
e) a group home; and
f) an employer that rents residences to employees.
6. Requires the ACC, beginning February 1, 2027, to annually report of any corporation or LLC that
purchased or sold a single-family resident in the previous year.
7. Prohibits a single-family residence from being conveyed to a hostile foreign entity without
majority approval of the Legislature.
8. Defines terms.
