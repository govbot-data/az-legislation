3 "Section 1. Fuel formulations; waiver request; definitions
4 A. [Notwithstanding the state implementation plan,] within thirty
5 days after the effective date of this act, the director of the department
6 of environmental quality shall in good faith collaborate with the
7 associate director of the weights and measures services division of the
8 Arizona department of agriculture to submit a fuel formulation waiver
9 request for Area A and Area C to the United States environmental
10 protection agency consistent with the waiver process for the regulation of
11 fuels provided in the clean air act.
12 B. The fuel formulation waiver request must:
13 1. Include the available information required by the criteria in
14 the clean air act and must request a twenty-day waiver period. The
15 director of the department of environmental quality shall submit a copy of
16 the request to the president of the senate, the speaker of the house of
17 representatives, the governor and the secretary of state.
18 2. Include all available information in an effort to demonstrate to
19 the United States environmental protection agency that extreme and unusual
20 fuel supply circumstances exist that prevent the distribution of an
21 adequate supply of fuel to consumers, that extreme and unusual fuel supply
22 circumstances are the result of an event that could not reasonably have
23 been foreseen or prevented and that it is in the public interest to grant
24 the fuel formulation waiver. The circumstances and events justifying the
25 requested fuel formulation waiver shall be limited to the adoption and
26 implementation of energy and environmental policies in the state of
27 California and the closure of refineries in that state and may not include
28 any other circumstance or condition.
