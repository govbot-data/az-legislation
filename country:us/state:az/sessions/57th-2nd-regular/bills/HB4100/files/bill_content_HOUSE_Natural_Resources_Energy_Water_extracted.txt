2 Section 1. City, town or private water providers; central
3 Arizona project subcontracts; rates; notice;
4 delayed repeal
5 A. On or before April 15, 2026, each [municipal provider] [city,
6 town or private water provider that provides potable water to customers in
7 the Phoenix, Tucson or Pinal active management areas and] that receives or
8 has an allocation of water from the central Arizona project pursuant to a
9 subcontract with the central Arizona water conservation district organized
10 under title 48, chapter 22, Arizona Revised Statutes, or that receives or
11 has an allocation of water from the central Arizona project pursuant to a
12 lease with another person or subcontract with the central Arizona water
13 conservation district shall do both of the following:
14 1. Estimate the total cost per acre-foot of securing, treating and
15 delivering an alternative water supply if one hundred percent of the water
16 that [is acquired or leased] [the city, town or private water provider
17 receives] directly or indirectly from the central Arizona project is no
18 longer available.
19 2. Provide [written] public notice to each customer of the
20 potential increase in rates resulting from losing one hundred percent of
21 the water that [is acquired or leased] [the city, town or private water
22 provider receives] directly or indirectly from the central Arizona
23 project. The notice shall be in substantially the same form and manner as
24 the public notices required of public service corporations in rate cases
25 by the corporation commission and shall include the following information:
26 (a) The potential revenue increase levels for each customer class,
27 in percent.
