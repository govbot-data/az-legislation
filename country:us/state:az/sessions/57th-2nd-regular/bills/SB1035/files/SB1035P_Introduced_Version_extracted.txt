SB1035 - 572R - I Ver PREFILED    DEC 18 2025 REFERENCE TITLE: state department of corrections; appropriation State of Arizona Senate Fifty-seventh Legislature Second Regular Session 2026 SB 1035 Introduced by Senator Petersen AN ACT appropriating monies to the state department of corrections. (TEXT OF BILL BEGINS ON NEXT PAGE) Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Arizona: Section 1. Appropriation; state department of corrections; pay increase The sum of $_____________ is appropriated from the state general fund in fiscal year 2026-2027 to the state department of corrections for a salary increase of five percent for sworn correctional officers and civilians employed by the department.
